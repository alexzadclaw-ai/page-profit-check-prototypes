About Attuned Studios

Founder-led care with an integrative wellness lens.

Attuned Studios is the evolution of Energetic Innovation & Healing — created to bring body-based wellness, energetic care, and whole-person support into one intentional space. Founder Carrie Christisen brings a broad background across energy medicine, coaching, hypnosis-based work, biofeedback-informed modalities, and body contouring services.

The result is a practice built for people who want personal attention, a gentler pace, and guidance that respects both physical and energetic dimensions of wellness.

Why this matters: clients are not dropped into a rigid menu. They are guided toward the service path that best fits their goals, readiness, and comfort level.
