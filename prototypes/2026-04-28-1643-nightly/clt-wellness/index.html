Meet Carolyn

CLT Wellness was shaped by Carolyn Tallents’ own transition to motherhood and by the gap she saw between clinical information and the kind of grounded support families actually need day to day.

That mix of professional training and lived experience is what makes the practice feel both informed and human: thoughtful movement guidance, practical postpartum support, and education that helps families feel calmer and more prepared.

  • Certified Postpartum Doula
  • Certified Childbirth Educator
  • Certified Holistic Health Coach
  • Certified Personal Trainer with pre/postnatal corrective exercise training

What getting started looks like

A free consultation helps narrow down the best fit before you commit to a program or service.

1. Reach out

Share where you are in the journey, what feels hardest, and what kind of support you are looking for.

2. Talk it through

Review goals, symptoms, schedule, family needs, and whether ongoing support, doula care, or education makes most sense.

3. Build the right plan

Choose a path that fits your season of motherhood, your body, and the amount of support you want right now.
