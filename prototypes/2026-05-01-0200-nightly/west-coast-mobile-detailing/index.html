Detailing menu

Clear service descriptions help customers understand what to ask for before they call.

Wash & Wax

Exterior wash plus carnauba wax and sealer applied with an oscillating buffer for a clean finish and added protection.

  • Good for shine and seasonal protection
  • Ask about vehicle size and current condition

Full Interior Detail

Seats and surfaces are vacuumed, shampooed, wiped down, dried, and treated so the cabin feels fresh again.

  • Helpful for daily drivers, family cars, and resale prep
  • Share any heavy stains or pet hair before booking

Paint Treatment

Targeted care for minor scratches, blemishes, bugs, and tar so the paint has a cleaner, more polished appearance.

  • Best for small defects and road grime
  • Carlos can confirm what is realistic for your vehicle
