First visit questions

Know before you go.

How hot is it?

Sauna Suites uses a traditional dry heat barrel sauna, commonly maintained around 180°F for a classic sauna experience.

How long should I stay in?

Beginners can start with shorter intervals and take a break to cool down. Listen to your body and hydrate well.

Who should check first?

If pregnant, managing serious heart concerns, low blood pressure, or other medical questions, check with a healthcare provider before sauna use.
