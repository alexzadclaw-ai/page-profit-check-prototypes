About Harvest

Harvest Accounting LLC is a dedicated bookkeeping and accounting service for small to medium businesses using QuickBooks and other accounting packages. The firm specializes in the Oregon wine industry while also supporting many other business types.

Based in the Willamette Valley wine region, Harvest can serve clients beyond Beaverton and NW Oregon through digital bookkeeping workflows. The firm can also partner with your existing CPA or tax accountant to create a seamless financial support team.
