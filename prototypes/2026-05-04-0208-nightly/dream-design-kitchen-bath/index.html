Our Process

A remodel has a lot of moving parts. Dream Design keeps the path simple: understand your goals, shape the design, price the work, then guide the details until the finished space feels complete.

1. Preconstruction Design

We discuss your wish list, existing layout, priorities and style direction so the project starts with a real plan instead of guesses.

2. Design & Construction Estimate

You get a clearer scope, material direction and estimate conversation before committing to the next phase.

3. On-Site Consultation

We review measurements, layout constraints, cabinet and bath needs, and what has to happen in your home.

4. Finishing Touches

Selections, punch-list items and final walk-through details are organized so the finished room feels intentional.
