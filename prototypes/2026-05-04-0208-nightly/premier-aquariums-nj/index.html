About Us

Premier Aquariums is a full-service aquarium company providing installation and maintenance of reef, saltwater, and freshwater aquariums.

We install new custom aquariums of all shapes and sizes, along with standard rectangle and square tank sizes. After installation, we can return on a regular schedule to keep the aquarium clean, healthy, and looking its best.

Owner, Karl Tulp Karl has more than 15 years of experience in the aquarium trade and is based in Morris County, New Jersey with his family.
