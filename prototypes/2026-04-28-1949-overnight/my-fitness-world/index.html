Welcome

Fitness coaching that feels personal from day one.

My Fitness World was built for people who want expert guidance without the chaos of a big-box gym. Whether you are getting back into shape, training toward an event, rebuilding confidence after a life change, or trying to move with less pain, the focus stays on a realistic plan you can actually follow.

1-on-1 Personal Training

Best for clients who want a fully customized program, hands-on coaching, and built-in accountability every session.

  • Personalized workouts matched to your current fitness level
  • Strength, fat-loss, mobility, and conditioning support
  • Coaching that adapts as your goals change

Couples & Duo Training

Train with a spouse, friend, partner, or coworker while still getting individualized attention and progress tracking.

Semi-Private Training

Small-group energy with personal coaching for clients who want support, structure, and a more budget-friendly option.
