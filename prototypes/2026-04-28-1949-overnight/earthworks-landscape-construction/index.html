Our services

Landscape construction organized by what homeowners actually need.

The original site hints at the range of work Earthworks does. Here it is laid out clearly, so first-time visitors can tell at a glance whether the company is the right fit.

Hardscape

Pavers, retaining walls, flagstone, stepping stones, and walkways built with attention to grading, layout, and finish details.

  • Retaining and garden walls

Outdoor Builds

Functional features that make the yard more usable for family time, entertaining, and everyday living.

  • Outdoor kitchens
