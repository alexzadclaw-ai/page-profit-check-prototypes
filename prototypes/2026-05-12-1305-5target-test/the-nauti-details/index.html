    About Us

    At The Nauti Details, LLC, we believe that every minute you spend scrubbing is a minute you aren't out enjoying the water. What started as a mobile interior detailing service in Perrysburg, Ohio has grown into a full detailing service, with a permanent home in Toledo, Ohio right on the Maumee River making it easy to visit us by both land or the waterways.
     

    We know that in Ohio and Michigan, our boating season is precious. Whether it’s fighting off Lake Erie’s seagulls, trying to outrun the mayflies, or battling the unpredictable Midwest weather, we’re here year-round to ensure your vessel is clean and always ready for the next adventure.
     

    We haven't forgotten our roots as a small, local business. While many have moved to digital-only quotes, we still prefer to meet you at your boat. Getting to know our boating community in person allows us to provide the most accurate, customizable, and affordable packages tailored specifically to your needs. From certified coatings and teak reclamation to those essential maintenance washes, we handle the dirty work so you can focus on the fun.

    ​

    Thank you so much for checking out our small business and supporting local! We look forward to meeting you at the docks, getting to know you and your vessel, and helping you take the 'work' out of your weekend so you can get the most out of your season. We’ll see you on the river!"
