FAQ + SEO support

Answer the questions families usually have before they send a message

A calm FAQ section reduces hesitation while also strengthening the page’s search relevance around pre/postnatal support.

When should I reach out?

You can seek support during preconception, pregnancy, the final weeks before birth, early postpartum, or later recovery when you are ready to rebuild strength and confidence.

What happens in the consultation?

The first conversation should help clarify your stage, goals, concerns, and which service or support path makes the most sense for you.

Do you help with postpartum recovery and return to exercise?

Yes — this is one of the clearest opportunities to position CLT Wellness as a practical bridge between recovery, movement, and full-spectrum support.

Can partners be part of the support process?

They should be invited into the appropriate pieces, especially childbirth education, postpartum preparation, and family transition planning.
