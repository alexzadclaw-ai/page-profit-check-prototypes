FAQ cleanup

Answer the questions that block people from calling

A small FAQ is one of the easiest low-effort additions for both trust and conversion clarity.

What happens during the free consultation?

You discuss goals, limitations, schedule, and the training path that makes the most sense before committing.

Do I need to be in shape already?

No. The page should reassure beginners and returning clients that the plan starts from their current level.

What if I want weight loss help and nutrition support?

That combination should be framed as a normal, guided path rather than separate disconnected services.

Who is sport-specific training for?

People preparing for performance goals, organized athletics, or a more advanced training target.

Why mention testing on the homepage?

Because VO2 max and resting metabolic rate testing make the business feel more specialized and credible.

Why this style of cleanup instead of a redesign?

Because the fastest value here likely comes from clarity, trust cleanup, and better CTA structure — not a total rebuild.
