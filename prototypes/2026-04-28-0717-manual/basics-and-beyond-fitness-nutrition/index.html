What to simplify on the live page

The opportunity here is mostly editorial and structural. This is a classic case where less noise would make the existing authority land better.

Trim repetition

The homepage repeats Nashville positioning, no-contract messaging, service breadth, and contact prompts more often than necessary.

Polish rough comparison sections

Some current comparison-style content feels more like draft material than premium positioning. Tightening that would raise trust quickly.

Shorten the FAQ

Keep only the most decision-helpful questions on the homepage and let deeper pages carry the extra detail.

Keep the trust, reduce the clutter

A shorter FAQ helps both conversions and SEO

Answer the practical questions people actually have, instead of making the homepage carry every persuasion point at once.

Do I need a contract?

No. Keep the pay-as-you-go message visible and easy to understand right away.

Can I train at home or online?

Yes. This flexibility is one of the strongest differentiators and should be easier to spot near the top.

Do you help with nutrition too?

Yes. Nutrition support is valuable, but it should be framed in a simpler, more scannable way.

Where do sessions happen?

Use a short locations answer on the homepage and push detailed area coverage to the dedicated locations page.

Is this beginner-friendly?

That reassurance should be more visible for prospects who may feel unsure about starting.

What should I do next?

Give one obvious next step: call or contact to talk through goals and trainer fit.
