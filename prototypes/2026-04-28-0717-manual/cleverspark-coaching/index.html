Proof framing

Founder credibility should support the CTA, not hide behind it

Kimberly’s story is one of the strongest assets on the site. A good conversion version pulls selected trust details closer to the main booking moment instead of leaving most of them on the About page.

Realistic reassurance beats hype

Because this brand is warm and supportive, the best proof angle is not dramatic claims. It is calm reassurance: clearer process, better package fit, and examples of the kinds of problems clients bring in.

Typo cleanup matters here

Coaching sells trust, clarity, and care. Small spelling and formatting issues do more damage on this kind of offer than on a rough local-service page.

FAQ + SEO basics

Answer the questions people likely have before reaching out

Do I need to know exactly what package I want?

No. The consult can be framed as the place to sort that out together, which reduces booking hesitation.

Is this only for people with ADHD?

No. ADHD-informed coaching is a differentiator, but the broader offer still serves career exploration, job search, and educational decision-making.

Can I start small?

Visible single sessions, add-ons, and payment plans already support this. The page should simply present those options more cleanly.

What happens after I submit the form?

A simple reassurance line on the contact page can explain response timing, consult expectations, and the low-pressure nature of the first conversation.
