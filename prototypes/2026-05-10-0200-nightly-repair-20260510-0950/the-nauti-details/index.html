About Us

At The Nauti Details, LLC, every minute you spend scrubbing is a minute you aren't out enjoying the water. We help Toledo, Perrysburg, Ohio, and Michigan boaters spend more time on the river and less time doing the dirty work.

From certified coatings and teak reclamation to maintenance washes, we handle the dirty work so you can focus on the fun. We’ll see you on the river!
