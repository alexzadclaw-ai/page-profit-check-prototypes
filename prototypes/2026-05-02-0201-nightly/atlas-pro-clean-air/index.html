When to call Atlas Pro Clean Air

Not sure if the ducts or dryer vent need attention? These are the moments when a quick inspection or quote makes sense.

  • Clothes take more than one cycle to dry.
  • The laundry room feels warmer or more humid than normal.
  • Dust, pet dander or allergy symptoms keep returning indoors.
  • You see buildup when you remove a floor vent cover.
  • Your home or business has not had vents cleaned in over a year.
