A clear one-week process

Some factors may change the duration, but most projects follow a clear sequence from removal to re-installation.

Day 1

Remove

Doors and drawers are removed from the kitchen and labeled for the project.

Days 2-3

Shop Work

Doors and drawers are worked on offsite in the shop for prep and coating.

Day 4

Prep Kitchen

M + M returns to prep the kitchen surfaces for painting.

Day 5

Cleanup happens and the doors and drawers are hung back in place.
