I was looking at the Premier Fitness site and noticed you already have the important pieces in place: real coaching credibility, group training, personal training, online programs, and clear contact info.

1. Which training path fits me?
2. Why should I trust Greg and Premier Fitness?
3. What happens after I reach out?

If you want, I can send over the $99 check and show you the specific before and after recommendations.

Best,
