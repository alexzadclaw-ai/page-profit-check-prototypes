I do a small $99 Page Profit Check where I review a page and send back a practical before/after cleanup plan focused on calls, catering inquiries, and trust. For your site, I would focus on:

- making the first button a clear call ahead action
- putting hours, closed days, location, and catering availability near the top
- separating plate lunches, poke bowls, and pupu platters by what customers are trying to order

- tightening the title, description, and image labels for local search

If you wanted help implementing the changes after the check, my rough estimate would be 3 to 6 hours in Wix, depending on access and how much you want changed. This would be a cleanup, not a full redesign.

Would you like me to send over the $99 Page Profit Check details?
