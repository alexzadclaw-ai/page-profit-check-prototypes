Hi Basics and Beyond team,

I spent a little time reviewing your site:

You clearly have the real credibility already in place — long experience in Nashville, multiple trainers, nutrition support, location flexibility, and a strong no-contract / no-membership angle.

What stood out to me is that the site may actually be underselling that credibility a bit because the pages are doing too much at once.

This feels like a **cleanup-and-clarity project**, not a redesign pitch.

I put together a simple direction focused on:
- clearer hero and CTA hierarchy
- tighter trust framing
- easier service-path scanning
- shorter, more practical FAQ content
- cleaner trainer preview structure
- better mobile readability
- basic Nashville-focused SEO cleanup
