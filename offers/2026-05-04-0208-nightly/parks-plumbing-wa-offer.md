## Draft note

Hi Park's Plumbing team,

I took a quick look at your website and liked that it already feels like a real local plumbing company: family owned, Arlington-based, clear phone number, license number, Snohomish County service area, and straightforward services.

The biggest opportunity I saw is a simple cleanup, not a full redesign. Right now the homepage has the right information, but a plumbing customer has to work a little too hard to find the exact path they need — emergency repair, water heater, drain cleaning, fixture work, sewer camera inspection, or piping.

I put together a small improved version that keeps the same Park's Plumbing look — the teal header, logo, plain local style, red buttons, and existing photos — but makes the first screen easier to act on:

- clearer headline for Arlington / Snohomish County plumbing help;
- click-ready **Call** and **Text for a free estimate** actions;
- license, family-owned, service area, and residential/commercial trust proof near the top;
- services grouped by what customers are trying to solve;
- a simple “what happens next” flow;
- cleaner contact/footer path without old template clutter.

This is the kind of thing I usually package as a **$99 Page Profit Check**: a mini-audit plus a focused example of how the homepage could be tightened for more calls/texts.

If you wanted help implementing the cleanup inside Wix, the likely range would be about **$450–$900** depending on whether you only want the homepage tightened or also want contact/mobile/SEO cleanup included.

No pressure — I just thought this was a good candidate because the site already has the local trust pieces. They just need to be easier for a rushed plumbing customer to see and act on.

Best,  
Alexey
