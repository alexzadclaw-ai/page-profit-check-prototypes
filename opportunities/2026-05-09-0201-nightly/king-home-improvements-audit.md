# King Home Improvements - Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
King Home Improvements is a strong fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current Wix site already has useful conversion ingredients: a recognizable logo, direct phone number, public email, service area, a free-estimate CTA, and a clear home improvement service list. The main issue is that the page looks unfinished in places, leaves key trust details spread out, and does not quickly organize services by what a homeowner is trying to get done.

**Recommendation:** keep the dark Wix contractor look, logo, bold uppercase headings, large hero photo, black/white buttons, and direct “Any job, we can fix it” tone, but tighten the homepage around one clearer action: **Call or request a free home improvement estimate**.

## Biggest conversion issues

### Critical findings
1. **Critical:** The hero headline says “ANY JOB, WE CAN FIX IT,” but does not immediately list the actual services or service area.
   - Prototype response: Keep the familiar line, but add a clearer first-screen headline for decks, flooring, remodeling, painting, repairs, and MA service.

2. **Critical:** Trust signals are not grouped near the top. Phone, email, free estimate, county service area, and service breadth are present, but visitors must scan around to connect them.
   - Prototype response: Add a trust strip directly under the hero with local MA service, frame-to-finish work, direct contact, and free estimate.

3. **Critical:** The page includes visible Wix/template residue that can make the business look less established, including the Wix ad banner, “Powered and secured by Wix,” and a “More” nav state created by hidden/overflow navigation.
   - Prototype response: Remove those template leftovers and make the nav intentional: Home, Services, About, Contact Us, Call Now.

### High findings
4. **High:** The primary CTA is split between “CALL NOW,” a phone number, and “GET A FREE ESTIMATE!” but the path is not consistent across the page.
   - Prototype response: Repeat one clear action pattern: Call 351-666-9365 or Get a Free Estimate.

5. **High:** Service names are listed, but they do not help a homeowner decide which path fits their need.
   - Prototype response: Keep the same five services and add short buyer-intent descriptions for decks, flooring, remodeling, painting, and renovations.

6. **High:** The homepage does not explain what happens after a visitor calls, emails, or submits the form.
   - Prototype response: Add a simple three-step section: tell us the job, review the details, get an estimate.

7. **High:** The mobile layout is harder to scan because the page starts with utility/template elements before the buyer sees the service story.
   - Prototype response: Put the brand, contact, specific hero, trust strip, service cards, and estimate path into a stacked mobile flow.

8. **High:** SEO metadata is generic: “HOME | King Home Improvements.” It misses home improvement services, MA counties, and estimate intent.
   - Prototype response: Use a service-and-location title and meta description.

### Medium findings
9. **Medium:** The supporting deck/house photo is useful but does not currently carry enough service context.
   - Prototype response: Use it beside the service explanation with descriptive alt text.

10. **Medium:** The form asks for details, but it could better guide people by service type.
    - Prototype response: Add a service dropdown before the message field.

11. **Medium:** The Facebook icon exists, but it is isolated at the bottom and not supported by other credibility proof.
    - Prototype response: Keep the public Facebook link in the footer while moving stronger trust cues higher.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Replace the generic hero line with a service-specific home improvement headline while retaining the “Any job, we can fix it” phrase.
- Make phone and free-estimate actions consistent in the header, hero, service cards, and contact area.
- Add a trust strip near the top with local service area, frame-to-finish work, direct contact, and free estimate.
- Organize the same services into buyer task paths so homeowners can self-select faster.
- Add a simple what-happens-next section before the contact form.
- Remove Wix/template leftovers that reduce trust.
- Tighten mobile scanning so the first screen answers: who, where, what, and how to start.
- Improve SEO basics around home improvement, decks, flooring, remodeling, painting, renovations, and MA counties.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title like: **Home Improvement, Decks, Flooring & Remodeling in MA | King Home Improvements**.
- Write a meta description that mentions Essex, Middlesex, and Suffolk Counties in MA plus the key services.
- Use one clear H1 that names the services instead of only a broad slogan.
- Use H2s for Services, What Happens Next, and Contact.
- Add descriptive alt text for the logo, hero, and house/deck image.
- Add LocalBusiness or HomeAndConstructionBusiness schema after confirming address and service-area details.
- Consider moving from the Wix subdomain to an owned domain when practical.
