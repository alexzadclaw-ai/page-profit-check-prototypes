# LMS Landscaping - Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take

The assigned public URL, https://lmsincorporated.wixsite.com/my-site, currently resolves to a Wix 404 page instead of an LMS Landscaping website. That is the highest possible conversion issue: any visitor from Google, a referral, a business card, or a social profile lands on a dead page and cannot confirm services or request an estimate.

Because the live business page was unavailable during inspection, the visual fingerprint available from the assigned URL is the Wix 404 state: white Wix header, black text navigation, large centered "ERROR: PAGE NOT FOUND" message, and no LMS Landscaping content. The Solution A page treats this as a surgical recovery: restore a recognizable simple Wix-service-business structure for LMS Landscaping with clear landscaping messaging, strong estimate CTA, trust cues near the top, service paths, and next steps.

## What is already working

- The business name and vertical are clear from the opportunity record: LMS Landscaping.
- The Wix-hosted URL suggests the business can likely be repaired quickly inside an existing site-builder workflow.
- A landscaping buyer journey is straightforward: visitors need to know services, trust basics, service area, and how to request an estimate.
- The current 404 page is simple, which makes the immediate fix focused: restore a functioning homepage before doing deeper SEO or design work.

## Biggest conversion issues

### Critical

1. **The public site is unavailable.** The assigned URL returns a Wix 404, so visitors cannot see LMS Landscaping content, services, proof, or contact options.
   - Prototype fix: provide a live-feeling restored homepage structure with LMS Landscaping name, landscaping services, and a clear estimate request path.

2. **No conversion path is visible.** The 404 page has no phone, email, service menu, estimate button, or explanation of what to do next.
   - Prototype fix: add a specific hero CTA, repeated estimate CTA, and a three-step "What Happens Next" section.

3. **No trust signal exists on the page.** A dead Wix page makes the business look inactive or unfinished.
   - Prototype fix: add top-of-page trust cards for local service, clear estimates, and season-ready maintenance.

### High

4. **No service clarity by buyer intent.** Landscaping customers often think in tasks like yard cleanup, mulch, trimming, or maintenance, not generic company language.
   - Prototype fix: organize services into buyer-oriented paths: make the yard presentable, refresh beds, clean up overgrowth.

5. **No brand or business identity is present.** The only visible brand on the assigned URL is Wix, not LMS Landscaping.
   - Prototype fix: introduce a simple LMS Landscaping logo mark, green landscaping color palette, outdoor imagery, and consistent header/footer naming.

6. **No mobile-friendly customer scan path.** On mobile, a 404 gives no helpful above-the-fold answer and no direct action.
   - Prototype fix: responsive single-column layout, full-width CTA buttons, compressed service cards, and simplified navigation.

### Medium

7. **No SEO basics are visible.** The page title and content visible to visitors are Wix 404 content, not landscaping keywords.
8. **No service area or location is listed.** The opportunity data did not include a city or state.
9. **No contact details were found.** The assigned URL did not expose a phone number or email address.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements

- Restore the page from a 404 into a credible LMS Landscaping homepage.
- Make the hero immediately answer: who this is, what they do, and how to request an estimate.
- Replace generic or absent CTAs with "Request a Landscaping Estimate."
- Add trust signals above the fold so visitors do not see an unfinished business page.
- Group services around buyer tasks rather than internal service categories.
- Add a short three-step process to reduce hesitation.
- Use a simple Wix-like service-business page structure so implementation can be quick and familiar.

## SEO basics to tighten

- Set the homepage title to include the business name and primary service, for example: `LMS Landscaping | Landscaping, Lawn Care & Outdoor Maintenance`.
- Add a meta description focused on landscaping, lawn care, cleanups, mulch, trimming, and maintenance.
- Add the actual city, state, and service area once confirmed.
- Add real contact details in schema, footer, and contact CTA.
- Replace the Wix 404 state with indexable business content.
- Add image alt text that describes landscaping services rather than leaving generic media labels.

## Realistic implementation angle

This is a high-priority recovery job, not a full redesign. The first paid implementation should focus on making the Wix site reachable again, restoring a homepage, replacing placeholder or missing content, and setting one clear estimate request path.

ROM implementation estimate: 3 to 6 hours if the Wix account is accessible and the missing page can be restored or rebuilt from the existing site structure. Add 1 to 2 hours if real photos, service area, phone, email, and service details need to be collected and inserted.
