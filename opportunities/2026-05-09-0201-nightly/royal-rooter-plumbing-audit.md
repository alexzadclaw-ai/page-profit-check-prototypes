# Royal Rooter & Plumbing — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Royal Rooter & Plumbing is a strong fit for a Solution A surgical cleanup. The existing Wix site has a very recognizable structure: white header, blue navigation bar, large dark-blue hero block, centered white content cards, Proxima/Helvetica-style type, simple blue/black/white sections, opening hours, promises, testimonial, and contact details.

The main opportunity is not a redesign. It is to keep the same Wix-style visual language while making the first screen, calls to action, service paths, and trust details easier for a plumbing customer to understand quickly.

## What is already working
- The business name, phone number, email, Facebook link, location, hours, and testimonial are present.
- The site already communicates free estimates, 24/7 phone support, daily hours, and Spanish-language availability.
- The blue, black, and white section rhythm is consistent and recognizable.
- The page has useful real service content: drains, mainlines, repipes, water heaters, disposals, toilets, washer/dryer, fixtures, and leak repairs.
- The owner would recognize the cleaned-up version because the section order and site-builder feel are preserved.

## Biggest conversion issues
1. **Critical:** The live site shows a Wix ad/banner and Wix footer language, which lowers trust for an urgent plumbing visitor.
   - Prototype fix: Remove third-party builder residue and keep the header/footer business-specific.
2. **Critical:** The hero headline is broad, and the first screen does not immediately say La Puente, free estimate, or the best action to take.
   - Prototype fix: Make the hero say “The Most Reliable Plumber in La Puente” with a direct “Call for a Free Estimate” CTA.
3. **High:** The top trust details are scattered. Hours, phone support, free estimates, and Se habla español appear lower or in paragraph copy.
   - Prototype fix: Place these trust cues directly under the hero CTA and reinforce them in the promise section.
4. **High:** Services are listed as only three large labels even though the business offers more buyer-intent tasks.
   - Prototype fix: Group services into drain/mainline, leaks/repipes/repairs, and water heater/fixtures paths.
5. **High:** The contact form path says “Submit” without explaining what happens next.
   - Prototype fix: Add a “What Happens Next” section before contact and use call/email CTAs.
6. **Medium:** Copy has small formatting issues such as missing spaces and generic wording.
   - Prototype fix: Clean copy lightly without changing the business voice.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Remove Wix/template leftovers and make the page feel owned by Royal Rooter & Plumbing.
2. Turn the hero into a clear local plumbing promise with a specific phone CTA.
3. Move free estimate, phone support, daily hours, and Spanish-language trust cues near the top.
4. Organize service choices by the way customers think about their problem.
5. Explain the next step so visitors know whether to call, email, or request an estimate.
6. Keep the same blue, black, white, centered-card Wix style instead of introducing a new brand direction.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Set the homepage title around “La Puente plumber” plus the brand name.
- Use one clear H1 focused on plumbing service in La Puente.
- Add a meta description mentioning drain cleaning, leak repair, water heaters, and free estimates.
- Add LocalBusiness or Plumber schema with phone, address area, hours, email, and social link.
- Use service headings for drain cleaning, leak repair, repipes, water heaters, and fixture installation.
- If the site remains on Wix, remove visible Wix promotional elements by moving to a paid plan or connected domain.

## Realistic implementation angle
This looks like a focused Wix cleanup rather than a rebuild. A realistic implementation would be 3 to 6 hours if the current Wix account is available: update the hero, remove builder branding if the account supports it, tighten services, add trust cues near the top, add a next-step block, clean contact copy, and improve title/meta basics. If domain connection or Wix plan changes are needed, that would be separate from the page cleanup.
