# BC Home Improvement Audit

## Quick take
BC Home Improvement already has the bones of a strong local contractor site: clear service area, a visible phone number, free estimates, 20+ years of experience, owner involvement, emergency roofing, and customer testimonials. The biggest revenue leak is trust friction from unfinished Wix/template signals and unclear project paths. A surgical cleanup can keep the current look while making the site feel more finished, local, and conversion-ready.

Visual fingerprint observed: Wix-built layout with a top Wix ad banner, white header, left logo, centered light Poppins navigation, phone number on the right, oversized centered hero over a luxury home image, black/dark gray text, warm brown accent for active navigation, pale gray/white CTA buttons, large centered section headings, kitchen/home service imagery in grid blocks, two text testimonials, and a bottom contact section with hours, Boston service area, email, phone, and form.

## What is already working
- Phone number is visible in the header and contact section: (857) 417-6281.
- Free estimate message is present in the hero.
- Strong trust hooks exist: 20+ years of experience, owner on every job, warranties available, veteran discount, emergency roofing services.
- Service area is stated as Boston, MA and surrounding areas.
- Contact options include phone, email, and a form.
- Real services are broad enough for multiple buyer intents: kitchens, bathrooms, renovations, additions, roofing, siding, painting, decks, masonry, carpentry, garages, and custom homes.

## Biggest conversion issues
### Critical
1. The Wix promotional banner at the top makes the business look unfinished and less professional.
2. The Services page still contains placeholder copy: “I'm a paragraph. Click here to add your own text...” repeated across service categories.
3. The Services page title appears as “Services | M&B Remodeling,” which conflicts with BC Home Improvement and damages trust.

### High
4. The hero headline is broad. “Superior Home Remodeling Services” is clean, but it does not immediately clarify Boston area, call/text action, or the most common jobs.
5. Trust signals are present but mostly below the fold instead of summarized near the first CTA.
6. Services are listed as a flat list and image grid, not grouped by how customers think: interiors, exteriors, and larger projects.
7. The contact journey is not explicit. Visitors see a form but do not know what happens after submitting or calling.
8. Testimonials include duplicated/awkward text, which can reduce credibility.

### Medium
9. Footer copyright says 2020, making the site feel stale.
10. The brand name appears inconsistently as “Bchomeimprovement,” “BC Home Improvement,” and “Best Choice Home Improvement.”
11. The header navigation is sparse and hides services from the main nav.
12. Mobile scanning can be improved by turning long paragraphs and broad lists into short cards.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Remove trust-damaging template/Wix leftovers and conflicting page metadata.
- Keep the current white, Poppins, contractor-image, Wix-style layout, but tighten copy and hierarchy.
- Make the primary CTA more specific: “Call or Text for a Free Estimate.”
- Add a trust bar directly under the hero: 20+ years, owner involved, free consultations, emergency roofing.
- Group services by buyer intent: inside the home, outside the home, bigger projects.
- Rewrite service copy so no placeholder language remains.
- Add a simple “What Happens Next” section before the form.
- Clean up testimonials to remove repeated copy while preserving the same customer proof.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Update the Services page title from “Services | M&B Remodeling” to a BC Home Improvement title.
- Use a focused home page title such as “BC Home Improvement | Boston Home Remodeling, Roofing, Siding & Masonry.”
- Add service-specific copy for kitchens, bathrooms, additions, roofing, siding, painting, decks, and masonry.
- Add Boston and surrounding-area wording near major service headings.
- Ensure phone/email links do not include hidden characters in tel/mailto URLs.
- Consider adding an updated footer year and consistent business name.
- Add image alt text that describes real services rather than generic stock labels where possible.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a light-to-moderate Wix cleanup, not a full rebuild. A realistic ROM is 4-8 hours if done directly in Wix: remove the Wix banner by upgrading or connecting the right plan/domain, fix page titles, replace placeholder service copy, adjust hero/CTA copy, add a trust strip, reorganize services, clean testimonials, add a “what happens next” section, and test mobile spacing. If a custom one-page rebuild is chosen outside Wix, a realistic ROM is 1-2 business days depending on form handling and hosting.

## Offer fit
Strong fit for a $99 Page Profit Check because the site has clear business value but obvious, fixable trust leaks. The opportunity is not a subjective redesign. It is a concrete cleanup that can help more Boston-area remodeling visitors trust the business enough to call, text, or submit the form.
