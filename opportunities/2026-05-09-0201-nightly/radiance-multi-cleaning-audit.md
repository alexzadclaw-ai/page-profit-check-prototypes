# Radiance Multi-cleaning / Sierra Vista Window Cleaning Audit

## Quick take
Radiance has the bones of a trustworthy local service page: local coverage, direct phone/email, simple service list, testimonials, and an owner story. The biggest conversion problem is that the page still feels like an unpolished Wix starter site, with the Wix ad/banner, repeated contact blocks, vague CTAs, and a form that asks for very little context. The surgical fix is not a redesign. It is the same light, simple, photo-led site cleaned up into a clearer estimate path.

## What is already working
- Clear local relevance for Sierra Vista, Hereford, Bisbee, Douglas, and surrounding areas.
- Phone number and email are visible.
- Strong trust cues already exist: licensed and insured, since 2016, repeat customers, and owner names.
- Service scope is practical and easy to understand: window cleaning, junk removal, general cleaning.
- Real photos and testimonials make the business feel personal and local.
- The light gray, white, black, and blue Wix visual language is recognizable and should be preserved.

## Biggest conversion issues
- **Critical:** Wix banner and footer language make the business look unfinished, especially for a home-service buyer deciding whether to call.
- **Critical:** Hero headline says “Sierra Vista Window Cleaning,” but it does not immediately explain the full service area, service mix, trust proof, or the best next action.
- **High:** Main CTA is generic: “Contact Us” / “Call for Questions.” It should be a specific estimate action.
- **High:** Trust proof is present but buried in paragraph text rather than grouped near the top.
- **High:** Services are listed by business category but not framed as buyer task paths: windows, junk removal, recurring cleaning.
- **High:** The appointment form asks only service and phone number, so visitors do not know what details help get a useful estimate.
- **Medium:** Contact information repeats in several places without adding clarity.
- **Medium:** Mobile scanning is text-heavy and lacks a persistent phone-first action.
- **Medium:** SEO title is short and could better combine service, location, and brand.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Keep the same brand footprint: simple Wix-like layout, mostly white and light gray, black text, blue accents, real job photos, testimonials, and Marco/Claudia story.
- Replace generic first screen with a clearer local promise: “Sierra Vista window cleaning with an easy estimate path.”
- Move “Licensed & Insured,” “Since 2016,” “Free Estimates,” and repeat-customer proof directly below the hero.
- Turn services into three decision cards: window cleaning, junk removal, and general cleaning.
- Make every CTA more concrete: “Call 520-256-5781,” “Request a Free Estimate,” “Check Availability.”
- Add a “What to include” estimate section so prospects know how to ask for pricing.
- Add a simple “What happens next” section to reduce uncertainty.
- Remove obvious template leftovers and replace the generic Wix footer with business-owned contact details.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a more specific homepage title such as “Sierra Vista Window Cleaning, Junk Removal & General Cleaning | Radiance Multi-cleaning.”
- Add a meta description that includes Sierra Vista, Bisbee, Douglas, Hereford, window cleaning, junk removal, and general cleaning.
- Use one clear H1 and descriptive H2s for services, estimate, testimonials, and about.
- Add alt text to work photos and owner photo.
- Keep NAP details consistent: Radiance Multi-cleaning / Sierra Vista Window Cleaning, rwindoww@gmail.com, 520-256-5781, Sierra Vista/Bisbee/Douglas service area.
- If implemented on the live Wix site, connect a custom domain if they do not already have one.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a small Wix cleanup, not a rebuild. A realistic implementation would be 3 to 6 hours inside Wix if the current site assets and page access are available. The work is mostly copy restructuring, CTA cleanup, section reordering, adding trust badges, replacing template/footer language, improving mobile spacing, and tightening page title/meta text. If they also want a custom domain connected and local SEO basics configured, budget another 1 to 2 hours.

## Offer fit
Good fit for a $99 Page Profit Check because the business already has enough proof and assets. The value is showing how a few focused changes can make the site feel more trustworthy and make the estimate request easier. This is likely easier to sell than a full website rebuild because the current site only needs a cleaner conversion path.
