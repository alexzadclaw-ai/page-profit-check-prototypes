# Liberty Plumbing — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Liberty Plumbing already has the ingredients of a workable local plumbing page: the business name, Willis focus, phone, email, hours, service area, and a long-running local presence. The page feels like a Wix-built small business site, which is useful for a Solution A cleanup because the right move is not a redesign. It is a tighter first screen, cleaner service grouping, and stronger trust placement.

The biggest conversion lift comes from removing template residue, making the hero more specific, and turning the existing service list into clearer buyer paths.

## What is already working
- The business identity is clear: Liberty Plumbing, Willis, Texas, locally owned and operated.
- Core contact details are already visible: email and phone.
- Hours are present.
- The site already names useful services: service and repair, water heaters, new construction, and water system repipe.
- The page already serves multiple nearby areas: Willis, Conroe, Montgomery, New Waverly, Huntsville, and surrounding areas.
- The owner would recognize the cleaned-up version because the same Wix-style structure can be preserved.

## Biggest conversion issues
1. **Critical:** The live page shows Wix promotional/footer residue, which weakens trust for a service business.
   - Prototype fix: Remove builder branding and keep the footer business-owned.
2. **Critical:** The hero is broad and generic. It says the business name and location, but it does not quickly tell a visitor what to do next.
   - Prototype fix: Make the hero say exactly what the company does and lead with a specific call for a free estimate.
3. **High:** Trust cues like locally owned, opened in 2011, hours, and service area are present but not pulled into the first screen.
   - Prototype fix: Place those cues directly under the hero CTA in short trust pills.
4. **High:** Services are listed, but they are not grouped by buyer intent or task path.
   - Prototype fix: Group into service and repair, water heaters, new construction, and repipe / system replacement.
5. **High:** The page does not clearly explain what happens after a call or email.
   - Prototype fix: Add a simple next-step section before contact.
6. **Medium:** Copy has a few awkward phrases and feels more like filler than customer guidance.
   - Prototype fix: Lightly tighten wording while keeping the same local plumbing voice.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Remove Wix residue and make the page feel owned by Liberty Plumbing.
- Rewrite the hero so the first screen is local, specific, and action-oriented.
- Put trust signals near the top.
- Group services by the way customers actually shop for help.
- Add a simple what-happens-next block.
- Keep the same general blue, white, and black site-builder feel so the owner still recognizes the site.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use one clear H1 around Willis plumbing service.
- Add a title tag that includes Willis and plumbing service.

- Keep NAP details visible in the footer and contact block.
- Use service headings that match real search intent, not vague labels.
- Add local business schema if the site setup allows it.
