# Moni's Professional Cleaning Services - Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Moni's Professional Cleaning Services is a strong fit for a **Solution A brand-faithful homepage cleanup**. The site already has real substance: the company name is clear, the phone and email are present, the service area is specific, the about page has an origin story, and the services page lists real cleaning tasks instead of fake fluff.

The visual fingerprint is very recognizable: an old-school Wix template with centered navigation, huge bold black/gray/purple typography, Arial Black and Impact-style headings, a simple hero, a three-benefit strip, and visible template residue like the Wix banner and placeholder-style content on the services page. The right move is not a redesign. It is a surgical cleanup that keeps the same site-builder feel while making the first screen more trustworthy and easier to act on.

**Recommendation:** keep the bold Wix-era look, the purple accent, the centered header, the big hero statement, and the family-business story, but tighten the hero promise, make the CTA specific, surface trust earlier, organize services by buyer intent, and remove filler/template leftovers.

## What is already working
- The business name is prominent and easy to recognize.
- Phone and email are visible on the homepage and contact page.
- The company has a real local history, with service since 2000.
- The about page adds founder and family continuity context.
- The services page already names useful cleaning tasks like carpet cleaning, deep spring cleaning, upholstery cleaning, inside oven, interior walls, and interior windows.
- The site clearly serves Fayetteville, Ft. Bragg, Cumberland County, Charlotte, and Concord, NC.
- The homepage is simple and short, so a cleanup can create a visible before/after quickly.
- The brand voice already leans toward affordability, quality, and professionalism.

## Biggest conversion issues
1. **Critical:** The hero is generic and not action-oriented.
   - `Professional Cleaning Service` plus `CALL FOR A FREE ESTIMATE` is real, but it does not explain the next step or separate home vs commercial buyers.
   - Prototype fix: turn the hero into a clearer promise with a specific estimate CTA and a stronger phone button.

2. **Critical:** Template residue weakens trust.
   - The Wix banner, `Get Started`, and placeholder-heavy service content signal that the page is still partly in builder mode.
   - Prototype fix: remove builder residue from the customer-facing page and replace it with business-owned copy only.

3. **High:** Service paths are not organized by buyer intent.
   - The live site says residential and commercial, but the homepage does not quickly show what each customer should ask for.
   - Prototype fix: add clear residential and commercial service panels with task examples.

4. **High:** The why-choose section is too shallow.
   - `Excellent Quality`, `Professional Staff`, and `Affordable Rates` are good themes, but they need supporting proof.
   - Prototype fix: keep the three claims, but add short trust copy and a founder story reference.

5. **High:** What happens next is unclear.
   - Visitors can see contact info, but the site does not explain how to request a bid or what details to send.
   - Prototype fix: add a simple three-step estimate flow.

6. **Medium:** The contact and address info should be easier to scan.
   - The contact page has the address and phone, but the homepage does not repeat them in a decision-friendly way.
   - Prototype fix: surface contact details in the hero area and footer.

7. **Medium:** SEO basics can be tightened.
   - The site needs a cleaner homepage title, one clear H1, descriptive section headings, and better alt text on images.
   - Prototype fix: add local service wording naturally and clean up heading hierarchy.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Keep the same bold Wix-era typography and purple accent, but make the hero feel like a real lead path.
- Replace the generic estimate line with specific CTA wording.
- Add a trust strip near the top for service since 2000, founder-led, and commercial references.
- Turn residential and commercial into scannable buyer paths.
- Use the live about-page story to make the business feel real, not templated.
- Remove Wix banner language and placeholder content from the customer-facing cleanup.
- Add a simple estimate process so the visitor knows what to do next.
- Tighten local SEO with Fayetteville, Ft. Bragg, Cumberland County, Charlotte, and Concord phrasing.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title like: `Moni's Professional Cleaning Services | Fayetteville Residential & Commercial Cleaning`.
- Add a meta description such as: `Moni's Professional Cleaning Services has served Fayetteville and surrounding NC communities since 2000 with residential and commercial cleaning. Request a free estimate.`
- Use one clear H1 that matches the main service promise.
- Add H2s for Services, Why Choose Moni's, Service Area, and Contact.
- Add descriptive alt text for hero and service images.
- Mention the core locations naturally in headings and body copy.
- Consider local business schema only after confirming the exact preferred address and service-area formatting.

The practical scope is hero copy, CTA cleanup, trust placement, service-path organization, SEO cleanup, and removal of builder/template residue.
