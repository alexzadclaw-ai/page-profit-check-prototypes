# My City Cleaning Services audit

## Quick take
This is a real local cleaning business trapped in a Wix default shell. The good news: the phone, email, service range, and service area are already there. The bad news: the page reads like a rough catalog, not a booking path. The hero is vague, trust cues are buried, and several template and copy issues make it feel unfinished.

## What is already working
- The business name is clear and the contact info is visible.
- The site lists multiple service categories, which helps with broad search intent.
- The service area is explicit: Nashville, Franklin, Brentwood, Murfreesboro, and surrounding areas.
- The page already has some credibility signals like CDC language, references, and a local tone.

## Biggest conversion issues
- **Critical:** The top of the page is generic and does not quickly tell a visitor what to do next.
  - Fix: Rewrite the hero with a specific local promise, a specific CTA, and a short trust line near the top.
- **High:** The services are listed as a long block, so buyers cannot quickly choose by job type.
  - Fix: Reorganize services into task paths such as commercial, residential, move out, specialty cleaning, and exterior cleaning.
- **High:** Template leftovers and Wix branding reduce trust.
  - Fix: Remove the Wix promo strip and clean up any builder-style filler.
- **High:** Copy quality issues weaken confidence.
  - Fix: Correct misspellings and tighten the wording so the page feels cared for.
- **Medium:** There is no clear next step after browsing services.
  - Fix: Add a simple how-it-works section and a repeated CTA.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Clearer hero that says what the company does and where it works.
- Specific CTA such as Call for a free estimate or Request a cleaning quote.
- Trust signals moved near the top, including local service area, fast response, and quality guarantee language.
- Services grouped by buyer intent so visitors can self-select faster.
- Short what happens next section so the visitor knows the process.
- Cleaner mobile stacking so the page is easier to scan.
- Remove template leftovers so the site feels like a real local business page, not a starter template.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Make the page title and main heading match the service and location.
- Add a stronger meta description around Nashville area cleaning services.
- Use one clear H1 and structured service headings.
- Add location language naturally for the main service area.
- Clean up spelling and grammar so search snippets and trust both improve.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a strong Solution A candidate because the site already has enough raw content to improve without reinventing anything. I would keep the same general Wix-style layout, tighten the hero, reorganize services, add a trust strip, and simplify the call to action. This is realistic as a focused one-page rebuild rather than a full redesign.

## Offer fit
A $99 Page Profit Check fits well here because the site has obvious conversion leaks that are easy to spot and easy to explain. The owner would likely recognize the same business, just presented in a clearer and more credible way.
