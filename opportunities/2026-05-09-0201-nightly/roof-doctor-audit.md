# Roof Doctor — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Roof Doctor is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup**. The live Wix site already has useful local contractor substance: the business has operated since 1992, lists direct phone/email contact, shows a Pelham NC address, names roofing/gutter/tree-work services, and has a simple contractor look that can be preserved.

The main conversion issue is not that the business lacks services. It is that the page feels like an older Wix template: the first screen is generic, contact/form paths are not explained, service categories are hard to scan, and visible Wix/template leftovers reduce confidence.

**Visual fingerprint evidence before implementation:** inspected https://roofdr.wixsite.com/roofdoctor on May 9, 2026 using lightweight public browsing and rendered browser inspection. The recognizable cues are a black header, small square Roof Doctor logo, Avenir/Helvetica-style type, large uppercase ROOF DOCTOR hero, rooftop background imagery, burgundy/dark red and charcoal page bands, white rectangular Call Now button, service rows with text and right-side images, About Me before Services in the rendered order, Residential/Commercial blocks, Facebook link, and Wix promotional/footer residue.

**Recommendation:** keep the dark/burgundy Wix-builder feel and existing public images, but restructure the page around one clear action: **Call Now for a Free Estimate**.

## Biggest conversion issues

### Critical findings
1. **Critical: The first screen does not quickly explain the full buyer promise.**  
   The hero says “Roofing Contractor at Your Service” and “ROOF DOCTOR,” but the specific local value, service range, free-estimate path, and since-1992 trust cue are not packaged together for a first-time visitor.

2. **Critical: The primary CTA is too generic and not repeated with context.**  
   “Call Now” exists, but it does not say why to call, what the visitor receives, or which service path it supports. The contact form later says “Submit” without setting expectations.

### High findings
3. **High: Trust signals are present but buried or scattered.**  
   Since 1992, the Pelham location, residential/commercial positioning, direct phone, email, and Facebook link are available, but the top of the page does not turn them into quick proof.

4. **High: Services are listed as long text blocks instead of buyer task paths.**  
   Complete re-roofing, shingle repair, gutters, and tree work are useful categories, but a homeowner scanning on mobile has to work too hard to find the right next step.

5. **High: What happens next is unclear.**  
   Visitors are not told whether to call, email, submit a form, provide photos, schedule a visit, or expect a free estimate.

6. **High: Template residue weakens contractor credibility.**  
   The live site shows the Wix promotional banner, “Book Online” navigation even though the main conversion path appears to be phone/email, “Home: Welcome”/section labels in extracted content, and “Proudly created with Wix.com” in the footer.

### Medium findings
7. **Medium: Contact section feels unfinished.**  
   The form is generic and the stronger direct contact information appears below it.

8. **Medium: SEO basics can be tightened.**  
   The page needs a more specific title, meta description, one clear H1, descriptive image alt text, and LocalBusiness/HomeAndConstructionBusiness schema after confirming final service area wording.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Preserve the black header, burgundy/dark red sections, uppercase headings, white button style, square logo, and public roofing imagery.
- Rewrite the hero around a clear local promise: roofing, gutters, remodeling/repairs, and tree work in Pelham and surrounding areas.
- Make **Call Now for a Free Estimate** the main CTA and repeat it in service sections.
- Move “Since 1992,” “Roofing First,” “Free Estimate,” and “Residential & Commercial” into a compact trust strip near the top.
- Reframe services by buyer intent: roofing/re-roofing, shingle repair, gutters, tree work, residential, and commercial.
- Add a three-step “What Happens Next” section so visitors know exactly how to start.
- Replace generic “Submit” energy with direct phone/email CTAs.
- Remove visible builder/template leftovers from the customer-facing page.
- Tighten SEO basics in the HTML structure.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a focused title such as `Roof Doctor | Roofing Contractor in Pelham, NC`.
- Add a concise meta description mentioning roofing, shingle repair, gutters, tree work, remodeling, Pelham, and free estimates.
- Use one H1: **Roof Doctor**.
- Use H2 headings for About, Services, Other Home Improvement Needs, What Happens Next, and Contact.
- Add descriptive alt text to the rooftop, ladder, logo, and exterior-work images.
- Add local business schema after confirming whether the owner wants the street address published prominently.
- Consider adding separate landing pages later for roofing, gutters, and tree/stump removal if those are priority services.
