# The Nauti Details, LLC - Audit-Driven Solution A

## Quick take
The site has a likable local voice and useful service depth, but the first screen does not quickly say what is sold, where it is sold, or what the visitor should do next. The strongest opportunity is not a redesign. It is a surgical Wix-style cleanup: clearer marine detailing hero, trust/quote process near the top, service groups by buyer intent, and a shorter quote path that explains the in-person vessel visit.

## What is already working
- The local story is strong: Perrysburg roots, Toledo/Maumee River location, Ohio/Michigan boating season, and owner-friendly dockside quoting.
- Services page contains real operational depth: scheduled washes, wax/sealant/coatings, oxidation removal, wet sanding, teak restoration, bottom paint, toon painting, and cabin/cockpit detailing.
- Contact information is visible: `Info@TheNautiDetails.com` and `5672888811`.
- The business explains a credible differentiator: they prefer to meet each owner and vessel rather than send generic digital quotes.
- Existing imagery and Wix aesthetic are recognizable and should be retained rather than replaced with a generic agency look.

## Biggest conversion issues
- Critical: Hero copy says “Relax & Enjoy / Leave the Details to Us” but does not immediately clarify boat detailing, service area, or exact next step.
- Critical: The quote process is explained deep in long copy, but visitors need to know early that a vessel walkthrough leads to an accurate package.
- High: Services are comprehensive but presented as a long list, which makes it hard for a buyer to pick the right starting point.
- High: Trust proof is mostly embedded in paragraphs. Local, certified, year-round, personal-quote cues should be nearer the top.
- Medium: Mobile scanning is difficult because long text blocks appear before service choices or next steps.
- Medium: Wix/template remnants, including the visible Wix promo and footer language, reduce polish. The prototype preserves the Wix feel but reduces the conversion damage by strengthening the business content around it.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Replace vague hero headline with a specific value proposition: boat detailing that keeps the season easy.
- Add a specific primary CTA: “Request a dockside estimate.”
- Add a trust bar immediately under the hero: local, personal, boat-by-boat quotes.
- Group services into buyer-intent cards: Keep It Clean, Restore & Protect, Interior & Specialty.
- Move “what happens next” above the form so visitors understand the walkthrough before submitting.
- Keep familiar imagery, typography, header rhythm, and story voice so the owner recognizes the site as cleaned up, not replaced.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Title should include service and location, for example: `Boat Detailing in Toledo, OH | The Nauti Details, LLC`.
- Add a meta description mentioning boat washing, waxing, coatings, teak restoration, bottom paint, cabin detailing, Toledo, Perrysburg, Lake Erie, and nearby marinas.
- Use one clear H1 that includes “boat detailing” or “marine detailing.”
- Add location/service language in body copy without keyword stuffing.
- Give image alt text service context instead of generic names like “Shore” or file names.
- Add internal anchor/section labels for Services, About, Gallery/The Details, and Estimate.

## Realistic implementation angle
This can be implemented inside the existing Wix site without a platform migration. A realistic scope is a 1 to 2 page content/layout refresh: update home hero, add trust/process blocks, reorganize service page content into clearer categories, improve the quote/contact section, and adjust mobile stacking. If the owner wants deeper form logic or SEO cleanup across all pages, that becomes a larger phase.

ROM estimate for implementation: 4 to 8 hours for the focused Wix cleanup, or roughly $400 to $900 depending on access, revisions, and whether service page restructuring is included.

## Offer fit
Good fit for a $99 Page Profit Check because the site already has legitimate content and trust. The value is making that proof easier to see and act on. The owner does not need a new brand system. They need a clearer version of what is already there.
