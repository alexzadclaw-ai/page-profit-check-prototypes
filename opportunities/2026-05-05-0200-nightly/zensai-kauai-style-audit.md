## Quick take
Zensai Kauai Style already feels personal and local: family-operated, Kaua'i-specific food, real menu photos, clear phone/email/address, and recognizable Wix-style simplicity. The main opportunity is not a rebrand. It is a surgical cleanup that keeps the same casual local food feel while making the first action obvious: call ahead for today's specials, poke bowls, or custom pupu platter/catering questions.

## What is already working
- Real local authenticity: `Behind every small business is a family` and `Mahalo for your continued support!` feel human and believable.
- The food offer is specific enough to be appetizing: poke, Hawaiian plate lunches, daily specials, poke bowls, and pupu platters.
- Contact information is present: phone, email, address, and hours are all on the page.
- Menu photos and food images give visitors confidence that this is an active local food business.
- The site does not need a premium agency redesign. Its casual, local, family-operated style is part of the trust signal.

## Biggest conversion issues
- **Critical: First action is vague.** `Get In Touch` exists, but the page does not immediately say whether visitors should call for daily specials, catering, pupu platters, or hours.
- **Critical: Hours and catering availability are buried.** The important note `CLOSED WEDNESDAY & SATURDAY Open for catering on all days` appears late and is hard to parse.
- **High: Header includes trust-damaging template/shop leftovers.** Public view shows Wix promotional copy, `Log In`, and cart `0`, which can make the business feel unfinished or ecommerce-focused when the main action is call/email.
- **High: Buyer intents are mixed together.** Walk-up plate lunch customers, poke bowl buyers, and catering/pupu platter customers need different next steps.
- **Medium: Navigation has a `More` item and duplicated menu cues.** That adds friction on a short local-food site.
- **Medium: Contact form asks for more fields than needed.** Name, email, phone, address, subject, and message can feel heavy for a simple catering or special inquiry.
- **Medium: Mobile scan path could be clearer.** Phone, hours, location, and what to ask for should be visible before the visitor scrolls too far.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Keep the recognizable Wix-local look, but rewrite the hero around the real conversion action: `Kaua'i-style poke, plate lunches & pupu platters`.
- Add two specific hero CTAs: `Call 808.378.4044` and `Ask About Specials or Catering`.
- Move hours, closed days, catering availability, and address into a three-column notice directly under the hero.
- Replace `More` with `Catering` and keep navigation simple.
- Group services by buyer intent: plate lunches, poke/bowls, and pupu platters/catering.
- Add a `What happens next` box near contact so catering customers know what information to send: date, pickup time, number of people, and daily-special question.
- Remove or suppress template/shop leftovers: Wix banner, login, and cart.
- Retain family/local wording, food photos, Playfair/Raleway typography feel, centered brand treatment, and simple section rhythm.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Page title should include location and services, for example: `Zensai Kauai Style LLC | Hanapepe Poke, Plate Lunches & Catering`.
- Meta description should mention family-operated, Hanapepe/Kauai, poke, plate lunches, daily specials, pupu platters, and catering.
- Use one clear H1 that includes primary services instead of repeating the business name as the main content headline.
- Add descriptive alt text to food photos instead of raw filenames like `IMG_3862_edited.jpg`.
- Ensure address uses consistent spacing and spelling: `1-3566 Kaumualii Hwy, Hanapepe, Kauai`.
- If implemented in Wix, add local business structured data if available through Wix SEO settings.
