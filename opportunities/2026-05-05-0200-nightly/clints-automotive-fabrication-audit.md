# Clint's Automotive & Fabrication LLC - Audit

## Quick take
Clint's Automotive & Fabrication LLC already feels like a real working shop: clear phone/email, a real Glenmora address, hours, payments accepted, Facebook link, and a broad service list. The biggest opportunity is not a full redesign. It is a surgical cleanup that keeps the practical Wix/shop feel while making the first screen say what the shop does, who it serves, why to trust it, and exactly how to request help.

## What is already working
- The site clearly identifies a real business with phone, email, Facebook, street address, hours, and accepted payment methods.
- It communicates honest shop values: transparency, fair pricing, professionalism, and customer satisfaction.
- The service range is valuable and local: diagnostics, maintenance, repairs, brakes, suspension, HVAC, lift kits, sound systems, ATV/UTV, boats, and fabrication.
- The free check engine light diagnostic is a strong conversion hook.
- Military and veteran discounts are a trust-builder and should be visible earlier.
- The Wix structure is simple enough that improvements can likely be made without rebuilding the whole site.

## Biggest conversion issues
| Finding | Severity | Why it matters |
|---|---:|---|
| Hero opens with generic welcome language instead of a specific repair/fabrication promise. | Critical | Visitors need to know immediately that the shop handles diagnostics, repair, fabrication, ATVs/UTVs, boats, and custom work in Glenmora/Cenla. |
| Estimate section still contains template copy: `This is your Price Quote section paragraph...`. | Critical | Template leftovers damage trust, especially before someone submits personal contact information. |
| Service list is long, repetitive, and not organized by buyer intent. | High | A customer with a brake issue, check engine light, ATV, or fabrication need must scan too much to confirm fit. |
| Long all-caps mission paragraph feels harder to read and less modern. | Medium | The sentiment is good, but the presentation makes it feel less polished and less mobile-friendly. |
| Veteran discount, hours, location, and next steps are too far down or visually under-emphasized. | High | Trust signals and logistics should help the visitor decide before they reach the form. |
| The quote/contact forms do not make clear what happens after submitting. | High | People hesitate when they do not know whether they are booking, asking a question, or starting an estimate. |
| SEO title and on-page phrasing could be more location/service specific. | Medium | The current page can better target `auto repair Glenmora LA`, `Cenla diagnostics`, and `custom fabrication` searches. |

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Rewrite the hero to say: auto repair, diagnostics, and custom fabrication in Glenmora/Cenla.
- Move trust near the top: military/veteran discount, fair pricing, Monday-Friday hours, real phone/address.
- Replace generic price-quote template copy with a specific repair estimate request flow.
- Group services by customer intent: diagnose a problem, repair/maintain, customize/fabricate.
- Turn the all-caps shop promise into a readable trust paragraph that keeps the original meaning.
- Add `what happens next` steps before the form: send basics, shop reviews, approve before work starts.
- Keep the Wix look and practical shop identity rather than replacing it with a premium agency design.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Page title should include the business name, `Auto Repair`, `Fabrication`, and `Glenmora, LA`.
- Meta description should mention diagnostics, brakes, suspension, HVAC, lift kits, ATV/UTV, boats, fabrication, and Cenla/Glenmora service area.
- Use one clear H1 tied to the main search intent, not only a welcome message.
- Add structured service headings that match how customers search.
- Make NAP details consistent: `37 Goodman Rd. Glenmora, LA 71433`, phone `318-612-2572`, email `clints_auto@yahoo.com`.
- Consider adding local business schema later if the site supports custom embed/code.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a light Wix cleanup, not a rebuild. The owner likely needs:
1. Hero text replacement and CTA updates.
2. Service section reorganization into 3-4 cards or grouped rows.
3. Estimate section copy replacement and form field labels adjusted.
4. Hours/contact/payment block tightened.
5. SEO title/meta/H1 updates.

Estimated implementation range: 3-6 hours in Wix if the existing site editor access is available. A practical ROM is $350-$750 for the cleanup, depending on whether form routing, image replacement, and SEO settings are included.
