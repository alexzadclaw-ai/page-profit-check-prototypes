# Miami Frameless Shower Doors - Audit

## Quick take
Miami Frameless Shower Doors already has the basics a local buyer needs: a phone number, email address, Miami address, project photos, service labels, and a clear shower-door niche. The biggest problem is trust leakage. Several visible paragraphs are still Wix template copy, the social links point to Wix, and the service hierarchy does not quickly explain which shower-door option fits which buyer.

The Solution A prototype keeps the same simple blue/white contractor feel and Wix-style section rhythm, but replaces placeholders with specific service copy, moves trust near the top, makes the free-quote CTA more specific, and shows what happens after a customer calls or emails.

## What is already working
- The business name clearly says what they do: frameless shower doors in Miami.
- Phone number and email are visible and clickable in the source.
- Real project photos are present, which is valuable for a visual trade.
- The site already separates services, projects, about, and contact sections.
- The blue/white contractor aesthetic is simple, recognizable, and appropriate for a local service business.
- The site includes local address information and a long-running year-establishing cue, 2008.

## Biggest conversion issues
1. **Critical: visible Wix placeholder paragraphs.** Multiple sections still say “I'm a paragraph. Click here to add your own text and edit me.” This undermines confidence immediately.
2. **Critical: generic hero message.** The first large message is not a buyer-centered value proposition. It says licensed/insured and call for quote, but does not clarify services, location, or next step.
3. **High: fake or leftover social links.** Social icons link to Wix/Facebook/Instagram/YouTube defaults, not business profiles.
4. **High: unclear service hierarchy.** “Frameless,” “L Shape,” and “Sliding” are present, but buyers do not get enough guidance about when to choose each.
5. **Medium: weak quote path.** Contact text says call or fill out a form, but the site does not set expectations for photos, measurements, measurement appointment, quote, or install.
6. **Medium: mobile scanning.** The original page is long and template-heavy, so mobile users have to scroll through weak copy before understanding fit.
7. **Low: image alt text and project labels.** Project labels like `image4 (1)` are unpolished and miss SEO/trust value.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Replace all template copy with trade-specific, plain-English content.
- Keep the hero photo and contractor layout, but make the hero headline specific: licensed and insured custom shower door installation.
- Add a trust strip directly under the hero with local, custom-measured, and contact-visible cues.
- Rewrite service cards by buyer intent: frameless shower doors, L-shape custom enclosures, sliding/hydroslide doors.
- Add a four-step quote process: call/email, measure, quote, install.
- Convert generic contact form intent into a simpler call/email quote checklist.
- Remove or neutralize fake Wix social destinations and template footer residue.
- Add descriptive alt text to project and service images.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Change the page title from `HOME | framelessshowerdoor` to a local service title such as `Miami Frameless Shower Doors | Custom Glass Shower Doors in Miami`.
- Add a meta description focused on Miami, custom frameless shower doors, sliding doors, hydroslide doors, and free estimates.
- Use one clear H1/hero concept and keep section headings semantic.
- Add descriptive image alt text instead of `image4 (1)` and blank project labels.
- Mention service area naturally in copy: Miami and surrounding Miami-area bathrooms.
- If they keep Wix, connect a real domain and remove the free Wix banner if their plan allows it.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is not a redesign from scratch. The most realistic implementation is a same-builder cleanup inside Wix:
- Keep the existing header, logo, phone number, section order, colors, and images.
- Replace placeholder text blocks section by section.
- Update button labels and links to `tel:` and `mailto:` actions.
- Swap the contact form or add a short quote checklist beside it.
- Remove default Wix social links or point them to real business profiles.
- Update page title, meta description, and image alt text in Wix SEO settings.

ROM implementation estimate: 3 to 6 hours for a focused one-page cleanup in Wix if the current assets and login are available. If adding new gallery organization, domain cleanup, or review/testimonial collection, estimate 6 to 10 hours.
