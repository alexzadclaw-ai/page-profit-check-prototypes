# Harvest Accounting LLC Audit

## Quick take
Harvest Accounting LLC already has credible raw material: a clear bookkeeping category, QuickBooks Certified ProAdvisor proof, wine-industry specialization, Beaverton / Willamette Valley relevance, and direct contact information. The current Wix page weakens those assets by leading with a broad headline, hiding the strongest trust cues in paragraph copy, leaving the consultation path vague, and showing Wix template residue.

## What is already working
- The business category is immediately understandable: bookkeeping and accounting for small / medium businesses.
- QuickBooks Certified ProAdvisor is a strong trust marker.
- Oregon wine industry specialization is a useful differentiator for Willamette Valley prospects.
- The site includes direct email, phone, and location information.
- The simple Wix aesthetic feels approachable and consistent with a solo professional service provider.

## Biggest conversion issues
1. **Critical: strongest trust proof is not near the first decision point.** QuickBooks certification, wine-industry fit, and Scott Philbrook’s business background appear after the visitor has already passed the hero.
2. **Critical: primary CTA is too generic.** “Get in Touch” does not tell a prospect what to ask for or what happens next.
3. **High: services are described by paragraph instead of buyer intent.** Wineries, small businesses, and CPA coordination are all valid audiences, but they are not separated into scan-friendly paths.
4. **High: Wix residue damages trust.** The visible Wix banner and “Proudly created with Wix.com” footer make the firm feel less established than the actual copy suggests.
5. **Medium: the contact section has a submit label but not enough expectation-setting.** A prospect does not know whether they are requesting a consultation, monthly bookkeeping, cleanup, or QuickBooks help.
6. **Medium: mobile scanning is weak.** Dense paragraphs make the most persuasive facts easy to miss on a phone.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Keep the same builder-style structure and visual fingerprint, but rewrite the hero to include QuickBooks, Oregon businesses, wineries/vineyards, and a specific consultation CTA.
- Move trust proof into three small cards directly under the About copy: QuickBooks Certified ProAdvisor, wine industry familiarity, business finance background.
- Convert the broad service paragraph into three buyer-intent cards: wineries/vineyards, small businesses, and CPA coordination.
- Add a short “simple next steps” section so prospects know how to start.
- Remove Wix/template leftovers from the visible experience.
- Make mobile sections shorter, stacked, and easier to scan.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Title tag should include service, location, and specialty, for example: `Harvest Accounting LLC | QuickBooks Bookkeeping for Oregon Small Businesses`.
- Meta description should mention QuickBooks bookkeeping, small businesses, Oregon, and wine-industry clients.
- Add a single clear H1-like hero message tied to bookkeeping and accounting.
- Add local relevance in visible body copy: Beaverton, NW Oregon, Willamette Valley, Oregon wine industry.
- Add descriptive alt text to the hero/background image if implemented in Wix as an image element.
- If the Blog remains, keep the QuickBooks ProAdvisor article but add internal links back to the consultation CTA.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a surgical Wix cleanup, not a rebuild. The likely implementation path is to duplicate the existing Wix page, update hero copy and CTA links, add three trust cards, add three service cards, add a simple next-steps strip, clean the footer/banner if possible via Wix plan/settings, and adjust mobile spacing. A realistic ROM is 3 to 6 hours for a polished one-page refresh if Wix account access and assets are available. If the Wix ad banner requires a paid Wix plan to remove, that is a platform/account decision outside the page-edit scope.

## Offer fit
Good fit for a $99 Page Profit Check because the business does not need a new brand system. The opportunity is a conversion cleanup: make the existing professional service page clearer, faster to trust, and more action-oriented while preserving the recognizable Wix site.
