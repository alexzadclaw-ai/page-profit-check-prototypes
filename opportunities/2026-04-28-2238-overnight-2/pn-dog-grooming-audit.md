# PN Dog Grooming — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
PN Dog Grooming has recognizable local-business value and enough existing proof to justify a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** rather than a redesign. The opportunity is to preserve the current visual family while making the first screen, trust proof, service choices, and next-step path easier for a first-time buyer to understand.

**Recommendation:** keep the site’s current brand cues and rebuild the homepage hierarchy around one clear CTA: **Schedule a Grooming Visit**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Existing logo/wordmark preserved in the prototype.
- **Dominant colors:** #d77813, #9e5205, #fff8ee, with dark text/contrast.
- **Typography feel:** Open Sans, Arial, sans-serif with Futura, Open Sans, sans-serif heading feel.
- **Header/nav pattern:** horizontal local-business navigation with phone/contact emphasis.
- **Hero/image treatment:** image-led hero using the same industry/brand direction.
- **Section rhythm:** service cards, trust proof, process/next-step block, contact section.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:** Network Solutions-style small business site with warm orange/brown palette, paw logo, Futura/Open Sans typography, large script-style heading, dog grooming imagery, and simple top nav.

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with real local-service intent.
- The site already contains useful proof: All sizes and breeds welcome, Safe eco-friendly products, Trained dog groomers.
- Contact information is available and the business can receive direct inquiries.
- The existing visual direction is recognizable enough to preserve instead of replacing.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The homepage has warm service proof but no strong appointment CTA in the hero.
   - Prototype response: Add a hero CTA labeled “Schedule a Grooming Visit” plus a phone CTA.
2. **High:** Service details are present but not packaged for quick buyer decisions.
   - Prototype response: Create three scan-friendly service cards: full grooming, bath/tidy, patient handling.
3. **High:** Trust bullets are buried in the “Why Choose” list rather than highlighted near the top.
   - Prototype response: Move breed/size, safe products, trained groomers, and phone/email into a trust strip.
4. **Critical:** Footer shows “Column” placeholder-like links, which hurts polish.
   - Prototype response: Replace placeholder columns with contact, hours, service area, and schedule links.
5. **Medium:** Hours/service-area information appears thin on the homepage.
   - Prototype response: Add a small “what to expect” and local Naperville framing section.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Replace scattered first-screen messaging with a clear buyer promise: “Gentle dog grooming in Naperville for every size and breed.”
2. Make **Schedule a Grooming Visit** the primary action in the hero, process section, and contact block.
3. Move trust proof near the top: All sizes and breeds welcome, Safe eco-friendly products, Trained dog groomers, Phone and email visible.
4. Organize services by buyer intent instead of making visitors decode every page/link.
5. Add a simple “what happens next” flow before the form.
6. Clean template residue, duplicate text, stale content, or technical form clutter where present.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + Naperville/local area + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add alt text for primary service images and logo assets.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a strong fit for a focused homepage cleanup sprint. The existing site does not need to be reinvented; it needs a cleaner top section, clearer CTA hierarchy, tighter service grouping, more visible trust, and a contact section that tells visitors what will happen next.
