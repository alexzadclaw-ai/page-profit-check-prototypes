# Sudzy Puppy — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Sudzy Puppy has recognizable local-business value and enough existing proof to justify a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** rather than a redesign. The opportunity is to preserve the current visual family while making the first screen, trust proof, service choices, and next-step path easier for a first-time buyer to understand.

**Recommendation:** keep the site’s current brand cues and rebuild the homepage hierarchy around one clear CTA: **Book an Appointment**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Existing logo/wordmark preserved in the prototype.
- **Dominant colors:** #006dad, #f3a8bd, #fff7fb, with dark text/contrast.
- **Typography feel:** Roboto, Arial, sans-serif with Quicksand, Roboto, sans-serif heading feel.
- **Header/nav pattern:** horizontal local-business navigation with phone/contact emphasis.
- **Hero/image treatment:** image-led hero using the same industry/brand direction.
- **Section rhythm:** service cards, trust proof, process/next-step block, contact section.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:** Polished WordPress pet salon with large illustrated logo, blue/pink/white palette, Quicksand/Roboto typography, award images, grooming photo grid, and tidy top navigation.

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with real local-service intent.
- The site already contains useful proof: Best of Naperville recognition, Full pet spa services, Walk-in services available.
- Contact information is available and the business can receive direct inquiries.
- The existing visual direction is recognizable enough to preserve instead of replacing.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **High:** The hero is visually pleasant but does not immediately explain which service to book first.
   - Prototype response: Add a hero subhead and two clear CTAs: book appointment and view services/pricing.
2. **High:** Service links are numerous and repeat “Details & Pricing,” making first-time scanning slower.
   - Prototype response: Group services into three buyer paths: full groom, bath/tidy, spa/specialty.
3. **Medium:** Best of Naperville proof is visible but could be integrated into the trust story.
   - Prototype response: Add a trust strip with award, walk-ins, spa services, and hours.
4. **Medium:** Footer/contact info is useful but appears after the service grid and slider controls.
   - Prototype response: Add contact and hours teaser higher on the page.
5. **Medium:** Slider labels and repeated controls add clutter.
   - Prototype response: Replace slider-style feature links with static cards.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Replace scattered first-screen messaging with a clear buyer promise: “Premier pet grooming in Naperville — book the right service faster.”
2. Make **Book an Appointment** the primary action in the hero, process section, and contact block.
3. Move trust proof near the top: Best of Naperville recognition, Full pet spa services, Walk-in services available, Open Mon–Sat 8am–6pm.
4. Organize services by buyer intent instead of making visitors decode every page/link.
5. Add a simple “what happens next” flow before the form.
6. Clean template residue, duplicate text, stale content, or technical form clutter where present.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + Naperville/local area + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add alt text for primary service images and logo assets.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a strong fit for a focused homepage cleanup sprint. The existing site does not need to be reinvented; it needs a cleaner top section, clearer CTA hierarchy, tighter service grouping, more visible trust, and a contact section that tells visitors what will happen next.
