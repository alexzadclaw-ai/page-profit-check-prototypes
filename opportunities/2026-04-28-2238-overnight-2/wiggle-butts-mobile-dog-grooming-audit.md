# Wiggle Butts Mobile Dog Grooming — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Wiggle Butts Mobile Dog Grooming has recognizable local-business value and enough existing proof to justify a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** rather than a redesign. The opportunity is to preserve the current visual family while making the first screen, trust proof, service choices, and next-step path easier for a first-time buyer to understand.

**Recommendation:** keep the site’s current brand cues and rebuild the homepage hierarchy around one clear CTA: **Request a Mobile Grooming Quote**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Existing logo/wordmark preserved in the prototype.
- **Dominant colors:** #f5b6c8, #8b4a5e, #fff6ef, with dark text/contrast.
- **Typography feel:** Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif with Chelsea Market, Arial, sans-serif heading feel.
- **Header/nav pattern:** horizontal local-business navigation with phone/contact emphasis.
- **Hero/image treatment:** image-led hero using the same industry/brand direction.
- **Section rhythm:** service cards, trust proof, process/next-step block, contact section.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:** Wix mobile dog grooming site with pale peach/pink nav, playful Chelsea Market-style headline, dog photo banner, rounded benefit cards, repeated phone text, and warm casual brand voice.

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with real local-service intent.
- The site already contains useful proof: Mobile salon at your home, No crates / hand drying, 1–2 hour typical appointments.
- Contact information is available and the business can receive direct inquiries.
- The existing visual direction is recognizable enough to preserve instead of replacing.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The first screen has repeated phone/announcement text and a nearly blank H1, weakening immediate clarity.
   - Prototype response: Create a clear mobile-grooming hero with service area, size limits, and quote CTA.
2. **High:** Important eligibility details are buried in a long announcement sentence.
   - Prototype response: Turn dog-size limits and service towns into a scan-friendly availability panel.
3. **High:** The value of mobile grooming is strong but presented after duplicated text.
   - Prototype response: Keep convenience/no crates/shorter process as three clear benefit cards.
4. **High:** Booking options are split across Book Online, Contact/Quote, phone, and email.
   - Prototype response: Unify into one “Request a Mobile Grooming Quote” path with phone/text as secondary.
5. **Medium:** Repeated text fragments make the site feel more cluttered than the friendly brand deserves.
   - Prototype response: Remove duplicate spans and simplify footer contact presentation.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Replace scattered first-screen messaging with a clear buyer promise: “Mobile dog grooming that wiggles to you in Naperville and nearby suburbs.”
2. Make **Request a Mobile Grooming Quote** the primary action in the hero, process section, and contact block.
3. Move trust proof near the top: Mobile salon at your home, No crates / hand drying, 1–2 hour typical appointments, New clients now welcomed.
4. Organize services by buyer intent instead of making visitors decode every page/link.
5. Add a simple “what happens next” flow before the form.
6. Clean template residue, duplicate text, stale content, or technical form clutter where present.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + Naperville/local area + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add alt text for primary service images and logo assets.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a strong fit for a focused homepage cleanup sprint. The existing site does not need to be reinvented; it needs a cleaner top section, clearer CTA hierarchy, tighter service grouping, more visible trust, and a contact section that tells visitors what will happen next.
