# Perfect Manners Dog Training — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Perfect Manners Dog Training has recognizable local-business value and enough existing proof to justify a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** rather than a redesign. The opportunity is to preserve the current visual family while making the first screen, trust proof, service choices, and next-step path easier for a first-time buyer to understand.

**Recommendation:** keep the site’s current brand cues and rebuild the homepage hierarchy around one clear CTA: **Schedule a Training Consultation**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Text-based brand treatment preserved with similar local-business styling.
- **Dominant colors:** #52b3c6, #a3d27d, #f4fbfd, with dark text/contrast.
- **Typography feel:** Quicksand, Arial, sans-serif with Quicksand, Arial, sans-serif heading feel.
- **Header/nav pattern:** horizontal local-business navigation with phone/contact emphasis.
- **Hero/image treatment:** image-led hero using the same industry/brand direction.
- **Section rhythm:** service cards, trust proof, process/next-step block, contact section.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:** WordPress dog-training site with blue/green accents, Quicksand typography, social icons, slider hero, service cards, testimonials, article grids, and a behavior-focused message.

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with real local-service intent.
- The site already contains useful proof: Professional dog trainer/behaviorist, In-home individualized training, Behavior modification focus.
- Contact information is available and the business can receive direct inquiries.
- The existing visual direction is recognizable enough to preserve instead of replacing.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The slider communicates the mission but does not provide a clear consultation CTA above the fold.
   - Prototype response: Add a direct “Schedule a Training Consultation” button in the hero.
2. **High:** Strong differentiators are list items under “Why Us” rather than a visible trust strip.
   - Prototype response: Move professional behaviorist, in-home, behavior-not-tricks, and results proof near the top.
3. **High:** Service cards exist but need a clearer problem-to-service path.
   - Prototype response: Group into obedience, behavior modification, and board/train or boarding options.
4. **Medium:** Articles and news create useful SEO depth but compete with lead generation on the homepage.
   - Prototype response: Keep article links lower after services, testimonials, and CTA.
5. **Medium:** Some wording is intense (“loaded pistol”) and may be better softened for first-touch trust.
   - Prototype response: Keep the urgency but rewrite around safety, calm, and communication.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Replace scattered first-screen messaging with a clear buyer promise: “In-home dog training in Naperville that solves behavior, not just tricks.”
2. Make **Schedule a Training Consultation** the primary action in the hero, process section, and contact block.
3. Move trust proof near the top: Professional dog trainer/behaviorist, In-home individualized training, Behavior modification focus, Results often seen immediately.
4. Organize services by buyer intent instead of making visitors decode every page/link.
5. Add a simple “what happens next” flow before the form.
6. Clean template residue, duplicate text, stale content, or technical form clutter where present.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + Naperville/local area + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add alt text for primary service images and logo assets.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a strong fit for a focused homepage cleanup sprint. The existing site does not need to be reinvented; it needs a cleaner top section, clearer CTA hierarchy, tighter service grouping, more visible trust, and a contact section that tells visitors what will happen next.
