# Awaken Massages & Skin Care — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Awaken Massages & Skin Care has recognizable local-business value and enough existing proof to justify a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** rather than a redesign. The opportunity is to preserve the current visual family while making the first screen, trust proof, service choices, and next-step path easier for a first-time buyer to understand.

**Recommendation:** keep the site’s current brand cues and rebuild the homepage hierarchy around one clear CTA: **Book Your Team Member**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Text-based brand treatment preserved with similar local-business styling.
- **Dominant colors:** #79624f, #c4a47c, #f7efe5, with dark text/contrast.
- **Typography feel:** Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif with Georgia, Times, serif heading feel.
- **Header/nav pattern:** horizontal local-business navigation with phone/contact emphasis.
- **Hero/image treatment:** image-led hero using the same industry/brand direction.
- **Section rhythm:** service cards, trust proof, process/next-step block, contact section.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:** Wix wellness site with neutral tan/brown tones, clean white sections, serif/Arial mix, team booking cards, $15-off form, and a broad service menu.

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with real local-service intent.
- The site already contains useful proof: Full 30/60/90/120-minute sessions, Chronic pain and nervous-system support, Massage + clinical skin care.
- Contact information is available and the business can receive direct inquiries.
- The existing visual direction is recognizable enough to preserve instead of replacing.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The captured page opens around FAQ/founder text before a crisp homepage hero, making orientation harder.
   - Prototype response: Lead with a clear massage/skin-care/wellness hero and booking CTA.
2. **High:** Copy has punctuation errors and repeated fragments that reduce polish.
   - Prototype response: Tighten mission copy into short, client-facing paragraphs.
3. **High:** Booking is strong but distributed by team member links and offer form.
   - Prototype response: Turn the existing three-step “Select Team Member / Choose Service / Book Appointment” into a prominent process block.
4. **High:** Service breadth is impressive but broad enough to overwhelm first-time visitors.
   - Prototype response: Group into massage, skin care, and additional wellness service cards.
5. **Medium:** The $15 offer competes with general booking without context.
   - Prototype response: Use it as a secondary CTA for new clients rather than the main hero action.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Replace scattered first-screen messaging with a clear buyer promise: “Personalized massage, skin care, and holistic bodywork in Naperville.”
2. Make **Book Your Team Member** the primary action in the hero, process section, and contact block.
3. Move trust proof near the top: Full 30/60/90/120-minute sessions, Chronic pain and nervous-system support, Massage + clinical skin care, Online booking by team member.
4. Organize services by buyer intent instead of making visitors decode every page/link.
5. Add a simple “what happens next” flow before the form.
6. Clean template residue, duplicate text, stale content, or technical form clutter where present.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + Naperville/local area + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add alt text for primary service images and logo assets.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a strong fit for a focused homepage cleanup sprint. The existing site does not need to be reinvented; it needs a cleaner top section, clearer CTA hierarchy, tighter service grouping, more visible trust, and a contact section that tells visitors what will happen next.
