# Page Profit Check Audit - Iron City Handyman Services LLC

## Quick take

Iron City Handyman Services LLC is a practical fit for a Page Profit Check because the site already has real handyman and property repair intent, public contact information, and enough source material to improve without replacing the identity. The main opportunity is a tighter first screen, clearer service grouping, and a faster quote path for visitors in Birmingham metro, AL.

## What is already working

- The business category is clear: Handyman and property repair.
- The service area is usable: Birmingham metro, AL.
- The current page has recognizable source assets from ironcityhandymanse.wixsite.com.
- The live page exposes service language around: handyman, reliable, birmingham, washing, iron, city.
- Contact information is available: IRONCITYHANDYMANSERVICES@GMAIL.COM.

## Biggest conversion issues

- The first screen asks visitors to work too hard before the next action is obvious.
- Service proof and contact prompts need to be closer to the top of the page.
- The page would convert better if the main service paths were grouped by buyer intent.
- Mobile visitors need shorter sections, more visible phone/email actions, and less hunting for next steps.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements

- Preserve the existing ironcityhandymanse.wixsite.com visual direction and tighten the hero around one primary estimate action.
- Add a compact trust strip with service area, response promise, and proof from the current page.
- Group the strongest services into three scan-friendly cards.
- Add a short what-happens-next section before the final contact block.
- Keep phone and email actions visible without introducing unrelated stock imagery.

## SEO basics to tighten

- Page title should combine Iron City Handyman Services LLC, handyman and property repair, and Birmingham metro, AL.
- H1 should clearly state the core service and service area, not only the brand name.
- Service-section headings should match local search intent.
- Reused source images should have descriptive alt text.
- NAP details should stay consistent between the header, contact area, and footer.

## Realistic implementation angle

Preserve the owner-operated service feel while making services, trust proof, and the quote action easier to scan.

This is a small implementation, not a rebuild. A practical sprint would focus on homepage hierarchy, CTA labels, service-card copy, mobile spacing, and the contact flow.

## Offer fit

The $99 Page Profit Check fits as an audit and diagnosis only. It would give Iron City Handyman Services LLC a prioritized punch list, CTA/link cleanup notes, and a simple before/after direction. Implementation would be separate from the $99 audit.
