# Alex Handyman Service Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take

Alex Handyman Service already has the core ingredients of a useful local handyman page: a phone number, email, service list, real about copy, customer testimonials, Orange County service-area language, a quote form, a headshot, Yelp/TaskRabbit links, and a memorable yellow/blue visual identity. The main opportunity is to make the same Wix page feel more current, more credible, and easier to act on without making it look like a different business.

## What is already working

- The phone number is visible in the header, hero, and contact section.
- The page has a simple one-page structure: hero, services, about, testimonials, contact.
- The bright yellow header and blue service/contact sections are distinctive and easy to remember.
- The hero image gives the page an immediate handyman/home-service feel.
- The services list names real jobs customers search for: furniture assembly, TV mounting, shelves, cabinets, painting, minor repairs, and fixture work.
- The about section includes a real person, a headshot, and a clear “not a licensed contractor” disclaimer.
- The testimonials provide social proof.
- Yelp and TaskRabbit links are present, which can support trust if they are active/current.

## Biggest conversion issues

- The Wix ad banner and “proudly created with Wix” footer make the business feel less polished.
- The page title is still `HOME | alexhandyman`, which looks unfinished in search and browser tabs.
- The hero headline is memorable but too broad. It does not immediately say Orange County, handyman repairs, furniture assembly, mounting, or installations.
- The main `GET A FREE QUOTE!` button points back to the same page rather than opening a call, email, or form path.
- The services section is visually recognizable but dense. Several columns are long lists with small text and no quote guidance.
- The term `Electricity` could imply licensed electrical work. It should be narrowed to small handyman-safe fixture tasks unless Alex has licensing.
- The page has a large blank gap between testimonials and contact, delaying the form and contact details.
- The testimonials mention Brooklyn and NY, which conflicts with the current Orange County, CA service area.
- The form button says `GET A JOB DONE`, while the rest of the page says `GET A FREE QUOTE!`. The action language should be consistent.
- The email and phone are visible, but the page does not tell visitors what details to send for a fast estimate.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements

- Keep the yellow header, blue/black section stack, hero worker photo, five service icons, headshot, testimonials, and contact form so the page still feels like the same site.
- Replace the Wix ad strip with a simple trust/quote strip for Orange County handyman work.
- Rewrite the first screen around a clearer offer: repairs, assembly, mounting, painting touchups, and installations in Orange County.
- Make the hero CTAs functional: call the phone number or email job details.
- Add a small trust strip above the fold: clear price before work, small repairs welcome, not a licensed contractor.
- Tighten service descriptions into scannable quote categories.
- Add quote instructions near services and the form: city, service needed, timeline, and photos.
- Soften `Electricity` into a narrower small-fixture/handyman context.
- Bring contact intent earlier and reduce unnecessary whitespace before the form.
- Keep Yelp and TaskRabbit, but only link to profiles that are real and current.

## SEO basics to tighten

- Change the title from `HOME | alexhandyman` to something like `Alex Handyman Service | Orange County Repairs and Assembly`.
- Use one clear H1 that includes handyman, Orange County, and the top services.
- Add a meta description mentioning furniture assembly, minor repairs, TV mounting, installations, painting, and Orange County.
- Add descriptive alt text for the hero photo, headshot, and service icons.
- Use consistent service-area wording across the page.
- Update or remove location-conflicting testimonials if stronger Orange County-specific reviews are available.
- Add LocalBusiness schema later if Wix settings allow it.
- Make phone and email links clickable everywhere they appear.

## Realistic implementation angle

This does not need a rebuild. The practical path is a focused Wix page edit:

1. Keep the existing visual identity, image assets, service categories, section order, and general Wix layout.
2. Replace template/platform leftovers where possible.
3. Rewrite the hero and services copy for a quote-first path.
4. Make the primary CTA click-to-call and the secondary CTA click-to-email.
5. Add quote instructions near the service list and contact form.
6. Reduce the oversized gap before contact.
7. Update page title, meta description, H1, alt text, and form/button labels.
