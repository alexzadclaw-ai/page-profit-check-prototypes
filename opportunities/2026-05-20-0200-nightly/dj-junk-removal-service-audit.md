# DJ Junk Removal Service Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
DJ Junk Removal Service is a strong Page Profit Check fit. The live Wix site already has what a local junk-removal visitor needs: phone and text numbers, an email address, Sarasota-area service copy, a real work gallery, service categories, eco-friendly donation/recycling language, and a simple free-estimate promise.

The conversion gap is hierarchy. The top of the page has a Wix promo banner, separate call/text strip, nav, social icons, a large hero, repeated estimate buttons, and a long service paragraph. The raw material is good, but visitors should understand within a few seconds what DJ removes, where they work, and whether to call, text, or send a free-estimate request.

Recommendation: keep the cream, dark green, and burnt orange color system, real trailer/work photos, service-card gallery feel, review, and contact form. Tighten the first screen around call/text/free estimate, service areas, and eco-friendly disposal.

## What is already working
- The page has direct phone and text actions: `(941) 323-7255` and `(941) 260-7928`.
- The email address `djjunkremovalservice@gmail.com` is visible in the contact section.
- The site clearly lists junk removal, furniture removal, appliance removal, construction removal, E-waste recycling, commercial junk removal, storage unit clean-out, and storm/disaster cleanup.
- Sarasota, Lakewood Ranch, Siesta Key, Venice, and Bradenton are named, which helps local relevance.
- The eco-friendly positioning is useful because donation and recycling can reduce price resistance and landfill concerns.
- The visual identity is recognizable: cream background, dark forest green blocks, burnt orange CTAs, logo graphics, work photos, and social icons.
- The work gallery gives the business more credibility than a generic service page.
- The contact form asks for enough information to support a quote request.

## Biggest conversion issues
- **Critical: The free-estimate path is not packaged clearly.** The site repeats `Free Quote`, `Get a Free Estimate`, and `Free Estimate`, but the user is not told what to send or whether call/text is fastest.
- **Critical: The first screen has too many competing elements.** Wix banner, phone strip, nav, social icons, hero photo, headline, paragraph, and CTAs all fight for attention.
- **High: Service-area proof is buried in a paragraph.** Sarasota, Lakewood Ranch, Siesta Key, Venice, and Bradenton should be visible as quick trust proof.
- **High: Eco-friendly proof is underused.** Donation and recycling language is valuable, but it appears mainly in longer copy rather than in a scannable proof strip.
- **Medium: Copy needs polishing.** Phrases such as `DJ. removal it for you` and inconsistent capitalization make the business feel less professional than the photos suggest.
- **Medium: The contact section could convert better.** It has a form, phone, text, address, and email, but the next-step instructions are vague.
- **Medium: Wix/template residue reduces trust.** The top Wix promo banner and builder language make the business look less established.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Turn the hero into a simple promise: eco-friendly junk removal and cleanouts around Sarasota, with call/text/free-estimate actions visible immediately.
- Add a quick trust strip for direct call, text quote, eco-friendly disposal, and Sarasota-area service.
- Keep the original logo, trailer/work photo, dark green blocks, cream background, and orange buttons so the page still feels like DJ Junk Removal Service.
- Give visitors instructions for a faster estimate: send photos, service address, item list, and preferred timing.
- Keep the service categories, but make each card easier to scan with short labels and consistent hierarchy.
- Move service-area and recycling/donation proof higher on the page.
- Polish awkward wording while keeping the plain local-service voice.
- Consider removing Wix promotional residue from the customer-facing version.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a focused title such as `DJ Junk Removal Service | Junk Removal in Sarasota, FL`.
- Use one primary H1 that includes the business name and primary service.
- Add a meta description mentioning junk removal, furniture removal, appliance removal, construction debris, E-waste recycling, Sarasota, Lakewood Ranch, Venice, and Bradenton.
- Add descriptive alt text to the logo, trailer, service cards, and work gallery images.
- Add LocalBusiness or HomeAndConstructionBusiness schema after confirming the preferred public address, phone, service areas, and social profiles.
- Use consistent NAP formatting for phone, text, email, address, and social links.
- Create short service sections for furniture removal, appliance removal, commercial cleanouts, E-waste, and construction debris so local search intent is easier to understand.

## Realistic implementation angle
This does not need a full rebrand. The best implementation is a surgical Wix homepage cleanup: reorganize the first screen, tighten copy, add a trust strip, improve service-card hierarchy, clarify quote instructions, polish contact copy, and remove or reduce template residue. Existing photos and brand colors should stay.

## Offer fit
Good fit for a **$99 Page Profit Check** because the site has enough real proof to diagnose specific conversion leaks. The $99 offer should be positioned as audit and prioritization only.

Implementation should be separate from the $99 diagnosis. A practical follow-up cleanup would likely be a small Wix polish sprint, not a rebuild. Rough ROM: 4 to 7 hours for homepage CTA, copy, service, contact, and SEO-field cleanup.
