# JustIn Time Contracting Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take

JustIn Time Contracting has a trustworthy local-handyman foundation: a clear phone number, direct email, insured-contractor language, service examples, and real project photos. The biggest opportunity is to make the homepage work harder as a quote path instead of a simple brochure: say where Justin works, name the most common jobs, and give visitors a quick call/email path with instructions for sending project details.

## What is already working

- The phone number is visible at the top and bottom of the site.
- The business name and `General Contracting & Handyman Services` positioning are prominent.
- The patriotic flag image creates a recognizable visual identity.
- The copy says Justin is reliable, insured, and handles repair, maintenance, remodeling, furniture assembly, and miscellaneous projects.
- The Services page adds useful specifics: remodeling and repairs, basement office work, cabinet handles, smoke alarms, furniture assembly, custom shelves, and broad project help.
- The Gallery page has real work examples, which is a strong trust asset for a handyman business.
- The email address is clickable via `mailto:`.

## Biggest conversion issues

- The Wix ad banner is still visible at the top, which makes the business look less polished.
- The homepage does not clearly say `Indianapolis` or the primary service area above the fold, even though the phone number suggests a local Indiana service.
- There is no strong primary CTA button in the hero. Visitors see the phone number, but they are not guided to call or email project photos.
- The hero headline repeats the business name but does not quickly sort visitors by job type.
- Good proof photos live on the Services/Gallery pages, but the homepage does not surface them near the first decision point.
- The site has no contact form or quote instructions, so visitors may not know what to send for a fast estimate.
- The page title is still `HOME | My Site 1`, which hurts professionalism and search appearance.
- Services and Gallery pages contain helpful detail, but navigation forces visitors away from the homepage instead of giving them enough confidence to act immediately.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements

- Replace the Wix promo strip with a small trust/quote strip while preserving the same top-bar geometry.
- Keep the dark header, white boxed nav, gold call box, flag hero image, centered hero copy, and gold bottom contact bar so the page remains recognizable.
- Rewrite the first screen around a clearer local offer: insured Indianapolis handyman and contracting help for repairs, remodels, furniture assembly, custom shelves, smoke alarms, and odd jobs.
- Add two explicit above-the-fold actions: `Call Justin Now` and `Email Job Details`.
- Add brief quote instructions: project type, location, timing, and photos.
- Pull a few real project photos from the Services page into the homepage as immediate visual proof.
- Update the title, H1, meta description, and image alt text with location and service language.

## SEO basics to tighten

- Change the title from `HOME | My Site 1` to something like `JustIn Time Contracting | Indianapolis Handyman Services`.
- Use one clear H1 with the business name and handyman/general contracting intent.
- Add a meta description mentioning insured handyman services, Indianapolis, repairs, remodeling, furniture assembly, custom builds, and smoke alarms.
- Add service-area language on the homepage.
- Add descriptive alt text to project images.
- If Wix allows it later, add LocalBusiness schema with phone, email, area served, and core service categories.

## Realistic implementation angle

This does not need a full rebuild. The practical path is a focused Wix edit:

1. Keep the existing black header, logo layout, nav boxes, gold call/contact bars, and flag hero image.
2. Remove or replace the Wix promo banner.
3. Rewrite the hero copy for a quote-first local handyman offer.
4. Add phone and email CTA buttons.
5. Bring three real project photos onto the homepage.
6. Add quote instructions and update SEO basics.

Good fit for a $99 Page Profit Check because the page already has a credible business, real assets, and obvious low-risk conversion friction. The $99 offer should stay limited to audit/diagnosis: review the page, identify the highest-impact fixes, and show a visual before/after direction.
