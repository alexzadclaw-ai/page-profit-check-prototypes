# Ninja Window Washing Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take

Ninja Window Washing already has a memorable brand, a real logo, a clear “We Fight Dirty!” line, and direct phone/email contact. The biggest conversion opportunity is clarity above the fold. A visitor should immediately know this is window washing and gutter cleaning for Central Montana and the Florida Panhandle, then choose the right phone number or request a free estimate.

The prototype keeps the same Wix-style visual texture: top bar, white header, ninja logo, huge hero image, oversized brand heading, blue connector lines, ladder photo, split gray service section, pale photo-backed quote section, and bright blue footer. The cleanup focuses on stronger CTAs, labeled region contact paths, trust near the top, and clearer service cards.

## What is already working

- The ninja logo and brand name are distinctive and easy to remember.
- The bright blue, white, and black palette is consistent across the page.
- The site includes a useful family-owned story and the “We Fight Dirty!” personality.
- Phone numbers, email, Facebook, and Instagram are all present.
- The page already states the two service regions: Central Montana since 2019 and Florida Panhandle since 2025.
- The core services are visible: residential windows, commercial windows, and gutters.
- Real photos and original logo assets make the page feel owner-specific rather than generic.

## Biggest conversion issues

- The document title is still `Home | Mysite`, which makes the page feel unfinished in browser tabs and search results.
- The Wix promo banner and “Proudly created with Wix.com” footer text reduce business-owned trust.
- The hero shows the business name but does not clearly state services, service regions, or a free-estimate action near the main headline.
- The two phone numbers appear in the header without Montana and Florida labels, so visitors may hesitate before calling.
- The strongest credibility details, family-owned, Central Montana since 2019, Florida Panhandle since 2025, and free quotes, are not grouped near the first CTA.
- CTA labels compete: Contact Us, Contact, Book An Appointment, Request A FREE ESTIMATE, and Get a Quote Now.
- The service section is simple but could better connect each service to the next step a homeowner or business owner should take.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements

- Replace the generic Wix promo area with a business-owned estimate/trust strip, or remove it if a paid Wix plan is available.
- Add a hero subheadline that says “Professional window washing and gutter cleaning” plus both service areas.
- Standardize the main CTA around “Request a Free Estimate.”
- Label both phone numbers everywhere: Montana and Florida.
- Add a compact trust strip near the top with family-owned, We Fight Dirty, residential/commercial, and free quotes.
- Keep the live logo, photos, blue palette, and site-builder section rhythm so the business still recognizes the page.
- Add short “what to send” guidance near the quote CTA: service needed, town/region, address, and photos if helpful.
- Tighten footer trust by removing template language and repeating the two regional contact paths.

## SEO basics to tighten

- Change the title from `Home | Mysite` to something like `Ninja Window Washing | Window Washing & Gutter Cleaning`.
- Add a meta description mentioning family-owned window washing, gutter cleaning, Central Montana, Florida Panhandle, residential, commercial, and free estimates.
- Use one clear H1 for the business name and supporting H2s for About, What We Do, and Request a Free Estimate.
- Improve image alt text. The logo alt should describe the business logo, the ladder photo should describe window cleaning, and blank hero/quote images should have meaningful decorative or descriptive handling.
- Make the service area readable in plain text near the top so search engines and visitors understand both regions.
- If Wix allows it later, add LocalBusiness schema with business name, service categories, phone numbers, email, social links, and service areas.

## Realistic implementation angle

This should be a focused Wix cleanup, not a full rebrand. The practical path is:

1. Keep the existing logo, brand color, photos, section order, and overall Wix look.
2. Fix title/meta and remove or replace obvious template leftovers.
3. Rewrite the hero with service, region, and free-estimate clarity.
4. Label Montana and Florida contact paths in the header, about section, quote section, and footer.
5. Standardize CTA language around free estimates while keeping appointment booking as a secondary action.
6. Add light trust and “what to send” copy without overcomplicating the page.
7. Check the mobile view so the logo, phone numbers, and estimate CTA stay easy to tap.

A realistic implementation ROM after the audit would likely be a small one-page Wix cleanup, roughly $350 to $900 depending on copy, mobile tuning, SEO basics, and whether template branding can be removed inside Wix.
