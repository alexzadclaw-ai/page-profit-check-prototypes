# Handyman Home Services Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Handyman Home Services is a strong fit for a focused Wix homepage cleanup. The live page already has useful trust signals: direct phone and email, Orlando/Orange County service-area copy, `se habla Espanol`, a clear process promise, written-price language, licensed/insured language, reviews, a satisfaction badge, and a credible engineer-by-trade owner story.

The conversion gap is that the first screen is trying to say too much at once. Visitors have to parse a long paragraph, promise list, commitment list, service-area list, and quote CTA before they understand the simplest path: call or email with job details, location, timeline, and photos.

Recommendation: keep the bright yellow/blue Wix identity, logo, satisfaction badges, worker hero photo, service-list structure, wood about band, testimonials, and contact section. Clean up the hierarchy so the quote path, service area, and highest-trust messages are obvious faster.

## What is already working
- The phone number `(813) 335-3419` is visible in the header.
- The email `davidrey.handyman@gmail.com` is visible in the contact section.
- The site says `se habla Espanol`, which is useful and should stay near the phone/CTA.

- The service list is broad and search-friendly: TV mounting, punch lists, minor plumbing/electrical, smart home, senior upgrades, babyproofing, shutters, maintenance, drywall, screens, doors, hardware, furniture assembly, blinds, and more.
- The owner story is credible because it mentions engineering education and long construction experience.
- Testimonials and five-star/satisfaction graphics provide social proof.

## Biggest conversion issues
- **Critical: The first screen is too busy.** The long paragraph, multiple large H1-style blocks, promise list, commitment list, service area, and quote button compete for attention.
- **Critical: The quote path is not direct enough.** `GET A FREE QUOTE!` points back to the same page instead of making the best next step explicit: call or email with project details and photos.
- **High: Trust cues are present but not packaged.** Written price, 24-hour callback, licensed/insured, satisfaction, and on-the-way text are strong, but they are buried in dense copy.
- **High: The service list is useful but overwhelming.** It reads as one long menu instead of grouped homeowner needs.
- **Medium: Wix/template residue reduces polish.** The top Wix promotional banner and footer Wix language make the page feel less finished.
- **Medium: SEO structure is weak.** The title is `HOME | HandymanHomeServices`, the meta description is empty, and multiple large H1 elements make the page less focused.
- **Medium: Contact section can be clearer.** The form button says `GET A JOB DONE`, but the rest of the page says `GET A FREE QUOTE!`; the visitor should not have to guess what to submit.
- **Medium: Service-area text needs cleanup.** `Altamone Springs` appears misspelled, and the area list can be normalized around Orlando/Orange County.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Keep the recognizable yellow header, blue CTA button, logo, badge, and worker hero image.
- Rewrite the first screen around one clear message: Orlando handyman repairs, installs, maintenance, and inspection punch lists with a clear quote path.
- Add a simple call/email quote instruction: send service needed, area, timeline, and photos if available.
- Turn the promise/commitment copy into quick proof points.
- Preserve the long service list, but group and space it so visitors can scan it.
- Keep the `se habla Español` note visible near the phone.
- Clean up `Altamone` to `Altamonte`, normalize area names, and include Orange County language.
- Replace the Wix promo strip with a customer-facing quote/trust strip while preserving the same site geometry.
- Update title, meta description, single H1, and descriptive image alt text.
- Align the contact form CTA with the quote goal.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a focused homepage title such as `Handyman Home Services | Orlando Handyman Repairs and Quotes`.
- Add a meta description mentioning Orlando, handyman repairs, maintenance, punch lists, installs, and written quotes.
- Use one primary H1 that includes local handyman intent.
- Make phone, email, service area, and service categories consistent across the page.
- Add descriptive alt text for the logo, satisfaction badges, hero image, stars, and handyman icon.
- If Wix allows it later, add LocalBusiness or HomeAndConstructionBusiness schema after confirming the preferred public address/service-area details.

## Realistic implementation angle
This does not need a full rebuild. The realistic move is a focused Wix homepage cleanup that keeps the same brand and content, but improves hierarchy and quote flow. The work would likely include first-screen copy, CTA behavior, trust-card formatting, service-list grouping, mobile spacing, contact-form language, and SEO basics.
