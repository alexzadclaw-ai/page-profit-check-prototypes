# H & T Gutter Services Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take

H & T Gutter Services already has a focused Wix page with clear services, a real estimate form, email contact, timing expectations, and a simple local service area message for Worcester and Dukes County, Massachusetts. The biggest opportunity is the first screen. Visitors see the business name and a general service paragraph, but they do not immediately see the strongest action path: request a free estimate, send the right job details, and understand that most estimates come back within one day. A focused Wix cleanup can keep the same look while making the gutter cleaning, installation, repair, and contact flow clearer before the long form.

## What is already working

- The site is tightly focused on one vertical: gutter cleaning, gutter installation, and gutter repairs.
- The business name is visible in the header and hero.
- The email address appears in the nav and again in the estimate section.
- The service area is stated in plain language: Worcester and Dukes County, Massachusetts.
- The page gives useful expectation-setting: most estimates returned within one day, cleanings usually scheduled and completed within one week, and advance scheduling available.
- The three service image tiles make the page feel specific to gutter work instead of generic home services.
- The estimate form asks relevant qualifying questions: last time cleaned, house height, preferred contact method, address/town, phone, and notes.
- The simple Wix structure is easy to improve without rebuilding the whole site.

## Biggest conversion issues

- The hero section does not include a clear call to action. The page asks visitors to scroll before they see the free-estimate path.
- The timing promise is strong but buried in the lower contact section.
- The first screen says the business is experienced and friendly, but it does not quickly summarize the practical reasons to contact H & T now.
- The contact path relies heavily on a long form. Some visitors may prefer a direct email CTA with clear instructions on what to send.
- The service tiles are helpful, but the intro above them could better connect each service to common homeowner needs: clogged gutters, new seamless gutters, repairs, downspouts, and maintenance.
- There are no trust or clarity cues near the top beyond the service area. Simple cues like “one-day estimate turnaround” and “cleanings usually within one week” would help.
- The browser title says “Gutter Cleaning | H & T Gutter Cleaning | Webster,” while the visible brand says “H & T Gutter Services.” That naming mismatch is small but worth tightening.
- There is no phone number visible, even though the form offers phone response. If phone is a real option, it should be shown. If not, the form copy should make email the main path.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements

- Add a primary hero CTA such as “Request Free Estimate” that links to the form or opens the email address.
- Move the strongest timing expectations into a compact strip near the top: estimates usually within one day, cleanings usually within one week, advance scheduling available.
- Clarify the service list before the images: gutter cleaning, seamless gutter installation, maintenance, repairs, and downspout/gutter issue photos.
- Add a short “what to send” note near the email CTA: address/town, last cleaning date, house height, service needed, and photos if available.
- Keep the current visual system: Wix banner, white header, pale gray nav, dark hero, centered service intro, three-image strip, dark estimate section, underline form fields, and white footer.
- Make the form feel less abrupt by adding helper copy that explains why the fields are needed.
- Tighten title/meta copy around the actual service area and current visible business name.

## SEO basics to tighten

- Update the title to something like “Gutter Cleaning, Installation & Repairs | H & T Gutter Services.”
- Add a meta description that mentions gutter cleaning, seamless gutter installation, repairs, Worcester County, and Dukes County.
- Keep one clear H1. The current H1 is the business name; a slightly stronger version could include “Gutter Services.”
- Use descriptive alt text on the three images, for example “gutter installation ladder work,” “rain gutter cleaning,” and “gutter repair and maintenance.”
- Make brand naming consistent between H & T Gutter Services and H & T Gutter Cleaning.
- If the business has a real phone number, add it consistently in header, form, schema, and footer.
- If Wix allows later, add local business schema with business name, email, service area, and service categories.

## Realistic implementation angle

This should be a surgical Wix homepage cleanup, not a full redesign. The practical path:

1. Keep the existing template, colors, image tiles, form structure, and footer.
2. Add one clear hero action for free estimates.
3. Move the estimate turnaround and scheduling expectations closer to the top.
4. Add a concise service/estimate helper strip before the image tiles.
5. Add “what to send” instructions next to the email and form.
6. Tighten page title, meta description, image alt text, and naming consistency.
7. Check mobile view so the CTA, service area, email, and form stay easy to tap.

## Offer fit

Good fit for a $99 Page Profit Check because the site is already close. It does not need a new brand or a large rebuild to create value. The diagnosis can focus on where visitors hesitate, what needs to move above the fold, and how to make the estimate request feel simpler. Implementation in Wix should remain separate after the audit, likely a small one-page cleanup if H & T wants help applying the recommendations.
