# Jake's Home Solutions Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Jake's Home Solutions is a strong fit for a focused homepage cleanup. The live Wix site already has useful proof: direct phone and email, a clear Anniston/Calhoun County area, surrounding counties listed, free estimates, insured/professional positioning, 20 years of experience, a real address, Facebook, and project photos.

The conversion gap is that the best sales message is buried inside a dense uppercase intro. The page needs the county service area, insured experience, and call/email estimate path to be obvious before visitors reach the service list.

Recommendation: keep the simple white Wix layout, underlined uppercase business name, orange/blue CTA palette, two-column service photo rhythm, and footer contact structure. Clean up the top message, add quick trust cues, and make `Get In Touch` feel like a free estimate action.

## What is already working
- The site has direct contact paths: `256-343-6261` and `3436261@gmail.com`.
- The service-area copy is valuable because it names Calhoun, Cleburne, Clay, St. Clair, and Talladega counties.
- The page states free estimates, insured construction service, and 20 years of experience.
- The brand line `Jake's Home Solutions` and `Your Home Improvement Pro` are easy to reuse.
- The current design is simple and recognizable: white page, dark uppercase text, orange active nav/button, blue CTA band, and project photos.
- The services are broad enough for homeowner punch-list searches: painting, doors, fence repair, drywall, water damage, windows, demolition, sidewalk repair, appliance installation, ceiling fans, tile, brick, and block work.
- The footer has address, email, phone, and Facebook paths.

## Biggest conversion issues
- **Critical: The first screen is too dense.** The visitor has to parse a long uppercase paragraph before understanding the service area, trust proof, and estimate instructions.
- **Critical: The quote path is not framed as the main action.** `Get In Touch` exists, but it does not clearly say call, text, or email for a free estimate with project location and basics.
- **High: Strong trust cues are buried.** Insured, professional, 20 years of experience, and free estimates should be packaged as quick proof near the top.
- **High: The service list is useful but hard to scan.** The current service rows use long mixed lists without customer-friendly grouping or supporting context.
- **Medium: Copy has small credibility issues.** `NO JOB TO SMALL`, inconsistent casing, and a few awkward phrases make the business feel less polished than the experience claim.
- **Medium: Wix/template residue weakens polish.** The top Wix banner and bottom `Proudly created with Wix.com` distract from the local service business.
- **Medium: SEO structure can be cleaner.** The live page title and meta description are useful, but the H1 starts with `Learn More About My Services` instead of the business, location, and primary service.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Turn the header into a compact local promise: Jake's Home Solutions, handyman and home repair around Anniston and nearby counties.
- Add a quick three-part trust strip before services: service area, insured with 20 years experience, call/text/email estimate path.
- Keep the visible phone, email, nav row, Facebook link, and orange `Get In Touch` button.
- Change the CTA meaning from generic contact to free estimate request.
- Preserve the original project photos and two-column service layout, but add short helper copy under each service group.
- Add a small `what to include` estimate section: location, project basics, photos if available, and best callback number.
- Fix obvious wording issues while keeping the plain local handyman voice.
- Remove or de-emphasize Wix promotional residue in the customer-facing cleanup.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a focused homepage title such as `Jake's Home Solutions | Handyman and Home Repair in Anniston, AL`.
- Use one primary H1 that includes the business name and local handyman/home repair intent.
- Keep the meta description specific to professional, insured handyman service, Anniston, Calhoun County, and free estimates.
- Use descriptive image alt text for the door, window, sidewalk, and porch/project photos.
- Add LocalBusiness or HomeAndConstructionBusiness schema after confirming the preferred public business address, service area, and phone format.
- Make sure the contact page has the same phone/email/estimate language as the homepage.
- Consider adding short project captions on the Projects page so photos can support local repair keywords.

## Realistic implementation angle
This does not need a full redesign. The realistic move is a surgical Wix homepage cleanup that keeps the same brand cues and assets while making the first screen easier to act on. The work would likely include header copy cleanup, CTA wording, a trust strip, service grouping, better mobile order, footer cleanup, and basic SEO fields.
