# C&L Home Improvement LLC - Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
C&L Home Improvement LLC is a strong fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current Wix site already has public contact details, real project imagery, local New Jersey positioning, and specific roofing and gutter services. The issue is not a lack of substance. The issue is that the homepage opens with generic contractor copy, visible spelling errors, weak service hierarchy, and template-style navigation that can reduce trust before a homeowner asks for an estimate.

**Recommendation:** preserve the straightforward Wix contractor feel, white background, simple text navigation, project photos, C&L logo, and plain-spoken service cards, but tighten the page around one primary buyer action: **Schedule a Free Roofing or Gutter Estimate**.

## Biggest conversion issues

### Critical findings
1. **Critical:** The first screen does not clearly state the core buyer outcome. It reads like a generic contractor introduction instead of “roofing repair, gutters, and roof replacement in New Jersey.”
   - Prototype response: Add a specific hero headline for roofing repair, gutters, and roof replacement while preserving the C&L identity and straightforward tone.

2. **Critical:** The free-estimate path exists, but it is not strong enough above the fold or repeated consistently through the page.
   - Prototype response: Add clear calls to action in the header, hero, service cards, and contact section using phone and email.

3. **Critical:** Visible spelling and wording errors reduce trust for high-value exterior work. Examples observed include “Improvemnt,” “trustworsty,” “aluminium,” and awkward punctuation around chimney caps.
   - Prototype response: Clean the buyer-facing copy while keeping the same meaning and service claims.

### High findings
4. **High:** The navigation includes “Plans & Pricing,” which feels like Wix template residue for a contractor site and may confuse homeowners looking for an estimate.
   - Prototype response: Replace pricing-plan language with a direct Free Estimate CTA and clearer section navigation.

5. **High:** Service cards are too generic. The taglines are pleasant, but they do not help a homeowner decide whether to ask about a roof leak, gutter issue, or full replacement.
   - Prototype response: Keep the same three services and images, but add practical homeowner context to each card.

6. **High:** Trust cues are not grouped near the top. Fully certified professionals, New Jersey service, roof system types, and free estimates are spread through paragraphs instead of being quickly scannable.
   - Prototype response: Add a top trust strip with fully certified team, free estimates, New Jersey service, and roof systems handled.

7. **High:** The contact section does not explain what happens after a visitor calls or emails.
   - Prototype response: Add a simple three-step estimate flow: choose the service, share the issue, get an estimate.

8. **High:** The current SEO title is generic: “Home | C&L Home Improvement LLC.” It misses roofing repair, gutters, roof replacement, and New Jersey.
   - Prototype response: Use a service-and-location page title and meta description in the static cleanup.

### Medium findings
9. **Medium:** The page has real photos, but they are not used to guide the story from problem to service to estimate.
   - Prototype response: Use project imagery in hero, services, and project sections with descriptive alt text.

10. **Medium:** The email in the opportunity record differs from the email observed on the live site.
    - Prototype response: Use the email observed publicly on the live site in the page while flagging the mismatch for confirmation.

11. **Medium:** Social icons point to Wix social accounts rather than C&L-specific social pages, which can look like leftover template content.
    - Prototype response: Omit generic Wix social links from the cleaned page unless real C&L social profiles are confirmed.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Replace the generic first impression with a service-specific roofing and gutter hero.
- Promote the free estimate path with a phone-first and email-second CTA structure.
- Clean spelling, grammar, and awkward punctuation that create avoidable trust friction.
- Move the most useful trust details into a scannable strip near the top.
- Convert generic service cards into clearer homeowner decision paths.
- Remove or replace template residue like “Plans & Pricing” and generic Wix social links.
- Add a simple what-happens-next estimate flow.
- Improve SEO basics around roofing repair, gutters, roof replacement, and New Jersey.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title like: **Roofing Repair, Gutters & Roof Replacement in New Jersey | C&L Home Improvement LLC**.
- Write a meta description focused on roofing repair, gutter cleaning/repair/replacement, roof replacement, New Jersey, and free estimates.
- Use one clear H1 that names the services and location instead of a generic company intro.
- Use descriptive H2s for About, Services, Projects, and Free Estimate.
- Add descriptive alt text for real project photos.
- Confirm the correct business email before adding schema or outreach copy.
- Add LocalBusiness or RoofingContractor schema after address, service area, and contact details are confirmed.
- Consider replacing the Wix subdomain with an owned domain when practical.
