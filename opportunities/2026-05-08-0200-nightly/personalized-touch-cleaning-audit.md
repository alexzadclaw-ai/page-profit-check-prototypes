# Personalized Touch Cleaning Service - Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Personalized Touch Cleaning Service is a strong fit for a **Solution A brand-faithful homepage cleanup**. The live Wix homepage already has clear local service-area copy, visible email and phone contact paths, a clean apartment-style hero image, a simple PT logo, and three service categories: Residential, Commercial, and Specialty Services.

The biggest opportunity is not a redesign. It is a surgical cleanup that keeps the same bright, airy apartment and office visual style while making the first screen easier to act on. A visitor should immediately know which path fits them, where the company serves, and how to request a free estimate.

**Recommendation:** preserve the calm white and pale aqua look, thin uppercase typography, circular service photos, logo-left header, and contact-forward footer, but add a clearer hero promise, stronger estimate/call CTAs, buyer-specific service cards, and a simple estimate-request flow.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The first screen does not give visitors a strong action path.
   - The free-estimate request is only mentioned in paragraph text, not as a dominant button or repeated visual action.
   - A mobile or impatient visitor can understand the company, but has to work harder than necessary to act.
   - Prototype response: add clear **Request a Free Estimate** and **Call 973-896-3987** actions in the hero while keeping the same image, title, and calm visual style.

2. **Critical:** Residential, commercial, and specialty buyers are not separated early enough.
   - The hero mentions homes, workspaces, and retail establishments, but the visitor does not get quick path choices until below the fold.
   - Prototype response: add a compact trust/path strip directly under the hero for Residential, Commercial, Specialty, and Local Area.

3. **High:** The service cards are too generic.
   - The live cards show only category names. They do not say what a residential client, office manager, or specialty-project visitor can request.
   - Prototype response: keep the three circular images and labels, but add short plain-English descriptions under each service category.

4. **High:** The service-area strength is buried inside a paragraph.
   - Northern New Jersey and NYC metro positioning is valuable for local relevance, but it is not repeated in headings or scannable elements.
   - Prototype response: add a dedicated service-area section with Hackensack, Northern New Jersey, NYC metro, homes, offices, and retail cues.

5. **High:** Wix/template residue weakens polish.
   - The live page includes the Wix promotional banner and `This website was built on Wix` text at the top.
   - Prototype response: remove builder residue from the customer-facing cleanup and keep only business-owned header, contact, logo, social, and footer cues.

6. **High:** The estimate process is unclear.
   - The page asks visitors to contact the business for a free estimate, but does not explain what information to send.
   - Prototype response: add a three-step estimate flow: choose service type, share location/space details, receive a free estimate.

7. **Medium:** SEO basics can be tighter.
   - The live title is generic: `Home | Personalized Touch Cleaning Service`.
   - The page would benefit from a local service title, meta description, one clearer H1, more descriptive headings, and better image alt text.
   - Prototype response: add a local cleaning-service title/meta description, one clean H1, service headings, and descriptive alt text.

8. **Medium:** Contact information is visible but not prioritized around decision points.
   - Email and phone are present, but the phone/email do not appear as strong decision-stage CTAs in the hero and service flow.
   - Prototype response: repeat email and phone in the hero, estimate panel, and footer.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Preserve the existing PT logo idea, white header, thin uppercase type, bright apartment hero, pale aqua service band, circular service images, and dark footer bar.
- Make **Request a Free Estimate** the dominant first-screen action and pair it with **Call 973-896-3987**.
- Add immediate buyer paths: Residential, Commercial, Specialty, and Local Area.
- Rewrite service cards so each category explains what the visitor can request.
- Turn the free-estimate promise into a simple three-step process.
- Repeat the Hackensack / Northern New Jersey / NYC metro service area in a scannable section.
- Remove Wix builder banner language from the cleaned-up customer-facing page.
- Add local SEO basics: title, meta description, H1, H2s, and descriptive image alt text.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title like: `Residential & Commercial Cleaning in Northern New Jersey | Personalized Touch Cleaning Service`.
- Add a meta description: `Personalized Touch Cleaning Service provides residential, commercial, and specialty cleaning throughout Northern New Jersey and the NYC metro area. Request a free estimate.`
- Use one clear H1: `Personalized Touch Cleaning Service`.
- Use H2s for Services, Estimate Request, and Service Area.
- Add descriptive alt text for the apartment hero, bedroom, office, and specialty interior images.
- Add visible local terms in natural places: Hackensack, Northern New Jersey, NYC metro, residential cleaning, commercial cleaning, office cleaning, retail cleaning.
- Consider LocalBusiness schema only after confirming exact preferred business name, service area, phone, and address formatting with the owner.
