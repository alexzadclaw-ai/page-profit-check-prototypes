# Ultimate Handyman, LLC — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Ultimate Handyman, LLC is a strong fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup**. The live Wix site already has meaningful trust assets: direct phone/email, License #029203, real project photos, specific service descriptions, and a clear personal craftsman voice. The conversion gap is that visitors land on a long services page rhythm before they get a crisp local promise, a dominant estimate path, and a fast way to decide whether Kyle handles their type of project.

**Recommendation:** preserve the dark contractor look, bright green accent band, deep blue page background, Avenir-style typography, small Kyle profile image, and real project photos. Rebuild the first screen and section order around one obvious action: **Request a Free Estimate**.

## Biggest conversion issues

### Critical findings
1. **Critical: No clear first-screen homepage promise.**  
   The visitor sees the Wix banner/header and then the page starts with **Services & Skills / Find Out What I Can Do**. It does not quickly say what Kyle does, where he works, why to trust him, and what action to take.

2. **Critical: Estimate request path is not visually dominant.**  
   Phone and email are available, and service copy mentions a free estimate, but there is no repeated primary CTA that guides the visitor through the page.

### High findings
3. **High: Trust proof is strong but too dispersed.**  
   License number, project images, customer praise, and social links are present, but the top of the page does not package them into a quick trust strip.

4. **High: Services are detailed but slow to scan.**  
   The main rows have useful descriptions, but first-time buyers need a faster “which job do you need?” overview before reading long copy.

5. **High: Project proof needs clearer outcome labels.**  
   The bathroom remodel, tile wall, and shelving examples are useful, but the page can make the result and buyer benefit clearer at a glance.

6. **High: Visitors are not told what happens next.**  
   A homeowner may not know what to send, whether to call or email, or how an estimate conversation starts.

### Medium findings
7. **Medium: Wix promotional and footer residue reduces polish.**  
   The visible Wix builder banner and “Proudly created with Wix.com” line make the business feel less custom than the craftsmanship described.

8. **Medium: SEO basics can be tightened.**  
   The title has useful keywords, but the homepage needs one clearer H1, a concise meta description, descriptive image alt text, and later LocalBusiness schema once final service area details are confirmed.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Add a local, benefit-driven hero: **Home improvement you can trust in Vacaville, Winters, Solano & Yolo County**.
- Make **Request a Free Estimate** the primary CTA in the hero and repeat it naturally in later sections.
- Keep email and phone visible, but structure them as secondary/direct contact options.
- Move License #029203, local service area, and service categories into a compact trust strip near the top.
- Preserve the dark/green/blue banded Wix rhythm while making the content shorter and more scannable.
- Group services by buyer intent: small remodels & upgrades, tile, custom projects, plus painting, welding/fabrication, and repairs.
- Label project images with clearer outcomes so visitors understand what they are seeing immediately.
- Convert long testimonials into shorter proof cards while preserving real customer language.
- Add a “what happens next” section explaining how to send project details, receive a response, and schedule an estimate.
- Remove builder/template residue in the customer-facing cleanup.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a focused homepage title like `Handyman in Vacaville, CA | Ultimate Handyman, LLC`.
- Add a concise meta description around small remodels, tile, custom projects, repairs, and free estimates.
- Use one clear H1 on the homepage instead of starting with a generic section heading.
- Use H2 headings for Services, Past Projects, Testimonials, and Contact / Estimate.
- Add descriptive alt text for each public project photo.
- Add LocalBusiness or HomeAndConstructionBusiness schema after confirming final service area, license format, and business address preferences.
- If staying on Wix, remove or hide Wix promotional/footer residue where the plan allows.

## Realistic implementation angle
This does not need a full rebrand. A practical implementation would be a focused Wix homepage cleanup sprint using the existing assets, copy, services, project photos, and proof. The work would likely include restructuring the homepage, tightening copy, adding CTA buttons, improving the mobile order, cleaning the footer, and updating basic SEO fields.
