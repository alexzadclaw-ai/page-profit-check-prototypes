- The business lists a broad real service area, including Cary, Clayton, Garner, Knightdale, Raleigh, Wake Forest, Zebulon, and other eastern NC towns.
- The page offers a simple first-visit promotion: $20 off the first deep clean and $10 off for paying referrals.

- The service list covers high-intent cleaning needs: deep cleaning, move-in/move-out cleaning, and recurring weekly, biweekly, and monthly cleaning.

## Biggest conversion issues
- **Critical:** The top Wix banner and footer line saying the site was created with Wix.com make the business look unfinished and less professional.
- **Critical:** The testimonial appears to be generic template copy attributed to “Casey Johnson,” which creates a fake-proof problem on a service business page.
- **Critical:** The contact area shows both “We want to hear from you” and a “Subscribe Form / Stay up to date” flow, which can confuse a visitor who just wants a cleaning quote.
- **High:** The main service list mixes core cleaning services with vacation, concierge, laundry, and transportation services before explaining what each means.
- **High:** The hero headline is only the business name plus “Your Satisfaction Is Our Priority,” so visitors do not immediately see deep cleaning, move-in/out, recurring cleaning, or the service area.
- **High:** “Book Online” is in the navigation, but the visible homepage still pushes people through generic contact and subscribe sections rather than a clear estimate path.
- **High:** The Thumbtack rating and high return customer rate claims are valuable, but they sit as loose text without context, review link, or “what this means for you” copy.
- **High:** The broad service area is useful but appears as one long comma-separated line, which is hard to scan on mobile.
- **Medium:** The email appears in both uppercase and lowercase forms, which is minor but inconsistent.
- **Medium:** The service card labels are generic Wix-style phrases such as “Professional Service,” “Always Available,” and “Satisfaction Guaranteed” instead of practical buyer-focused explanations.
- **Medium:** Local SEO is thin because the title and H1 do not include Raleigh, Wake County, Zebulon, or specific cleaning services.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Remove Wix promotional residue and replace the footer with business-owned contact information.
- Replace the generic testimonial with practical, supportable trust points from the current page: Thumbtack rating claim, high return customer rate, referral discount, and extra care for elderly and disabled clients.
- Rewrite the hero around service and geography: deep cleaning, move-in/move-out cleaning, recurring cleaning, and concierge/vacation support in Raleigh, Wake County, Zebulon, and nearby eastern NC towns.
- Separate the offer into two clean paths: “Cleaning Services” and “Concierge & Home Support,” so transportation and vacation services do not dilute the cleaning conversion path.
- Turn the long service-area line into scannable town chips.
- Make the first action “Call or email for a cleaning estimate,” with the promotion visible near the first CTA.
- Replace the generic subscribe form with a quote-prep section explaining what to include: location, home size, service type, timing, and special needs.
- Rewrite service cards with buyer-specific details for deep/general cleaning, move-in/out cleaning, and concierge/vacation support.
