# Pixi Cleaning Services - Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Pixi Cleaning Services already feels warm, local, and specific enough to be believable. The strongest material is buried below the fold: Lakes Region service area, insured status, recurring and one-time options, detailed deep-cleaning checklist, custom cleaning plans, and a 24-hour satisfaction promise. The biggest opportunity is not a redesign. It is a same-site cleanup that moves buying intent, trust, and quote instructions closer to the top.

## Visual fingerprint evidence before implementation
Observed from the public Wix homepage at `https://pixicleaningservic.wixsite.com/website`:

- **Builder/header:** 50px Wix promo bar, then a white contact strip with `pixicleaningservices@gmail.com`, `603-998-1441`, and a Log In link.
- **Brand row:** small square logo image at the left, brand name in a light Futura-style face around 22px, tagline “Making Your House Feel More like a Home,” simple black navigation links, and an outlined “Get In Touch” button.
- **Hero layout:** wide white page with a large left-side “Clean Kitchen” photo, roughly 603px by 900px, and a right-side text panel. Main heading is very large, black, light-weight, Futura-style, around 72px.
- **Typography:** headings use a Futura-style light sans serif; body copy uses an Avenir-style light sans serif around 15px; script accent appears on “Specializing in Deep Cleaning” and “Considered Deep Cleaning.”
- **Color system:** mostly white background, black text, thin black outlined buttons, little color beyond the kitchen and bedroom photography.
- **Page rhythm:** spacious centered sections, a large welcome section, then a long center-aligned cleaning checklist with kitchen, bathroom, bedroom, and living area details.
- **Content assets:** public Wix images include a kitchen hero image, a small logo PNG, a square bedroom image, and later kitchen/blog imagery.

## What is already working
- The business name, phone, and email are visible near the top.
- The tagline feels personal and appropriate for home cleaning.
- “Specializing in Deep Cleaning” creates a useful niche signal.
- The Lakes Region of New Hampshire is named on the page.
- The business says it is fully insured.
- Weekly, bi-weekly, monthly, and one-time cleaning are mentioned.
- The cleaning checklist is unusually detailed, which can build confidence if made easier to scan.
- The site includes a satisfaction promise to return within 24 hours if the customer is not satisfied.
- Contact information appears again in the footer.

## Biggest conversion issues

### Critical
1. **The hero does not immediately explain the best next step.** It says “Contact Us,” but does not tell visitors what to include for a free estimate or separate deep, recurring, and one-time needs.
2. **The strongest buyer-fit information is buried.** Insurance, recurring schedules, one-time cleanings, custom plans, and satisfaction follow-up are below long intro copy instead of appearing near the top.
3. **Quote intent is not organized.** A visitor looking for deep cleaning, weekly cleaning, move-in/move-out, or seasonal cleaning has to read paragraphs and checklist content to understand fit.

### High
1. **The navigation includes low-intent or unfinished-looking items.** Blog, Log In, Subscribe Form, and a default first post distract from estimate requests.
2. **The service checklist is valuable but too long and centered for scanning.** It should be grouped into cards or columns so people can quickly see what is included.
3. **The “Since 2000” line may create trust, but it is generic around “professional home services.”** The copy should quickly say house cleaning, deep cleaning, recurring cleaning, and one-time cleanings in Lakes Region homes.
4. **The phone and email are visible, but not framed as estimate actions.** Contact links should say “Request a Free Estimate” and “Call 603-998-1441.”

### Medium
1. **The page title is generic: “Home | Pixi Cleaning Services.”** It should include house cleaning and Lakes Region, NH.
2. **Some image alt text is file-name-like.** It can be more descriptive for accessibility and SEO.
3. **There are small copy issues and grammar misses.** Examples include missing apostrophes and “Make beds is requested.”
4. **The footer includes Wix branding and a subscribe form that do not help a local cleaning buyer.**

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Rewrite the hero to say what Pixi does, where it serves, and how to request a free estimate.
- Preserve the original white, black, airy, home-cleaning feel and kitchen image.
- Add a top trust strip: Fully insured, Lakes Region, deep cleaning focus, recurring and one-time options.
- Add three clear service paths: Deep Cleaning, Recurring Cleaning, and One-Time Cleaning.
- Convert the long checklist into grouped, scannable cards while keeping the original substance.
- Add a simple “how to request an estimate” section with what customers should send.
- Keep phone and email prominent in the header, hero, estimate section, and footer.
- Remove or de-emphasize Wix/template distractions from the cleaned customer-facing page.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Page title: `House Cleaning & Deep Cleaning in Lakes Region, NH | Pixi Cleaning Services`.
- Meta description should mention Lakes Region, house cleaning, deep cleaning, recurring cleaning, one-time cleaning, insured, and free estimates.
- Use one clear H1 focused on service and location.
- Add descriptive headings for deep cleaning, weekly/biweekly/monthly cleaning, move-in/move-out, and seasonal cleaning.
- Use descriptive image alt text like “Clean kitchen for Lakes Region deep cleaning service.”
- Keep NAP consistency: Lakes Region, New Hampshire, `pixicleaningservices@gmail.com`, `603-998-1441`.

## Realistic implementation angle
This should be a focused Wix homepage refresh rather than a full rebuild. The owner-recognition goal is high: keep the logo, kitchen photo, white space, black outline buttons, light sans typography, script accent line, and detailed checklist. The refresh should mainly rearrange and rewrite the page so a house-cleaning buyer can choose deep, recurring, or one-time service and request a quote without digging.

## Offer fit
Good fit for a $99 Page Profit Check because the site already has real service information and contact details, but the conversion path is underorganized. A small, practical cleanup could make the site feel more professional without asking the business to rebrand.
