# B&B Home Improvement LLC - Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
B&B Home Improvement LLC is a strong Page Profit Check fit because the site already has the right ingredients: a licensed general contractor message, real service categories, license numbers, review proof, phone, email, and a local Rock Hill / Charlotte metro footprint. The issue is hierarchy, not substance. The homepage makes visitors work too hard to find the best proof and the simplest next step.

**Recommendation:** keep the current project-photo feel and blue-and-gray Wix look, but make the first screen say what they do, where they work, and how to get a free estimate.

**Visual resemblance:** High
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Visual fingerprint evidence before implementation
- Free Wix banner at the very top.
- White header with a large B&B logo on the left.
- Light gray navigation bar with Home, About, Services, Testimonials, and Contact.
- Hero uses a bright kitchen remodel photo with a big blue underlined headline.
- Headline copy reads: "B&B Home Improvement LLC" with the subline "Making your home remodel dreams come true" and license numbers shown in the hero.
- Blue accent text, white backgrounds, and soft gray section breaks.
- Services are presented as a simple list of project types, not a guided buyer path.
- Reviews and contact proof sit lower on the page than the hero and intro.
- Footer is dark gray with a Wix-powered credit line.

## What is already working
- The business is clearly positioned as a licensed general contractor.
- License numbers are visible in the hero: SC. 51785 and NC. L106355.
- The site says "Licensed. Bonded. Insured. Reliable."
- The service mix is commercially useful: kitchen remodels, bathroom remodels, flooring installation, wainscoting, fireplace face lift, and recessed lighting.
- The business has a public phone number and email.
- The contact section includes the Rock Hill, SC location.
- There is real review proof, which is much better than placeholder testimonial copy.

## Biggest conversion issues

### Critical findings
1. **Critical:** The hero headline is brand-first instead of buyer-first.
   - It says the company name, but not the main jobs a homeowner can hire them for.
   - Fix: lead with kitchens, baths, flooring, lighting, and licensed contractor proof.

2. **Critical:** The free estimate path is present, but it is buried inside body copy and repeated too little near the top.
   - Fix: make Request a Free Estimate a top-level CTA in the header and hero.

3. **Critical:** The page title is generic and does not help searchers understand the service area or core jobs.
   - Fix: replace the title with a service plus location format.

### High findings
4. **High:** License proof is real, but it is not framed as a first-screen trust signal.
   - Fix: bring the license numbers, licensed/bonded/insured proof, and service area into a compact trust strip.

5. **High:** The service list reads like a flat checklist, not a clear set of homeowner job paths.
   - Fix: group the services into kitchen, bath, flooring, finish carpentry, and lighting pathways.

6. **High:** The review proof is useful but disconnected from the contact action.
   - Fix: move the review section closer to the estimate CTA and visually connect it to the decision flow.

7. **High:** The contact section gives the basics, but it does not explain what happens after someone reaches out.
   - Fix: add a simple 3-step estimate process.

### Medium findings
8. **Medium:** Navigation is functional but generic.
   - Fix: use clearer labels like Services, Project Types, Reviews, and Estimate.

9. **Medium:** The footer credit and free-site look reduce polish for a licensed contractor.
   - Fix: keep the same site family, but reduce visible template residue in the buyer-facing layout.

10. **Medium:** Service copy is informative, but the visual scan path is too text-heavy.
   - Fix: add short service blurbs, scannable cards, and a tighter mobile hierarchy.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Put the service promise before the company name in the hero.
- Promote license numbers and licensed/bonded/insured proof near the top.
- Add a stronger, repeated free estimate CTA.
- Reframe services into homeowner job paths instead of a flat list.
- Bring the review proof into the decision path.
- Add a simple "what happens next" section.
- Keep the blue/gray Wix-style family so the site still feels like the same business.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a title like: **Kitchen, Bath, Flooring & Lighting Contractor in Rock Hill, SC | B&B Home Improvement LLC**
- Write a meta description that includes licensed general contractor, Rock Hill, Charlotte metro, kitchens, baths, flooring, wainscoting, recessed lighting, and free estimates.
- Use one clear H1 that is service-first, not just brand-first.
- Keep H2s focused on About, Services, Reviews, Estimate Process, and Contact.
- Add LocalBusiness or HomeAndConstructionBusiness schema if the final build includes structured data.
- Make photo alt text descriptive instead of generic.

## Realistic implementation angle
This should be a surgical homepage cleanup, not a rebrand. The page already has trust proof, real project categories, and local contact information. The opportunity is to tighten hierarchy, make the top section say the right thing faster, and make the estimate path obvious.
