# A Honey Do List Handyman Services LLC - Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
A Honey Do List Handyman Services LLC already has the ingredients that usually make a small handyman site credible: real project photos, a direct email, a phone number, clear service categories, and a memorable "No Job Too Small" positioning. The biggest opportunity is not a full redesign. It is a surgical cleanup that keeps the owner-operated Wix feel while moving the estimate path, trust proof, and service clarity closer to the top.

## Visual fingerprint evidence before implementation
Observed public homepage: https://ahdlhandyman.wixsite.com/website

- Platform and title: Wix site titled "Home | A Honey Do List Handyman Services LLC".
- Header: white background, large uppercase Oswald-style business name, simple text navigation for Home, My Services, Contact, Recent Projects, email, and phone.
- Hero: split layout with large uppercase yellow/gold business name on the left, "No Job Too Small" tagline, short owner/rental-property paragraph, and a tall handyman/plumber work image on the right.
- Brand palette: mostly white and black text with strong yellow/gold accent around rgb(255, 192, 58).
- Typography: condensed uppercase heading style similar to Oswald, body copy similar to Raleway.
- Project section: centered "MY BEST WORK" heading in yellow/gold, paragraph, three real project photos, project labels, and black "Get in Touch" buttons.
- Services section: left-aligned "MY SERVICES" heading with a single-column list of service names and short descriptions.
- Contact section: centered "LET'S GET GOING!", email, phone, then footer phone and Wix-created footer text.

## What is already working
- Real project photos provide proof that the business actually does the work.
- Phone number and email are visible in the desktop header.
- "No Job Too Small" is a strong fit for handyman intent because many visitors have small repair lists and do not want to feel dismissed.
- Service range is broad and useful: fencing, painting, deck staining, flooring, window and patio door replacement, minor electrical, deck repair, minor plumbing.
- The tone feels personal and owner-operated, which should be preserved instead of replaced with a generic contractor template.

## Biggest conversion issues

### Critical
1. **No clear estimate CTA in the first screen.** Visitors see the business name, tagline, and bio, but there is no prominent "Request a Free Estimate" or "Call Now" action in the hero.
2. **The strongest proof and services sit too far down the page.** Visitors must scroll past a broad intro and project area before the full service list and final contact prompt.

### High
1. **Contact links are present but not framed as next steps.** Email and phone appear as plain contact details, not as a guided estimate path.
2. **The hero copy explains history, but not who should call and what they can expect.** It mentions rental properties and Embry Riddle, but does not quickly say "Daytona Beach handyman for home and rental repairs".
3. **Service list is hard to scan.** A long single-column service stack makes it slower to confirm fit for common jobs.
4. **Trust proof is implied but not summarized.** Since 2015, real photos, rental property experience, and no-job-too-small are good trust points, but they are not grouped near the top.

### Medium
1. **Wix banner and footer language add friction.** The free Wix banner and "Proudly created with Wix.com" can make the business look less established.
2. **Project image alt text is file-name based.** Several images use labels like `part0.jpg`, which is weaker for accessibility and image SEO.
3. **Navigation anchors do not create a strong funnel.** Home, My Services, Contact, Recent Projects are fine, but the main visitor intent is estimate request.
4. **The page could use more local context.** Daytona Beach / Volusia County relevance is not prominent in the visible text.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Add a hero CTA pair: "Request a Free Estimate" and "Call 386-400-8839".
- Rewrite the hero paragraph to keep the personal feel while saying the service area and core job types faster.
- Add a compact trust strip below the hero: Since 2015, No Job Too Small, Real Photos, Daytona Beach / Volusia County.
- Keep the same white, black, gold, Oswald/Raleway visual language and the same real project photos.
- Convert the services list into a faster two-column scan on desktop and single-column on mobile.
- Add contextual email subject lines for estimate requests.
- Make the phone CTA sticky on mobile so visitors do not need to scroll back to call.
- Improve image alt text and meta description.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Page title should include the service and location, for example: "A Honey Do List Handyman Services LLC | Daytona Beach Handyman".
- Meta description should mention handyman services, Daytona Beach, Volusia County, and key services.
- Use one clear H1 instead of repeating the company name without local/service context.
- Add descriptive alt text for project photos: deck built and stained, cross pattern fence, fence back section.
- Add local copy such as "Daytona Beach and Volusia County" near the top.
- If implemented on the live site later, add structured LocalBusiness or HomeAndConstructionBusiness schema with the public phone, email, service area, and same business name.

## Realistic implementation angle
This should be a small Wix cleanup, not a rebuild. The owner can keep the current design and assets while updating the page hierarchy:

1. Add hero buttons and stronger local service copy.
2. Add a trust strip under the hero.
3. Reformat services into a cleaner grid or columns.
4. Update project photo captions and alt text.
5. Clean up contact CTA language.
6. Consider a paid Wix plan or domain later if the free Wix branding is reducing trust.

A realistic implementation range is 4 to 8 hours if staying inside Wix and using the current page/assets. If migrating to a separate custom static page or adding stronger local SEO/schema, a fair ROM is 8 to 14 hours.
