## Summary

Evolve already has the bones of a solid local service page: visible phone number, free consultation intent, a broad training-and-recovery offer, testimonials, and clear Naperville relevance. The opportunity here is **not** to reinvent the brand. It is to make the existing page feel cleaner, more trustworthy, and easier to scan on mobile.

This conservative pass stays close to the current trainer / site-builder style and focuses on low-effort, high-value fixes a small cleanup sprint could realistically ship.

## What is already working

- Free-consultation offer is present
- Broad service mix: personal training, at-home training, nutrition, stretch therapy, massage
- Local signals are visible
- Trust assets already exist: testimonials, process, address, phone
- Site performance appears decent enough that trust/clarity matters more than a redesign

## Best conservative cleanup opportunities

### 1) Remove template residue that weakens trust
The reported testimonial heading **“Stories from Satisfied Homeowners”** does not fit a personal training business. Small leftover template copy like this can quietly damage credibility.

### 2) Replace placeholder alt text and unfinished SEO details
Observed placeholder-style alt text such as **`[company_name], [city], [postcode]`** makes the page feel unfinished and misses easy local SEO value.

### 3) Tighten the hero around local intent
The existing hero appears broadly positive but too generic. A stronger conservative version should simply say what the business does, where, and who it helps — without changing the overall look of the page.

### 4) Clarify service paths instead of listing everything equally
The business offers several useful services, but first-time visitors need a faster path:
- personal training
- at-home training
- mobility / recovery
- nutrition support

A light reorganization can help visitors self-identify without a major layout change.

### 5) Clean up testimonial framing and copy
Testimonials are valuable here, but sloppy labels, grammar issues, or duplication can reduce trust. This is a strong low-effort cleanup item.

### 6) Strengthen CTA wording
A button like **“Request Free Consultation”** matches the real action better than generic contact language such as **“Send Message.”**

### 7) Add FAQ and local copy support
A compact FAQ and short local service blurbs can improve both mobile scannability and local SEO without making the page feel stuffed.
