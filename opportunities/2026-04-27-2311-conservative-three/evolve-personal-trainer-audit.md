## Quick take

Evolve already has the bones of a solid local service page: visible phone number, free consultation intent, a broad training-and-recovery offer, testimonials, and clear Naperville relevance. The opportunity here is **not** to reinvent the brand. It is to make the existing page feel cleaner, more trustworthy, and easier to scan on mobile.

This conservative pass stays close to the current trainer / site-builder style and focuses on low-effort, high-value fixes a small cleanup sprint could realistically ship.

## What is already working

1. **Free-consultation offer is present.**
2. **Broad service mix** including personal training, at-home training, nutrition, stretch therapy, and massage.
3. **Local signals are visible.**
4. **Trust assets already exist** through testimonials, process, address, and phone.
5. **Performance appears decent enough** that trust and clarity matter more than a redesign.

## Biggest conversion issues

### 1) Template residue weakens trust
The reported testimonial heading **“Stories from Satisfied Homeowners”** does not fit a personal training business and makes the page feel borrowed rather than maintained.

### 2) Placeholder alt text suggests unfinished SEO work
Observed placeholder-style alt text such as **`[company_name], [city], [postcode]`** reduces trust and leaves easy local SEO value unused.

### 3) The hero is too generic for local-intent visitors
The page likely needs a clearer first-screen explanation of what the business does, where it serves, and who it helps.

### 4) Service breadth is presented before visitor guidance
The business offers several useful services, but first-time visitors need a faster self-sort path:
- personal training
- at-home training
- mobility / recovery
- nutrition support

### 5) Testimonial framing and cleanup matter more here than usual
Testimonials are central proof for this kind of business. Sloppy labels, grammar issues, or duplication can quietly reduce conversion.

### 6) CTA wording can match intent better
A button like **Request a Free Consultation** is stronger than generic contact language such as **Send Message**.

### 7) FAQ and local support copy are underused opportunities
A compact FAQ and short local service blurbs can improve mobile scannability and local SEO without making the page feel heavy.

## Best conservative cleanup opportunities

### 1) Remove template residue that weakens trust
Small leftover copy from unrelated templates should be removed or rewritten first.

### 2) Replace placeholder alt text and unfinished SEO details
Swap placeholder text for real service- and location-specific descriptions.

### 3) Tighten the hero around local intent
The hero should simply state what the business does, where, and who it helps — without changing the overall look of the page.

### 4) Clarify service paths instead of listing everything equally
A light reorganization can help visitors self-identify without a major layout change.

### 5) Clean up testimonial framing and copy
Better section labels and cleaner testimonial presentation are strong low-effort wins.

### 6) Strengthen CTA wording
Use **Request a Free Consultation** as the main action language.

### 7) Add FAQ and local copy support
Add short sections that answer common objections and reinforce Naperville-area intent.
