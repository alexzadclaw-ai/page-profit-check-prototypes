# FITLY LLC — Conservative Landing Page Cleanup Audit

- **Business:** FITLY LLC
- **Site:** https://www.fitlyco.net/
- **Batch:** 2026-04-27-2311-conservative-three
- **Prepared:** 2026-04-27
- **Primary contact observed:** fitlyllc25@gmail.com
- **Scope note:** This package is a **conservative cleanup concept, not a full redesign**. The goal is to stay close to the current site-builder feel and improve clarity, trust, and conversion with low-effort edits.

## Snapshot

FITLY already has the hard part: a clear in-home training offer, repeated consultation CTAs, a testimonial, and real credentials. The opportunity is to make the page feel more polished and more complete **without changing its overall style or structure too much**.

This is a good candidate for a small cleanup sprint rather than a dramatic redesign.

## What is already working

- Clear convenience angle: personal training that comes to the client
- Useful audience fit for busy adults and post-rehab clients
- Repeated free-consultation CTA
- Real trust ingredients already exist: NASM credentials, rehab/PTA background, testimonial
- Local Naperville / surrounding suburbs relevance is already present in the site positioning

## Best conservative improvements

### 1) Tighten the hero without changing the overall feel
The current hero is understandable, but it can do more work.

**Conservative fix:**
- keep the simple headline/subheadline structure
- make the first message more specific to in-home training, busy adults, and local service area
- keep the CTA in the same visual spirit, just with clearer support copy

**Example direction:**
> In-home personal training for busy Naperville-area adults  
> Get stronger, move with confidence, and train at home without the gym commute.

## 2) Add one short “what happens in the consultation” block
The current page asks for a consultation several times, but it does not fully explain what visitors get when they click.

**Conservative fix:**
Add a short support block near the first CTA explaining that the consultation covers goals, training history, schedule, and whether in-home coaching is a fit.

This is a low-effort, high-value trust improvement.

### 3) Move trust cues higher on the page
The page already has credentials and experience, but they appear later than they should.

**Conservative fix:**
Add a short trust strip near the top using simple text items such as:
- Certified trainer
- Rehab-informed approach
- Beginner-friendly support
- Free consultation

This should feel like a cleanup, not a redesign.

### 4) Add a small service-area section
The business is local, so the page should make that feel obvious earlier.

**Conservative fix:**
Add a short section or line that references likely service areas such as Naperville, Aurora, Plainfield, Bolingbrook, and nearby western suburbs if accurate.

This helps both visitors and local SEO.

### 5) Add a short FAQ instead of adding more long sales copy
A simple FAQ is one of the easiest ways to reduce hesitation.

**Good topics:**
- Do I need equipment at home?
- Is this good for beginners?
- Do you work with post-rehab clients?
- What areas do you serve?
- What happens during the free consultation?

### 6) Clean up placeholder or low-trust elements
The source notes observed Wix-branded social links and a few copy inconsistencies.

**Conservative fix:**
- replace placeholder social links with real ones or remove them
- clean up grammar and consistency issues
- make sure experience/credential statements do not conflict

### 7) Strengthen the final form copy
The current form area can close more clearly.

**Conservative fix:**
- remind visitors what kinds of goals they can ask about
- use button language like **Request Free Consultation** instead of a generic form action

## Why this should stay conservative

A full visual redesign is not necessary to create value here. The existing brand impression is simple, approachable, and serviceable. The smarter move is a focused pass on:

1. clarity
2. trust order
3. CTA support
4. local relevance
5. placeholder cleanup

That is the direction reflected in the prototype included in this batch.
