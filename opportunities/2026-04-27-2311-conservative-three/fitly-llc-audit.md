# FITLY LLC — Conservative Landing Page Cleanup Audit

- **Business:** FITLY LLC
- **Site:** https://www.fitlyco.net/
- **Primary contact observed:** fitlyllc25@gmail.com
- **Reviewed:** 2026-04-27
- **Priority:** A
- **Positioning:** Conservative cleanup opportunity for an in-home trainer site with strong existing trust ingredients

## Quick take

FITLY already has the hard part: a clear in-home training offer, repeated consultation CTAs, a testimonial, and real credentials. The opportunity is to make the page feel more polished and more complete **without changing its overall style or structure too much**.

This is a good candidate for a small cleanup sprint rather than a dramatic redesign.

## What is already working

1. **Clear convenience angle** with personal training that comes to the client.
2. **Useful audience fit** for busy adults and post-rehab clients.
3. **Repeated free-consultation CTA.**
4. **Real trust ingredients already exist** through NASM credentials, rehab/PTA background, and testimonial proof.
5. **Local Naperville / surrounding suburbs relevance** is already present in the positioning.

## Biggest conversion issues

### 1) The hero could be more specific without changing tone
The current hero is understandable, but it can work harder by naming in-home training, likely audience, and local service context more directly.

### 2) The consultation CTA is repeated more than it is explained
The page asks for a consultation several times, but it does not fully tell visitors what they get when they click.

### 3) Trust cues appear later than ideal
Credentials and experience are present, but they would have more impact if surfaced closer to the first CTA.

### 4) Local service-area support could be clearer
The business is local, so the page should make that feel obvious earlier.

### 5) FAQ is a missed low-effort trust and SEO opportunity
A compact FAQ could reduce hesitation without adding long-form sales copy.

### 6) Placeholder or low-trust elements weaken polish
The source notes observed Wix-branded social links and a few copy inconsistencies. Those are quiet but meaningful trust leaks.

### 7) Final form copy can close more clearly
The contact area should better connect the form action to real fitness and rehab-adjacent goals.

## Best conservative improvements

### 1) Tighten the hero without changing the overall feel
**Conservative fix:**
- keep the simple headline/subheadline structure
- make the first message more specific to in-home training, busy adults, and local service area
- keep the CTA in the same visual spirit, just with clearer support copy

**Example direction:**
> In-home personal training for busy Naperville-area adults  
> Get stronger, move with confidence, and train at home without the gym commute.

### 2) Add one short “what happens in the consultation” block
Add a short support block near the first CTA explaining that the consultation covers goals, training history, schedule, and whether in-home coaching is a fit.

### 3) Move trust cues higher on the page
Add a simple trust strip near the top using items such as:
- Certified trainer
- Rehab-informed approach
- Beginner-friendly support
- Free consultation

### 4) Add a small service-area section
Add a short section or line that references likely service areas such as Naperville, Aurora, Plainfield, Bolingbrook, and nearby western suburbs **if accurate**.

### 5) Add a short FAQ instead of more long sales copy
Good topics:
- Do I need equipment at home?
- Is this good for beginners?
- Do you work with post-rehab clients?
- What areas do you serve?
- What happens during the free consultation?

### 6) Clean up placeholder or low-trust elements
- replace placeholder social links with real ones or remove them
- clean up grammar and consistency issues
- make sure experience/credential statements do not conflict

### 7) Strengthen the final form copy
Use action language like **Request Free Consultation** and remind visitors what kinds of goals they can ask about.

## SEO basics to tighten

- FAQ support for search intent
