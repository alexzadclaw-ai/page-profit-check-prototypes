## Quick take
HMS, LLC already feels like a real neighborhood handyman business: personal, approachable, and easy to contact. The biggest opportunity is not a full redesign. It is a sharper first screen, clearer service paths, stronger near-top trust proof, and a more specific free-estimate flow so Fort Myers area homeowners know what to do next.

## What is already working
- The message “Your Neighborhood Handyman” is personable and fits the business.
- Free estimate language is already present.
- Phone and email are visible in the page content.
- The site uses real handyman/home-improvement imagery instead of feeling overly corporate.
- The existing white space and simple layout can be upgraded without losing the original feel.

## Biggest conversion issues
- **Critical:** The first screen does not quickly turn “handyman services” into clear buyer paths or a specific action.
- **Critical:** The Wix promotional banner and “Proudly created with Wix.com” residue reduce trust for a service business asking for home access.
- **High:** The CTA language is generic: “Learn More” and “Submit” are weaker than “Call for a Free Estimate” or “Email Your To-Do List.”
- **High:** Trust proof is delayed or absent near the top, even though the copy makes a strong personal promise.
- **High:** Services are described broadly but not organized into scannable categories such as repairs, odd jobs, improvements, and renovations.
- **Medium:** SEO basics are thin: homepage title is generic, headings could target the Fort Myers area more directly, and the meta description should summarize service area and estimate offer.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Rebuild the hero around a clearer promise: handyman repairs, odd jobs, home improvements, and renovations with a free estimate.
- Put phone and email CTAs directly in the first screen.
- Add a simple trust strip near the top: treats homes carefully, small jobs welcome, large projects handled, free estimates.
- Organize services into scan-friendly cards based on what homeowners actually need.
- Add a “what happens next” section so visitors know the estimate process before they call.
- Keep the original HMS tone, white/cream spacing, rust contact color, and real site imagery so the owner recognizes the page.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a location-aware title such as “HMS, LLC | Handyman Services in Southwest Florida.”
- Add a meta description that mentions Fort Myers / Southwest Florida, home repairs, odd jobs, renovations, and free estimates.
- Use one clear H1 and supporting H2s for services, process, and contact.
- Add descriptive alt text to the existing handyman and repair images.
- Make the phone number clickable with a `tel:` link and the email clickable with `mailto:`.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a surgical homepage cleanup, not a full rebuild. The practical work is to remove Wix/template residue, rewrite the top copy and CTAs, reorganize the services into clearer homepage sections, add a short process block, and tune title/meta/headings. If HMS stays on Wix, most of this can be implemented in the existing editor using the current images and layout direction.
