## Quick take
Stark Electric LLC already has the right essentials for a local electrician site: a real logo, phone number, email, service categories, licensed and insured language, and 24-hour/free-estimate messaging. The biggest gap is that the site currently feels like a lightly edited Wix template, so the most valuable trust and conversion cues are not visible or specific enough in the first screen.

## What is already working
- Clear business name and electrician category.
- Licensed and insured appears near the logo.
- Phone and email are present in the header and contact area.
- The existing wiring/tool imagery fits the trade and should be preserved.
- The three core services are easy to understand at a basic level: new wiring, rewiring/repairs, and installations.
- The dark, simple style is recognizable and appropriate for a straightforward contractor site.

## Biggest conversion issues
- **Critical:** The Wix promo banner and “Proudly created with Wix.com” footer reduce professionalism for a licensed trade business.
- **Critical:** The hero CTA says “Contact for Estimate,” but it does not make the free estimate or phone action specific enough.
- **High:** Licensed, insured, 24-hour availability, and free estimate language are scattered instead of packaged as trust proof near the top.
- **High:** Service cards use generic “Book Now” labels and do not explain what each service includes.
- **High:** The page does not explain what happens after a prospect submits the form or calls.
- **Medium:** Social icons include unrelated/default Wix platform links in the inspect data, which can create trust leakage.
- **Medium:** The form is present, but the submit label is generic and the fields do not help prospects describe an electrical need.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Replace the template banner/footer residue with a cleaner Stark Electric-owned presentation.
- Keep the same hero photo and dark block, but make the first screen immediately say: licensed, insured, 24-hour availability, free estimate, and call the phone number.
- Add a top trust strip so prospects do not have to hunt for proof.
- Keep the three-card service structure, but add buyer-friendly descriptions and service-specific CTAs.
- Add a simple “What Happens Next” block so calling or submitting a form feels lower risk.
- Improve the mobile path by making the phone CTA and service cards easier to scan.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a stronger title such as “Stark Electric LLC | Licensed Electrician in Metro Detroit.”
- Add a meta description that mentions residential and commercial electrical services, licensed and insured, 24-hour availability, free estimates, and the service area.
- Use one clear H1 and logical H2s for services, next steps, and contact.
- Add descriptive image alt text for wiring, repair, and installation photos.
- Remove or replace default Wix/social links that do not represent Stark Electric.
- Add local service-area wording once the exact cities served are confirmed.
