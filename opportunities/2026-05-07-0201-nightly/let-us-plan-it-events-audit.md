## Quick take
Let Us Plan It, LLC already has a simple Wix presence with real package names, visible starting prices, contact details, social links, and a warm owner/event-photo feel. The main conversion problem is that the homepage jumps from a basic header and image into booking cards without first answering, “Is this right for my event, can I trust them, and what should I do next?”

## What is already working
- The business name and logo are visible near the top.
- The site lists specific service categories: weddings, parties, baby showers, personalized items, and rentals.
- Starting prices are published, which reduces uncertainty.
- The homepage uses real event imagery rather than generic stock-only design.
- Email, phone, Facebook, and Instagram are present.
- The phrase “Any Occasion, Any Event, Let Us Plan It” is a useful brand promise.

## Biggest conversion issues
- **Critical:** No clear first-screen explanation of who the service is best for, where they work, or what action to take before booking.
- **Critical:** Trust proof is too thin near the top. A new visitor sees packages before seeing planning credibility, owner context, or what the process includes.
- **High:** Services are presented as booking cards, but there is not enough event-fit guidance to help visitors choose wedding planning, party support, shower setup, personalized items, or rentals.
- **High:** The next step is unclear. “Book Now” can feel premature for events that usually require a consultation, date check, and details.
- **High:** Wix promotional/template residue weakens professionalism, especially for an event-planning business where presentation matters.
- **Medium:** SEO basics are underbuilt. The title is generic and the page copy does not strongly target Cincinnati-area event planning, wedding packages, baby showers, party planning, personalized items, or rentals.
- **Medium:** Mobile scanning likely feels long because the cards do not have enough grouping, summary copy, or sticky action support.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Replace the vague top flow with a clearer hero: Cincinnati-area event planning, packages, personalized details, and rentals with a consultation-first CTA.
- Add a trust strip near the top with concrete reasons to continue: package pricing, occasion range, local planning support, and owner-led details.
- Organize service cards by buyer intent: wedding, party, shower, personalized items, and rentals.
- Keep starting prices, but add context around what each package helps with so pricing feels helpful instead of bare.
- Add a “How planning starts” section with simple steps: share your date, choose your occasion path, receive package/rental guidance.
- Remove Wix/banner/template distractions and make contact details feel intentional.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a more descriptive homepage title such as “Cincinnati Event Planner | Weddings, Parties & Showers | Let Us Plan It, LLC”.
- Add a meta description that mentions Cincinnati-area event planning, weddings, parties, baby showers, personalized items, and rentals.
- Use one clear H1 rather than relying on package card text.
- Add local service copy in natural language, not keyword stuffing.
- Add descriptive alt text to owner and event photos.
- Make email and phone click-to-contact links prominent.
