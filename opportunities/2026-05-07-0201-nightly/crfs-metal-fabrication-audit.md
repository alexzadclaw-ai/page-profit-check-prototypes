## Biggest conversion issues
- **Critical:** Template booking leftovers, including repeated “Service Name” and “$19.99” blocks, make the site look unfinished and can reduce trust immediately.
- **Critical:** The hero says “Making Metal Fabrication Affordable,” but does not clearly tell visitors what to request or how to start a real quote.
- **High:** Service labels include spelling/wording issues such as “CUTING,” “LAZER,” “3D PARTS PRINTING,” and “CELLER RESTORATION.”
- **High:** Services are listed as disconnected booking blocks instead of grouped by customer need, so a first-time buyer has to work too hard.
- **High:** Trust signals are thin near the top. The page should quickly show local service area, custom fabrication capability, and repair/project intake.
- **Medium:** Navigation includes extra Wix/store/blog/member paths that distract from quote requests.
- **Medium:** Footer and social links appear generic or duplicated, which adds to the template feel.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Replace the first screen with a clearer headline: custom metal fabrication, welding, CNC plasma cutting, engraving, 3D printing, and CAD design for Filer, Twin Falls, and nearby Idaho customers.
- Swap generic “Book Now” blocks for a quote-first action: “Request a Fabrication Quote.”
- Add a compact trust strip directly under the hero: local service area, custom one-off jobs, repair work, and CAD-to-cut capability.
- Organize services into scannable cards with plain-English descriptions and corrected spelling.
- Add a “What happens next” block so prospects know to send dimensions, photos, sketches, or project details.
- Keep the CRFS logo, welding photo, metal image, simple white nav, and industrial card rhythm so the owner recognizes the page as CRFS, not a generic agency template.
