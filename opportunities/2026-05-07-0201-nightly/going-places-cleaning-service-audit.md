# Biggest conversion issues

**Critical**
- The first screen says the mission is to provide high-quality services, but it does not quickly say who the service is for, where they work, or what action a visitor should take.
- The live page includes Wix promotional/template residue, which damages trust for a local service business.

**High**
- Every service is treated like a flat tile with a generic “Book Now” action, so visitors do not know what information to provide or which service path fits their need.
- Trust signals are not near the top beyond contact information. The page needs local context, direct communication, and service range presented earlier.
- Contact details repeat in multiple places without making the next step clearer.
- “Coronavirus” as a standalone service label now feels dated and could be reframed as sanitizing or high-touch cleaning.

**Medium**
- Image alt text is missing or thin on most images.
- The title is generic and does not include the main service/location phrase.
- The footer credits Wix instead of reinforcing the business.
- Mobile visitors have to scroll through long generic sections before reaching clear action options.
