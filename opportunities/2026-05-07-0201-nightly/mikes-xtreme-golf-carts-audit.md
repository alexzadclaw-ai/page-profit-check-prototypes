# Mike's X-Treme LLC Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Mike's X-Treme LLC is a strong fit for a Solution A surgical homepage cleanup. The site already has a memorable golf-cart niche, a real local address, hours, phone number, email, and a full list of services. The biggest opportunity is not a rebrand. It is making the first screen explain the repair and upgrade paths faster, then pushing shoppers toward a free estimate.

## What is already working
- The business name and niche are distinctive. A golf-cart repair and upgrade shop is easy to remember.
- The homepage already says "You Think It, We Do It," which is useful personality and worth keeping.
- The service list is broad and specific: electrical service, audio, vinyl wrapping, battery service, wheel alignment, performance upgrades, lift kits, rims, and tires.
- The page includes address, hours, email, and phone number.
- The existing site has real visual assets, so a brand-faithful cleanup can feel recognizable rather than generic.

## Biggest conversion issues
1. **Critical:** The hero does not immediately separate repair needs from upgrade/custom work, so visitors may not know whether their cart issue fits.
2. **Critical:** The primary CTA is too generic. "Get in Touch" and many separate "Book Now" buttons compete with the real desired action, which is a free estimate.
3. **High:** Trust and practical details exist, but the address, hours, phone, free estimate, and local-shop cues are not reinforced near the top.
4. **High:** The services are presented as a flat booking list instead of buyer-ready paths like repair/diagnostics, performance/ride, and custom look/sound.
5. **High:** The page does not explain what happens after someone asks for a free estimate.
6. **Medium:** Wix/template residue and social/footer leftovers make the page feel less finished than the shop itself.
7. **Medium:** The long vertical layout can be harder to scan on mobile because every service has similar weight.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Rewrite the hero around the plain-English promise: golf cart repairs, batteries, lift kits, audio, wraps, rims, tires, and custom upgrades.
2. Make the main CTA specific: "Call for a Free Estimate" and a secondary "Email Cart Details."
3. Move trust and contact proof near the top: free estimates, McHenry address, repair/custom scope, and phone number.
4. Group services by buyer intent so visitors can self-identify quickly.
5. Add a short "What happens next" section that explains call/email, confirm the service, and set the estimate.
6. Remove template leftovers and make the footer/contact area feel business-specific.
7. Tighten mobile scanning with fewer, clearer sections and full-width action buttons.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Change the homepage title to include service, location, and brand, for example: "Golf Cart Repair & Upgrades in McHenry, IL | Mike's X-Treme LLC."
- Add a concise meta description around repairs, batteries, lift kits, audio, wraps, tires, rims, and free estimates.
- Use one clear H1 and H2s for services, next steps, and contact.
- Add descriptive alt text for real shop/cart images.
- Add LocalBusiness or AutoRepair-style schema if the live platform allows it, including address, phone, hours, and service area.
- Consider a short FAQ later for "golf cart battery service," "lift kit installation," and "golf cart audio installation" searches.

## Realistic implementation angle
This can be implemented as a focused Wix homepage cleanup. The owner does not need a new brand system. The practical scope is to rewrite and reorganize the homepage, keep the recognizable imagery and shop feel, clean up template residue, and make phone/email/free-estimate actions much clearer.

A realistic implementation ROM is 4 to 7 hours if the current Wix site access and assets are available. If new photos, booking configuration, or deeper SEO/schema setup are added, the range is closer to 7 to 10 hours.
