## Biggest conversion issues
- **Critical:** The top Wix banner and “Proudly created with Wix.com” style residue make the site feel unfinished and less professional.
- **Critical:** The hero copy is friendly but not sharp enough about the next step. Visitors have to infer whether they should call, book online, request a walkthrough, or choose a service.
- **High:** Service cards repeat generic filler language, so Basic, Advanced, and Restoration do not feel meaningfully different.
- **High:** Trust signals exist, especially the guarantee, but they are buried in paragraph copy instead of being visible near the top.
- **High:** The booking path is fragmented across “Book Now,” “Services,” “Get in Touch,” and multiple nav items.
- **Medium:** Spelling and wording issues such as “Communitcation” reduce polish.
- **Medium:** SEO basics can be tightened with a clearer title, meta description, local language, and better heading hierarchy.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Replace the generic first screen with a specific Medford window-cleaning hero and clear “Request a Walkthrough” CTA.
- Put the guarantee, local service area, package clarity, and open communication in a trust strip immediately below the hero.
- Rewrite the service section so Walk Through, Basic, Advanced, and Restoration each answer a different buyer question.
- Add a “What happens next” section so customers know how the walkthrough and quote process works.
- Keep the original tall window-photo feel, plain typography, and simple Wix-style rhythm so the owner recognizes the site.
- Remove obvious template residue and stale social/template signals from the customer-facing experience.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title like “C&S Window Cleaning LLC | Window Cleaning in Medford, OR.”
- Add a meta description that mentions Medford, window cleaning, walkthroughs, basic cleaning, advanced cleaning, restoration, and pressure washing.
- Use one clear H1 focused on the service and location.
- Add descriptive service headings instead of repeated “Services” link labels.
- Add alt text to meaningful images and remove empty social-image links where possible.
- Consider adding city/service copy for nearby Oregon service areas if C&S serves beyond Medford.
