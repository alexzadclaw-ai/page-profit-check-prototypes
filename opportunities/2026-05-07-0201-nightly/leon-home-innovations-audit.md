# Quick take
Leon Home Innovations LLC already has the bones of a straightforward service-business site: direct email, phone number, booking options, a simple black/yellow visual identity, and clear willingness to do free estimates. The biggest leak is trust and clarity. The homepage looks like a Wix starter site in several places, especially the fake testimonial block and generic service copy, so a visitor may hesitate before submitting a project request.

# What is already working
- Email and phone are visible, giving visitors direct contact paths.
- The brand colors are memorable and consistent: black, yellow, white, and blue-gray buttons.
- The homepage already offers three estimate types: in person, virtual, and photo/text/email.
- The simple Wix layout is easy for the owner to recognize and maintain.
- Social links exist, even if they are currently underused as proof.

# Biggest conversion issues
- **Critical:** The testimonial is still Wix placeholder copy with a fake-looking name, which damages trust immediately.
- **Critical:** The hero headline says welcome but does not explain what projects Leon handles or where they serve.
- **High:** The CTA is generic. “Get in Touch” does not match the useful estimate options already on the page.
- **High:** Services are not organized by customer intent, so visitors cannot quickly tell if repairs, renovations, installations, or general improvements are a fit.
- **High:** There is no clear “what happens next” explanation after someone reaches out.
- **Medium:** The Wix promotional banner and “proudly created with Wix.com” residue make the business look less established.
- **Medium:** SEO basics are thin: the title is generic, there is no local/service phrase in the first screen, and the page needs stronger heading structure.

# Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Replace the welcome-only hero with a clearer service promise: reliable repairs, renovations, and installations for New Jersey and the NYC metro area.
- Make the primary CTA specific: “Choose a Free Estimate” and “Request a Free Estimate.”
- Add a trust strip near the top using proof Leon can already support: free estimate options, direct communication, local service, and direct phone/email contact.
- Convert the three booking cards into service/estimate cards that explain when each option makes sense.
- Replace the fake testimonial with a neutral call-to-action/proof section until real reviews are available.
- Add a four-step process so homeowners know what happens after they submit the form.
- Clean the contact form labels so the message field asks for project details, timing, location, and preferred estimate type.

# SEO basics to tighten
- Use a more descriptive title such as “Leon Home Innovations LLC | Home Improvement Estimates in NJ and NYC Metro.”
- Add a meta description focused on home repairs, renovations, installations, and free estimates.
- Make the H1 describe the service, not just the business name.
- Add local language on the page for New Jersey and NYC metro area.
- Use descriptive alt text for the logo, hero image, and service images.
- Add internal anchors or navigation labels for Services, Estimates, Process, and Contact.

# Realistic implementation angle
This is a light homepage cleanup, not a full rebuild. The fastest path is to keep the existing Wix design language and update the homepage sections: hero copy, CTA labels, three service/estimate cards, trust strip, process section, replacement for the placeholder testimonial, contact form helper text, page title, and meta description. A realistic implementation range is 3 to 6 focused hours in Wix if the current images and sections are reused.
