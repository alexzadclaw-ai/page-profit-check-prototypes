# Steve Piper & Sons, Inc. — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Steve Piper & Sons, Inc. is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current website already has recognizable local-service proof, a clear service category, and reachable contact paths — but the first-screen decision flow can be made more direct without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current visual direction and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Request Tree Service**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #2f5d37, #c98a2c, #f4f0df, #132417.
- **Typography feel:** Practical local-service sans-serif with bold contractor-style display headings.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Phone/contact-forward navigation with fewer competing choices.
- **Hero/image treatment:** Same service-business positioning, but with the main action visible immediately.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable elements to preserve:**
- earthy green and gold tree-service identity, long-standing local-company tone, and phone-forward header
- Deeply rooted in your community since 1964
- phone-forward local service navigation
- service categories from the live site
- estimate/contact-forward ending

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with clear service intent in Yorkville, Naperville, Joliet, Aurora, Plainfield, Oswego, and surrounding IL suburbs.
- The homepage already communicates real local services: tree trimming, removal, stump grinding, certified arborist services, mulch, firewood, and wood waste recycling.
- The site includes direct phone, contact, quote, estimate, appointment, or booking paths.
- The brand has proof points worth preserving: Serving communities since 1964, Certified arborists on staff, Insured union contractor, Free estimates.
- The service range is broad enough to support multiple buyer-intent paths.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The first screen has several competing menu and product paths before the visitor sees the strongest call-to-action.
   - Prototype response: Make Free Estimate the dominant action and keep the phone visible.

2. **High:** Repeated tree-health blocks and slider residue make a trustworthy company feel less polished than it is.
   - Prototype response: Compress repeated proof into a compact trust strip.

3. **High:** Tree service, mulch, firewood, and recycling are valuable but need a clearer first-choice path.
   - Prototype response: Group tree care, stump grinding, mulch/firewood, and arborist diagnosis by buyer intent.

4. **High:** The consultation/contact flow can better explain what details to share before requesting help.
   - Prototype response: Add a simple next-step section before contact.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Make Free Estimate the dominant action and keep the phone visible.
- Compress repeated proof into a compact trust strip.
- Group tree care, stump grinding, mulch/firewood, and arborist diagnosis by buyer intent.
- Add a simple next-step section before contact.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
