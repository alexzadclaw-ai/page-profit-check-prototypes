# Royal Contractors & Remodeling Inc. — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Royal Contractors & Remodeling Inc. is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current website already has recognizable local-service proof, a clear service category, and reachable contact paths — but the first-screen decision flow can be made more direct without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current visual direction and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Request a Free Estimate**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #7b1f24, #d5a446, #f7f1e8, #241515.
- **Typography feel:** Practical local-service sans-serif with bold contractor-style display headings.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Phone/contact-forward navigation with fewer competing choices.
- **Hero/image treatment:** Same service-business positioning, but with the main action visible immediately.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable elements to preserve:**
- red and gold remodeling-company feel, free-estimate messaging, and value-focused local contractor tone
- Quality work and reasonable prices in Naperville remodeling
- phone-forward local service navigation
- service categories from the live site
- estimate/contact-forward ending

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with clear service intent in Naperville and Chicago suburbs, IL.
- The homepage already communicates real local services: kitchen remodeling, bathroom remodeling, basement remodeling, complete home renovation, flooring, painting, and drywall.
- The site includes direct phone, contact, quote, estimate, appointment, or booking paths.
- The brand has proof points worth preserving: 20+ years of experience, A+ BBB rated positioning, Free estimates & consultations, Free 3-D design options.
- The service range is broad enough to support multiple buyer-intent paths.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The homepage has strong value proof, but duplicate forms and repeated estimate copy compete for attention.
   - Prototype response: Make Request a Free Estimate the primary CTA.

2. **High:** Service breadth is useful, yet kitchen, bath, basement, floors, painting, and drywall need a cleaner selection path.
   - Prototype response: Group core remodeling paths into four scannable cards.

3. **High:** A+ BBB, free 3-D design, and 20+ years proof should appear before long service text.
   - Prototype response: Move BBB, experience, consultation, and design proof near the top.

4. **High:** The “lower price” message can be presented with more trust and less clutter.
   - Prototype response: Add a calmer consultation sequence before contact.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Make Request a Free Estimate the primary CTA.
- Group core remodeling paths into four scannable cards.
- Move BBB, experience, consultation, and design proof near the top.
- Add a calmer consultation sequence before contact.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
