# ADV Masonry Contractor — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
ADV Masonry Contractor is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current website already has recognizable local-service proof, a clear service category, and reachable contact paths — but the first-screen decision flow can be made more direct without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current visual direction and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Request a Free Estimate**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #8a3f22, #d8a15d, #f5eee6, #27180f.
- **Typography feel:** Practical local-service sans-serif with bold contractor-style display headings.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Phone/contact-forward navigation with fewer competing choices.
- **Hero/image treatment:** Same service-business positioning, but with the main action visible immediately.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable elements to preserve:**
- brick-and-stone color palette, portfolio-forward masonry structure, and Naperville contractor credibility
- Residential and commercial masonry from Naperville
- phone-forward local service navigation
- service categories from the live site
- estimate/contact-forward ending

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with clear service intent in Naperville, DuPage County, Will County, and western Chicago suburbs, IL.
- The homepage already communicates real local services: residential masonry, commercial masonry, masonry restorations, brickwork, stonework, chimneys, fireplaces, tuckpointing, and new construction.
- The site includes direct phone, contact, quote, estimate, appointment, or booking paths.
- The brand has proof points worth preserving: 20 years of experience, Fully insured and bonded, Residential & commercial, Naperville-based team.
- The service range is broad enough to support multiple buyer-intent paths.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The page has strong masonry content but shows duplicate navigation and staging-domain links that weaken confidence.
   - Prototype response: Use a simplified header with one project CTA.

2. **High:** Residential, commercial, restoration, fireplace, and repair content appears in many places without a first-choice structure.
   - Prototype response: Group masonry services by residential, commercial, restoration, and fireplace/outdoor needs.

3. **High:** The 20-year and insured/bonded proof should be closer to the top.
   - Prototype response: Place experience and insured/bonded proof in the trust strip.

4. **High:** The contact path can better tell homeowners what project details to share.
   - Prototype response: Add a project-request sequence before the contact panel.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Use a simplified header with one project CTA.
- Group masonry services by residential, commercial, restoration, and fireplace/outdoor needs.
- Place experience and insured/bonded proof in the trust strip.
- Add a project-request sequence before the contact panel.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
