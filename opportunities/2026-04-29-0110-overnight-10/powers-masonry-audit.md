# Powers Masonry — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Powers Masonry is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current website already has recognizable local-service proof, a clear service category, and reachable contact paths — but the first-screen decision flow can be made more direct without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current visual direction and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Request Masonry Help**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #2b2b2b, #b66b3e, #f4ede5, #171717.
- **Typography feel:** Practical local-service sans-serif with bold contractor-style display headings.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Phone/contact-forward navigation with fewer competing choices.
- **Hero/image treatment:** Same service-business positioning, but with the main action visible immediately.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable elements to preserve:**
- minimal craftsman layout, brick-warm accent color, and direct phone-forward messaging
- Craftsmanship that stands out
- phone-forward local service navigation
- service categories from the live site
- estimate/contact-forward ending

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with clear service intent in Naperville, Oswego, Aurora, Wheaton, and surrounding IL communities.
- The homepage already communicates real local services: residential masonry, fireplace faces, chimney repairs, outdoor grills, brickwork, and custom masonry.
- The site includes direct phone, contact, quote, estimate, appointment, or booking paths.
- The brand has proof points worth preserving: Licensed, bonded & insured, Naperville-based, Daily service hours, Residential masonry focus.
- The service range is broad enough to support multiple buyer-intent paths.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The current homepage is short and recognizable, but the primary call action is repeated instead of supported by project guidance.
   - Prototype response: Keep the simple header and make Call or Request Masonry Help dominant.

2. **High:** Licensed/bonded/insured proof is valuable but appears low on the page.
   - Prototype response: Lift license, insurance, location, and hours proof to the top.

3. **High:** Service cards are present but could better explain when to choose each path.
   - Prototype response: Expand the four service choices with clearer buyer intent.

4. **High:** The contact section needs a stronger prompt for homeowners to describe the masonry need.
   - Prototype response: Add a short three-step request sequence.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Keep the simple header and make Call or Request Masonry Help dominant.
- Lift license, insurance, location, and hours proof to the top.
- Expand the four service choices with clearer buyer intent.
- Add a short three-step request sequence.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
