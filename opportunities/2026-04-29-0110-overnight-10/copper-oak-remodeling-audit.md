# Copper Oak Remodeling — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Copper Oak Remodeling is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current website already has recognizable local-service proof, a clear service category, and reachable contact paths — but the first-screen decision flow can be made more direct without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current visual direction and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Request a Free Estimate**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #7b4f2c, #c98243, #f8f2eb, #24180f.
- **Typography feel:** Practical local-service sans-serif with bold contractor-style display headings.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Phone/contact-forward navigation with fewer competing choices.
- **Hero/image treatment:** Same service-business positioning, but with the main action visible immediately.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable elements to preserve:**
- warm copper and cream palette, premium remodeling tone, and design-consultation positioning
- Warm, timeless, and tailored — just like home
- phone-forward local service navigation
- service categories from the live site
- estimate/contact-forward ending

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with clear service intent in Naperville, Aurora, Geneva, and western Chicago suburbs, IL.
- The homepage already communicates real local services: kitchen remodeling, bathroom remodeling, mudrooms, basements, laundry rooms, closets, built-ins, offices, EV chargers, and interior renovations.
- The site includes direct phone, contact, quote, estimate, appointment, or booking paths.
- The brand has proof points worth preserving: 25+ years of excellence, 2-year workmanship warranty, In-house craftsmen, Free design consultation.
- The service range is broad enough to support multiple buyer-intent paths.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The site has many strengths, but the large service menu and long sections can overwhelm the first consultation decision.
   - Prototype response: Make Schedule Your Free Design Consultation the dominant action.

2. **High:** Premium proof appears in multiple places instead of one compact trust story near the top.
   - Prototype response: Bring warranty, experience, in-house craftsmen, and consultation proof into a trust strip.

3. **High:** Kitchen, bath, interiors, EV chargers, closets, offices, and basements need a simpler first-choice path.
   - Prototype response: Group services into four homeowner-friendly paths.

4. **High:** Current availability and consultation value can be made more action-oriented.
   - Prototype response: Add a design-build process block before the contact panel.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Make Schedule Your Free Design Consultation the dominant action.
- Bring warranty, experience, in-house craftsmen, and consultation proof into a trust strip.
- Group services into four homeowner-friendly paths.
- Add a design-build process block before the contact panel.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
