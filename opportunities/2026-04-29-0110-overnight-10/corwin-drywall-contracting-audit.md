# Corwin Drywall Contracting LLC — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Corwin Drywall Contracting LLC is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current website already has recognizable local-service proof, a clear service category, and reachable contact paths — but the first-screen decision flow can be made more direct without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current visual direction and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Request a Free Estimate**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #263a59, #f0a83a, #f2f5f9, #111827.
- **Typography feel:** Practical local-service sans-serif with bold contractor-style display headings.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Phone/contact-forward navigation with fewer competing choices.
- **Hero/image treatment:** Same service-business positioning, but with the main action visible immediately.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable elements to preserve:**
- blue-gray contractor styling, free-estimate banner, and wall/ceiling specialist language
- Walls and ceiling specialists with virtual estimates
- phone-forward local service navigation
- service categories from the live site
- estimate/contact-forward ending

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with clear service intent in Naperville, Aurora, Woodridge, Lisle, Yorkville, St. Charles, and surrounding IL counties.
- The homepage already communicates real local services: drywall installation, drywall repair, finishing, basement build-out, remodeling, room additions, acoustical ceiling installation, and commercial wall/ceiling work.
- The site includes direct phone, contact, quote, estimate, appointment, or booking paths.
- The brand has proof points worth preserving: 35+ years of experience, Family-owned contractor, Virtual estimates available, Free estimate by call or text.
- The service range is broad enough to support multiple buyer-intent paths.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** Strong testimonials and specialist proof are buried below a simple hero and gallery blocks.
   - Prototype response: Move call/text free estimate and virtual-estimate proof into the hero.

2. **High:** The site still shows ecommerce-style elements like login/cart that do not support contractor lead generation.
   - Prototype response: Remove ecommerce-style distractions in the page structure.

3. **High:** Drywall, ceilings, remodeling, and build-outs need clearer segmentation.
   - Prototype response: Group services by drywall, repair/finish, ceilings, and build-outs.

4. **High:** The free estimate path can be more visible for call/text and virtual-estimate visitors.
   - Prototype response: Add a quick estimate-prep section before contact.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Move call/text free estimate and virtual-estimate proof into the hero.
- Remove ecommerce-style distractions in the page structure.
- Group services by drywall, repair/finish, ceilings, and build-outs.
- Add a quick estimate-prep section before contact.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
