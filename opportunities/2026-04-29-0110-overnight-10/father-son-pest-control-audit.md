# Father & Son Pest Control — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Father & Son Pest Control is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current website already has recognizable local-service proof, a clear service category, and reachable contact paths — but the first-screen decision flow can be made more direct without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current visual direction and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Request a Free Pest Quote**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #2e7d32, #f39c12, #f2f8ef, #17351d.
- **Typography feel:** Practical local-service sans-serif with bold contractor-style display headings.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Phone/contact-forward navigation with fewer competing choices.
- **Hero/image treatment:** Same service-business positioning, but with the main action visible immediately.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable elements to preserve:**
- green pest-control palette, urgent quote flow, and pest-type selector feel
- Top-rated pest control services in Naperville
- phone-forward local service navigation
- service categories from the live site
- estimate/contact-forward ending

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with clear service intent in Naperville, IL and nearby suburbs.
- The homepage already communicates real local services: pest control, mice, bugs and insects, birds and bats, mosquito and tick, termite, bed bug, and emergency pest services.
- The site includes direct phone, contact, quote, estimate, appointment, or booking paths.
- The brand has proof points worth preserving: Available 24/7, Free quote path, Common household pests, Naperville service focus.
- The service range is broad enough to support multiple buyer-intent paths.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The page opens with useful urgency, but long generic pest copy pushes visitors away from the quote action.
   - Prototype response: Keep the phone and quote CTA in the hero.

2. **High:** Several pest categories are repeated in different sections without a clear hierarchy.
   - Prototype response: Turn pest categories into four clean service cards.

3. **High:** Emergency phone support and free quote messaging can be made more compact and trustworthy.
   - Prototype response: Place 24/7 and free quote proof in the top trust strip.

4. **High:** The contact flow needs less scrolling for homeowners who already know the pest type.
   - Prototype response: Add a short “what to share” sequence before contact.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Keep the phone and quote CTA in the hero.
- Turn pest categories into four clean service cards.
- Place 24/7 and free quote proof in the top trust strip.
- Add a short “what to share” sequence before contact.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
