# U.S. Home Services — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
U.S. Home Services is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current website already has recognizable local-service proof, a clear service category, and reachable contact paths — but the first-screen decision flow can be made more direct without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current visual direction and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Request a Free Estimate**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #1f4f8f, #d3272c, #eef4fb, #111b2b.
- **Typography feel:** Practical local-service sans-serif with bold contractor-style display headings.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Phone/contact-forward navigation with fewer competing choices.
- **Hero/image treatment:** Same service-business positioning, but with the main action visible immediately.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable elements to preserve:**
- blue/red home-services styling, financing banner, and practical remodeling contractor structure
- Complete home remodeling services in Naperville
- phone-forward local service navigation
- service categories from the live site
- estimate/contact-forward ending

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with clear service intent in Naperville and Chicagoland, IL.
- The homepage already communicates real local services: kitchen remodeling, bathroom remodeling, basement remodeling, whole-home remodeling, room additions, interior remodeling, insulation, and home service upgrades.
- The site includes direct phone, contact, quote, estimate, appointment, or booking paths.
- The brand has proof points worth preserving: 0% financing available, 21+ years of experience, 3,500+ projects finished, Naperville remodeling team.
- The service range is broad enough to support multiple buyer-intent paths.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The homepage has strong numbers and financing proof, but long repeated service paragraphs slow down first decisions.
   - Prototype response: Use Contact Us and Call as the primary actions.

2. **High:** Broad remodeling categories need a clearer hierarchy for homeowners choosing a starting point.
   - Prototype response: Show financing, experience, projects, and local proof in a top trust strip.

3. **High:** Financing, years in business, project count, and reviews should be compact and visible near the top.
   - Prototype response: Group services into kitchen, bath, basement, and whole-home/additions paths.

4. **High:** The contact path can reduce duplicate CTA buttons and explain what details to share.
   - Prototype response: Add a simple planning sequence before the contact panel.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Use Contact Us and Call as the primary actions.
- Show financing, experience, projects, and local proof in a top trust strip.
- Group services into kitchen, bath, basement, and whole-home/additions paths.
- Add a simple planning sequence before the contact panel.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
