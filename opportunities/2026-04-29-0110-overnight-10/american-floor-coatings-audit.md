# American Floor Coatings — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
American Floor Coatings is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current website already has recognizable local-service proof, a clear service category, and reachable contact paths — but the first-screen decision flow can be made more direct without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current visual direction and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Get a Free Flooring Estimate**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #1b4d89, #d7a23a, #edf3fb, #18212e.
- **Typography feel:** Practical local-service sans-serif with bold contractor-style display headings.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Phone/contact-forward navigation with fewer competing choices.
- **Hero/image treatment:** Same service-business positioning, but with the main action visible immediately.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable elements to preserve:**
- blue contractor-style identity, practical service copy, and simple local flooring-company structure
- Decorative epoxy flooring for homes and commercial spaces
- phone-forward local service navigation
- service categories from the live site
- estimate/contact-forward ending

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with clear service intent in Naperville, IL.
- The homepage already communicates real local services: decorative epoxy, garage floor coatings, commercial flooring, acid stain, and floor repair/update projects.
- The site includes direct phone, contact, quote, estimate, appointment, or booking paths.
- The brand has proof points worth preserving: Family owned & operated, Licensed and insured, Free estimates, Prompt project completion.
- The service range is broad enough to support multiple buyer-intent paths.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The homepage has useful trust proof, but it is split between small sidebar text, gallery notes, and a buried contact area.
   - Prototype response: Move trust proof into a clear strip under the hero.

2. **High:** The primary estimate action is not as prominent as the phone number and service value could support.
   - Prototype response: Make Get a Free Flooring Estimate the main action.

3. **High:** Certification copy includes a typo-like fragment that can distract from credibility.
   - Prototype response: Use clean certification wording without distracting fragments.

4. **High:** The gallery/service story needs a stronger bridge from “photos” to “request an estimate.”
   - Prototype response: Organize garage, commercial, decorative, and repair/update paths before contact.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Move trust proof into a clear strip under the hero.
- Make Get a Free Flooring Estimate the main action.
- Use clean certification wording without distracting fragments.
- Organize garage, commercial, decorative, and repair/update paths before contact.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
