# ProGlass Windshield Repair — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
ProGlass Windshield Repair is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current website already has recognizable local-service proof, a clear service category, and reachable contact paths — but the first-screen decision flow can be made more direct without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current visual direction and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Get a Quick Glass Quote**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #0e4f7a, #32a0c2, #edf8fb, #102333.
- **Typography feel:** Practical local-service sans-serif with bold contractor-style display headings.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Phone/contact-forward navigation with fewer competing choices.
- **Hero/image treatment:** Same service-business positioning, but with the main action visible immediately.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable elements to preserve:**
- blue auto-glass identity, mobile repair urgency, and quote-form-first structure
- Honest, quick, and precise mobile glass help
- phone-forward local service navigation
- service categories from the live site
- estimate/contact-forward ending

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with clear service intent in Naperville, Aurora, Lisle, Plainfield, Bolingbrook, and nearby IL communities.
- The homepage already communicates real local services: mobile windshield rock chip repair, long crack repair, windshield coatings, and headlight restoration.
- The site includes direct phone, contact, quote, estimate, appointment, or booking paths.
- The brand has proof points worth preserving: Mobile service based in Naperville, Quick quote path, Rock chip and long-crack help, Headlight restoration available.
- The service range is broad enough to support multiple buyer-intent paths.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The page starts with a useful quote form, but long headlight-restoration education competes with the core windshield repair intent.
   - Prototype response: Make Get a Quick Glass Quote and Call Today the top actions.

2. **High:** CTA language is energetic but less specific than a driver needs during a glass problem.
   - Prototype response: Group rock chip, long crack, coatings, and headlights into service cards.

3. **High:** Repair, coating, and headlight services need clearer separation.
   - Prototype response: Move mobile/Naperville/quick quote proof near the top.

4. **High:** The coupon/announcement copy can be presented without distracting from booking.
   - Prototype response: Add a short damage-details flow before contact.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Make Get a Quick Glass Quote and Call Today the top actions.
- Group rock chip, long crack, coatings, and headlights into service cards.
- Move mobile/Naperville/quick quote proof near the top.
- Add a short damage-details flow before contact.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
