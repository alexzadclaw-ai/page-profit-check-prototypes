# Donna’s Home Fitness — Landing Page Audit

- **Business:** Donna’s Home Fitness
- **URL:** https://www.donnashomefitness.com/
- **Location signal:** Naperville / South Naperville
- **Primary CTA today:** Book a free trial class
- **Audit date:** 2026-04-27

## Snapshot

The current page already has a believable local offer: personal training and small-group fitness in a welcoming home-gym setting. The opportunity is not a full redesign. It is a conversion-focused cleanup that makes the first screen clearer, brings trust proof higher, and reduces friction around the free-trial CTA.

## What’s working

1. Clear local positioning for Naperville visitors
2. Warm, personal tone that fits the business
3. Good low-friction offer with a free trial
4. Real trust signals: long experience, certifications, direct contact info
5. Straightforward page without obvious clutter overload

## Main missed opportunities

### 1) Hero describes the service, but not the visitor
The current value proposition is friendly, but it can be more specific about who it helps and why it feels different from a big gym.

**Better direction:**
> Friendly personal training and small-group classes for Naperville adults who want strength, confidence, and support without the big-gym pressure.

### 2) The free-trial CTA needs reassurance nearby
A first-time visitor still wonders what happens next. A short expectation-setting block under the CTA would reduce hesitation.

Recommended points:
- meet Donna and see the space
- try a supportive class atmosphere
- good fit for beginners and returners
- no-pressure next step

### 3) Proof should appear earlier
The page has credibility, but some of the strongest proof should be visible near the hero:
- 25+ years of coaching experience
- ACE certified
- Medical fitness background
- local home-gym setting in South Naperville

### 4) Key benefits should scan faster on mobile
Longer explanatory sections can be tightened into short cards like:
- Personal attention
- Comfortable home-gym environment
- Strength and confidence at your pace
- Welcoming for beginners and active older adults
- Flexible class options

### 5) CTA hierarchy should be clearer
“Book a Free Trial Class” should stay primary. Schedule/pricing should support the decision, not compete with it.

## Priority fixes

1. Rewrite the hero around the visitor’s pain and desired outcome
2. Add trust badges directly below the hero CTA
3. Add a “What happens next” section near the top
4. Turn dense benefits into short visual cards
5. Repeat a strong final CTA with contact details and low-friction language

## Mobile / technical angle

The site is usable, but mobile performance is a likely weak spot because of Wix and third-party runtime overhead. A practical cleanup sprint should focus on:
- simplifying the mobile hero
- reducing decorative motion/effects
- reviewing unnecessary Wix apps/integrations
- checking image sizing and tracking load

## Prototype direction included in this batch

The companion prototype demonstrates:
- sharper local hero copy
- trust badges above the fold
- clearer CTA hierarchy
- a “what happens next” explainer
- scannable benefits
- testimonials/proof
- a stronger final CTA section
