# Basement Bros A-Z — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Basement Bros A-Z is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current site already has recognizable local-service proof, brand material, and a reachable contact path — but the first-screen decision flow can be made more direct without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current site family and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Schedule a Basement Visit**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #164f72, #43a047, #edf7fb, #102c3d.
- **Typography feel:** Practical local-service sans-serif body copy with stronger display headings for hierarchy.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Contact-forward local service navigation with fewer competing choices.
- **Hero/image treatment:** Same service-business positioning, but with the main action visible immediately.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:**
- blue/green water-control feel
- basement specialist positioning
- online scheduling path
- guarantee language
- Aurora/Naperville service area

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with clear service intent in Aurora / Naperville, IL.
- The homepage already communicates a real local service category: basement waterproofing, foundation repair, crawl space, sump pump, and basement remodeling.
- The site includes direct phone, contact, quote, estimate, appointment, or booking paths.
- The brand has proof points worth preserving: 24-hour call-back goal, 100% Dry Guarantee, Lifetime crack warranty, Online scheduling available.
- The service range is broad enough to support multiple buyer-intent paths.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The site opens with valuable scheduling intent, but placeholder contact/social content competes with real phone and email details.
   - Prototype response: Make real contact options and online scheduling dominant.

2. **High:** Waterproofing, crack repair, drain tile, sump pump, crawl space, and remodeling pages feel like a long technical list.
   - Prototype response: Organize services by homeowner problem rather than menu depth.

3. **High:** Guarantees and training proof are present but appear after a lot of navigation and copy.
   - Prototype response: Move guarantee, training, and callback proof near the first CTA.

4. **High:** Some page copy appears borrowed or inconsistent, which can reduce confidence in a high-stakes water issue.
   - Prototype response: Replace scattered wording with a cleaner specialist story.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Make real contact options and online scheduling dominant.
2. Organize services by homeowner problem rather than menu depth.
3. Move guarantee, training, and callback proof near the first CTA.
4. Replace scattered wording with a cleaner specialist story.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
