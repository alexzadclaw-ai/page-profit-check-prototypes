# Teran Concrete Inc. — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Teran Concrete Inc. is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current site already has recognizable local-service proof, brand material, and a reachable contact path — but the first-screen decision flow can be made more direct without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current site family and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Request a Free Estimate**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #7c1f1f, #d8b057, #f7f4ef, #231b18.
- **Typography feel:** Practical local-service sans-serif body copy with stronger display headings for hierarchy.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Contact-forward local service navigation with fewer competing choices.
- **Hero/image treatment:** Same service-business positioning, but with the main action visible immediately.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:**
- red/gold established contractor feel
- 47-year headline
- two-phone local service area
- owner-supervised proof
- long concrete-service list

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with clear service intent in Wheaton / Naperville / DuPage County, IL.
- The homepage already communicates a real local service category: residential and commercial concrete driveways, patios, foundations, slabs, and decorative concrete.
- The site includes direct phone, contact, quote, estimate, appointment, or booking paths.
- The brand has proof points worth preserving: 47 years of experience, Owner-supervised work, Licensed and bonded, Free estimates.
- The service range is broad enough to support multiple buyer-intent paths.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The estimate path exists but is surrounded by crowded header text, multiple phone regions, and duplicate navigation.
   - Prototype response: Make a free-estimate CTA dominant while retaining phone options.

2. **High:** The large expertise list is useful but difficult to scan quickly.
   - Prototype response: Group services into four homeowner/business-friendly categories.

3. **High:** 47 years, owner supervision, licensing, bonding, and free estimates should appear before visitors scroll.
   - Prototype response: Move proof into the top trust strip.

4. **High:** Legacy footer/legal and content-platform details make the page feel less premium than the experience claims.
   - Prototype response: Use a concise service footer and focused contact panel.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Make a free-estimate CTA dominant while retaining phone options.
2. Group services into four homeowner/business-friendly categories.
3. Move proof into the top trust strip.
4. Use a concise service footer and focused contact panel.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
