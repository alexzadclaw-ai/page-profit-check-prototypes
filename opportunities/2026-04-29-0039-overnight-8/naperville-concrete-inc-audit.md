# Naperville Concrete Inc. — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Naperville Concrete Inc. is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current site already has recognizable local-service proof, brand material, and a reachable contact path — but the first-screen decision flow can be made more direct without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current site family and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Get a Concrete Quote**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #1f2933, #e2a225, #f6f5ee, #15191d.
- **Typography feel:** Practical local-service sans-serif body copy with stronger display headings for hierarchy.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Contact-forward local service navigation with fewer competing choices.
- **Hero/image treatment:** Same service-business positioning, but with the main action visible immediately.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:**
- simple black/gold local-contractor feel
- flat concrete and hardscape mix
- Naperville-first positioning
- project stats
- guarantee/licensing proof

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with clear service intent in Naperville, IL.
- The homepage already communicates a real local service category: flat concrete, hardscaping, driveways, sidewalks, patios, fire pits, retaining walls, and decorative concrete.
- The site includes direct phone, contact, quote, estimate, appointment, or booking paths.
- The brand has proof points worth preserving: 20+ years of experience, Licensed, bonded, insured, 200+ projects completed, All work guaranteed.
- The service range is broad enough to support multiple buyer-intent paths.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The contact path exists but is mostly an anchor lower on the page, so first-screen quote intent could be stronger.
   - Prototype response: Add a dominant quote CTA and phone CTA above the fold.

2. **High:** Service cards are helpful, but the value proof and guarantee should appear sooner.
   - Prototype response: Add proof points immediately under the hero.

3. **High:** Some informal copy and placeholder-style link symbols reduce polish for a contractor asking for project trust.
   - Prototype response: Use business-specific wording and remove empty link clutter.

4. **Medium:** Testimonials and stats are strong but should support the estimate path instead of sitting separately.
   - Prototype response: Use a concise proof band with stats and testimonials.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Add a dominant quote CTA and phone CTA above the fold.
2. Add proof points immediately under the hero.
3. Use business-specific wording and remove empty link clutter.
4. Use a concise proof band with stats and testimonials.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
