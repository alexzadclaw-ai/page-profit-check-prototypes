# Henson Concrete Construction — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Henson Concrete Construction is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current site already has recognizable local-service proof, brand material, and a reachable contact path — but the first-screen decision flow can be made more direct without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current site family and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Request a Concrete Estimate**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #23303a, #f28a24, #f4f6f7, #141b21.
- **Typography feel:** Practical local-service sans-serif body copy with stronger display headings for hierarchy.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Contact-forward local service navigation with fewer competing choices.
- **Hero/image treatment:** Same service-business positioning, but with the main action visible immediately.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:**
- dark contractor palette with orange accent
- commercial/residential concrete split
- request-estimate form focus
- warranty and OSHA proof
- social/project update energy

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with clear service intent in Aurora / Naperville / Chicagoland and Southern Wisconsin.
- The homepage already communicates a real local service category: commercial and residential concrete construction, foundations, driveways, patios, slabs, and parking lots.
- The site includes direct phone, contact, quote, estimate, appointment, or booking paths.
- The brand has proof points worth preserving: One-year workmanship warranty, 100+ years combined experience, OSHA-required training, $0 down for residential projects.
- The service range is broad enough to support multiple buyer-intent paths.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The homepage includes a request form, but placeholder tagline text and duplicate navigation reduce first-impression trust.
   - Prototype response: Remove template residue and make request-estimate the clear hero action.

2. **High:** Warranty, safety, experience, and $0-down proof are excellent but should be compact near the CTA.
   - Prototype response: Use a proof strip immediately under the hero.

3. **High:** Commercial and residential paths are both important but compete with long narrative and social content.
   - Prototype response: Split concrete work into clear buyer paths.

4. **High:** Long Instagram/project feeds are useful later but heavy for first-touch conversion.
   - Prototype response: Use a short proof section before a focused estimate panel.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Remove template residue and make request-estimate the clear hero action.
2. Use a proof strip immediately under the hero.
3. Split concrete work into clear buyer paths.
4. Use a short proof section before a focused estimate panel.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
