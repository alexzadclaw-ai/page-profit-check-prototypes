# Pave Tech Asphalt, LLC — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Pave Tech Asphalt, LLC is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current site already has recognizable local-service proof, brand material, and a reachable contact path — but the first-screen decision flow can be made more direct without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current site family and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Request a Paving Quote**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #24282b, #d5a11e, #f5f1e6, #17191b.
- **Typography feel:** Practical local-service sans-serif body copy with stronger display headings for hierarchy.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Contact-forward local service navigation with fewer competing choices.
- **Hero/image treatment:** Same service-business positioning, but with the main action visible immediately.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:**
- charcoal/asphalt visual direction
- gold first-impression accent
- uppercase CTA style
- project-gallery proof
- owner-involved paving story

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with clear service intent in Naperville / Chicago western suburbs, IL.
- The homepage already communicates a real local service category: asphalt driveways, parking lots, tar and chip, walkways, and gravel paving.
- The site includes direct phone, contact, quote, estimate, appointment, or booking paths.
- The brand has proof points worth preserving: Fourth-generation paving technician, Owner involved on every job, Residential and commercial work, Naperville office.
- The service range is broad enough to support multiple buyer-intent paths.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** Request-a-quote links are present but repeated navigation and duplicate CTA text make the path feel less controlled.
   - Prototype response: Use one dominant quote CTA repeated intentionally.

2. **High:** Service choices are strong but spread across duplicated menus and repeated Learn More links.
   - Prototype response: Turn the service set into four clear buyer paths.

3. **High:** Fourth-generation owner involvement is a persuasive proof point that should sit closer to the first action.
   - Prototype response: Group owner-on-site and project proof near the hero.

4. **High:** Project/review content is valuable but can pull attention away from quote completion.
   - Prototype response: Use short proof cards before a focused quote form.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Use one dominant quote CTA repeated intentionally.
2. Turn the service set into four clear buyer paths.
3. Group owner-on-site and project proof near the hero.
4. Use short proof cards before a focused quote form.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
