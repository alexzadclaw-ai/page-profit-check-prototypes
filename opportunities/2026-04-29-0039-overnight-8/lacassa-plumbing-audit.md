# LaCassa Plumbing — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
LaCassa Plumbing is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current site already has recognizable local-service proof, brand material, and a reachable contact path — but the first-screen decision flow can be made more direct without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current site family and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Schedule Plumbing Service**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #174c87, #f47d30, #eef6fb, #102235.
- **Typography feel:** Practical local-service sans-serif body copy with stronger display headings for hierarchy.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Contact-forward local service navigation with fewer competing choices.
- **Hero/image treatment:** Same service-business positioning, but with the main action visible immediately.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:**
- blue/orange plumbing-service palette
- family-owned owner story
- same-day plumbing message
- A+ BBB and Google-rating proof
- large service menu

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with clear service intent in Naperville, IL.
- The homepage already communicates a real local service category: residential, commercial, emergency plumbing, water heaters, sewer repair, water softeners, filtration, and drain cleaning.
- The site includes direct phone, contact, quote, estimate, appointment, or booking paths.
- The brand has proof points worth preserving: Family-owned and operated, A+ BBB rating, Rated 4.8 on Google, Same-day plumbers.
- The service range is broad enough to support multiple buyer-intent paths.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** Schedule and call actions exist, but multiple #call links and long scroll sections make the top path feel less direct than the service promise.
   - Prototype response: Make schedule/call actions explicit and repeated intentionally.

2. **High:** The site has excellent proof, but some metrics appear as 0% counters, which undermines trust.
   - Prototype response: Use stable proof points instead of unfinished counters.

3. **High:** The service list is comprehensive but long enough to overwhelm a stressed visitor.
   - Prototype response: Group plumbing into emergency, water systems, drain/sewer, and residential/commercial.

4. **Medium:** Coupons, careers, testimonials, and long education sections compete with service booking.
   - Prototype response: Move the homepage flow toward issue selection, schedule, and next steps.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Make schedule/call actions explicit and repeated intentionally.
2. Use stable proof points instead of unfinished counters.
3. Group plumbing into emergency, water systems, drain/sewer, and residential/commercial.
4. Move the homepage flow toward issue selection, schedule, and next steps.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
