# Wiz Plumbing — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Wiz Plumbing is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current site already has recognizable local-service proof, brand material, and a reachable contact path — but the first-screen decision flow can be made more direct without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current site family and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Book Plumbing Service**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #4c2785, #f2b63d, #f6f0fb, #1c1430.
- **Typography feel:** Practical local-service sans-serif body copy with stronger display headings for hierarchy.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Contact-forward local service navigation with fewer competing choices.
- **Hero/image treatment:** Same service-business positioning, but with the main action visible immediately.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:**
- purple/gold wizard brand
- emergency plumbing promise
- large service taxonomy
- review-count proof
- Book Now and phone-forward actions

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with clear service intent in Naperville, IL.
- The homepage already communicates a real local service category: residential, sewer, detection, water systems, piping, outdoor, and commercial plumbing.
- The site includes direct phone, contact, quote, estimate, appointment, or booking paths.
- The brand has proof points worth preserving: 24/7 emergency service, 390+ 5-star Google reviews, Financing available, Residential and commercial.
- The service range is broad enough to support multiple buyer-intent paths.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The brand has a strong Book Now CTA, but the huge duplicated mega-menu creates friction before the visitor reaches the main promise.
   - Prototype response: Simplify the header and make booking/call actions immediate.

2. **High:** Service depth is impressive but too dense for urgent plumbing visitors.
   - Prototype response: Group services by emergency, water systems, drain/sewer, and piping.

3. **High:** Review proof appears with repeated counts and long testimonial blocks.
   - Prototype response: Use one consistent review proof strip near the top.

4. **High:** The playful wizard theme is memorable but should not obscure serious emergency clarity.
   - Prototype response: Balance brand voice with direct repair and scheduling language.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Simplify the header and make booking/call actions immediate.
2. Group services by emergency, water systems, drain/sewer, and piping.
3. Use one consistent review proof strip near the top.
4. Balance brand voice with direct repair and scheduling language.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
