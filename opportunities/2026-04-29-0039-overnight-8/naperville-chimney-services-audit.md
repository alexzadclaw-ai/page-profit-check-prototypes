# Naperville Chimney Services — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Naperville Chimney Services is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current site already has recognizable local-service proof, brand material, and a reachable contact path — but the first-screen decision flow can be made more direct without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current site family and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Book Chimney Service**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #8c1d18, #d39a27, #fbf4ea, #2a1714.
- **Typography feel:** Practical local-service sans-serif body copy with stronger display headings for hierarchy.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Contact-forward local service navigation with fewer competing choices.
- **Hero/image treatment:** Same service-business positioning, but with the main action visible immediately.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:**
- red/gold hearth and chimney feel
- certified chimney-service language
- firefighter safety proof
- Oswego/Naperville local service area
- phone-forward service header

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with clear service intent in Oswego / Naperville, IL.
- The homepage already communicates a real local service category: chimney sweeping, inspections, repairs, gas logs, flue caps, and water prevention.
- The site includes direct phone, contact, quote, estimate, appointment, or booking paths.
- The brand has proof points worth preserving: Serving since 1994, CSIA-certified technicians, Firefighter-owned leadership, Fully insured.
- The service range is broad enough to support multiple buyer-intent paths.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The page has multiple Contact Us actions, but phone and form options compete instead of forming one appointment path.
   - Prototype response: Give one clear booking CTA plus a phone CTA.

2. **High:** CSIA, NCSG, firefighter, and insurance proof is excellent but not compact enough near the first action.
   - Prototype response: Group safety credentials in the top trust strip.

3. **High:** The testimonial area contains repeated slide labels and button artifacts that distract from service choice.
   - Prototype response: Condense reviews into proof highlights and move services forward.

4. **High:** Long service areas and expert suggestions make the homepage feel heavy after the hero.
   - Prototype response: Create a short appointment-prep section and simpler footer.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Give one clear booking CTA plus a phone CTA.
2. Group safety credentials in the top trust strip.
3. Condense reviews into proof highlights and move services forward.
4. Create a short appointment-prep section and simpler footer.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
