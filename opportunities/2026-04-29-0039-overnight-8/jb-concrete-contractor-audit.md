# JB Concrete Contractor, Inc. — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
JB Concrete Contractor, Inc. is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current site already has recognizable local-service proof, brand material, and a reachable contact path — but the first-screen decision flow can be made more direct without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current site family and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Request Concrete Information**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #1f4f78, #e87522, #f3f6f8, #142433.
- **Typography feel:** Practical local-service sans-serif body copy with stronger display headings for hierarchy.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Contact-forward local service navigation with fewer competing choices.
- **Hero/image treatment:** Same service-business positioning, but with the main action visible immediately.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:**
- blue/orange practical contractor identity
- phone-forward header
- commercial concrete emphasis
- Naperville address and 40+ year proof
- simple service categories

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with clear service intent in Naperville / Chicagoland suburbs, IL.
- The homepage already communicates a real local service category: commercial and residential concrete construction.
- The site includes direct phone, contact, quote, estimate, appointment, or booking paths.
- The brand has proof points worth preserving: 40+ years of experience, Commercial and residential work, Clean finish guarantee, Naperville-based contractor.
- The service range is broad enough to support multiple buyer-intent paths.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The homepage has phone and form access, but the top section does not make one quote path feel dominant.
   - Prototype response: Make the concrete information request the primary first-screen action.

2. **High:** Concrete services are real but appear as scattered service/category text instead of buyer-intent choices.
   - Prototype response: Group commercial, residential, paving, and resurfacing into easy service cards.

3. **High:** 40+ years, Naperville address, clean finish promise, and broad job capability are not grouped near the first CTA.
   - Prototype response: Place the proof points directly under the hero.

4. **High:** Footer details, phone/fax text, and legacy site elements make the final contact area feel less intentional.
   - Prototype response: Use a focused contact panel with phone, address, and project fields.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Make the concrete information request the primary first-screen action.
2. Group commercial, residential, paving, and resurfacing into easy service cards.
3. Place the proof points directly under the hero.
4. Use a focused contact panel with phone, address, and project fields.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
