# Royal Garage Doors Repair LLC — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Royal Garage Doors Repair LLC is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current site already has recognizable local-service proof, brand material, and a reachable contact path — but the first-screen decision flow can be made more direct without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current site family and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Schedule Garage Door Help**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #3b2178, #d6a431, #f6f1fb, #171025.
- **Typography feel:** Practical local-service sans-serif body copy with stronger display headings for hierarchy.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Contact-forward local service navigation with fewer competing choices.
- **Hero/image treatment:** Same service-business positioning, but with the main action visible immediately.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:**
- royal purple and gold identity
- 24/7 emergency garage-door promise
- Naperville/Aurora service area
- review-driven proof
- repair + installation breadth

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with clear service intent in Naperville / Aurora / Chicagoland suburbs, IL.
- The homepage already communicates a real local service category: garage door repair, installation, openers, springs, panels, tracks, maintenance, and emergency service.
- The site includes direct phone, contact, quote, estimate, appointment, or booking paths.
- The brand has proof points worth preserving: 24/7 emergency service, Repair and installation, Naperville-based team, Satisfaction guaranteed.
- The service range is broad enough to support multiple buyer-intent paths.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** Urgent 24/7 service is strong, but repeated review blocks and duplicated content push the next action down the page.
   - Prototype response: Make schedule/call CTAs dominant and reduce repeated proof blocks.

2. **High:** The service list is broad but long enough to slow a homeowner with a stuck door.
   - Prototype response: Group services into emergency repair, installation, openers, and maintenance.

3. **High:** Review snippets include repeated names/handles and duplicate entries, which can feel less polished.
   - Prototype response: Summarize review proof as concise trust points.

4. **High:** Small typo-level polish issues weaken a service that depends on trust and safety.
   - Prototype response: Use clean service language and focused next-step copy.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Make schedule/call CTAs dominant and reduce repeated proof blocks.
2. Group services into emergency repair, installation, openers, and maintenance.
3. Summarize review proof as concise trust points.
4. Use clean service language and focused next-step copy.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
