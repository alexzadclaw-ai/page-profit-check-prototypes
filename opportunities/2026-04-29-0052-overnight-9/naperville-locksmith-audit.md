# Naperville Locksmith — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Naperville Locksmith is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current site already has recognizable local-service proof, service breadth, and reachable contact paths — but the first-screen decision flow can be made more direct without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current site family and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Call For Locksmith Help**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #1b1b1d, #d5a11e, #f7f2e6, #111114.
- **Typography feel:** Practical local-service sans-serif with bold contractor-style display headings.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Phone/contact-forward local service navigation with fewer competing choices.
- **Hero/image treatment:** Same service-business positioning, but with the main action visible immediately.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:**
- black/gold locksmith feel
- 24/7 emergency emphasis
- phone-first layout
- auto/home/business service split
- Naperville local focus

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with clear service intent in Naperville, IL.
- The homepage already communicates a real local service category: 24/7 emergency, automotive, residential, and commercial locksmith service.
- The site includes direct phone, contact, quote, estimate, appointment, or booking paths.
- The brand has proof points worth preserving: 24/7 emergency service, 15-minute response message, Licensed, bonded and insured, Automotive, home and business.
- The service range is broad enough to support multiple buyer-intent paths.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** Urgent visitors need the phone number and emergency path before long keyword-heavy copy.
   - Prototype response: Make the call-for-help CTA the hero focus.

2. **High:** Automotive, residential, commercial, and emergency services are useful but buried in long lists.
   - Prototype response: Group service paths into four focused cards.

3. **High:** Trust signals like licensing and insurance should support the call decision near the top.
   - Prototype response: Place trust points directly under the hero.

4. **High:** Dense repeated copy makes the page harder to scan on mobile.
   - Prototype response: Use short sections, next-step guidance, and a focused contact panel.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Make the emergency call path immediate.
2. Reduce dense keyword lists.
3. Group locksmith service choices.
4. Put licensing proof near action.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
