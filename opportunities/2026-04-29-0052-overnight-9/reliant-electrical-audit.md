# Reliant Electrical — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Reliant Electrical is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current site already has recognizable local-service proof, service breadth, and reachable contact paths — but the first-screen decision flow can be made more direct without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current site family and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Request Electrical Help**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #0f4c81, #d64535, #f3f6fb, #152236.
- **Typography feel:** Practical local-service sans-serif with bold contractor-style display headings.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Phone/contact-forward local service navigation with fewer competing choices.
- **Hero/image treatment:** Same service-business positioning, but with the main action visible immediately.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:**
- blue/red electrical contractor palette
- Naperville/St. Charles service focus
- deep service-library credibility
- residential/commercial split
- project-request intent

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with clear service intent in Naperville, St. Charles, and surrounding Illinois service areas.
- The homepage already communicates a real local service category: residential electrical repair, EV charging, panel upgrades, lighting, generators, safety inspections, emergency, and commercial work.
- The site includes direct phone, contact, quote, estimate, appointment, or booking paths.
- The brand has proof points worth preserving: EV charger installation, Panel and wiring upgrades, Lighting and generators, Residential and commercial.
- The service range is broad enough to support multiple buyer-intent paths.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The mega-menu is extremely deep and can overwhelm visitors before they reach a service request.
   - Prototype response: Use a short header and primary Request Electrical Help CTA.

2. **High:** Many services are valuable but need grouping around common buyer intent.
   - Prototype response: Group services into repair, upgrade, EV/smart, and commercial paths.

3. **High:** Expertise is visible through service breadth, but the homepage needs compact proof near the top.
   - Prototype response: Add a four-point trust strip immediately under the hero.

4. **High:** The contact path should help visitors explain the category instead of selecting from dozens of links.
   - Prototype response: Add next-step guidance and an electrical-project request panel.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Replace mega-menu feel with a short path.
2. Group dozens of services into common categories.
3. Put expertise proof near the top.
4. Make the request path category-aware.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
