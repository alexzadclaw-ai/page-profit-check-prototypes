# Able-Warnecke Roofing Inc — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Able-Warnecke Roofing Inc is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current site already has recognizable local-service proof, service breadth, and reachable contact paths — but the first-screen decision flow can be made more direct without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current site family and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Request A Free Estimate**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #1d476f, #c7372f, #f1f5f8, #12283c.
- **Typography feel:** Practical local-service sans-serif with a more traditional serif heading feel.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Phone/contact-forward local service navigation with fewer competing choices.
- **Hero/image treatment:** Same service-business positioning, but with the main action visible immediately.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:**
- traditional blue/red roofing identity
- Quality Does Not Cost It Pays message direction
- family-owned local proof
- free estimate intent
- roof/gutter service list

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with clear service intent in Naperville and Chicago west suburbs, IL.
- The homepage already communicates a real local service category: roof installations, roof repairs, tear-offs, chimney caps, gutter cleaning, and gutter guards.
- The site includes direct phone, contact, quote, estimate, appointment, or booking paths.
- The brand has proof points worth preserving: 50+ years serving Naperville, BBB accredited since 1980, Licensed, insured and bonded, Family-owned and operated.
- The service range is broad enough to support multiple buyer-intent paths.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** Template remnants such as field-label and slide-title content reduce confidence around an otherwise established brand.
   - Prototype response: Remove template residue and create an intentional first-screen estimate path.

2. **High:** Duplicated navigation and generic button labels compete with the free estimate action.
   - Prototype response: Use a compact header with service, proof, next-step, and contact links.

3. **High:** Longevity, BBB proof, licensing, and family ownership are strong but scattered.
   - Prototype response: Group proof directly under the hero.

4. **High:** Service specialties appear in a long builder sequence rather than easy roof-care choices.
   - Prototype response: Organize roof installation, repair, tear-off, and gutter/chimney work into cards.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Remove template residue from the visible flow.
2. Make free estimate the primary action.
3. Group proof near the top.
4. Organize roof-care services into simple paths.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
