# Naperville Fence Company — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Naperville Fence Company is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current site already has recognizable local-service proof, service breadth, and reachable contact paths — but the first-screen decision flow can be made more direct without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current site family and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Request Fence Estimate**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #24613f, #b77a32, #f2f7ef, #153326.
- **Typography feel:** Practical local-service sans-serif with bold contractor-style display headings.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Phone/contact-forward local service navigation with fewer competing choices.
- **Hero/image treatment:** Same service-business positioning, but with the main action visible immediately.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:**
- green/brown outdoor contractor feel
- material-first fence offerings
- Naperville/suburban service area
- phone-forward estimate intent
- broad fence service list

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with clear service intent in Naperville and Chicago suburbs, IL.
- The homepage already communicates a real local service category: aluminum, cedar, vinyl, composite, pressure-treated, chain-link, staining, refinishing, and fence rental.
- The site includes direct phone, contact, quote, estimate, appointment, or booking paths.
- The brand has proof points worth preserving: Aluminum, cedar and vinyl, Composite and pressure-treated, Staining and refinishing, Naperville-area service.
- The service range is broad enough to support multiple buyer-intent paths.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The location and service menus are so large that the estimate path gets buried.
   - Prototype response: Use a simple header and make Request Fence Estimate the primary CTA.

2. **High:** Fence materials are listed, but visitors need a faster comparison by use case.
   - Prototype response: Group materials into privacy, low-maintenance, chain-link/rental, and refinishing paths.

3. **High:** The many local pages should not dominate the homepage before core services are clear.
   - Prototype response: Move local-service context into a compact trust strip and proof band.

4. **High:** Contact should ask for fence-specific details such as length, gates, material, and timing.
   - Prototype response: Add a focused estimate panel with fence-project fields.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Shrink oversized navigation.
2. Make estimate request primary.
3. Group materials by buyer need.
4. Add fence-specific next-step guidance.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
