# Naperville Gutters — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Naperville Gutters is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current site already has recognizable local-service proof, service breadth, and reachable contact paths — but the first-screen decision flow can be made more direct without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current site family and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Schedule Gutter Service**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #126994, #45a64a, #eef8f5, #113345.
- **Typography feel:** Practical local-service sans-serif with bold contractor-style display headings.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Phone/contact-forward local service navigation with fewer competing choices.
- **Hero/image treatment:** Same service-business positioning, but with the main action visible immediately.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:**
- blue/green home-service identity
- call-now gutter service intent
- cleaning/repair/guard service mix
- Naperville local focus
- practical homeowner education

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with clear service intent in Naperville, IL.
- The homepage already communicates a real local service category: gutter cleaning, gutter repair, custom installation, and gutter guards.
- The site includes direct phone, contact, quote, estimate, appointment, or booking paths.
- The brand has proof points worth preserving: Cleaning and repair, Written cost estimates, Gutter guard installation, Local Naperville technicians.
- The service range is broad enough to support multiple buyer-intent paths.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The page has a phone CTA, but repeated all-caps urgency makes the schedule path feel less polished.
   - Prototype response: Create a calm, direct Schedule Gutter Service hero.

2. **High:** Cleaning, repair, guards, and installation are described in long blocks instead of fast choices.
   - Prototype response: Convert service explanations into four scannable cards.

3. **High:** Written estimates and technician evaluation are valuable proof but appear too far into the text.
   - Prototype response: Move practical trust points into the top strip.

4. **High:** Visitors should know what details to provide when requesting service.
   - Prototype response: Add a simple three-step scheduling section and focused contact panel.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Make scheduling calmer and clearer.
2. Turn long service blocks into cards.
3. Elevate written-estimate proof.
4. Show what to share before calling.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
