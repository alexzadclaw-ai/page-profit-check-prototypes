# Adept Construction, Inc. — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Adept Construction, Inc. is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current site already has recognizable local-service proof, service breadth, and reachable contact paths — but the first-screen decision flow can be made more direct without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current site family and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Request A Free Quote**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #263a4f, #e7652d, #f7f3ee, #171f29.
- **Typography feel:** Practical local-service sans-serif with bold contractor-style display headings.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Phone/contact-forward local service navigation with fewer competing choices.
- **Hero/image treatment:** Same service-business positioning, but with the main action visible immediately.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:**
- orange/charcoal exterior contractor feel
- free quote emphasis
- family-owned positioning
- residential/commercial/storm service mix
- Naperville roofing focus

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with clear service intent in Naperville and surrounding suburbs, IL.
- The homepage already communicates a real local service category: residential roofing, commercial roofing, storm damage, siding, and gutters.
- The site includes direct phone, contact, quote, estimate, appointment, or booking paths.
- The brand has proof points worth preserving: Family-owned since 1997, Residential roofing, Commercial roofing, Storm damage help.
- The service range is broad enough to support multiple buyer-intent paths.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The free quote CTA exists, but duplicated navigation and broken-looking relative links weaken the first impression.
   - Prototype response: Make the free quote path the dominant first-screen action with cleaner navigation.

2. **High:** Residential, commercial, storm, siding, and gutter paths are split across multiple menus instead of simple buyer choices.
   - Prototype response: Group services into four scannable cards.

3. **High:** Family-owned history and quality workmanship proof do not sit close enough to the quote action.
   - Prototype response: Place proof points directly below the hero and in the proof band.

4. **High:** Project/gallery links and social links compete with the quote path early in the experience.
   - Prototype response: Prioritize quote, call, services, and next-step sections before secondary content.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Make the free quote path dominant.
2. Simplify duplicated navigation.
3. Group service paths by buyer intent.
4. Move proof closer to action.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
