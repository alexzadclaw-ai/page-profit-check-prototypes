# Aurora Electric — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Aurora Electric is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current site already has recognizable local-service proof, service breadth, and reachable contact paths — but the first-screen decision flow can be made more direct without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current site family and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Request Electrical Service**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #1559a6, #f4b331, #eef5ff, #10233f.
- **Typography feel:** Practical local-service sans-serif with bold contractor-style display headings.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Phone/contact-forward local service navigation with fewer competing choices.
- **Hero/image treatment:** Same service-business positioning, but with the main action visible immediately.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:**
- blue and yellow electrical identity
- Service Is Our Spark messaging
- residential/commercial/industrial split
- 24-hour emergency promise
- phone-forward local contractor feel

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with clear service intent in Aurora and surrounding cities, IL.
- The homepage already communicates a real local service category: residential, commercial, industrial, and emergency electrical services.
- The site includes direct phone, contact, quote, estimate, appointment, or booking paths.
- The brand has proof points worth preserving: 24-hour emergency service, 40+ years of experience, A+ BBB certification, One-year labor and materials warranty.
- The service range is broad enough to support multiple buyer-intent paths.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The urgent request-service path is diluted by duplicated navigation and several competing CTAs.
   - Prototype response: Make Request Electrical Service the primary CTA and use a simplified header.

2. **High:** Residential, commercial, industrial, and emergency service paths need clearer separation for first-time visitors.
   - Prototype response: Create four service cards based on buyer intent.

3. **High:** Warranty, A+ BBB, emergency service, and experience proof are valuable but not presented as a compact trust strip.
   - Prototype response: Move the strongest proof immediately below the hero.

4. **High:** Long explanatory sections can be shortened so urgent electrical users can act faster.
   - Prototype response: Add a three-step next-step section and focused contact panel.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Make service request dominant.
2. Separate service paths clearly.
3. Elevate warranty and BBB proof.
4. Shorten the route to contact.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
