# Customer Service Experts — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Customer Service Experts is a strong fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current Wix site already has a recognizable visual fingerprint: the tiny support-logo mark, white navigation bar, bright modern-office hero image, large golden-yellow uppercase title, navy copy, yellow dedication band, booking cards, Johnson City / Tri-Cities positioning, and direct phone/email contact.

The opportunity is not to replace the brand. It is to keep the same consultant/training look while making the first screen answer buyer questions faster: what training is offered, who it is for, where it is available, what it costs, and how to schedule a class.

**Recommendation:** preserve the white-header / yellow / navy / office-consulting style, but rebuild the homepage around one dominant action: **Schedule a Training Class**.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The first screen says the business name but does not immediately explain the buyer outcome or next step.
   - Prototype response: keep the same office hero and yellow title, but add “Customer service training and business-performance consulting for Tri-Cities teams” plus a dominant **Schedule a Training Class** CTA.

2. **Critical:** The booking/contact path is split between Book Online buttons, a form, two emails, and two phone numbers without one visually dominant action.
   - Prototype response: make **Schedule a Training Class** the primary action, while keeping both emails and both phone numbers easy to find.

3. **High:** The current homepage hides useful service detail on separate pages and gives booking cards very little explanation.
   - Prototype response: keep the two booking cards, add short outcome-focused descriptions, and surface the three service-page categories on the homepage.

4. **High:** Trust/fit signals are present but not packaged for quick scanning.
   - Prototype response: add a top trust strip using only public claims: Johnson City, Tri-Cities area, 1-hour classes, $60 booking options, customized customer-service training, and business-performance consulting.

5. **High:** The page uses stock-consulting imagery well, but some sections feel unfinished or template-driven, including the blank gray booking-card image and Wix promotional/footer residue.
   - Prototype response: preserve the business-consulting image style, replace the blank visual slot with a public site image, and remove builder-residue language in the prototype.

6. **Medium:** The contact form is generic and long relative to the simple scheduling ask.
   - Prototype response: keep the form feel but simplify the surrounding copy around what to send: class goal, team size, preferred timing, and whether training or performance consulting is needed.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Add a clear first-screen promise for customer-service training and business-performance consulting in Johnson City / the Tri-Cities area.
- Make **Schedule a Training Class** the dominant CTA.
- Keep the original yellow/navy/white design and business-consulting photography.
- Surface the two public booking offers with useful descriptions: Customer Service Experts and Business Performance Consulting, both 1 hour / $60.
- Add a compact trust strip near the top using concrete public details.
- Bring the Services page categories onto the homepage so visitors can quickly self-select.
- Add a “what happens next” section so prospective clients know what to include when they reach out.
- Clean up Wix/template residue and dated footer copy in the customer-facing experience.
- Improve mobile scanning by shortening paragraphs, stacking cards, and repeating the primary CTA.

- Write a concise meta description around customer-service skills training, business-performance consulting, seminars, Johnson City, and the Tri-Cities area.
- Use one clear H1 that combines brand + service category, instead of relying only on the business name.
- Add descriptive H2s for Training Options, Services, About, What Happens Next, and Contact.
- Add descriptive alt text to the public stock/business images.
