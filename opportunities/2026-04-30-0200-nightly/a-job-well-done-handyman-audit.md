## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The first screen has contact details, a phone callout, address, service-area text, and service description, but the action path is not structured like a clear CTA.
   - Prototype response: Keep the phone-first identity, but turn the top right into a stronger call/email action panel with “Call for Maintenance Help” as the dominant action.

2. **Critical:** The Wix advertising banner and generic Wix URL weaken trust for a commercial maintenance business before visitors evaluate the service list.
   - Prototype response: Remove the Wix banner in the prototype and recommend a custom-domain/plan cleanup as part of implementation.

3. **High:** The service list is valuable but hard to scan because many small all-caps items are spread across columns with limited hierarchy.
   - Prototype response: Preserve the black service header grid, but group services into clearer categories with short bullet lists.

4. **High:** The long service-area paragraph is important but dense, so visitors may miss whether the business serves their city.
   - Prototype response: Move service-area proof into a concise yellow/black strip and a short “Western Upstate SC” block.

5. **High:** The site does not explain what a customer should provide when calling or emailing.
   - Prototype response: Add a simple “Before you call” section with location, issue, timing, and access details.

6. **Medium:** SEO basics are thin: the homepage title is generic (“HOME | handyman”), headings are repeated as large all-caps blocks, and the page lacks a concise local-service meta framing.
   - Prototype response: Use a specific title, meta description, one clear H1, and structured service headings.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Keep the memorable yellow/black/red contractor look while removing the Wix banner from the first impression.
- Make phone and email the obvious first actions for light commercial maintenance.
- Rework the dense service list into scannable service categories without losing the original categories.
- Put Western Upstate service-area coverage into a readable proof strip.
- Add a short “what to tell us when you call” section so commercial customers know how to start.
- Tighten SEO basics around light commercial maintenance, handyman repair, and Western Upstate SC.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Replace the generic page title with a service-and-location title, e.g. “Light Commercial Maintenance in Western Upstate SC | A Job Well Done Handyman.”
- Use one clear H1 and reserve H2s for service categories.
- Add a concise meta description that mentions light commercial maintenance, plumbing, drywall, painting, electrical, doors/locks, and Western Upstate SC.
- Add descriptive alt text to the public handyman illustration if retained.
- Consider LocalBusiness/Service schema after the final business details and service area are approved.
- Correct visible typos such as the duplicate “SC SC” and “GRAFFITTI” during implementation.

The highest-value implementation step would be a custom domain / Wix plan cleanup plus a revised homepage layout.
