## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The first screen is a dense all-caps paragraph, so the visitor has to work too hard to find the main action.
   - Prototype response: Preserve the text-first header but split it into a brand line, concise promise, service-area line, and clear call/text/email CTAs.

2. **Critical:** The visible Wix banner and Wix footer language make the business feel less established than its 20 years of experience suggests.
   - Prototype response: Remove Wix ad/template residue and replace it with a clean local-service header and footer.

3. **High:** The homepage says “Get In Touch,” but the strongest conversion action should be a free-estimate request with project basics.
   - Prototype response: Make **Request a Free Estimate** and **Call/Text 256-343-6261** the repeated primary actions.

4. **High:** Services are shown as long sentence lists beside photos, which makes it difficult to scan common repair categories quickly.
   - Prototype response: Group work into doors/windows/installations, drywall/painting/interior repairs, exterior/concrete/masonry, and honey-do/small jobs.

5. **High:** The site tells visitors to send project information, but the process is buried in the header paragraph.
   - Prototype response: Add a simple “Send the location, basics, photos if helpful” process block before the contact section.

6. **Medium:** SEO basics are weakened by older title wording, misspellings, inconsistent capitalization, and one-page heading hierarchy.
   - Prototype response: Use clearer title/meta/H1 structure and cleaner service-area phrasing.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Turn the dense top paragraph into a clear local handyman value proposition with direct estimate CTAs.
- Remove Wix banner/footer residue while preserving the familiar white/blue/orange site family.
- Bring 20 years of experience, insured status, free estimates, and direct call/text/email proof above the service list.
- Reframe service photos into buyer-ready categories that homeowners can scan in seconds.
- Add a practical “what to send” next-step section so the contact request feels easier.
- Use public review proof from the About page near the decision point.
