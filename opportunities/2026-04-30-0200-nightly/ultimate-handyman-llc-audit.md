# Ultimate Handyman, LLC — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Ultimate Handyman, LLC is a strong fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current site already has recognizable visual DNA, real project photos, a license number, direct contact information, and detailed testimonials. The opportunity is not to rebrand it. The opportunity is to make the first screen answer the buyer's immediate questions faster: what work Kyle does, where he works, why he can be trusted, and how to request an estimate.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The first useful page section starts with “Services & Skills,” but there is no clear homepage hero explaining the main customer promise, local service area, and best next step.
   - Prototype response: add a bright green first-screen hero that preserves the existing title-band style but states the value proposition and makes **Request a Free Estimate** the primary action.

2. **Critical:** Contact info exists, but the page does not make one estimate request path visually dominant.
   - Prototype response: keep email/phone visible in the header and add repeated estimate CTAs in the hero, service section, process section, and contact block.

3. **High:** The site has strong proof, but it is spread out in long sections; testimonials are dense and hard to scan quickly.
   - Prototype response: compress proof into a top trust strip and shorter testimonial cards while keeping the original testimonial language and names.

4. **High:** Services are useful but presented as long text rows before the visitor sees a clear “which job do you need?” scan path.
   - Prototype response: preserve the three main homepage service rows and add a compact supporting-services grid for painting, welding/fabrication, and quick repairs.

5. **High:** Project photos are valuable, but the page can make before/after-style proof easier to understand.
   - Prototype response: keep the same public project images and label them with clearer project outcomes.

6. **Medium:** Wix ad/footer residue and dated footer copy make the business feel less polished than the work described.
   - Prototype response: remove template-builder residue in the prototype and use a cleaner business-specific footer.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Add a clear homepage hero: “Home improvement you can trust in Vacaville, Solano & Yolo County.”
- Make **Request a Free Estimate** the primary CTA, with phone and email still easy to find.
- Move License #029203, service categories, and local focus into a compact trust strip near the top.
- Preserve the dark/green/blue section rhythm but reduce long paragraphs and empty spacing.
- Reframe services around buyer intent: remodels & upgrades, tile, custom projects, plus quick fixes/painting/fabrication.
- Turn long testimonials into short, scannable proof cards.
- Add a “what happens next” section so visitors know what to send before calling or emailing.
- Remove obvious Wix/template residue from the customer-facing page.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines service, location, and brand: `Handyman in Vacaville, CA | Ultimate Handyman, LLC`.
- Write a concise meta description around small remodels, tile, custom projects, and free estimates.
- Use one clear H1 on the homepage instead of starting with a generic services heading.
- Use H2 headings for Services, Past Projects, Testimonials, and Contact / Estimate.
- Add descriptive alt text for project images, using the existing public image topics.
- Add LocalBusiness / HomeAndConstructionBusiness schema after the final service area and business details are confirmed.
- If keeping the Wix site, remove or upgrade template/footer residue where the plan allows.
