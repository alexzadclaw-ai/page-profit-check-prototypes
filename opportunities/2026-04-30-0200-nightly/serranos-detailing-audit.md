# Serrano's Detailing — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Serrano's Detailing & Carpet Cleaning is a strong fit for a **Solution A surgical cleanup** because the current Wix site already has recognizable visual bones: dark patterned header, white/blue hero type, San Diego service-area copy, mobile detailing packages, starting prices, direct phone/email contact, and a real owner story.

The biggest opportunity is not a rebrand. It is to keep the same simple Wix-style identity while combining the homepage, service/pricing, floor-care, and booking signals into a clearer first-touch page with one obvious next step: **Request a Detailing Quote**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Brand/logo treatment:** Name-forward text treatment: “Serrano's Detailing & Carpet Cleaning.” No standalone logo was visible in the public page capture.
- **Dominant colors:** black, white, dark charcoal header, bright blue headline accents, lavender-blue accent text, and gray package panels.
- **Typography feel:** Wix Futura/DIN-style fonts, thin condensed headings, all-caps navigation, centered package headings.
- **Header/nav pattern:** dark textured horizontal header with HOME, SERVICES & PRICING, VISIT, ABOUT, BOOK ONLINE, phone CTA, and cart indicator.
- **Hero/image treatment:** soapy-car hero image with blue/white business-name overlay and “Serving San Diego and Surrounding Areas.”
- **Section rhythm:** sparse Wix blocks, large centered all-caps headings, three-column service/pricing cards, black Book Now buttons, black contact/footer area.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:** 100% satisfaction guarantee line, gray price panels, direct phone number, direct Gmail address, San Diego area, Chris owner story, mobile-service-at-your-doorstep framing.

## What is already working
- The business category is clear: mobile detailing, ceramic coatings, carpet cleaning, upholstery cleaning, tile cleaning, and engine bay detailing.
- The site includes direct contact options: (619) 919-2666 and SerranosDetailing@gmail.com.
- The site shows concrete starting prices for exterior, interior, full detail, coatings, carpet extraction, upholstery, and engine bay work.
- The homepage states the service area: San Diego and surrounding areas.
- The About page adds a personal trust signal by introducing Chris and his passion for detailing.
- The booking page already contains useful service durations and starting-price cards.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The homepage is mostly a hero image and brand name, so visitors do not immediately see the service scope or a specific quote path.
   - Prototype response: Keep the same hero image and brand treatment, but add a concise service summary plus “Request a Quote” and “Call Now” actions above the fold.

2. **Critical:** Service and pricing details are split across separate pages, while the mobile-detailing page starts with long package lists and repeated Book Now buttons without explaining what information to send.
   - Prototype response: Put the core packages, starting prices, and what-happens-next flow on one scannable page.

3. **High:** Trust signals are present but not assembled near the top. “Since 2023,” San Diego area, owner-led care, direct contact, and mobile service are scattered across pages.
   - Prototype response: Add a compact trust strip directly after the hero.

4. **High:** The service mix is broader than the mobile-detailing page suggests. Ceramic coatings and floor care are valuable, but they are hidden behind secondary pages.
   - Prototype response: Add a “More Services” block for ceramic coatings, carpet/upholstery/tile, and engine bay detailing while keeping the mobile-detailing packages primary.

5. **Medium:** Template residue and small errors weaken trust, including Wix promotional/footer residue and the footer typo “Deatailing.”
   - Prototype response: Remove Wix promotional residue from the page content and correct the business-name spelling in the footer.

6. **Medium:** SEO basics are thin on the mobile-detailing page title/metadata, which currently repeats “Mobile Detailing” instead of combining service, location, and brand.
   - Prototype response: Add a clear title, meta description, one H1, and descriptive section headings.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Make the first screen explain the full offer: mobile auto detailing, ceramic coatings, and floor/upholstery care in San Diego.
2. Add one clear quote path above the fold, supported by the existing phone number.
3. Keep the original package pricing but make it easier to compare exterior, interior, and full detail options.
4. Surface trust signals near the top: mobile service, San Diego area, direct contact, and owner-led since 2023.
5. Add a short “what happens after you book” section so customers know to share size, condition, location, stains, and preferred timing.
6. Clean up footer/template residue and spelling issues.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a title like: “Mobile Detailing & Carpet Cleaning in San Diego | Serrano's Detailing.”
- Add a meta description that mentions mobile auto detailing, ceramic coatings, upholstery, tile, carpet cleaning, San Diego, and quote intent.
- Use one H1 for the business name and H2s for mobile detailing, services/pricing, more services, process, about, and contact.
- Add descriptive alt text for the soapy-car and service-card images.
- Add LocalBusiness/AutoDetailing schema once final business address/service-area details are confirmed.
- Correct spelling consistency for “Detailing” across footer and page copy.

## Realistic implementation angle
This can be a light Wix cleanup rather than a rebuild. The owner can keep the existing site family, images, pricing, navigation, and brand feel while reorganizing the homepage so first-time visitors immediately understand the services, location, trust signals, and quote steps.
