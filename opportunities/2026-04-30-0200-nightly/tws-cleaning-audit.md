# TWS Cleaning — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
TWS Cleaning is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current Wix site already has recognizable local-service ingredients: a TWS Cleaning logo/name block, Harrison-area positioning, direct email and phone, office/school/medical cleaning intent, service photos for window/restroom/floor cleaning, a contact form, and a physical address.

The opportunity is not a rebrand. It is to keep the same simple, high-contrast Wix/Anton look while making the first screen say what TWS does, who it serves, and how a facility manager should request service.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Brand/logo treatment:** Small logo mark at top left, text lockup “TWS Cleaning,” and service category line: “Cleaning Service · Janitorial Service · Waxing Service.”
- **Dominant colors:** black and white, with large full-bleed cleaning imagery and a very simple no-color visual system.
- **Typography feel:** Heavy condensed Anton-style display headings; light Avenir/Helvetica-style navigation and contact text.
- **Header/nav pattern:** centered 940px-ish Wix layout with logo/name at left, Home / Book Online / More navigation, email, phone, and Facebook icon on the right.
- **Hero/image treatment:** large full-page window-cleaning image background with oversized centered display copy.
- **Section rhythm:** very tall sections, centered copy, huge display headings, wide 940px service images stacked vertically, sparse captions, and a dark contact/form area.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:** “TWS Cleaning is....,” “We do...Offices, Schools, Medical,” “Areas of Service,” Window Cleaning / Restroom Sanitation / Floor Cleaning, testimonial quote, contact form, Harrison AR address, direct Gmail, and phone number.

## What is already working
- The business category is visible: cleaning, janitorial, and waxing services.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The hero headline “TWS Cleaning is....” is recognizable but incomplete; it does not immediately say “commercial cleaning for offices, schools, and medical facilities in Harrison.”
   - Prototype response: Preserve the phrase, but add a clear commercial-cleaning headline and a specific quote/call path above the fold.

2. **Critical:** The primary CTA is generic (“Learn More” / “Contact Us”) instead of a buyer-action CTA for facility cleaning.
   - Prototype response: Add “Request a Cleaning Quote” and “Call 870-416-1073” as the top actions.

3. **High:** Trust and practical buying cues are scattered. Harrison area, office/school/medical service, direct phone/email, and satisfaction language appear separately.
   - Prototype response: Add a compact trust strip immediately after the hero.

4. **High:** Service sections list “Window Cleaning,” “Restroom Sanitation,” and “Floor Cleaning,” but do not explain what each service includes or who it is for.
   - Prototype response: Keep the same stacked service-photo rhythm while adding short facility-focused service descriptions.

5. **High:** The contact form is generic and does not prompt for the information needed to quote commercial cleaning work.
   - Prototype response: Keep the simple form style, but ask for facility type, service needs, address/area, schedule, and timing.

6. **Medium:** Template residue weakens trust, including the Wix banner and “Proudly created with Wix.com” footer residue.
   - Prototype response: Remove promotional Wix residue in the cleaned-up page while preserving the simple Wix-built look.

7. **Medium:** SEO basics are thin: the page title is “Home | TWS Cleaning,” and the page does not strongly connect commercial cleaning, janitorial service, Harrison, AR, and the service categories.
   - Prototype response: Add a service/location/brand title, meta description, one H1, and clearer H2 section structure.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Make the first screen explain the buyer value: commercial cleaning and janitorial service for offices, schools, and medical spaces around Harrison.
2. Replace vague CTAs with a clear quote path and phone call option.
3. Surface trust cues near the top: Harrison area, direct contact, facility cleaning categories, and satisfaction-focused service.
4. Keep the current service images and large heading style, but add short descriptions under each service.
5. Add a “what happens next” section so prospects know how the quote process works.
6. Make the contact form ask for the details TWS needs to respond usefully.
7. Remove public template residue and tighten SEO basics.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a title like: “Commercial Cleaning & Janitorial Service in Harrison, AR | TWS Cleaning.”
- Add a meta description mentioning office cleaning, janitorial service, schools, medical facilities, window cleaning, restroom sanitation, floor cleaning, Harrison, and quote intent.
- Use one H1 focused on commercial cleaning in Harrison.
- Use H2s for Areas of Service, What Happens Next, Testimonial, and Contact.
- Add descriptive alt text for service images.
- Add LocalBusiness / janitorial-service schema after confirming final service area, hours, and business details with the owner.

This can be a light Wix cleanup instead of a rebuild. The owner can keep the current logo, typography feel, large cleaning images, stacked service sections, contact information, and simple form while rewriting the homepage hierarchy and moving the quote path higher.
