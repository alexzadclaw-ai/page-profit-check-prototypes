## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The first screen has a good tagline, but the next step is not strong enough for a homeowner ready to call or request help.
   - The “Get A Quote” area is small and visually competes with the hero background and Wix/banner clutter.
   - Prototype response: keep the hero image and tagline, but add a clear local service promise plus dominant **Request a Free Quote** and **Call (949) 929-4878** actions.

2. **Critical:** Trust signals are too far down the page and too thin before the visitor reaches the service list.
   - A phone number and email are present, but service area, category breadth, “fair price” proof, and quote process are not organized near the top.
   - Prototype response: add a compact trust strip immediately after the hero: Orange County handyman service, repairs/installations/maintenance, phone/email contact, and fair-price/timely testimonial proof.

3. **High:** Services are specific, but the live section reads like small keyword fragments instead of a homeowner-friendly service story.
   - The current list includes useful tasks, but it is hard to scan because the copy is broken into many tiny items and some words are misspelled.
   - Prototype response: keep the six service cards and photos, but turn each into a short plain-English service card with grouped bullets.

4. **High:** Obvious spelling and copy issues reduce confidence.
   - Visible examples include **“MAINTENENCE,” “hrdwood,”** and the testimonial typo **“commited.”**
   - Prototype response: correct customer-facing spelling while preserving the original service meaning.

5. **High:** Wix/template residue and unused-looking social/newsletter elements make the business feel less polished.
   - The top Wix banner, repeated social icons, generic “Subscribe,” old blog counters, and template-like footer compete with the main service action.
   - Prototype response: remove builder residue from the customer-facing prototype, keep only useful navigation/contact/social-style cues, and replace newsletter clutter with a clearer quote/next-step block.

6. **High:** Blog content is old and not aligned with immediate service conversion.
   - The visible posts are from September 11, 2019 with low view/comment counts.
   - Prototype response: keep a small “Helpful home tips”/proof-style nod to the blog area, but move it below services and prevent it from competing with quote/contact actions.

7. **High:** Contact form flow is unclear and visually disconnected from the main quote promise.
   - The page has “Submit” and “Contact Us,” but it does not explain what homeowners should send or what happens next.
   - Prototype response: add a three-step process and a focused contact panel asking for name, contact info, project type, location, and project details.

8. **Medium:** SEO basics are underdeveloped.
   - The page title mentions handyman and Orange County, but the meta description is empty; H1 has a typo; headings are fragmented service keywords.
   - Prototype response: add a focused title/meta description, one clean H1, structured H2s, and descriptive image alt text.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Preserve the existing Flawless logo, orange/black identity, toolbench hero, dark service bands, and six service photos.
- Make **Request a Free Quote** the dominant CTA and repeat phone/email contact at the right decision points.
- Rewrite the hero as: **Home repairs, installations, and maintenance in Orange County** while preserving **Make Your Home Look New Again**.
- Move trust proof near the top: local Orange County positioning, direct phone/email, six service categories, and David S.’s fair-price/timely-work testimonial.
- Convert fragmented service lists into six easy cards with homeowner-friendly bullets.
- Correct visible spelling and copy issues.
- Reduce old blog/newsletter clutter so the page works harder as a service homepage.
- Add a simple “how to request a quote” section: describe the job, share the location/photos, get the next step.
- Clean up the footer/contact section so it feels business-specific instead of Wix/template-driven.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title like: `Orange County Handyman | Flawless Handyman Service`.
- Add a meta description: `Flawless Handyman Service helps Orange County homeowners with drywall, painting, electrical, carpentry, flooring, plumbing, repairs, installations, and maintenance. Request a free quote.`
- Use one clean H1: `Home Repairs, Installations & Maintenance in Orange County`.
- Use H2s for Services, What to Expect, Client Feedback, and Contact.
- Correct spelling in key headings: “maintenance,” “hardwood,” and “committed.”
- Add descriptive alt text to service images.
- If implementing on Wix, remove or suppress builder banner/footer residue where possible.
- Consider LocalBusiness/HomeAndConstructionBusiness schema after confirming business address/service area details.
