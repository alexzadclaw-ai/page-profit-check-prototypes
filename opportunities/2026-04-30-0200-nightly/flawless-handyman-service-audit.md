# Flawless Handyman Service — Page Profit Check Audit

Flawless Handyman Service is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the existing Wix site already has a recognizable black/orange handyman brand, a large home-repair hero image, direct phone/email, a quote form, and a broad service menu. ## What is already working
- The site has a clear business name and direct public contact paths: `flawlesshandyman@gmail.com` and `(949) 929-4878`.
- The hero promise is simple and usable: **Make your home look new again**.
- The page shows a broad service range: drywall, painting, electrical, carpentry, flooring, plumbing, TV mounting, and furniture assembly.
- The orange/black logo and orange headings create a memorable handyman visual identity.
- The service-card images quickly communicate the type of work offered.
- The site includes blog-style proof of handyman know-how and at least one testimonial quote.
- There is already a “Get A Quote” path, so the prototype can improve the conversion flow rather than invent one from scratch.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The top of the site is weakened by the Wix advertising banner and an unpolished first impression before the business message lands.
   - Prototype response: preserve the Wix-like white top banner/nav rhythm for recognition, but remove advertising copy and make the strip business-specific.

2. **Critical:** The main CTA is generic and form-like: “Get A Quote / Submit” appears without telling customers what to include or what happens next.
   - Prototype response: make **Request a Free Quote** the primary action in the hero, repeat it near services and contact, and add a simple three-step quote flow.

3. **Critical:** Visible spelling and copy issues hurt credibility, especially “HOME MAINTENENCE,” “commited,” “hrdwood,” and inconsistent capitalization.
   - Prototype response: correct customer-facing copy while keeping the original service categories and orange all-caps style.

4. **High:** The service list is useful but crowded; each card has many tiny bullets, making it hard for homeowners to quickly choose the right path.
   - Prototype response: preserve the six-card grid but group bullets into cleaner scan paths for drywall/painting, electrical/lighting, carpentry/doors, flooring/tile, plumbing/fixtures, and mounting/assembly.

5. **High:** Trust proof is too low and too thin; the only testimonial appears after a large blog area and does not support the quote CTA early.
   - Prototype response: add a compact trust strip directly under the hero and move the testimonial/proof into a clearer section before final contact.

6. **High:** Blog posts and newsletter signup interrupt the estimate path for a service buyer.
   - Prototype response: keep two blog/article cards as “Helpful home tips” proof, but shorten them and remove the newsletter distraction in favor of quote/contact.

7. **Medium:** Footer/contact areas are duplicated and slightly messy, with inconsistent casing and social links pointing to Wix defaults.
   - Prototype response: use one intentional contact section and a clean business-specific footer.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Keep the Flawless logo, dark hero, orange accent, and six service-card visual system.
- Correct spelling and visible trust-damaging copy.
- Change the hero subheading to “Home repairs · Home installations · Home maintenance.”
- Make **Request a Free Quote** and **Call (949) 929-4878** the top actions.
- Add a near-top trust strip: Orange County, six service groups, phone/email, fast quote path.
- Reorganize the dense service bullets into clearer homeowner categories.
- Add a “What happens next” quote-flow block: describe the job, send photos, schedule the repair.
- Keep blog/helpful tips as credibility proof, not as the main conversion path.
- Replace newsletter/template clutter with a clean contact CTA.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a specific title: `Handyman in Orange County | Flawless Handyman Service`.
- Add a meta description mentioning home repairs, installations, maintenance, drywall, painting, electrical, carpentry, flooring, plumbing, TV mounting, and quote requests.
- Use one clear H1 instead of relying on the misspelled service tagline as the main H1.
- Use H2s for Services, Helpful Home Tips, What Clients Say, and Contact / Quote.
- Add descriptive alt text for service and hero images.
- Add LocalBusiness/HomeAndConstructionBusiness schema after confirming exact service area, license/insurance language, and business address.
- Remove or upgrade Wix ad/banner residue if the live site remains on Wix.
