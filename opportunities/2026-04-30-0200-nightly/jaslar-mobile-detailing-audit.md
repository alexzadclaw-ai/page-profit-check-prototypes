# Jaslar Mobile Detailing — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Jaslar Mobile Detailing is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup**. The current Wix site already has a recognizable clean-detailing look, real package/pricing content, direct phone/email contact, and strong mobile-service positioning. The main opportunity is to make the quote path clearer, move trust details closer to the hero, and organize the services so visitors can pick the right package faster.

**Recommendation:** preserve the existing white/navy Wix visual family, hero/detailing imagery, split service layout, star package markers, and simple contact tone while rebuilding the first-screen CTA around **Call 267-279-0601 for a Quote**.

## What is already working
- Clear business name and service category: Jaslar Mobile Detailing.
- Strong public contact paths: jaslardetailing@gmail.com and 267-279-0601.
- The site lists practical buyer-intent packages: Basic, Full, Express, Interior, Ceramic Coating, and Paint Correction.
- The mobile value proposition is useful: Lower Bucks service, customer provides time/place, and Jaslar brings water and electricity.
- The existing look is recognizable: white header, dark navy Poppins-style headings, large dashboard/detailing hero image, grey translucent hero band, and two-column service/image rhythm.

## Biggest conversion issues
1. **Critical:** The hero CTA says only “Contact,” while the strongest real action is buried as “Call 267-279-0601 for a quote today.”
   - Prototype response: Make “Call 267-279-0601 for a Quote” the primary hero CTA and repeat it in contact.
2. **High:** The mobile-service proof appears in the “What We Do” paragraph below long package content instead of near the first decision point.
   - Prototype response: Add top trust strip for “We Come To You,” “Own Water & Power,” and “Quote By Phone.”
3. **High:** Services are real but dense; some package details are hard to scan and spelling/formatting issues reduce polish.
   - Prototype response: Keep the same package names/prices while tightening copy into scannable cards.
4. **High:** Contact section repeats phone/email without explaining what details to provide.
   - Prototype response: Add a simple three-step next-step section and a contact card explaining what to share.
5. **Medium:** Wix banner/footer residue is visible and dates the site, but preserving it lightly helps the high-fidelity prototype stay recognizable.
   - Prototype response: Keep a minimal Wix-style top/footer cue but make the business CTA hierarchy cleaner.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Promote the phone quote CTA in the hero and contact sections.
- Keep the recognizable hero photo/grey overlay while making the value proposition more specific.
- Move mobile-service trust proof near the service packages.
- Clean up service-card formatting, spelling, and package descriptions.
- Add “what happens next” so visitors know how to request a quote.

## SEO basics to tighten

This is not a rebrand. It is a focused Wix homepage cleanup: keep the existing images, white/navy style, simple navigation, service packages, and contact information, then improve CTA hierarchy, package scannability, proof placement, and contact expectations.
