# Page Profit Check audit - Green Queen Eco Clean LLC

## Quick take
Green Queen Eco Clean LLC already has the basics that matter for a local cleaning lead: a clear business name, visible email, phone number, Wix quote form, contact form, eco-friendly positioning, and real cleaning/carpet imagery. The biggest opportunity is to move the strongest trust and service details higher so a visitor understands the eco-cleaning value before they hit the long form.

## What is already working
- Clear eco-friendly cleaning angle in the business name and copy.
- Email and phone are visible near the top: greenqueenecocleanllc@gmail.com and 253-326-3787.
- The page includes both a quote form and a contact form.
- Real cleaning, kitchen, product, and carpet images give the page a local-service feel.
- The existing minimalist white, black, cream, and green look is simple and easy to preserve.

## Biggest conversion issues
- The first major action is a long quote form before the visitor gets service proof or reasons to trust the company.
- Natural products, steam, essential oils, and limited chemical use are valuable differentiators, but they are buried in the lower about copy.
- Services are written in paragraph form instead of a quick scan-friendly list.
- Carpet shampooing, stain removal, upholstery cleaning, and carpet spot dyeing are strong specialty services but not surfaced early.
- The phone number is not formatted for quick scanning and the contact path feels split across two different forms.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Keep the existing Wix look, but add a stronger first-screen headline and service promise above the quote form.
- Turn cleaning frequency and specialty services into clear cards: recurring house cleaning, move-in/move-out, carpet and upholstery, stain removal.
- Add trust cues near the CTA: eco-friendly approach, steam and essential oils, Tacoma/Pierce County service area, direct phone/email.
- Keep the original photos but use them to support the service story instead of letting them sit as isolated decorative blocks.
- Make the quote form feel like a natural next step after the service/value explanation.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a clearer page title and H1 around eco-friendly cleaning in Tacoma / Pierce County.
- Add service terms in visible headings: house cleaning, move-in cleaning, carpet shampooing, upholstery cleaning, stain removal.
- Format phone and email consistently and add location/service-area language near the top.
- Add alt text for the cleaning, kitchen, product, and carpet images.

## Realistic implementation angle
A focused Wix cleanup could keep the current site and reorganize the first screen, quote section, service cards, contact area, and about proof without rebuilding everything. A realistic rough implementation range is about $500 to $1,000 depending on whether the forms and mobile layout need deeper Wix edits.

## Offer fit
This is a good fit for a $99 Page Profit Check because the business does not need a full brand reinvention. It needs a prioritized diagnosis and visual before/after direction showing how to make the current Wix page feel more trustworthy, faster to understand, and easier to request a quote from.
