# Home and Beyond Cleaning Services, LLC - Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take

Home and Beyond Cleaning Services already has several important basics in place: a real founder story, insured language, phone, email, Facebook link, a quote form, a Google review link, and a broad service list for residential and commercial cleaning. The highest-impact opportunity is not a full redesign. It is a first-screen cleanup that makes trust and the quote path obvious before visitors scroll through the long story and service list.

The live site palette is lime green, burgundy, black, and white. The prototype follows the inspected live site rather than the earlier white-and-blue summary.

## What is already working

- Clear local small-business identity with the business name visible in the header.
- Phone and email appear near the top of the page.
- Strong, human founder story around Adam's restaurant background and attention to detail.
- Good trust cue with "We're Insured!" already present.
- Service breadth is substantial: make readies, construction/remodel cleaning, vacation rentals, deep cleaning, bulk trash, offices, gyms, windows, and custom work.
- Real quote form already exists and asks for useful contact details.
- Contact section gives owner/operator name, address, email, phone, and Facebook.
- Source site has enough original imagery and logo assets to improve the page without adding generic stock photography.

## Biggest conversion issues

- The first screen leads mostly with a slogan and logo. It does not immediately ask for the quote or call.
- "We're Insured!" is valuable trust proof, but it appears after the about section instead of near the first decision point.
- The service list is very long vertically. Visitors must scroll a lot before reaching the quote form.
- Service names are visible, but there is little explanation of what each service includes.
- The quote path is too low on the page, after a long story and many service image blocks.
- Form fields are small and the submit button is visually quiet compared with the importance of the action.
- The review link appears as a raw URL instead of a clear review button.
- The contact information is repeated but not packaged as fast actions like call, text, email, and quote.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements

- Add a strong hero CTA: "Get a Walk-through Quote" plus a secondary phone CTA.
- Move insured proof and service scope into a small hero trust badge.
- Keep the founder story, but make it easier to skim with larger text and a short trust strip.
- Keep the recognizable Wix layout, lime green header, white hero, green about block, image service banner, and burgundy quote/contact area.
- Convert the long service stack into consistent service cards with image, title, short explanation, and quote CTA.
- Add a short "Need it fast?" callout near the service list with phone action.
- Strengthen the quote section with call/text and email options next to the form.
- Turn the raw Google review URL into a button.

## SEO basics to tighten

- Page title should include the main service area and core offering, for example: "Home and Beyond Cleaning Services | Bryan College Station Cleaning".
- Add a concise meta description mentioning insured cleaning, Bryan/College Station, and quote requests.
- Use one clear H1 that combines service and location, while keeping the existing slogan as support text.
- Add short service descriptions for each cleaning category so search engines understand the breadth.
- Add internal anchor links that land on About, Services, Quote, and Contact.
- Add local business schema with name, phone, email, address, service area, and sameAs Facebook URL.
- Make phone links clickable with `tel:` and email links clickable with `mailto:` consistently.
- Replace the raw review link with descriptive linked text such as "Leave Us a Google Review".

## Realistic implementation angle

This should be a light Wix page tune-up, not a brand rebuild. The site already has the right small-business feel, colors, photos, and content. A realistic implementation would adjust the top section, add CTA buttons, tighten the about copy, restructure services into consistent cards, improve form contrast, and polish contact/review actions.

Estimated implementation scope after a paid audit: roughly $300 to $900 depending on whether it is a single-page Wix content/layout pass or includes SEO schema, mobile polish, and form testing.
