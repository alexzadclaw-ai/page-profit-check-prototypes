# Serio Home Services LLC Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take

Serio Home Services LLC already has the essentials for a handyman site: a visible phone number, email address, service categories, Las Vegas location, large home-repair imagery, and multiple calls to action. The biggest opportunity is not a full redesign. It is to make the first screen more specific and trust-building so visitors can quickly understand what jobs are handled and how to request a free estimate.

## What is already working

- Phone and email are visible in the header, hero, service sections, and footer.
- The site has a simple owner-led handyman feel rather than an overbuilt agency template.
- The dark navy/purple background, bright blue CTAs, and large home-service imagery create a recognizable visual direction.
- Core services are named clearly: drywall repairs, home repair and maintenance, TV wall mounting, and shelf installation.
- The contact section includes a form, direct email, phone number, location, and hours language.

## Biggest conversion issues

- The hero headline is brand-heavy but does not immediately answer the visitor's practical question: "Can this handyman handle my job near Las Vegas?"
- The first screen has phone/email buttons, but no quick service list, response cue, or estimate flow cue to reduce hesitation.
- Calls to action repeat as generic "Get in Touch," "Email Me," and "Submit," which are less persuasive than a free-estimate path.
- Trust signals are thin. The page does not surface owner-led service, local service area, availability, or what to send for a quote until lower on the page.
- Service sections are visually strong but copy is generic and could be more scan-friendly.
- The footer and contact form are functional, but the form button does not reinforce the visitor's goal.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements

- Keep the existing dark Wix-style layout, header, hero split, service image panels, and footer structure.
- Add compact service cards in the first screen for drywall, home repair, and TV mounting/shelving.
- Add small trust cues near the primary CTAs: owner-led service, Las Vegas area, and M-F 8am-8pm availability.
- Make the free-estimate action more explicit in the hero, services intro, and contact form.
- Tighten service copy so each panel says what the visitor should send or do next.
- Keep both click-to-call and email options prominent because handyman leads often convert through the fastest available contact method.

## SEO basics to tighten

- Add a meta description focused on handyman services in the Las Vegas area.
- Make the hero and service headings include practical search terms such as handyman, drywall repair, TV mounting, shelving, home repair, and maintenance.
- Use descriptive image alt text where possible. The live site already has useful basic alt text for the image assets.
- Add a concise service-area cue near the top of the page, not only in the footer.
- Make phone and email links consistently clickable with clean `tel:` and `mailto:` targets.

## Realistic implementation angle

This should be positioned as a surgical homepage improvement, not a rebuild. A realistic implementation would preserve the Wix page's current rhythm and assets, then revise the hero copy, add a compact service/trust block, improve CTA language, and adjust the contact form button. If implemented separately after the audit, this would likely be a small Wix/homepage content and layout pass rather than a full site replacement.

## Offer fit

This is a good fit for a $99 Page Profit Check because the site is not broken. It already has real contact information, service descriptions, and usable imagery. The value is in diagnosing the few highest-impact changes and showing a visual before/after direction for the first impression, trust cues, and quote path. Implementation should be offered separately from the $99 audit.
