# Industry Pros Pressure Washing LLC Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take

Industry Pros Pressure Washing LLC already has the essentials a local service page needs: a business name, phone number, email address, physical address, opening hours, service cards, book buttons, a quote form, and pressure-washing imagery. The biggest opportunity is not a total redesign. It is a focused first-impression pass that removes Wix/template leftovers, clarifies the Charlotte service offer, and turns the scattered contact options into one obvious quote path.

## What is already working

- Phone, email, address, and hours are visible on the page.
- The site has a recognizable blue pressure-washing logo and several relevant washing photos.
- Services are already separated into bookable items, including deck washing, house washing, driveway cleaning, and vehicle washing.
- The current page has a quote form with name, email, phone, and comments fields.
- The simple Wix layout can be improved without changing the overall brand feel.

## Biggest conversion issues

- The first meaningful action is a generic Subscribe Form instead of a quote or call path.
- The Wix promotional strip, Wix social placeholder links, Wix footer credit, placeholder testimonials, and generic quote copy make the business look unfinished.
- Pricing/service copy says “we charge per sq in.” twice on every service card. That looks accidental and probably should be quote-based or surface-size-based.
- Contact details exist, but they are separated from the services and the final form, so visitors have to piece together what to do next.
- Service labels have small wording issues, such as “Soft Home Washing” instead of “Soft House Washing.”
- Some service images are less relevant than the strongest pressure-washing assets already on the site.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements

- Replace the generic subscribe block with “Get a Fast Charlotte Quote,” while keeping the same simple Wix geometry.
- Add a top contact strip with the phone number so mobile and desktop visitors immediately see how to reach the business.
- Keep Business Details near the top, but make phone, email, address, hours, and service area more scannable.
- Change hero copy from only the company name to company name plus a clear service/quote message.
- Group the four services into clear cards with quote-based language instead of repeated “sq in.” copy.
- Replace placeholder testimonials with a clear recommendation to add real customer quotes or job notes, rather than fake praise.
- Strengthen the final quote section with action-oriented copy and specific instructions about the surface needing cleaning.

## SEO basics to tighten

- Set a descriptive title tag, for example: “Industry Pros Pressure Washing LLC | Charlotte, NC.”
- Add a meta description mentioning Charlotte, deck power washing, house washing, driveway cleaning, vehicle washing, and exterior pressure washing.
- Use one strong H1 with the business name and a local service phrase.
- Use service headings that match search intent: Deck Power Washing, Soft House Washing, Driveway Cleaning, Vehicle and Exterior Washing.
- Add local context around Charlotte, NC and the address at 2227 Bancroft Street.
- Replace template social links that point to Wix with real business profiles or remove them.

## Realistic implementation angle

This is a surgical Wix pass, not a rebuild. The brand can keep the current logo, white space, blue pressure-washing direction, image bands, business-details block, service cards, and quote form. The implementation would mainly be copy replacement, section ordering, CTA linking, better service grouping, real testimonial content, and basic SEO fields.

A realistic separate implementation estimate after the $99 audit would likely be in the $300 to $700 range if it is limited to a focused Wix page update. More would be needed if new photos, review gathering, booking system setup, or multi-page SEO work is included.

## Offer fit

This is a strong Page Profit Check fit because the page has enough real business information to diagnose and enough visible template friction to make the before/after direction obvious. The $99 audit can show the highest-impact changes without promising implementation.
