# Christelle Cleaning Services LLC website audit

Business: Christelle Cleaning Services LLC  
URL: https://christellecleaning.wixsite.com/website-2  
Location: Marshfield / South Shore, MA  
Vertical: office cleaning, house cleaning, and move-in or move-out cleaning

## Quick take

Christelle Cleaning Services already has the important basics that many small cleaning sites miss: live online booking, visible service options, price ranges, a contact form, email, phone numbers, customer comments, and a Marshfield address. The main opportunity is not a full redesign. It is a surgical cleanup of the current Wix service template so first-time visitors immediately understand what to book, why the company is local and reachable, and how to request the right cleaning.

## What is already working

- Clear business name and simple tagline: "Always at Your Service."
- Online booking is available from the main navigation and service cards.
- Three core services are already named: Office Cleaning, House Cleaning, and Move in/out Cleaning.
- Price ranges are visible on the cards, which helps reduce uncertainty.
- The site has a contact form, email address, two phone numbers, and a Marshfield mailing address.
- The blue and white palette is clean and service-appropriate.
- The hero, about image, service photos, reviews, contact section, and footer already provide a complete one-page flow.

## Biggest conversion issues

- The first screen has no strong booking or quote CTA near the hero message.
- The Wix promotional banner and Wix footer language make the business feel less polished.
- The navigation has too many equal-weight items, so booking and quote actions do not stand out.
- The service cards list duration and price but do not explain what is included or when a quote is better.
- "1 hr" for move-in or move-out cleaning can create unrealistic expectations.
- Reviews are useful but very thin, anonymous, and not connected to specific services or towns.
- The contact form is generic. It does not ask for service type, timing, ZIP/town, frequency, or home/office size.
- Local trust is buried in the footer instead of appearing above the fold.
- Footer social links appear to be Wix default/social placeholder links rather than verified business profiles.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements

- Add a top contact/credibility strip that says the business serves Marshfield and the South Shore, with a direct phone CTA.
- Keep the existing hero photo and centered title, but add "Book Online" and "Get a Quote" buttons below the tagline.
- Rewrite the about block so it still feels like the existing Wix copy but states the practical value: local team, clear communication, booking/quote path.
- Make each service card answer "what is this for?" in one sentence while preserving the original images, prices, and Book Now rhythm.
- For move-in or move-out cleaning, shift the CTA toward "Get a Quote" so the larger job feels properly scoped.
- Upgrade the reviews section by keeping the current customer quotes but presenting them as cleaner testimonial cards.
- Make the contact section quote-oriented with fields for cleaning type and preferred date.
- Replace the blank-feeling map area with a local service-area block that reinforces Marshfield and South Shore coverage.

## SEO basics to tighten

- Use one clear H1 that includes the business name and cleaning service context.
- Add page title and meta description around "Marshfield cleaning service," "South Shore house cleaning," "office cleaning," and "move-out cleaning."
- Use service-specific H2s and short descriptions for each cleaning package.
- Add descriptive alt text to the logo, hero, about image, and service photos.
- If the business has a real Google Business Profile or social profiles, link those instead of Wix default social URLs.
- Add local business schema with business name, address, phone, email, service area, and sameAs links once verified.
- Consider separate service pages later if they want to rank for office cleaning, recurring house cleaning, and move-out cleaning individually.

## Realistic implementation angle

This is a good fit for a focused Wix homepage pass. The site should keep the recognizable template, imagery, section order, and blue-white palette. The implementation work would be mostly copy, CTA hierarchy, service-card detail, form fields, footer cleanup, and replacing placeholder/default links. No new photo sourcing is needed unless the business wants stronger real-team or before/after proof.

A realistic first implementation range after the audit would likely be around $500 to $900 for a focused Wix homepage pass, depending on access, form/booking constraints, and whether verified reviews/social/profile links are ready.

## Offer fit

The $99 Page Profit Check fits because the business already has enough structure to diagnose quickly and show a before/after direction. The value is identifying which small changes would most improve first impression, trust, and contact/booking flow before anyone pays for implementation.
