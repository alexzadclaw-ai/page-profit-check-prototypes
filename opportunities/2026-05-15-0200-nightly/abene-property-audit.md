# Abene Property LLC Page Profit Check Audit

Live URL inspected: https://abenepropertyprese.wixsite.com/abenepropertyllc  
Business: Abene Property LLC  
Vertical: landscape, lawn, snow removal, and year-round outdoor property maintenance  
Primary conversion action: free estimate request or phone call

## Quick take

Abene already has the essentials a local service page needs: a real logo, visible email and phone number, a quote page, suburb coverage, year-round property maintenance positioning, and a deep service list. The biggest opportunity is not a full redesign. It is making the first impression easier to understand and easier to act on.

The cleaned-up prototype keeps the same Wix-style structure, green landscaping palette, hero image, logo, header/nav, second image, and footer contact pattern, but moves the estimate path, service area, year-round positioning, and scan-friendly services higher on the page.

## What is already working

- The business name and logo are visible in the header.
- Email and phone number appear near the top and again in the footer.
- The site already has a dedicated Get a Quote page with form fields.
- The page clearly says Abene provides year-round property maintenance.
- The copy includes useful local coverage: NW Chicago, Park Ridge, Des Plaines, Niles, and surrounding suburbs.
- The live page lists strong service depth, including lawn care, landscaping, snow removal, cleanups, power washing, gutter cleaning, window washing, tree trimming, mulch, rock, dirt installation, and bobcat services.
- The existing garden and backyard images fit the landscaping category and should be reused.

## Biggest conversion issues

- The first screen has no large, obvious free-estimate CTA. “Get a Quote” exists in the nav, but it is small and easy to miss.
- The best trust proof is hidden inside long paragraph copy, especially the local service area, “since 2012,” year-round work, and service breadth.
- Decorative script-style body copy is hard to scan, especially over the hero photo.
- The services section reads like one dense list instead of grouped, visitor-friendly proof.
- Contact information is present, but the page does not clearly tell visitors what to do next after reading.
- The Wix banner and Wix subdomain reduce trust compared with a custom domain and no third-party banner.
- The page title is generic, “Home | Abene Property LLC,” and does not use the strongest local service keywords.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements

- Keep the same header/nav/logo layout, but make the quote path more obvious with a hero-level “Get a Free Estimate” button and a phone CTA.
- Add a first-screen service area pill: NW Chicago, Park Ridge, Des Plaines, and Niles.
- Replace the long hero paragraph with a shorter value statement focused on lawn care, landscaping, cleanups, snow removal, and all-season maintenance.
- Pull the most important services into quick visual chips above the fold.
- Rework the lower services section into scan-friendly bullets while preserving the original “More About Us & The Services We Offer” section order.
- Repeat contact options in the section CTA and footer.
- Keep the source images so the page still feels like Abene’s current site, not a generic agency template.

## SEO basics to tighten

- Use a stronger title tag, such as “Abene Property LLC | Lawn, Landscape & Snow Removal in NW Chicago.”
- Add a concise meta description with year-round maintenance, lawn care, landscaping, snow removal, and local suburbs.
- Use one clear H1 that includes the brand and core service category.
- Add descriptive image alt text for the logo, garden hero photo, and backyard photo.
- Consider LocalBusiness or HomeAndConstructionBusiness schema with phone, email, service area, and social links.
- Clean up the keyword meta content, which currently appears broken or low-value.
- Link the quote page from multiple relevant CTAs, not only the top navigation.

## Realistic implementation angle

This should be a focused Wix homepage improvement, not a full rebuild. The practical path is to keep the current structure and assets, then update the hero copy, add a more prominent free-estimate CTA, group the services, and make the quote/contact path clearer.

A realistic implementation would likely be a small one-page Wix edit plus copy restructuring. If Abene also wants the Wix banner removed and a custom domain connected, that is a separate trust improvement that depends on their Wix plan and domain setup.

## Offer fit

This is a good fit for a $99 Page Profit Check because the site already has strong ingredients but needs prioritization and visual direction. The $99 offer should stay framed as audit and diagnosis only: review the page, identify highest-impact fixes, and show a before/after direction.

Implementation should be sold separately. A focused Wix homepage implementation would likely land around $400 to $900 depending on final copy, form routing, mobile cleanup, and whether custom domain or Wix plan work is included.
