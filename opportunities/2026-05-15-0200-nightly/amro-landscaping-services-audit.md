# Amro LLC Lawncare/Landscaping Services Page Profit Check audit

## Quick take

Amro LLC already has the important basics for a local lawn and landscaping site: Des Moines area language, residential and commercial positioning, a visible phone number, email link, a free-estimate form, and real outdoor service imagery. The main opportunity is not a full redesign. It is a tighter first screen that tells visitors faster where Amro works, what services are available, and how to request a free estimate.

## What is already working

- The page has a calm, professional landscaping feel with white space, navy service blocks, light gray content panels, and outdoor photos.
- Contact information is visible near the top and again near the bottom.
- The page already says Amro serves Des Moines and surrounding areas.
- Residential and commercial audiences are both mentioned.
- Core service categories are present: lawn care, landscaping, retaining walls, borders, flower beds, fall cleaning, spring cleaning, trimming, branches, leaves, weed solutions, and planting.
- A Request a Free Estimate section exists on the page.
- The Wix layout is recognizable and consistent, so conversion fixes can be surgical rather than a rebuild.

## Biggest conversion issues

- The live page title is just "Home," which misses lawn care, landscaping, and local search intent.
- The first screen starts with a Wix promo banner and a quiet brand block instead of a direct local value proposition.
- The strongest CTA, Request a Free Estimate, appears too far down the page.
- The header phone number is visible but not treated as a clear call action.
- The navigation only has Home and About Us, so visitors do not get quick paths to Services, Estimate, or Contact.
- The estimate form still includes placeholder Wix copy, which weakens trust at the moment of conversion.
- Service descriptions are useful but generic, and they do not repeat the Des Moines service-area context.
- The large blue free-estimate section is visually memorable but oversized and not connected clearly enough to the quote path.
- The footer still says the site was created with Wix, which makes the business feel less polished.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements

- Replace the Wix promo strip with a local service-area and free-estimate strip while preserving the same top-of-page geometry.
- Keep the text-logo header and contact-first feel, but update the third brand line to make the free-estimate promise immediate.
- Add Services and Get Estimate navigation items so visitors have a clear next step without scrolling.
- Reframe the first gray About panel as a first-screen local landscaping message with a call button and free-estimate button.
- Keep the same About image, light gray panel, navy service blocks, and section order so the prototype feels like the same site cleaned up.
- Rewrite the estimate intro so it asks for property location, needed service, and preferred contact path instead of showing placeholder copy.
- Keep the contact card and footer, but make phone and email actions more consistent.

## SEO basics to tighten

- Change the homepage title from "Home" to a descriptive title such as "Amro LLC Lawncare/Landscaping Services | Des Moines Free Estimates."
- Add a meta description mentioning Des Moines, Clive, lawn care, landscaping, retaining walls, spring cleanup, and fall cleanup.
- Use one clear H1 that includes a service and location phrase, for example "Des Moines Landscaping."
- Add local service-area language near the top of the page, not only in the About paragraph.
- Use descriptive image alt text for the garden, lawn mower, backyard, leaves, and hardscape photos.
- Make the phone number a consistent `tel:` link and the email a consistent `mailto:` link.
- Consider a later FAQ section for retaining walls, seasonal cleanup timing, recurring lawn care, and residential versus commercial estimates.

## Realistic implementation angle

This is a focused Wix homepage tune-up, not a new site build. Amro can keep the same one-page flow, text-logo treatment, serif navy headings, light gray About block, three service rows, wide landscaping photo, estimate form, blue free-estimate banner, and contact card. The work is mainly copy hierarchy, CTA placement, local SEO basics, form copy, and small header/navigation improvements.

## Offer fit

This is a strong fit for a $99 Page Profit Check because the page already has enough useful content and imagery to diagnose what is holding conversions back. The audit can show how to clarify the first impression, trust signals, service-area language, and contact path. If Amro wants implementation in Wix afterward, that should be estimated separately from the $99 audit.
