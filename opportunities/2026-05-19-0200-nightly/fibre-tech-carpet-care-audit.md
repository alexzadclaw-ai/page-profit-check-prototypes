# Fibre Tech Carpet Care Ltd Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take

Fibre Tech Carpet Care Ltd already has the foundation of a credible local service page: a clean teal palette, real service imagery, visible email/contact forms, and a long-experience message. The biggest opportunity is to make the first screen more useful for a Victoria homeowner who is ready to book: clarify what services are offered, use one consistent phone number, and replace template copy with practical estimate instructions.

## What is already working

- The page has a calm, recognizable visual system with teal text, thin-line forms, pale mint sections, and large carpet/rug imagery.
- The business name and email are visible near the top of the page.
- The site includes several lead paths: Book Online navigation, contact form, free estimate form, email, phone, and chat-style CTA.
- The existing copy communicates reliability, communication, and over 36 years of industry experience.
- The page already has useful sections for About, Contact, Reviews, Free Estimate, and Footer.
- The service imagery feels related to carpet, upholstery, and home cleaning instead of random generic construction imagery.

## Biggest conversion issues

- The above-the-fold hero uses a tall image on the left and leaves a lot of empty white space on the right, so the most valuable screen area does not explain services or next steps.
- The page title and tagline say carpet cleaning, but the visible hero does not quickly list carpet cleaning, rug cleaning, upholstery, stain treatment, or service area details.
- The phone number is inconsistent: the header shows `250 532 3801`, while the contact and footer sections show `778 433 9348`. This should be verified before any implementation.
- The Free Estimate section still includes template placeholder copy: “This is your Price Quote section paragraph.” That hurts trust right before the visitor is asked to submit details.
- “Victoria's #1 Carpet Cleaning Service” is a strong claim, but there is no proof nearby such as reviews, rating, years served, photos, or a guarantee.
- The Contact section overlays form fields on a busy image, which makes some text and inputs harder to read.
- The page asks visitors to add a review before it has fully strengthened the sales/booking path.
- The Vista x Wix banner and Wix footer language make the business feel less finished than the service likely is.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements

- Keep the teal/mint identity, thin form style, real logo, and existing imagery so the page still feels like Fibre Tech.
- Use the blank right side of the hero for a clear service and estimate CTA card.
- Rewrite the hero to mention Victoria, carpet cleaning, rug cleaning, upholstery care, stain/spot treatment, and the 36 years of experience.
- Make the primary contact action obvious: call, email, or request a free estimate.
- Replace placeholder estimate copy with instructions that tell customers what to send: room count, material, stain concerns, photos, address/service area, and preferred timing.
- Verify and standardize the phone number across header, contact section, and footer.
- Add trust cues near the conversion path: established 2010, 36 years of experience, clear communication, careful home service, and review prompts.
- Improve contrast in the contact section by using darker text backing or a softer overlay behind the form.

## SEO basics to tighten

- Use a title like “Fibre Tech Carpet Care Ltd | Carpet Cleaning in Victoria BC.”
- Make the visible H1 or hero headline mention the core service and location, for example “Victoria Carpet Cleaning by Fibre Tech.”
- Add body copy for carpet cleaning, rug cleaning, upholstery cleaning, stain removal, and Victoria-area service.
- Add descriptive alt text for the logo, technician/carpet images, rug image, and interior image.
- Replace template copy before publishing any changes.
- If Wix allows it later, add LocalBusiness schema with business name, verified phone, email, service area, and social links.

## Realistic implementation angle

This is a surgical Wix refresh, not a rebuild:

1. Keep the logo, teal palette, mint sections, image stack, contact form, review form, free estimate form, and footer layout.
2. Use the empty hero space for service clarity and a stronger estimate CTA.
3. Clean up the estimate copy and contact instructions.
4. Standardize the phone number after the owner confirms which number is correct.
5. Add compact trust and service-area wording without changing the site into a generic contractor template.

## Offer fit

This is a good fit for a $99 Page Profit Check because the page has enough structure and assets already. The value is in diagnosing the conversion friction and showing a visual direction that keeps the existing Fibre Tech style while making the page easier to trust and use.
