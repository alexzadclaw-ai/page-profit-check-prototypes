# Fitzgerald Lawn Solutions Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take

The live Services page has a good foundation: Fitzgerald Lawn Solutions already shows its logo, phone, email, address, social links, service descriptions, and service photos. The main conversion issue is speed. A homeowner landing on the first screen has to read a long paragraph or scroll before quickly confirming mowing, hedge trimming, cleanup, mulch/pine straw, and bed work. The best Page Profit Check direction is a surgical Wix cleanup that keeps the same white, green, and black look while making the service menu and free-estimate path easier to scan immediately.

## What is already working

- The business name, logo, phone number, email, address, Facebook, and Instagram are visible on the page.
- The green Free Estimate button is prominent in the header and hero.
- The page is focused on one clear service category: lawn care and landscape maintenance around Fitzgerald.
- The six core services are specific and useful: lawn maintenance, shrub/hedge trimming, leaf and pine needle removal, property cleanup, mulch and pine straw installation, and garden bed defining.
- Each service has a dedicated image and a short explanation.
- The alternating black and green split-section pattern gives the page a recognizable Wix design system.
- The footer repeats the contact details and address, which is helpful for local trust.

## Biggest conversion issues

- The first screen has too much empty white space before the service rows begin, so visitors do not immediately see the full service range.
- The hero paragraph is useful, but it is a wall of text instead of a quick scan path for mowing, cleanup, hedge trimming, mulch, pine straw, and free estimates.
- The Free Estimate button appears to link back to the home page rather than opening a clear estimate request path.
- Phone and email are visible, but they are small in the gray nav/contact strip and not reinforced near the main hero CTA.
- The service buttons all say “Learn More >>,” which is fine for browsing but less direct for estimate-ready visitors.
- The page has a spelling issue: “LAWN MAINTENENCE” and “Lawn Maintenence” should be “LAWN MAINTENANCE” and “Lawn maintenance.”
- Fitzgerald, GA appears in the footer address but not in the page title or first-screen conversion message.
- The service rows are long and repetitive, so the first screen needs to do more work before the visitor scrolls.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements

- Keep the same logo, header, nav strip, white hero, green CTA, alternating service rows, images, and footer, but make the top section more useful.
- Add a compact first-screen service strip: lawn mowing, property cleanup, hedge trimming, mulch and pine straw, and free estimates.
- Make the header and hero Free Estimate buttons point to a real contact action, such as email with a prefilled estimate subject or the contact page.
- Add short estimate instructions near the hero copy: include address, service frequency, photos if available, and preferred contact method.
- Fix the maintenance typo across the page.
- Keep “Learn More >>” links for continuity, but make the primary CTA clearly about requesting an estimate.
- Add Fitzgerald, GA and the main services to the title/meta description.
- Improve alt text so the service photos describe the service, not just file names.

## SEO basics to tighten

- Change the page title to include location, for example: “Lawn Care & Landscape Services in Fitzgerald, GA | Fitzgerald Lawn Solutions.”
- Add a meta description that mentions mowing, hedge trimming, property cleanup, mulch, pine straw, and Fitzgerald, GA.
- Keep one clear H1: “Lawn Care & Landscape Services.”
- Correct the “Maintenence” typo so search engines and visitors see the standard spelling.
- Use descriptive alt text for each service photo: zero-turn mowing, hedge trimming, leaf removal, property cleanup, pine straw, and bed edging.
- Make the Free Estimate CTA link behavior consistent across the header, hero, and contact page.
- If Wix supports it later, add LocalBusiness schema with business name, phone, email, address, service area, and service types.

## Realistic implementation angle

This should be a focused Wix services-page cleanup, not a rebuild. The practical path:

1. Keep the existing Wix layout, logo, photos, green/black palette, and service order.
2. Update the hero section with a quick service scan strip and clearer estimate instructions.
3. Fix the Free Estimate button targets so visitors can actually request an estimate.
4. Correct the maintenance spelling issue.
5. Tighten title, meta description, headings, and image alt text.
6. Check mobile so the service strip, CTA, phone, and email remain easy to tap.
