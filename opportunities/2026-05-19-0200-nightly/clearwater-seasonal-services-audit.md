# Clearwater Seasonal Services Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take

Clearwater Seasonal Services already has the ingredients of a useful local exterior-cleaning site: clear Nelson, BC positioning, four focused services, real service imagery, a service-area list across the West Kootenays, a guarantee/policy page, a reviews page, phone, email, address, and an estimate form. The biggest opportunity is not a full redesign. It is tightening the page flow so visitors immediately understand what Clearwater does, where the team works, which number to call, and how to request an estimate without scrolling through large empty sections.

## What is already working

- Strong local positioning: seasonal services in Nelson, BC and the greater West Kootenay region.
- Clear core services: Window Cleaning, Gutter Cleaning, Power Washing, and Moss Removal.
- Useful service-page navigation already exists for the four primary services.
- The site includes real operational details: email, phone, physical address, service area towns, guarantee/policy, and reviews navigation.
- Existing imagery is relevant: house exterior hero, window/gutter/power-washing/moss photos, truck/team/gallery photos, and contact-gallery images.
- The business story includes credibility points: based in Nelson since 2013, professional network, affordability, punctuality, reliability, and office management that returns calls or emails.
- The simple black/white/charcoal style is consistent and easy to preserve in Wix.

## Biggest conversion issues

- Phone numbers appear inconsistent. The hero and contact copy show 250-777-3659, while a contact heading says CALL: 250-354-7151. That can create hesitation right when someone is ready to ask for an estimate.
- The hero starts with contact info but does not clearly say what Clearwater does until lower on the page.
- The main CTA is gray and visually muted, so it looks like a secondary button instead of the primary estimate path.
- The page has very large whitespace gaps between the hero, services, CTA, gallery, and about sections. That weakens momentum on desktop and likely feels long on mobile.
- Service cards are helpful but do not include clear service-specific actions such as "Request window cleaning" or "Get a gutter cleaning estimate."
- The dense centered about copy has good proof, but it is hard to scan quickly.
- The service-area list is valuable for SEO and visitor qualification, but it is visually plain and separated from the estimate path.
- The "Satisfaction Guaranteed" section is extremely faint, which hides a useful trust signal.
- The contact form is near the bottom and does not clearly explain what details or photos a customer should send for a faster estimate.
- The wixsite URL makes the business feel less established than the service quality implied by the copy and photos.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements

- Standardize the primary phone number across header, hero, contact copy, form area, and footer.
- Add a benefit-driven H1 near the top: window, gutter, power washing, and moss removal services in Nelson and the West Kootenays.
- Pair the phone/email with two clear actions: Request a Free Estimate and Call Clearwater.
- Keep the four-service order and existing images, but add short descriptions and service-specific links.
- Reduce vertical whitespace so the page flows from hero to services to estimate CTA more quickly.
- Pull credibility above the fold: Nelson based since 2013, West Kootenay coverage, and callback expectations.
- Rewrite the about proof into shorter scan blocks while preserving the existing business story.
- Make the service-area list easier to scan and connect it to the estimate request.
- Increase contrast on the guarantee section so it reads as real trust proof.
- Make the form feel like an estimate checklist: name, email, service/town, project details, and photos if available.

## SEO basics to tighten

- Use one clear H1 that includes the service and location, for example: "Window, Gutter, Power Washing, and Moss Removal Services in Nelson, BC."
- Keep dedicated pages for Window Cleaning, Gutter Cleaning, Power Washing, and Moss Removal and link to them from the homepage cards.
- Use consistent NAP details: Clearwater Seasonal Services Inc., 1011 Fourth St., Nelson, BC V1L 2T5, clearwater.ss@gmail.com, and the correct primary phone number.
- Add descriptive alt text for all real service images and the Clearwater logo.
- Add concise copy mentioning Nelson, West Kootenays, window cleaning, gutter cleaning, pressure washing, power washing, moss removal, roof moss, decks, patios, siding, and exterior cleaning.
- If possible, connect a custom domain instead of relying on the wixsite URL.
- Add LocalBusiness or HomeAndConstructionBusiness schema later if Wix implementation allows it.
- Make the reviews page more visible by featuring one or two review snippets on the homepage.

## Realistic implementation angle

This should be sold as a focused Wix cleanup, not a rebuild:

1. Confirm and standardize the correct phone number.
2. Keep the existing logo, charcoal header, service imagery, service order, gallery, service-area list, guarantee link, contact form, and address.
3. Replace the hero contact-only message with a service/location headline plus clickable call and estimate actions.
4. Tighten section spacing and make the CTA button more visible.
5. Add proof chips and service-specific links.
6. Make the contact form and guarantee section easier to understand.
7. Optionally connect a custom domain as a trust upgrade.

## Offer fit

Good fit for a $99 Page Profit Check because the page already has enough real business content and imagery to diagnose quickly. The audit can show exactly where leads may be lost: inconsistent phone numbers, muted CTAs, service value hidden below the hero, excessive whitespace, and trust proof that is too faint.

Implementation should be separate from the $99 audit/diagnosis. A focused Wix homepage/contact cleanup would likely be a small follow-on project after Clearwater reviews the diagnosis and visual direction.
