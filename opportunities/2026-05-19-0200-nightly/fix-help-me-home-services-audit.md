# Fix Help Me Home Services Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take

Fix Help Me Home Services already has the right small-business ingredients for a local handyman and home-watch site: a real phone number, direct email, Bob's personal trust story, insurance language, service descriptions, project samples, badges, and customer feedback. The biggest opportunity is first-screen clarity. The current page has personality, but the visitor has to work too hard to understand the core offer, service area, and best next action.

## What is already working

- The site has a distinctive older Wix look with warm yellow, brown, red-orange, and gold colors.
- The phone number and email are visible near the top: 978-747-4137 and fixhelpme@gmail.com.
- The site clearly positions Bob as the person behind the business, which is useful for trust in handyman/home-watch work.
- The page lists high-intent services including home watch, painting, plumbing repairs, electrical repairs, shelving, TV mounting, picture hanging, and unusual requests.
- Customer comments from Janice B., Mike J., and Cindy W. provide real social proof.
- The project samples page includes relevant same-site photos for repairs, painting/wainscotting, fan and light installation, picture hanging, doors, and storage work.
- The site includes trust badges such as HomeGuide, ACHP, Angie's List, and Thumbtack badges.
- The brand has a memorable visual style that should be cleaned up rather than replaced with a generic modern template.

## Biggest conversion issues

- The headline says "Home Services Handyman," but it does not quickly explain the strongest buying reasons: handyman repairs, home watch while away, senior-friendly help, and insured service.
- The phone number is visible, but it is not treated as the main click-to-call action.
- Email is present, but there is not a clear "send project details/photos" path in the first screen.
- The Wix promotional banner at the top makes the page feel less professional.
- The first screen is visually busy, with badges, title text, phone box, and navigation competing for attention.
- The service descriptions are useful, but the page does not prioritize the most urgent services or clarify how to start.
- Customer proof is lower on the page and could support the contact decision earlier.
- The contact page has a form and Bob's contact details, but the home page should not make visitors navigate away before calling or emailing.
- Footer/social links include a Twitter/Wix-style destination, which can make the site feel unfinished.
- SEO signals are thin: page title and headings do not strongly include location, handyman services, home watch, or North Shore context.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements

- Keep the original warm yellow/brown/red visual identity, old-Wix geometry, star icon, badges, nav, and service-card style.
- Add a stronger top action strip with local service clarity, click-to-call, and email.
- Make the phone number and email into obvious first-screen CTAs.
- Add a short subheadline that explains the core offer: home maintenance, handyman repairs, installations, and home watch while away.
- Bring insured, dependable, time-efficient, and senior-friendly proof closer to the first decision point.
- Add a small first-screen contact prompt explaining that photos and a short description help with quoting.
- Keep the service grid, but tighten each card around one action: call, email details, send photos, or ask Bob.
- Move customer proof higher without removing the original testimonial section.
- Preserve the original badges and small-business feel so the prototype looks like an improved Fix Help Me page, not a replacement brand.

## SEO basics to tighten

- Change the home-page title to something location and service focused, for example: "North Shore MA Handyman and Home Watch | Fix Help Me Home Services."
- Use one clear H1 that includes handyman and home watch services.
- Add concise copy mentioning Massachusetts North Shore, home watch, handyman repairs, painting, plumbing repairs, electrical repairs, TV mounting, shelving, picture hanging, door hardware, and senior-friendly service.
- Add descriptive alt text for the top tool image, star mark, badges, Bob's photo, service thumbnails, and project photos.
- Make phone, email, business name, and insurance language consistent on every page.
- Confirm that footer/social links point to Bob's owned profiles, not Wix defaults.
- Consider LocalBusiness schema later if Wix allows custom code.

## Realistic implementation angle

This should be a focused Wix cleanup rather than a full rebuild. The practical path is:

1. Keep the existing brand colors, badges, top image, star icon, navigation, service categories, testimonials, and project photos.
2. Replace or reduce the Wix promotional distraction where possible.
3. Rewrite the first screen for service clarity and direct call/email action.
4. Add concise trust and quote-path copy near the top.
5. Tighten service cards, contact labels, image alt text, page title, and social/footer links.
