# Logans Gutter Services Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take

The live Wix site already has useful conversion pieces: a memorable dark/tan look, a big Logans Gutter Services hero, Westchester context, service photos, phone, email, call/text links, and a contact form. The biggest opportunity is the first screen. It should immediately say that Logans handles gutter cleaning, installs and repairs, gutter guards, and pressure washing in Westchester, then make call, text, and email estimate actions impossible to miss.

## What is already working

- The business name is large and memorable in the first screen.
- The site already says Westchester and includes phone and email contact details.
- The hero has direct call/text and email links, which is better than hiding everything behind a form.
- The dark charcoal, beige, and cream palette feels recognizable and can be improved without changing the brand.
- The page uses gutter, ladder, roofline, and pressure-washing related imagery instead of unrelated generic graphics.
- The services section already lists the core offers: gutter cleaning, gutter install and repairs, gutter guards, and pressure wash.
- The site includes multiple conversion paths: header contact, hero links, service CTAs, contact form, hours, and footer.
- The Book Online nav item gives the page an existing appointment/request flow that should be preserved.

## Biggest conversion issues

- The first screen does not clearly list the specific services until the visitor scrolls far below the hero.
- The subtitle "Exceptional Service All Westchester" is nice but too generic for searchers who want gutter cleaning, guards, repairs, or pressure washing.
- The phone number appears as 9144334835 in several places, which is harder to scan than a formatted number.
- The call and email actions are small text links. They work, but they do not feel like primary estimate actions.
- Service cards use generic support lines like "Expert Service" and "Always Prepared" instead of explaining what the visitor can request.
- The repeated "Click here for a free estimate" links are useful, but each service should also explain what kind of estimate the visitor is requesting.
- The contact form is present, but the page does not tell visitors what details to send, such as address, photos, clogged/downspout issue, repairs, or pressure washing scope.
- The opening hours line says "Sun: 8am - 2am," which may be accurate but looks like a possible typo and could reduce trust if unverified.
- Footer social links point to Wix social pages rather than Logan's own profiles, which creates a credibility mismatch.
- The visible Wix banner and Wix-created footer are recognizable, but they also make the business feel less finished if left on the public site.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements

- Rewrite the hero subtitle to include the money services and location: Westchester gutter cleaning, repairs, guards, and pressure washing.
- Keep the existing dark hero and roof/gutter background, but make the estimate path clearer with call/text and email actions above the fold.
- Add a compact service strip in the hero so visitors can confirm the right service without scrolling.
- Format the phone number consistently as 914-433-4835 while keeping the existing tel link.
- Rewrite service-card support text around real customer intents: clogged gutters, downspouts, leaks, gutter guards, installs, repairs, siding, walkways, and decks.
- Add guidance near the contact form: send address, service type, photos if available, and preferred call/text/email response.
- Verify the Sunday hours and either correct them or remove the questionable line until confirmed.
- Replace Wix social links with actual business profiles or remove them.
- If budget allows, upgrade Wix to remove the ad banner and connect a branded domain.

## SEO basics to tighten

- Change the page title from "Home | LOGANS GUTTER SERVICES" to a local service title such as "Westchester Gutter Cleaning and Repairs | Logans Gutter Services."
- Add or tighten the meta description around gutter cleaning, gutter installation, repairs, gutter guards, pressure washing, Westchester, NY, and free estimates.
- Keep one clear H1 with the business name, then use H2/H3 headings for services and contact.
- Add descriptive image alt text for each service photo, for example "gutter cleaning before and after" and "gutter guard installation on roofline."
- Format NAP details consistently: Logans Gutter Services, Westchester NY, 914-433-4835, and logansgutterservicesny@gmail.com.
- Add LocalBusiness or HomeAndConstructionBusiness schema later if Wix supports it cleanly.
- Consider dedicated service pages or sections for gutter cleaning, gutter guards, gutter repair, gutter installation, and pressure washing if SEO becomes a priority.

## Realistic implementation angle

This should be a focused Wix cleanup, not a rebuild. The practical path:

1. Keep the recognizable Wix layout, dark charcoal sections, beige accents, hero/background image, service-card structure, contact form, opening-hours block, and footer flow.
2. Update only the high-leverage hero copy so the first screen names the services, Westchester, and the estimate actions.
3. Add call/text and email CTAs in the hero and keep the existing Book Online nav.
4. Rewrite service-card copy so each card answers what the visitor can request.
5. Tighten contact-form helper copy and phone formatting.
6. Verify Sunday hours and social links before changing the live site.
7. Check mobile carefully because most gutter-service traffic will likely come from phones.
