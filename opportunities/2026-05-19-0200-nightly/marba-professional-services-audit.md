# M@RBA Professional Services - Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
M@RBA Professional Services is a good fit for a focused Page Profit Check because the current Wix site already has usable raw material: a clear lawn-care business name, tagline, phone number, email, schedule links, family-owned since 2014 positioning, three core service categories, a LawnStarter Certified Pro badge, and a strong customer quote.

The main opportunity is not a full redesign. It is a brand-faithful Wix homepage cleanup that keeps the dark brown and olive palette, centered lightweight typography, large section rhythm, lawn imagery, circular service photos, testimonial band, and bottom contact/photo section while making the estimate and scheduling path easier to see.

## What is already working
- The business name is prominent: `M@RBA Professional Services`.
- The tagline gives the site personality: `"Making a Million Love Your Yard!"`.
- The page has direct contact details: `Marbapros@gmail.com` and `816-582-6398`.
- The site includes a schedule page link: `Services/Schedule Online`.
- The page clearly lists the main services: lawn manicure maintenance, landscaping restoration, retaining walls, stone pathways, and general lawn beautification.
- The business has a family-owned since 2014 story.
- The LawnStarter Certified Pro badge is a useful trust cue.
- The customer quote adds warmth and proof.
- The dark brown, olive, white, and lawn-photo visual system feels cohesive for a lawn-care brand.
- The Wix section order is already understandable: header, hero, about, services photo, service cards, testimonial, contact photo, footer.

## Biggest conversion issues
1. **Critical: the first screen does not clearly drive scheduling.** The live hero has a large brand headline and thank-you line, but the main schedule action is not presented as a primary above-the-fold button.

2. **Critical: contact actions are present but underused.** The phone and email are visible in the header and footer area, but they are not reinforced as click-to-call or estimate actions in the hero.

3. **High: Wix/template residue weakens trust.** The live site shows the Wix promo banner, a `wixsite.com/lawns` URL, and an overall template feel. The site can still be Wix, but the first impression should feel less unfinished.

4. **High: excessive hero whitespace delays useful content.** The first dark section is very tall and sparse, pushing the about section and service proof far down the page.

5. **High: the LawnStarter badge appears visually misplaced.** The badge loads off to the right of the main visible layout, creating a broken or misaligned impression instead of a trust-building one.

6. **Medium: the maid-services link distracts from the lawn-care conversion path.** `We Now Offer Maid Services! Click Here!` may be real, but on the lawn page it competes with scheduling lawn service unless visually deprioritized or framed clearly.

7. **Medium: service copy is warm but dense.** The service cards communicate value, but the paragraphs are long and could be tightened for quicker scanning.

8. **Medium: the final contact section lacks an explicit estimate path.** The contact photo section has email and phone, but it does not tell visitors what to send or make scheduling online feel like the natural next step.

9. **Medium: SEO basics can be sharper.** The title and meta description mention lawn care, but the page can better reinforce services, scheduling, and contact intent through semantic headings, alt text, and clear service language.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Replace the Wix promo strip with a small branded scheduling strip while preserving the original top-bar geometry.
- Keep the dark header, centered brand, tagline, left nav, and right contact rhythm, but add a compact estimate/scheduling action.
- Bring the LawnStarter Certified Pro badge back inside the header so it works as a trust cue instead of looking off-canvas.
- Keep the large dark hero style, but add primary `Schedule Service`, `Schedule Estimate`, and `Call` actions above the fold.
- Preserve the maid-services link from the source site, but visually separate it from the main lawn-care CTA path.
- Add quick trust notes in the hero: family owned since 2014, 5-star service focus, and online scheduling.
- Keep the olive `Who We Are` section and original story, while making the business name consistent.
- Reuse the original lawn banner and circular service images.
- Keep the three service-card layout, but tighten wording and correct awkward phrasing such as `a all around touch up`.
- Keep the testimonial band and dots/chevrons to preserve the Wix carousel feel.
- Strengthen the bottom contact/photo section with email, phone, schedule link, and guidance on what to send for a fast estimate.
- Add better title, meta description, semantic headings, link labels, and descriptive alt text.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a focused title such as `M@RBA Professional Services | Lawn Care & Landscaping Restoration`.
- Use a meta description that includes lawn care, landscaping restoration, schedule online, and `816-582-6398`.
- Use one clear H1 for the business name and H2s for `Who We Are`, `Our 5-Star Services`, and `Want to Customize?`.
- Add descriptive alt text to the lawn photos, circular service photos, and certification badge.
- Make the phone number a consistent `tel:` link.
- Make the email a consistent `mailto:` link.
- Keep service phrases readable and searchable: lawn manicure maintenance, landscaping restoration, yard cleanup, retaining walls, stone pathways, outdoor beautification.
- If service areas are known later, add city/service-area language and local business schema after confirmation.

## Realistic implementation angle
This should be positioned as a focused Wix homepage cleanup, not a full rebuild. The current page has enough personality and structure to work with. A realistic implementation would keep the same visual identity and section order, reduce template residue, move scheduling actions higher, bring trust cues into the visible layout, tighten service copy, and improve the contact flow.

The owner-facing value is simple: help visitors understand what M@RBA does and how to schedule service or request an estimate before they scroll too far.
