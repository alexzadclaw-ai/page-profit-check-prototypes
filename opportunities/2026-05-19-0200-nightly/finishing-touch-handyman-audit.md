# Finishing Touch Handyman Services Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take

The current Wix page already has a recognizable local handyman brand, original site imagery, a clear services section, and important trust proof: Lehigh Valley coverage, 20+ years of experience, licensed contractor PA176483, and fully insured. The main opportunity is to make those proof points and the free-quote path visible before the long form, so visitors can immediately tell what Finishing Touch handles and whether to call, text, email photos, or request a quote.

## What is already working

- Strong local-service positioning: handyman and home improvement needs in the Lehigh Valley.
- Clear service categories are already present in a good order: Carpentry, Plumbing, Electrical, Painting, Remodeling, and "And More."
- The page includes a real phone number, email address, free-quote link, social links, service-area language, and licensing/insurance proof.
- The existing visual identity is distinct: muted blue header/sections, large centered "FINISHING TOUCH" brand text, tool logo, service icons, large handyman hero photo, wood about section, and contact form over a work-site photo.
- The about copy is useful and specific: owned by Nick, Lehigh Valley native, more than 20 years in home improvement and housing, based near Nazareth, and serving Northampton, Lehigh, Northern Bucks, and Southern Monroe Counties.
- The free quote button links to an existing Jotform, so the business already has a quote intake path.

## Biggest conversion issues

- The top screen has the free quote button, but the strongest trust proof, licensed/insured and PA176483, appears much later on the page.
- Phone and email are present, but the phone/email block is visually awkward and partly clipped in the current desktop layout.
- The hero headline is helpful, but visitors still have to scroll to see the common repair categories.
- The free quote action sends people to a long form without first giving a simple "call/text" or "email project photos" alternative.
- The services section is visually on-brand, but some service descriptions are small and hard to scan quickly.
- The contact form section is busy: pale background image, semi-transparent fields, a large ghosted "GET A FREE QUOTE" text layer, and a country-code selector make the form feel longer than necessary.
- The footer/copyright text references "The Handy Gang" and Wix, which weakens brand consistency.
- The free Wix banner and wixsite URL reduce trust for homeowners comparing licensed local contractors.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements

- Add a compact proof row above the fold: Licensed & insured PA176483, Lehigh Valley service area, 20+ years experience.
- Keep the existing hero photo and headline, but make the quote button and call/text option clearer.
- Bring phone and email into a visible header/contact area instead of leaving them partially off-canvas.
- Preserve the five-service order while adding a short scan line that frames them as common repairs, installs, and improvement projects.
- Keep the wood about section, but add a small trust strip under the existing bio so licensing and insurance are easier to notice.
- Clean up the contact form into a more opaque, readable quote card with the fields homeowners actually need: name, location, email, phone, and project description.
- Add an "Email Project Photos" path because photos often make handyman estimates easier.
- Standardize the footer around Finishing Touch Handyman Services and PA License 176483.

## SEO basics to tighten

- Use one clear H1 that includes the business, service, and location, for example: "Lehigh Valley Handyman Services | Finishing Touch Handyman Services."
- Add concise page copy mentioning handyman services, home repair, home improvement, carpentry, plumbing fixture installation, electrical fixture installation, painting, drywall repair, remodeling, and Lehigh Valley.
- Add descriptive alt text to the logo, hero image, service icons, wood background, and contact background image where Wix allows it.
- Make the phone number, email, PA license number, service areas, and business name consistent in header, footer, and quote form.
- If possible, connect a custom domain and remove the free Wix ad banner for credibility.
- Add LocalBusiness or HomeAndConstructionBusiness schema later if Wix implementation supports it.
- Use the service page link more deliberately, or convert the one-page navigation into real section anchors so Home, Services, About, and Contact move visitors to the right place.

## Realistic implementation angle

This is not a full redesign. The best implementation is a focused Wix cleanup:

1. Keep the current header, blue palette, logo, hero photo, service icons, wood about section, contact background photo, and footer license block.
2. Fix the top header alignment so phone and email are visible and clickable.
3. Add licensed/insured proof and a call/text CTA before the long quote form.
4. Tighten the services and contact sections for readability without changing the site identity.
5. Simplify the quote card copy and add an email-photos option.
6. Replace the inconsistent footer/copyright text with Finishing Touch branding.

## Offer fit

Good fit for a $99 Page Profit Check because the site already has enough real business information and visual assets to produce a useful diagnosis quickly. The audit can show exactly where conversion is being lost: trust proof too low, phone/email not prominent enough, service details hard to scan, and the form feeling heavy. Implementation should be sold separately after the owner reviews the diagnosis and visual direction.
