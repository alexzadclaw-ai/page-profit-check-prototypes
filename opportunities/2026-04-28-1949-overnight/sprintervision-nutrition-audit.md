# Sprintervision Nutrition — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Sprintervision already has a strong founder story, a clear niche around nutrition and performance, and enough service detail to support a compelling coaching homepage. The problem is that the first screen is too abstract for a new visitor. “There is a more excellent way” sounds aspirational, but it does not quickly explain what Sprintervision does, who it helps, or how someone should start. The service packages are useful, yet the homepage still feels light on structure, trust packaging, and consultation flow.

**Recommendation:** keep the same personal-coach identity, performance-meets-wellness tone, and simple independent-business feel — but clarify the hero, move founder/trust proof higher, organize services into clearer paths, and explain what happens after someone books a consultation.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Platform / feel:** simple independent coach site with a centered layout, light builder texture, and straightforward mobile-style nav.
- **Color direction:** light backgrounds with dark text and simple accent treatment.
- **Typography feel:** basic sans-serif with centered headline treatment and minimal decorative styling.
- **Header pattern:** phone number, Instagram, compact menu, and founder-led brand story.
- **Section rhythm:** hero, short promise, consultation CTA, support statement, footer/contact.
- **Recognition elements worth preserving:** Keith Lewis founder identity, performance/wellness crossover, nutrition-first positioning, and the “Sprint to your vision” philosophy.

## What is already working
- Founder story is real and differentiated: collegiate track athlete, All-American sprinter, personal-performance credibility.
- Consultation CTA already exists.
- Service packages give concrete pricing and scope, which is useful trust material.
- The wellness/performance positioning can appeal to both active adults and goal-oriented clients.
- Phone number and direct contact path are visible.
- The message around personalized support and accountability is strong once visitors keep reading.

## Biggest conversion issues

### Critical
1. **The hero is too abstract for first-time visitors.**
   - The promise sounds inspirational, but not concrete enough about nutrition coaching, performance support, or the kinds of outcomes people can expect.
2. **The homepage does not package trust quickly enough.**
   - Keith’s athlete background, founder story, consultation path, and structured offers should show up much earlier and more clearly.
3. **The next step is under-explained.**
   - Visitors can book a consultation, but the page does not clearly explain what happens in that first conversation or which program type may fit.

### High
1. **Service clarity is split across pages instead of organized on the homepage.**
   - Reset plans, coaching, testing, accountability, and performance support exist, but visitors need a faster overview.
2. **The homepage feels lighter than the business actually is.**
   - There is more substance in the service and team pages than the homepage suggests.
3. **Trust proof and specialization are too general.**
   - Performance, nutrigenomics, accountability, and personalized planning should be translated into simpler visitor-facing proof.
4. **The CTA hierarchy is too soft.**
   - “Book consultation today” needs stronger support and more specific action framing.

### Medium
1. Local geography is less clear than it could be.
2. The site can use stronger section hierarchy for mobile scanning.
3. SEO phrasing can more directly target nutrition coaching, performance coaching, and consultation intent.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. **Rewrite the hero around a clearer coaching promise.**
   - Make nutrition coaching, performance support, and consultation intent obvious in the first screen.
2. **Add a trust strip below the hero.**
   - Founder-led, personalized plans, consultation-based onboarding, and concrete program options.
3. **Turn the service stack into clearer buyer paths.**
   - Quick reset, hands-on coaching, testing/data-driven support, and higher-accountability transformation plans.
4. **Add a “what happens first” section.**
   - Explain consultation, goals review, plan recommendation, and ongoing accountability.
5. **Bring the founder story higher in a more scannable way.**
   - Keep the athlete/performance background, but tie it more directly to client outcomes.
6. **Use pricing/program proof as reassurance, not buried detail.**
   - Show that there are structured ways to start without forcing visitors to dig.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a title closer to **Nutrition Coaching & Performance Wellness Support | Sprintervision Nutrition**.
- Tighten the meta description around personalized nutrition coaching, accountability, performance support, and consultation booking.
- Keep one clear H1 focused on the business promise rather than a purely abstract tagline.
- Use heading structure that surfaces nutrition coaching, performance coaching, and wellness transformation terms.
- Strengthen internal linking to service packages and founder/team pages.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a solid **Solution A** candidate. The business identity is already there. It does not need a reinvention. It needs a clearer first impression, better trust sequencing, and a more specific consultation path so the business feels as structured as its service packages already are.
