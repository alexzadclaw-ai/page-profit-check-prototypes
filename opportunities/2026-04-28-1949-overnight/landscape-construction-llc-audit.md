# Landscape Construction LLC — Audit-Driven Solution A

## Quick take
Landscape Construction LLC already has a recognizable old-school local-contractor identity: dense service navigation, practical copy, broad service coverage, and a no-frills “we do the work” tone. The opportunity is not a reinvention. It is a careful cleanup that keeps the same family-business personality while removing the elements that make the homepage feel dated, cluttered, and lower-trust than it should.

Right now the site has broken-looking inline paths, admin-style links, spelling issues, overpacked navigation, scattered CTAs, and too many services competing at once. The company has real depth — family ownership, long history, broad installation capability, references, seasonal services — but the homepage does not package that story clearly. A cleaned-up estimate-focused homepage can still look like the same company while feeling far easier to trust and contact.

**Visual fingerprint before building**
- Old-school hosted-site layout with dense left-nav/top-nav utility feel
- Straightforward blue-link service menu and practical text-heavy content
- Family-business tone with little marketing gloss
- Broad service catalog spanning hardscape, planting, lawn care, snow, and specialty work
- Simple headline/tagline pattern: “From dirt to design”
- Functional local-business presentation centered on phone, address, and email visibility

**Top findings by severity**
- **Critical:** homepage exposes broken-looking inline path fragments and admin-style links that damage trust
- **Critical:** navigation is overloaded and makes the site feel chaotic on first visit
- **Critical:** hero and CTA hierarchy are weak relative to the number of services offered
- **High:** copy and spelling issues reduce professionalism
- **High:** the service story is too broad and insufficiently prioritized for first-time buyers
- **High:** trust signals exist, but they are buried in long text rather than packaged near the top
- **Medium:** contact flow exists, but the page does not frame consultation or estimate expectations well
- **Medium:** SEO/local targeting can be tighter around Southwest Wisconsin and key outdoor projects

## What is already working
- Strong breadth of services across landscape construction and seasonal upkeep
- Clear family-owned positioning
- Physical address, phone, fax, and email are easy to find
- The company mentions references and quality materials/workmanship
- The “From dirt to design” tagline is memorable and worth preserving
- Long business history and owner experience provide real credibility material

## Biggest conversion issues
### Critical
1. **Template/admin residue and broken-looking paths hurt trust**
   Inline fragments like `/grilloutdoor_kitchen`, `/firepits`, `/contact_us`, and admin-style links make the homepage feel unmaintained.

2. **Navigation overload creates decision fatigue**
   The visitor is hit with many service links, seasonal links, and subpages before they understand the main offer.

3. **The homepage lacks a strong primary action path**
   There is no clean estimate-first hierarchy for homeowners who want to know whether to call, request a consultation, or browse examples.

### High
4. **Copy quality feels dated and uneven**
   Spelling issues like “Noteable” and “Maintence,” plus dense legacy phrasing, reduce professionalism.

5. **Too many services compete equally**
   Firepits, mole extermination, snowplowing, ponds, patios, lawncare, and plantings all appear at once, making it harder to understand the core profitable landscape-construction story.

6. **Trust is present but hidden in long paragraphs**
   Family ownership, formation in 1998, references, and geographic reach should be surfaced much faster.

### Medium
7. **Contact flow is functional but not persuasive**
   Contact details are visible, but there is little guidance on what the visitor should expect after reaching out.

8. **Local SEO framing is under-optimized**
   Southwest and Southcentral Wisconsin coverage is there, but it should be tightened around specific service intent and geography.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Keep **From dirt to design** but pair it with a clearer landscape/hardscape value proposition
- Collapse the service sprawl into a smaller set of primary categories: patios & walls, water/fire features, planting & lawn care, seasonal/property services
- Add a top trust strip: **Family-owned since 1998**, **Southwest Wisconsin**, **Residential + commercial**, **References available**
- Make the main CTA **Request a Consultation** with a secondary **Call 608-924-2065**
- Remove admin-style links, broken inline paths, and unnecessary menu clutter
- Add a short **How we get your project started** section to frame consultation and estimating
- Keep the practical contractor tone instead of over-polishing the site into something unrecognizable

## SEO basics to tighten
- **Suggested title direction:** Landscape Construction, Patios, Retaining Walls & Outdoor Living in Southwest Wisconsin | Landscape Construction LLC
- **Suggested meta description direction:** Family-owned landscape contractor serving Southwest and Southcentral Wisconsin with patios, retaining walls, plantings, water features, lawn care, and seasonal services. Request a consultation.
- Use one clear H1 focused on outdoor project outcomes
- Push Southwest Wisconsin and Madison-area service language higher on the page
- Clean up service headings so they reflect real search intent
- Use unique page titles for major service pages in production

## Realistic implementation angle
This is still a good **Solution A** case, but it needs firmer cleanup than Earthworks. The site has a strong recognizable fingerprint — older hosted-site structure, practical copy, and a deep local-service menu — so the prototype should keep that family-contractor identity while simplifying the homepage dramatically.

The pitch is not “new brand.” It is “same company, cleaner homepage, clearer service story, stronger consultation path.”
