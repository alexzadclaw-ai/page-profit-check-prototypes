# Earthworks Landscape Construction, Inc. — Audit-Driven Solution A

## Quick take
Earthworks already has a usable visual base: a clean Squarespace shell, restrained typography, simple white-space rhythm, and a straightforward South Bay landscape positioning. This should not be a redesign. It should be a surgical cleanup that keeps the same calm contractor feel while fixing the trust leaks and weak quote flow.

Right now the homepage repeats navigation, repeats CTAs, exposes Squarespace config links in the footer, and gives too little service clarity up top. The business sounds established, but the page feels unfinished. A cleaned-up estimate-first homepage can still look like Earthworks while converting more like a real local contractor site.

**Visual fingerprint before building**
- Minimal white/light-neutral Squarespace layout
- Simple black/charcoal typography with generous spacing
- Clean, understated contractor presentation rather than flashy marketing
- Repeated centered sections with sparse copy
- Straightforward navigation: Home, Our Work, Our Services, Company, Contact
- Quiet premium feel driven by whitespace and restraint

**Top findings by severity**
- **Critical:** footer exposes internal Squarespace config links, which damages trust immediately
- **Critical:** navigation and CTA duplication makes the page feel unfinished
- **Critical:** homepage is too thin on service clarity and quote intent for first-time visitors
- **High:** trust signals are present but under-packaged near the top
- **High:** services are listed, but not framed around homeowner project goals
- **High:** contact flow asks for a free estimate, but the page does not explain what happens next
- **Medium:** SEO/local phrasing is broad and could be more specific to South Bay landscape demand
- **Medium:** the page relies heavily on generic statements instead of proof-oriented copy

## What is already working
- Clear local-service category fit for landscape construction
- Real business longevity since 1997
- Family-run story adds credibility
- Services are broad enough to support a strong homeowner-intent homepage
- The current design is clean and recognizable enough to preserve
- Phone number and contractor license are visible

## Biggest conversion issues
### Critical
1. **Internal/footer residue hurts credibility**
   The footer links point to Squarespace config/admin destinations instead of real public pages. That instantly makes the site look unfinished.

2. **The homepage feels duplicated instead of intentional**
   Repeated nav blocks and repeated CTA links create visual noise without adding clarity.

3. **Hero copy is too general for a high-intent visitor**
   “Full-service landscape company” is accurate, but it does not quickly tell a homeowner what Earthworks actually builds or why they should request an estimate now.

### High
4. **Trust is buried instead of packaged**
   Since 1997, family ownership, South Bay service, and CSL #948496 should be grouped near the top as fast reassurance.

5. **Service story is too flat**
   Hardscape, builds, softscape, and enhancements exist, but the homepage does not guide buyers toward patios, walls, kitchens, planting, turf, lighting, and similar outcomes.

6. **No clear next-step explanation**
   The contact page mentions a free estimate, but the homepage does not explain consultation, scope, or scheduling.

### Medium
7. **SEO basics can be more local and specific**
   The homepage should lean harder into South Bay landscaping, hardscaping, outdoor living, and estimate intent.

8. **Proof is lighter than it should be**
   The site references quality work and customer service, but offers little structured proof beyond years in business and project imagery.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Replace the vague hero framing with a clearer outdoor-living and landscape-construction message
- Add a top trust strip: **Since 1997**, **Family-run**, **South Bay**, **CSL #948496**
- Turn the main CTA into **Request a Free Estimate** with a secondary **Call (408) 607-4653**
- Rebuild the service section around buyer outcomes: hardscape, outdoor living builds, planting/turf/drainage, lighting
- Add a short **How your project starts** block to reduce friction
- Remove duplicated nav and repeated CTA clutter
- Replace broken/footer config links with real public navigation only
