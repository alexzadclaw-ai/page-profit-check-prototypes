# Wellabee — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Wellabee has a useful niche: parent-facing support for children and teens who need help with attention, organization, study skills, emotional regulation, and confidence. The brand name is memorable, the bee/honeycomb identity is recognizable, and Jennifer's background gives the business real credibility. The immediate problem is much more basic: the public domain currently fails over to a Wix “domain not connected” page, so a prospective parent cannot reach the actual site at all.

Even if the domain were reconnected, the available public footprint points to a broader conversion issue: the offer needs to be packaged more clearly for parents. A family wellness / tutoring visitor should understand in the first few seconds who Wellabee helps, what kind of sessions are available, why Jennifer is qualified, and what happens after they request help.

**Recommendation:** treat this as a domain-recovery plus homepage-clarity cleanup. Keep the warm bee-themed Wellabee identity and calm parent-support tone, but replace the dead-domain experience with a focused homepage that quickly explains child/teen support, parent trust signals, and a simple consultation path.

**Visual resemblance:** Medium  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Platform / feel:** Wix-based small-service-business site, currently showing a Wix disconnected-domain page from the assigned URL.
- **Color direction:** soft honey / golden yellow, warm cream, charcoal text, and white space suggested by the bee / honeycomb brand assets.
- **Typography feel:** friendly sans-serif with approachable coaching/tutoring tone rather than a clinical medical feel.
- **Header pattern:** simple brand-first nav with parent-oriented pages such as services, about, tutoring, and contact.
- **Image treatment:** bee / honeycomb motifs and warm child/family learning imagery.
- **Section rhythm:** should remain simple and scannable: hero, trust points, support paths, founder credibility, what happens next, contact.
- **Recognition elements worth preserving:** Wellabee name, bee/honeycomb metaphor, family-wellness positioning, Jennifer's child/adolescent support background, and parent-friendly coaching language.

## What is already working
- The business name is memorable and naturally supports a warm “busy families / steady support / build skills” theme.
- Jennifer's public profile includes a master's degree in psychology and experience with children, adolescents, families, school settings, learning challenges, ADHD, organization, mindfulness, and wellness coaching.
- The Wyzant footprint gives useful proof: 4.9 rating, 28 ratings, 138 tutoring hours, background check passed, and parent-facing testimonials.
- The niche is specific enough to sell clearly: study skills, ADHD support, reading/writing/elementary math help, organization, focus, and confidence.
- The Naperville / DuPage-area context makes the business feel local and approachable.

## Biggest conversion issues

### Critical
1. **The assigned homepage is not reachable as a Wellabee website.**
   - `https://www.wellabee.com/` refuses HTTPS and the HTTP version redirects to Wix's “This Domain Has Flown Away” disconnected-domain page. That is a complete trust and conversion break.
2. **The current first impression does not explain the business.**
   - A parent landing on the domain sees Wix recovery/template messaging instead of child, teen, family wellness, tutoring, or coaching content.
3. **There is no usable primary CTA on the public domain.**
   - Parents cannot request a consultation, ask about fit, or understand how to start from the assigned URL.

### High
1. **Parent trust proof is not packaged on the homepage.**
   - Jennifer's psychology, school, clinical, tutoring, wellness-coaching, and parent-review credibility should be visible near the top.
2. **The offer risks feeling fragmented.**
   - Tutoring, wellness coaching, ADHD support, mindfulness, family strategies, and study skills are related, but they need to be grouped into clear parent decision paths.
3. **The next step needs reassurance.**
   - Parents of children with attention, anxiety, or learning challenges need a calm explanation of the first call, fit check, session plan, and progress support.
4. **The site needs careful non-clinical wording.**
   - Because the work touches ADHD, emotional regulation, and family wellness, the homepage should avoid overpromising clinical outcomes and focus on support, skills, strategies, and coaching/tutoring fit.

### Medium
1. Local relevance can be stronger around Naperville / DuPage-area and online support where appropriate.
2. SEO phrasing should target tutoring, study skills, ADHD support, and child/teen wellness coaching rather than only the brand name.
3. Mobile scanning should rely on shorter sections, parent-facing headings, and repeated contact prompts.
4. The footer should reinforce contact, service fit, and credentials rather than default Wix or disconnected-domain residue.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. **Reconnect or redirect the domain before anything else.**
   - The most valuable fix is making `wellabee.com` land on an active Wellabee homepage.
2. **Rewrite the hero around parent clarity.**
   - Make the first screen say who Wellabee helps and what parents can request.
3. **Add a trust strip immediately below the hero.**
   - Master's in psychology, child/adolescent support background, 4.9 Wyzant rating, and tutoring / wellness-coaching experience.
4. **Group services into parent-friendly paths.**
   - Study skills and organization, reading/writing/elementary math tutoring, ADHD/focus strategies, and family wellness coaching.
5. **Add a “what happens next” section.**
   - Parent fit call, goals review, support plan, regular sessions, and strategy adjustments.
6. **Clean up risk language.**
   - Use supportive coaching/tutoring copy without implying diagnosis, treatment, or guaranteed outcomes.
7. **Use the bee/honeycomb identity as a familiar visual system.**
   - Keep the brand recognizable with warm honey accents and calm white space rather than turning it into a generic tutoring template.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a title like **Child & Teen Study Skills, ADHD Support & Wellness Coaching | Wellabee**.
- Write a meta description around Naperville-area and online support for study skills, organization, focus, tutoring, and parent-guided wellness coaching.
- Keep one clear H1 focused on parent outcomes.
- Use H2s for Study Skills, Tutoring, Focus & Organization, Family Wellness Support, and How to Start.
- Remove disconnected-domain / Wix placeholder indexing risk as soon as the live site is restored.
