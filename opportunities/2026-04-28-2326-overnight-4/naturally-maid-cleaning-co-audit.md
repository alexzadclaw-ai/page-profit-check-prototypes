# Naturally Maid Cleaning Co. — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Naturally Maid Cleaning Co. is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current site has a recognizable local-service identity and real trust proof, but the first-screen clarity, proof hierarchy, service grouping, and action path can be tightened without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current site family and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Book a Professional Clean**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Current business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #2f7d32, #8bc34a, #f6c453, #17351d.
- **Typography feel:** Poppins, Arial, sans-serif heading direction with Nunito, Arial, sans-serif body copy direction.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Local-service navigation with phone/contact emphasis.
- **Hero/image treatment:** leaf sparkle visual cue with direct service promise.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:** Eco cleaning positioning, BookingKoala-style booking path, broad suburb list, and multiple category pages.

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with real local-service intent in Arlington Heights / Naperville, IL.
- The site already contains useful proof: Eco-friendly products, Fully insured, Locally owned, Easy online booking.
- The existing visual direction is recognizable enough for Solution A instead of a full redesign.
- There is a direct contact, quote, or booking path that can be made easier to notice.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The page contains many services and service areas, but the first-screen booking decision is not simple enough.
   - Prototype response: Clarify the hero around eco-friendly cleaning and one booking action.
2. **High:** Book Now appears repeatedly without a short explanation of which cleaning path to choose.
   - Prototype response: Create a top trust strip for eco-friendly, insured, local, and booking proof.
3. **High:** Eco-friendly, insured, and locally owned proof should be concentrated earlier.
   - Prototype response: Group cleaning options into three practical paths.
4. **High:** Residential, Airbnb, move, and commercial cleaning need buyer-ready grouping.
   - Prototype response: Add a simple what-happens-next sequence.
5. **Medium:** Long galleries/reviews/area lists may push practical booking expectations too far down the page.
   - Prototype response: Keep area coverage and galleries secondary to the booking decision.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Clarify the hero around eco-friendly cleaning and one booking action.
2. Create a top trust strip for eco-friendly, insured, local, and booking proof.
3. Group cleaning options into three practical paths.
4. Add a simple what-happens-next sequence.
5. Keep area coverage and galleries secondary to the booking decision.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
