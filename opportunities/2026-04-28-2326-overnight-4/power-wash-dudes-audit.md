# Power Wash Dudes — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Power Wash Dudes is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current site has a recognizable local-service identity and real trust proof, but the first-screen clarity, proof hierarchy, service grouping, and action path can be tightened without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current site family and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Book a Service Now**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Current business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #0f4c81, #12a8d7, #f59e0b, #0d2638.
- **Typography feel:** Montserrat, Arial Black, sans-serif heading direction with Montserrat, Arial, sans-serif body copy direction.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Local-service navigation with phone/contact emphasis.
- **Hero/image treatment:** spray wave visual cue with direct service promise.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:** Very broad Wix service menu, typo-prone Naperville wording, strong licensed/insured and 250+ five-star proof.

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with real local-service intent in Naperville / Yorkville / Oswego / Plainfield / Aurora, IL.
- The site already contains useful proof: Licensed & insured, 250+ happy customers, 100% satisfaction, Warrantied service.
- The existing visual direction is recognizable enough for Solution A instead of a full redesign.
- There is a direct contact, quote, or booking path that can be made easier to notice.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The homepage has strong branding but the enormous service menu makes the next step feel harder than it should.
   - Prototype response: Preserve the bold Power Wash Dudes feel while cutting the first-screen choice overload.
2. **High:** Book a Service Now is compelling but competes with many read-more paths.
   - Prototype response: Make Book a Service Now the primary CTA and phone the secondary CTA.
3. **High:** Licensed/insured, 250+ customers, satisfaction, and warranty proof should sit immediately below the hero.
   - Prototype response: Add a top proof strip with license, reviews, satisfaction, and warranty points.
4. **High:** Service categories need to be grouped by surface/problem instead of listed as a wall of options.
   - Prototype response: Group services into house/roof, hardscapes, and specialty exterior cleaning.
5. **Medium:** Typos and Wix-like repetition can reduce trust even though the actual service proof is strong.
   - Prototype response: Clean spelling/repetition and explain the booking process.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Preserve the bold Power Wash Dudes feel while cutting the first-screen choice overload.
2. Make Book a Service Now the primary CTA and phone the secondary CTA.
3. Add a top proof strip with license, reviews, satisfaction, and warranty points.
4. Group services into house/roof, hardscapes, and specialty exterior cleaning.
5. Clean spelling/repetition and explain the booking process.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
