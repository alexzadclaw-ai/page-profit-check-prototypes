# Chicago Simply Clean — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Chicago Simply Clean is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current site has a recognizable local-service identity and real trust proof, but the first-screen clarity, proof hierarchy, service grouping, and action path can be tightened without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current site family and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Get a Free Quote**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Current business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #39a96b, #8fd14f, #ffb703, #14351f.
- **Typography feel:** Nunito, Arial, sans-serif heading direction with Open Sans, Arial, sans-serif body copy direction.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Local-service navigation with phone/contact emphasis.
- **Hero/image treatment:** green clean visual cue with direct service promise.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:** Older navigation with separate Naperville/green/cleaning tips pages, Angie’s List award copy, and dual quote/booking actions.

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with real local-service intent in Naperville, IL.
- The site already contains useful proof: Angie’s List Super Service Award, Eco-friendly options, Online booking, Quality service guarantee.
- The existing visual direction is recognizable enough for Solution A instead of a full redesign.
- There is a direct contact, quote, or booking path that can be made easier to notice.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** Quote, online booking, cleaning plans, and commercial quote paths are all present but not clearly prioritized.
   - Prototype response: Make Get a Free Quote the main CTA and keep Book Online as a secondary ready-to-schedule option.
2. **High:** Award proof and eco-friendly value should reinforce the first screen instead of sitting in older page sections.
   - Prototype response: Move award, eco, booking, and guarantee proof into a top trust strip.
3. **High:** Recurring, one-time, and commercial services need a clearer decision structure.
   - Prototype response: Simplify cleaning plans into recurring, one-time, and commercial paths.
4. **High:** Older navigation and service-area content create a dated feel despite useful proof.
   - Prototype response: Add a short plan-to-quote process section.
5. **Medium:** Testimonials and cleaning checklist content are helpful but should support the quote path, not distract from it.
   - Prototype response: Modernize hierarchy without erasing the green Simply Clean feel.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Make Get a Free Quote the main CTA and keep Book Online as a secondary ready-to-schedule option.
2. Move award, eco, booking, and guarantee proof into a top trust strip.
3. Simplify cleaning plans into recurring, one-time, and commercial paths.
4. Add a short plan-to-quote process section.
5. Modernize hierarchy without erasing the green Simply Clean feel.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
