# Elite Landscape Group — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Elite Landscape Group is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current site has a recognizable local-service identity and real trust proof, but the first-screen clarity, proof hierarchy, service grouping, and action path can be tightened without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current site family and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Get a Landscaping Quote**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Current business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #2f6b2f, #8cc63e, #d9a441, #1b321b.
- **Typography feel:** Georgia, Times New Roman, serif heading direction with Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif body copy direction.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Local-service navigation with phone/contact emphasis.
- **Hero/image treatment:** lawn lines visual cue with direct service promise.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:** #1/top-rated claims, broad year-round services, long service-area menu, and old-school landscaping layout.

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with real local-service intent in Naperville, IL.
- The site already contains useful proof: 25+ years serving Naperville, Licensed/qualified team, Total customer satisfaction, Residential + commercial.
- The existing visual direction is recognizable enough for Solution A instead of a full redesign.
- There is a direct contact, quote, or booking path that can be made easier to notice.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The homepage is service-rich but the dense navigation and many claims make the quote decision feel less direct.
   - Prototype response: Keep the green/gold landscape identity while simplifying the hero around one quote CTA.
2. **High:** Lawn care, landscaping, irrigation, snow, holiday decor, and service-area pages compete before the visitor gets a simple path.
   - Prototype response: Group services into maintenance, landscape/outdoor living, and irrigation/snow paths.
3. **High:** 25+ years and customer-satisfaction proof should be closer to the top.
   - Prototype response: Move 25+ years, qualified/licensed, satisfaction, and residential/commercial proof into a top strip.
4. **High:** Strong #1/top-rated language needs a more grounded proof hierarchy to feel credible.
   - Prototype response: Tone down claim overload and make proof more specific.
5. **Medium:** Project gallery and service-area content can remain, but should not crowd the primary quote CTA.
   - Prototype response: Add a simple property-need-to-quote process section.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Keep the green/gold landscape identity while simplifying the hero around one quote CTA.
2. Group services into maintenance, landscape/outdoor living, and irrigation/snow paths.
3. Move 25+ years, qualified/licensed, satisfaction, and residential/commercial proof into a top strip.
4. Tone down claim overload and make proof more specific.
5. Add a simple property-need-to-quote process section.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
