# Family Window Washing — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Family Window Washing is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current site has a recognizable local-service identity and real trust proof, but the first-screen clarity, proof hierarchy, service grouping, and action path can be tightened without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current site family and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Get a Quote**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Current business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #1769aa, #38bdf8, #f97316, #15324a.
- **Typography feel:** Poppins, Arial, sans-serif heading direction with Open Sans, Arial, sans-serif body copy direction.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Local-service navigation with phone/contact emphasis.
- **Hero/image treatment:** bright windows visual cue with direct service promise.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:** Neighbor-discount idea, family-owned positioning, broad exterior-cleaning menu, and visible email/phone top bar.

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with real local-service intent in Naperville / Chicago suburbs, IL.
- The site already contains useful proof: Local family-owned team, Fully insured, 5-star reviews, 100% satisfaction.
- The existing visual direction is recognizable enough for Solution A instead of a full redesign.
- There is a direct contact, quote, or booking path that can be made easier to notice.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The homepage has useful trust and service detail, but the quote path competes with many service/add-on sections and tips.
   - Prototype response: Make Get a Quote the clear primary CTA across the page.
2. **High:** Neighbor discount and multi-service messaging are interesting but not prioritized around the main buyer decision.
   - Prototype response: Consolidate local/family/insured/5-star proof near the top.
3. **High:** Trust proof exists but is spread across the page rather than reinforcing the hero.
   - Prototype response: Group window, gutter, and exterior washing choices into easy cards.
4. **High:** The service menu is broad enough to benefit from three simple buyer paths.
   - Prototype response: Keep neighbor-booking and helpful tips as secondary proof, not first-screen clutter.
5. **Medium:** A few technical/agency artifacts and secondary email references could distract from the local family-owned feel.
   - Prototype response: Clean minor template/agency residue and make the contact section intentional.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Make Get a Quote the clear primary CTA across the page.
2. Consolidate local/family/insured/5-star proof near the top.
3. Group window, gutter, and exterior washing choices into easy cards.
4. Keep neighbor-booking and helpful tips as secondary proof, not first-screen clutter.
5. Clean minor template/agency residue and make the contact section intentional.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
