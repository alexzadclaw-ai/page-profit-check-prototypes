# Pinpoint Maintenance — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Pinpoint Maintenance is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current site has a recognizable local-service identity and real trust proof, but the first-screen clarity, proof hierarchy, service grouping, and action path can be tightened without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current site family and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Schedule a Free Quote**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Current business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #0d5f73, #2bb4c5, #f5b640, #0f2f3a.
- **Typography feel:** Montserrat, Arial, sans-serif heading direction with Open Sans, Arial, sans-serif body copy direction.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Local-service navigation with phone/contact emphasis.
- **Hero/image treatment:** water shine visual cue with direct service promise.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:** Broad “Window Cleaning Services and MORE” positioning; free-quote anchor; family-owned service proof; residential/commercial mix.

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with real local-service intent in Naperville / Downers Grove, IL.
- The site already contains useful proof: Family owned, 20+ years of experience, Free quote scheduling, Residential + commercial.
- The existing visual direction is recognizable enough for Solution A instead of a full redesign.
- There is a direct contact, quote, or booking path that can be made easier to notice.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The homepage leads with broad “and more” messaging, so a visitor has to decode whether the site is primarily window cleaning, pressure washing, carpet, or janitorial.
   - Prototype response: Lead with a clearer local cleaning promise and keep Schedule a Free Quote as the primary CTA.
2. **High:** The free-quote CTA appears in several places but is not supported by a simple what-happens-next explanation.
   - Prototype response: Move family-owned / 20+ years / residential + commercial trust proof directly below the hero.
3. **High:** Residential and commercial services are presented together without a buyer-ready grouping.
   - Prototype response: Group services by buying situation instead of a long mixed menu.
4. **High:** Strong proof — family-owned, 20+ years, client relationships — is present but not concentrated near the top.
   - Prototype response: Add a short three-step quote flow before the contact section.
5. **Medium:** Recent blog/tip content competes with the core estimate path before the service story is fully resolved.
   - Prototype response: Trim secondary distractions so the homepage feels like a direct estimate path.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Lead with a clearer local cleaning promise and keep Schedule a Free Quote as the primary CTA.
2. Move family-owned / 20+ years / residential + commercial trust proof directly below the hero.
3. Group services by buying situation instead of a long mixed menu.
4. Add a short three-step quote flow before the contact section.
5. Trim secondary distractions so the homepage feels like a direct estimate path.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
