# Helping Hands Cleaning Services — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Helping Hands Cleaning Services is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current site has a recognizable local-service identity and real trust proof, but the first-screen clarity, proof hierarchy, service grouping, and action path can be tightened without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current site family and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Get a House Cleaning Quote**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Current business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #275ea8, #4cc3ff, #f6b545, #14304a.
- **Typography feel:** Poppins, Arial, sans-serif heading direction with Open Sans, Arial, sans-serif body copy direction.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Local-service navigation with phone/contact emphasis.
- **Hero/image treatment:** clean home visual cue with direct service promise.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:** Very strong guarantee language, founder story, western-suburbs coverage, and detailed cleaning FAQs.

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with real local-service intent in Naperville, IL.
- The site already contains useful proof: Locally owned since 2001, 200% satisfaction guarantee, Bonded & insured, Background-checked teams.
- The existing visual direction is recognizable enough for Solution A instead of a full redesign.
- There is a direct contact, quote, or booking path that can be made easier to notice.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The guarantee and local-owner story are strong but arrive in a dense page where the quote path has to compete with many service and FAQ sections.
   - Prototype response: Make Get a House Cleaning Quote the dominant action.
2. **High:** Multiple cleaning types need clearer first-choice organization.
   - Prototype response: Bring the guarantee, since-2001 story, bonding/insurance, and background checks into the top proof strip.
3. **High:** 200% satisfaction guarantee, bonded/insured, and background checks should reinforce the hero immediately.
   - Prototype response: Group cleaning services around recurring, deep/move, and add-on paths.
4. **High:** The process is present but can be simplified before visitors reach the quote form.
   - Prototype response: Simplify the how-it-works flow for fast scanning.
5. **Medium:** Long FAQ/SEO sections are valuable but should not dominate the conversion story.
   - Prototype response: Keep FAQs lower as reassurance instead of first-screen burden.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Make Get a House Cleaning Quote the dominant action.
2. Bring the guarantee, since-2001 story, bonding/insurance, and background checks into the top proof strip.
3. Group cleaning services around recurring, deep/move, and add-on paths.
4. Simplify the how-it-works flow for fast scanning.
5. Keep FAQs lower as reassurance instead of first-screen burden.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
