# Xcellent Cleaning, Inc. — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Xcellent Cleaning, Inc. is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current site has a recognizable local-service identity and real trust proof, but the first-screen clarity, proof hierarchy, service grouping, and action path can be tightened without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current site family and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Book a Free On-Site Estimate**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Current business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #1f5f8b, #65b7d8, #8bc34a, #172b3a.
- **Typography feel:** Montserrat, Arial, sans-serif heading direction with Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif body copy direction.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Local-service navigation with phone/contact emphasis.
- **Hero/image treatment:** sparkle checklist visual cue with direct service promise.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:** Strong trust proof, customer-login path, residential/commercial split, and local family-owned cleaning copy.

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with real local-service intent in Naperville, IL.
- The site already contains useful proof: 25+ years experience, A+ BBB rating, Insured & bonded, Background-checked staff.
- The existing visual direction is recognizable enough for Solution A instead of a full redesign.
- There is a direct contact, quote, or booking path that can be made easier to notice.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The page has excellent proof but no single dominant action; phone, customer login, book online, and contact all compete.
   - Prototype response: Make Book a Free On-Site Estimate the primary action.
2. **High:** Residential and commercial copy is thorough but dense, making the first choice harder than necessary.
   - Prototype response: Bring the proof stack directly under the hero.
3. **High:** A+ BBB, insured/bonded, background checks, and 25+ years deserve stronger top-of-page hierarchy.
   - Prototype response: Separate residential, office, and specialty add-on service paths.
4. **High:** The current homepage buries what happens during a free on-site estimate.
   - Prototype response: Explain the estimate and checklist process before the form.
5. **Medium:** Repeated phone blocks and gallery/social paths add noise before the quote decision.
   - Prototype response: Reduce repeated secondary links while preserving phone access.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Make Book a Free On-Site Estimate the primary action.
2. Bring the proof stack directly under the hero.
3. Separate residential, office, and specialty add-on service paths.
4. Explain the estimate and checklist process before the form.
5. Reduce repeated secondary links while preserving phone access.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
