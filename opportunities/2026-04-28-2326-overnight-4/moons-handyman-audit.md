# Moon's Handyman Services — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Moon's Handyman Services is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current site has a recognizable local-service identity and real trust proof, but the first-screen clarity, proof hierarchy, service grouping, and action path can be tightened without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current site family and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Request a Free Estimate**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Current business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #0f766e, #14b8a6, #f59e0b, #102a43.
- **Typography feel:** Inter, Arial, sans-serif heading direction with Inter, Arial, sans-serif body copy direction.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Local-service navigation with phone/contact emphasis.
- **Hero/image treatment:** tool grid visual cue with direct service promise.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:** Modern app-like one-pager with Readdy-style assets, strong review claims, and a broad “what we fix” list.

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with real local-service intent in Naperville, IL.
- The site already contains useful proof: 15+ years experience, Fully insured, 5.0 · 41 Thumbtack reviews, Fast response.
- The existing visual direction is recognizable enough for Solution A instead of a full redesign.
- There is a direct contact, quote, or booking path that can be made easier to notice.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The first screen contains good proof but the repair promise, review proof, and estimate CTA are compressed into a generic one-page layout.
   - Prototype response: Keep the teal Naperville handyman identity while grounding the page in practical repair categories.
2. **High:** The service list is wide enough that homeowners may not know where their repair fits.
   - Prototype response: Make Request a Free Estimate the dominant action from hero to contact.
3. **High:** Readdy/widget and app-like residue can make the business feel less owner-operated than the proof suggests.
   - Prototype response: Bring 15+ years, fully insured, review proof, and fast response into a top trust strip.
4. **High:** The estimate process is not explained before visitors are asked to call.
   - Prototype response: Group repairs into interior, exterior, and small-job paths.
5. **Medium:** Service-area details are useful but consume attention before the most common repair paths are clear.
   - Prototype response: Remove app/widget feel and explain the estimate flow clearly.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Keep the teal Naperville handyman identity while grounding the page in practical repair categories.
2. Make Request a Free Estimate the dominant action from hero to contact.
3. Bring 15+ years, fully insured, review proof, and fast response into a top trust strip.
4. Group repairs into interior, exterior, and small-job paths.
5. Remove app/widget feel and explain the estimate flow clearly.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
