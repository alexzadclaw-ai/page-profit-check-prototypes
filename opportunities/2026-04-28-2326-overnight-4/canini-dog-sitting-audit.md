# Canini Dog Sitting — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Canini Dog Sitting is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current site has a recognizable local-service identity and real trust proof, but the first-screen clarity, proof hierarchy, service grouping, and action path can be tightened without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current site family and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Contact Canini Dog Sitting**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Current business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #8a4f2a, #f2b66d, #5aa469, #2f241d.
- **Typography feel:** Georgia, Times New Roman, serif heading direction with Nunito, Arial, sans-serif body copy direction.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Local-service navigation with phone/contact emphasis.
- **Hero/image treatment:** paw path visual cue with direct service promise.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:** Shop link and artisan-market promo on homepage, broad pet service set, Plainfield plus Marco Island service mention.

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with real local-service intent in Plainfield, IL / Marco Island, FL.
- The site already contains useful proof: 7+ years in business, Fully insured, Private engaging dog walks, Since 2019.
- The existing visual direction is recognizable enough for Solution A instead of a full redesign.
- There is a direct contact, quote, or booking path that can be made easier to notice.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The homepage mixes pet-care services, shop links, and event promotion before the main appointment/contact path is fully clear.
   - Prototype response: Lead with pet-care services and contact path before shop/event promos.
2. **High:** 7+ years, fully insured, and private dog-walk proof should support the hero.
   - Prototype response: Move 7+ years, fully insured, private walks, and since-2019 proof into a trust strip.
3. **High:** The service list is broad and needs simpler owner decision paths.
   - Prototype response: Group sitting, walking/daycare, and sleepover/event care into clear cards.
4. **High:** Facebook appointment routing can feel less direct than a business-owned contact path.
   - Prototype response: Keep the warm paw/pet-care personality while making the page easier to scan.
5. **Medium:** The Plainfield/Marco Island geography needs clearer expectations for visitors.
   - Prototype response: Clarify service geography and what information to send when contacting.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Lead with pet-care services and contact path before shop/event promos.
2. Move 7+ years, fully insured, private walks, and since-2019 proof into a trust strip.
3. Group sitting, walking/daycare, and sleepover/event care into clear cards.
4. Keep the warm paw/pet-care personality while making the page easier to scan.
5. Clarify service geography and what information to send when contacting.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
