# Fix It Quick: Handyman Services LLC - Page Profit Check

## Quick take
Fix It Quick already has the raw ingredients of a useful local handyman page: a memorable business name, direct service promise, Richmond / Tri-Cities coverage, email, phone, and a simple owner-operated feel. The main opportunity is to make the first screen answer three buyer questions faster: what do you fix, where do you work, and how do I request help today?

## What is already working
The site feels local and specific rather than generic. The tool photo, "Short, Sweet, and Simple" line, service navigation, email address, phone number, and Wix-built texture all make it clear this is a small business that can be contacted directly. The navigation includes Services, Pricing / Rates, Photo Gallery, About, Contact, and Feedback, which gives visitors enough proof paths if they want to dig deeper.

## Biggest conversion issues
The first screen gives the business name and a broad paragraph, but the quote action is easy to miss and the service categories are not visible until a visitor explores the navigation. The contact block sits low on the page, so mobile visitors with an urgent repair may need to scroll before they see phone or email. The hero copy says the business can fix, assemble, and repair, but it does not package those jobs into quick scannable decision points.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
The highest-value improvement is a tighter hero that keeps the same tool imagery and local handyman tone while adding a visible "Request a quick fix" CTA, phone/email contact strip, and three service chips for repairs, assembly, and small improvements. A second improvement is moving trust cues closer to the top: Richmond / Tri-Cities service area, professional and reliable wording, and a simple "send photos for faster estimate" prompt. A third improvement is a clearer section order: urgent contact, common jobs, why choose Fix It Quick, then the existing footer/contact details.

## SEO basics to tighten
The title already includes the business name, but the page could work harder for local intent with visible phrases like "Richmond VA handyman", "Tri-Cities handyman repairs", and "furniture assembly and small home repairs". The home page should include one focused H1, descriptive section headings, alt text for the tool image, and consistent NAP formatting for email and phone. Internal links to Services and Pricing / Rates should stay visible because those pages support buyer intent.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is not a full redesign. The realistic implementation is a source-faithful cleanup of the Wix home page: preserve the tool-photo hero, simple white/nav structure, muted gray text, direct local language, and footer contact details, then reorganize the above-the-fold content so a repair lead can contact the business in one click.

## Offer fit
This is a strong fit for the $99 Page Profit Check because the business already has a live site, real contact details, and enough message clarity to audit. The $99 offer should stay positioned as audit and diagnosis only. Implementation should be separate, with a rough order-of-magnitude estimate for a small homepage cleanup after the audit findings are reviewed.
