# Page Profit Check Audit — Landmark Steel Buildings LLC

## Quick take
Landmark Steel Buildings has the basics buyers need — owner name, phone number, service area, services, and free estimates — but the current Wix page makes a serious construction business feel unfinished. The fastest win is not a redesign; it is a cleaner version of the same simple site with a clearer hero, stronger contact path, corrected wording, service cards, and trust cues near the top.

## What is already working
- Phone and email are visible on the homepage.
- Ron Payne is named as the owner, which helps a local contractor feel reachable.
- The page states the service area: Kentucky and Southern Indiana.
- The business explains that it can help with plans, engineers, architects, and permits.
- Services include new builds, remodels, installs, and repairs.

## Biggest conversion issues
### Critical
1. **The hero does not clearly say what action a serious buyer should take.** The page has phone numbers, but the main message is fragmented and there is no polished estimate path.
   - Prototype fix: Rebuilt the hero copy around “Steel buildings built start to finish” with “Call for a Free Estimate” and “Email Plans for a Quote.”
2. **Trust is weakened by spelling, spacing, and template leftovers.** Examples include “Southern INol;,” “engineeres,” “architecs,” “abtain,” repeated contact blocks, and Wix/social defaults.
   - Prototype fix: Corrected copy, removed obvious template noise, and kept a simple Wix-like one-page rhythm.

### High
3. **Services are hard to scan.** “Install” and “Repair” run into headings, and the start-to-finish capability is buried in paragraph text.
   - Prototype fix: Converted services into three clear cards: New Steel Buildings, Remodel, Install & Repair.
4. **The process is unclear.** Buyers with or without plans need to know what happens after calling.
   - Prototype fix: Added a three-step “What happens next” section: call/email, review details, get a quote.
5. **Trust points appear too late.** Permit help, engineer/architect coordination, and local owner access should be visible early.
   - Prototype fix: Added a compact trust bar directly below the hero.

### Medium
6. The page title and SEO description can be more location/service specific.
7. The contact section repeats information but does not help visitors prepare for a quote.
8. The site would benefit from real project photos, proof of completed buildings, and clearer licensing/insurance language if available.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Clarify the top-of-page promise and free-estimate CTA.
- Keep the same straightforward builder-site feel, but remove trust-damaging copy issues.
- Make service paths legible for commercial buyers: new building, remodel, install/repair.
- Bring owner, service area, and permit/engineering coordination into the first screen.
- Add “what to send” guidance for quote-ready prospects.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a title like: `Landmark Steel Buildings LLC | Steel Building Contractor in Kentucky & Southern Indiana`.
- Add a meta description mentioning steel buildings, warehouses, offices, remodels, repairs, Kentucky, Southern Indiana, and free estimates.
- Use one H1 focused on steel buildings, not just the company name.
- Clean repeated contact/social footer links and remove Wix default social links if they are not business profiles.
- Add structured local business information if the site is rebuilt or upgraded.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a strong fit for a small surgical cleanup. The existing site appears to be a simple Wix one-page site, so the likely implementation is copy cleanup, section reordering, button/link fixes, service cards, and removal of template leftovers. If the owner has project photos, adding 3–6 real images would increase trust more than any visual redesign.

## Offer fit
Good $99 Page Profit Check candidate. The prototype demonstrates a low-risk improvement that preserves the small local contractor feel while making the business look more credible. A later implementation cleanup sprint could be positioned as a simple Wix polish, not a full rebuild.
