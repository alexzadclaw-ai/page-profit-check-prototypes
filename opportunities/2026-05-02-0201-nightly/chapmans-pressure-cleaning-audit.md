# Chapman's Pressure Cleaning LLC — Page Profit Check

## Quick take
Chapman's already has the right ingredients for a strong local exterior-cleaning site: real services, a phone number, a free-quote CTA, local address/hours, and bookable service detail. The homepage is the weak point. It currently feels like an unfinished Wix template, with a freemium banner, sparse above-the-fold messaging, generic navigation, visible account/login elements, and template leftovers that undercut trust before a visitor reaches the service list.

## What is already working
- Phone number is visible in the header: `252-955-6606`.
- Primary conversion path exists: `GET FREE QUOTE` mailto link.
- Business has a real local footprint: 222 Rose Street, Littleton, NC 27850.
- Services page contains specific offerings and some pricing/time expectations.
- Service mix is useful: house wash, roof soft wash, gutters, driveways, decks/patios/docks, commercial pads, boat/jet ski detailing.
- The Wix builder aesthetic is clean enough to improve without a full rebuild.

## Biggest conversion issues
### Critical
1. **Homepage looks unfinished because of Wix/template artifacts.** The page shows “This website was built on Wix,” account/login items, “Use tab to navigate…” text, a placeholder social link to Wix/Yelp, and footer copyright “© 2035 by Clean Bees.”
   - Prototype fix: remove template leftovers, keep the simple Wix-like header/footer rhythm, and replace with Chapman's-specific copyright/contact details.
2. **The hero does not clearly say what CPC does or where it serves.** A visitor should know within 5 seconds that this is exterior cleaning / pressure washing around Littleton, NC.
   - Prototype fix: add a clear hero headline, service/location subhead, phone CTA, and free quote CTA.

### High
3. **Trust signals are buried near the footer.** Local ownership, address, phone, hours, and soft-wash capability are not used near the top.
   - Prototype fix: add a three-column trust band directly under the hero.
4. **Services are hard to scan by buyer intent.** The services page lists many items, but the homepage does not group the most common reasons someone hires a pressure washing company.
   - Prototype fix: create concise service cards for house wash, roof wash, driveway restoration, gutters, decks/patios/docks, and commercial cleaning.
5. **The quote process is vague.** “Book Now” and “GET FREE QUOTE” exist, but visitors do not know what information to send or what happens after contact.
   - Prototype fix: add a four-step “What Happens Next” section explaining photos/address/scope/scheduling.

### Medium
6. Email text displays `chapmanspressurecleaning@gmail.com`, but one footer mailto points to `info@mysite.com`.
7. Page title and brand text are inconsistent: “CPC LLC. 2.0” vs. Chapman's Pressure Cleaning LLC.
8. SEO could better target “pressure washing,” “exterior cleaning,” and Littleton/NC service phrases.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Replace the blank/unfinished homepage with a focused local-service landing page.
- Keep the existing brand cues: CPC LLC. wording, simple centered Wix header, white/black/tan palette, Avenir/Lulo-style typography, rectangular buttons, and footer contact columns.
- Move the free quote CTA and phone number above the fold.
- Turn the service list into homeowner/commercial decision cards.
- Add “what to send for a quote” to reduce back-and-forth and increase qualified inquiries.
- Remove trust-damaging template leftovers.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Suggested title: `Chapman's Pressure Cleaning LLC | Pressure Washing in Littleton, NC`.
- Suggested meta description: `Residential and commercial exterior cleaning in Littleton, NC: house washing, roof soft washing, gutters, driveways, decks, patios, docks, and commercial pads.`
- Use one H1 focused on exterior cleaning / pressure washing.
- Add service headings with local intent: House Washing, Roof Soft Washing, Driveway Cleaning, Gutter Cleaning, Deck/Patio/Dock Cleaning, Commercial Pressure Washing.
- Make phone and email links consistent.
- Remove `© 2035 by Clean Bees` and Wix/Yelp placeholder social links.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a surgical Wix cleanup, not a rebuild. Most of the value can come from one homepage pass: clean header/footer, rewrite hero, add service cards, add trust band, add process section, and fix email/social/footer leftovers. The existing Wix service booking pages can remain in place.

## Offer fit
Strong fit for a $99 Page Profit Check because the business has clear demand and real services, but the homepage has obvious trust leaks that can be demonstrated quickly. A follow-on implementation cleanup sprint could be positioned around one Wix homepage refresh plus basic SEO/contact fixes.
