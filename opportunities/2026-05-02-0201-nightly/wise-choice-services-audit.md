# Wise Choice Services LLC — Page Profit Check

## Quick take
Wise Choice Services has the bones of a credible local security company site: direct phone/email, clear South Florida service area, years-in-business copy, service categories, testimonials, and real security imagery. The conversion problem is that those pieces feel like a default Wix page rather than a trust-first security vendor page. For security guard buyers, that creates avoidable doubt at the exact moment they need confidence.

## What is already working
- Phone number and email are visible near the top: `786-357-2477` and `wisechoiceservicesllc@gmail.com`.
- The site states South Florida service coverage and references operating since 2008.
- Core services are present: bodyguard, front gate, hotel 24-hour, and event security.
- Existing red accent color, white header, large hero, black/dark sections, Poppins/Raleway typography, and Wix gallery rhythm give the site a recognizable visual fingerprint.
- There are testimonials and social links, even if they need stronger context.

## Biggest conversion issues
### Critical
1. **Hero is too generic for a high-trust service.** “Professional and affordable services” does not immediately say who they protect, what jobs they handle, or why a buyer should call today.
   - Prototype fix: A clearer South Florida security hero with service types, since-2008 proof, and two direct CTAs.
2. **Template/Wix leftovers weaken credibility.** Visible Wix banner, “Home: Welcome” labels, generic “Submit,” and thin placeholder-style sections make the page feel unfinished.
   - Prototype fix: Remove internal labels and make the contact section business-specific.

### High
3. **Trust proof is buried and underspecified.** Since 2008 appears in paragraph copy instead of as a quick confidence signal.
   - Prototype fix: Add a trust strip directly below the hero with since-2008, South Florida, 24-hour, and direct-contact cues.
4. **Services are flat cards with generic subtitles.** “Satisfaction Guaranteed” and “Professional Service” do not help buyers choose a path.
   - Prototype fix: Reframe each service around the buyer’s likely need: personal protection, gate/access control, hotel/property coverage, event crowd support.
5. **No clear next step after contact.** Buyers do not know what information to provide or what happens after calling.
   - Prototype fix: Add a three-step “What Happens Next” section.

### Medium
6. Testimonials use generic names and no context; they help less than they could.
7. SEO basics are light: title/meta, H1 specificity, service-area phrasing, and service-specific copy can be improved.
8. The pricing nav item may create expectations but does not appear supported by the homepage flow.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Tighten the first screen around “South Florida Security Guard Service” with clear services and direct call/email actions.
- Move trust signals above the fold: operating since 2008, 24-hour availability, service area, and direct phone.
- Replace generic service subtitles with short use-case descriptions.
- Explain the intake flow: share location/date/hours, confirm coverage plan, secure schedule.
- Remove template labels and make the form/contact area feel intentional.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Page title should include service + location: “South Florida Security Guard Services | Wise Choice Services LLC.”
- H1 should include “security guard services” or location support, not only the company name.
- Add concise sections for event security, hotel security, front gate/community security, and bodyguard services.
- Add service-area copy for South Florida and relevant nearby cities if accurate.
- Add alt text to meaningful security images.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a light Wix cleanup, not a rebuild. The owner could keep the existing logo, red accent, header rhythm, hero/gallery imagery, and section order while rewriting the hero, tightening service cards, removing builder leftovers, and adding a simple next-step section.

## Offer fit
Strong fit for a $99 Page Profit Check because the site has a clear business offer and reachable contact details, but the homepage under-sells trust. A small before/after cleanup can visibly demonstrate value without requiring a full redesign.
