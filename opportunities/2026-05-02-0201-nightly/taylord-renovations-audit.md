# Taylor'd Renovations LLC — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Taylor'd Renovations has a warm, personal story and a useful renovation positioning: creative refreshes and room-by-room work for realistic budgets. The current Wix homepage, however, buries the practical offer under biography text, has weak above-the-fold trust/CTA clarity, and shows template/platform residue that can make a local contractor feel less established than they likely are.

## What is already working
- The phrase “Reinvention within reach” is memorable and fits the budget-conscious renovation angle.
- Christie and Michael’s stories create a human, local, hands-on feel.
- Services are already grouped around “Design” and “Do,” which can become a simple customer path.
- Contact details are present: email, phone, Crozet, VA, and Facebook.
- Portfolio categories give proof of varied work: landscape installation, kitchen installation, and interior design.

## Biggest conversion issues
### Critical
1. **The hero does not quickly say what the company does, where it serves, or how to start.** A visitor sees the brand and tagline, but has to scroll to understand renovation services and find contact details.
   - Prototype fix: keep the same tagline, but add “Crozet-area home renovation and design help,” a direct free quote CTA, phone CTA, and local trust strip immediately below.
2. **The contact form area appears too thin and unclear.** The page text says “Tell me a little…” and shows “Submit,” but the captured text does not expose clear fields or a reassuring next step.
   - Prototype fix: replace ambiguity with a specific quote section, email CTA with pre-filled project prompts, phone link, and a four-step “what happens next” section.

### High
3. **Trust and proof are below the fold and mixed with long biography copy.** The personal story is valuable, but it currently delays service clarity.
   - Prototype fix: move trust cues near the top and turn the “Who We Are” content into scannable Christie/Michael cards.
4. **Service names are too broad for a homeowner scanning quickly.** “Design” and “Do” are friendly but do not immediately map to cabinets, refreshes, room renovations, landscape, or DIY support.
   - Prototype fix: preserve “Design” and “Do,” add a middle “Refresh” service card, and describe task-based outcomes.
5. **Wix ad/template residue damages professionalism.** “This website was built on Wix. Create yours today” and “Proudly created with Wix.com” are visible in page text.
   - Prototype fix: remove platform advertising and keep a clean branded footer.

### Medium
6. The homepage title and SEO basics can better include Crozet, renovation, remodeling, and design.
7. Portfolio image labels are useful, but the presentation should feel more intentional than raw filename-like image references.
8. Mobile scanning likely needs stronger sections, buttons, and shorter text blocks.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Above-the-fold: clear local service statement + free quote CTA + phone CTA.
- Trust strip: Crozet, budget-minded planning, design plus hands-on work, DIY-friendly partner.
- Services: turn broad labels into homeowner intent paths: Design, Refresh, Do.
- Quote path: explain what happens after inquiry so the visitor is less hesitant.
- Professional polish: remove Wix promotional copy and dated footer language.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a descriptive title such as `Taylor'd Renovations, LLC | Crozet Home Renovation & Design`.
- Add a meta description mentioning Crozet, renovation, design, kitchens, room refreshes, and quote requests.
- Use one clear H1 tied to the existing tagline and service/location context.
- Add crawlable contact links: `mailto:` and `tel:`.
- Add visible Crozet, VA location copy near the top and in the footer.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a lightweight Wix cleanup, not a rebrand. The owner could keep the current brand name, tagline, monochrome/neutral look, serif headline feel, Oswald-like uppercase headings, simple section rhythm, and personal story. Most value comes from reordering sections, rewriting the hero/services/quote copy, removing template residue, and making the CTA more concrete.

## Offer fit
Strong fit for a $99 Page Profit Check because the site has enough existing assets and personality to improve quickly. A small implementation cleanup sprint could reasonably focus on the hero, services, quote CTA, footer, SEO title/meta, and mobile spacing without needing a full custom rebuild.
