# Dreamscape Erie LLC — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Dreamscape Erie LLC has a real local service offer, clear contact details, and a pleasant Wix landscaping template, but the homepage still reads partly unfinished. The biggest revenue leak is trust: Wix advertising, placeholder testimonials, generic copy, and vague booking steps make a homeowner hesitate before calling for a landscaping or hardscape estimate.

## What is already working
- Phone and email are visible on the homepage: `8148440102` and `Dreamscapeerie21@gmail.com`.
- The site already states the core location/service area around Erie, Pennsylvania and McKean, PA.
- The overall visual direction fits the category: garden imagery, a calm natural palette, Playfair-style serif headings, simple Wix sections, and soft outdoor photography.
- The business has an obvious entry offer: free consultation and estimate.
- Services already include seasonal work and hardscapes / pool surrounds.

## Biggest conversion issues
### Critical
1. **Template leftovers damage trust.** The page shows the Wix ad banner and three untouched testimonial placeholders: “This is your Testimonial quote…” with fake names. That makes the business look unfinished.
   - Prototype fix: removed the Wix/social-template clutter from the page experience and replaced fake testimonials with real trust/next-step content.
2. **Hero does not make the buying action specific enough.** The current CTA says “Learn More,” while the real buyer action is a free consultation/estimate.
   - Prototype fix: changed the hero CTA to “Request a Free Estimate” and added a secondary service path.

### High
1. **Trust signals are too low or unclear.** Contact details exist, but buyers do not immediately see local service area, free estimate, all-season coverage, and phone/email confidence near the top.
   - Prototype fix: added a compact trust strip directly below the hero.
2. **Services are too thin for decision-making.** “Seasonal” and “Hardscapes/ pool surrounds” are present, but the buyer has little context on what each includes.
   - Prototype fix: expanded service cards into clear task paths with short bullet examples.
3. **No visible quote process.** The homepage asks visitors to book/contact, but it does not explain what happens next.
   - Prototype fix: added a three-step “Share your project / Schedule a look / Get your estimate” flow.

### Medium
- Brand capitalization and spacing are inconsistent (`Dreamscape erie llc`, `hardwork`, phone with no separators). Keep the recognizable name, but tighten the most visible copy.
- Contact section is functional but generic; it needs to feel intentional and local.
- Social icons point to Wix accounts rather than the business, which creates confusion.
- SEO title/description should include landscaping, hardscapes, Erie PA, McKean PA, seasonal property care, and pool surrounds.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Replace generic hero copy with a clear landscaping/hardscaping value proposition and a specific estimate CTA.
2. Put local trust and contact proof above the fold.
3. Remove fake testimonials and irrelevant Wix social links.
4. Reframe services around homeowner intent: seasonal property care, hardscapes/pool surrounds, and outdoor oasis planning.
5. Add a simple quote flow so prospects know what happens after they reach out.
6. Tighten the contact block with phone, email, address, and a direct estimate subject line.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Suggested title: `Dreamscape Erie LLC | Landscaping & Hardscapes in Erie, PA`
- Suggested meta description: `Dreamscape Erie LLC creates landscaping, seasonal property care, hardscapes, pool surrounds, patios, and outdoor spaces around Erie and McKean, Pennsylvania.`
- Add one H1 only, focused on the business and service area.
- Use descriptive service headings: `Seasonal Landscaping`, `Hardscapes & Pool Surrounds`, `Outdoor Living Spaces`.
- Add alt text that describes the actual service intent rather than generic stock labels.
- If real project photos are available, replace stock images and add project/location captions.

## Realistic implementation angle
This does not need a full redesign. The fastest practical fix is a surgical Wix cleanup:
- Update hero headline/subheadline/button text.
- Remove or hide Wix/template leftovers and fake testimonials.
- Add a four-item trust strip near the top.
- Expand the two existing service blocks with clearer bullets and add one “outdoor oasis” planning card if accurate.
- Add a simple three-step estimate process before the contact form.
- Clean up footer social links unless business-owned social pages exist.
