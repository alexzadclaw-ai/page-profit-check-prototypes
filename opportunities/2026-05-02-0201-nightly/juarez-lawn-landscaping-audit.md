# Page Profit Check Audit — Juarez Lawn & Landscaping LLC

## Quick take
Juarez Lawn & Landscaping LLC already has the essentials online: a real local service area, clear service categories, email, phone, and a simple way to start contact. The biggest opportunity is to turn the Wix homepage from a booking-template page into a quote-focused local services page that immediately answers: what do they do, where do they work, why trust them, and what should a visitor do next?

## What is already working
- The site communicates local outdoor services in Dodge City, KS.
- The business has a useful “Since 2018” trust point.
- The homepage lists core buyer-intent services: sod, landscaping, trees, fencing, and concrete.
- Contact details are visible: `landscapingjuarez0@gmail.com` and `6203909787`.
- The Wix structure is simple enough for a surgical cleanup rather than a full rebuild.

## Biggest conversion issues

### Critical
1. **The top of the page is not quote-focused.** “Welcome to Juarez outdoor services LLC” is friendly, but it does not quickly say what services are offered, where, or what action to take.
2. **Booking-widget pricing looks untrustworthy for project work.** Items like “SOD Installation — 2 US dollars” and “Flat-work concrete — 8 US dollars” can make visitors doubt whether the site is current or accurate.
3. **Wix/template artifacts hurt credibility.** The Wix banner, “Home: Welcome,” duplicate price labels, and generic submit flow make the business look unfinished.

### High
1. **Trust is buried below the opening message.** “Since 2018,” local area, residential/commercial work, and quote process should appear near the top.
2. **Services are listed as appointment products instead of project paths.** Landscaping, sod, fencing, concrete, and tree work need short intent-based explanations.
3. **The next step is vague.** Visitors need to know whether to call, email photos, request a quote, or book online.

### Medium
1. Page title capitalization is inconsistent: “Juarez lawn & Landscaping LLC.”
2. Phone number formatting should be easier to tap and read: `620-390-9787`.
3. SEO basics are thin: homepage copy should include Dodge City, KS, service terms, and “quote” language.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Replace the generic welcome with a specific hero: landscaping, lawn care, concrete, fencing, and tree work in Dodge City.
- Use one primary CTA: “Request a quote.”
- Move trust signals directly below the hero: local, since 2018, residential/commercial, easy quote.
- Convert the booking-widget list into three clearer paths: lawn/landscaping, trees, and build/repair.
- Remove fake-looking fixed prices for project work and explain that pricing depends on scope, access, materials, and timing.
- Add a simple “what happens next” flow: send details/photos, confirm scope, receive quote.
- Clean up footer/contact copy so it feels business-specific rather than template-generated.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a stronger title such as: `Juarez Lawn & Landscaping LLC | Dodge City Outdoor Services`.
- Add a concise meta description mentioning Dodge City, KS and the main services.
- Put primary service terms in visible headings: landscaping, sod installation, tree trimming/removal, fence repair/install, flat-work concrete.
- Add consistent NAP formatting: Juarez Lawn & Landscaping LLC, Dodge City, KS, 620-390-9787.
- If they keep Wix, remove visible editor labels and ensure contact/book pages share the same updated service language.

## Realistic implementation angle
This does not need a full redesign. The fastest win is a light homepage cleanup inside the current Wix style: keep the simple nav, outdoor image feel, lime/green landscape color direction, service cards, and contact section, but replace confusing template/booking elements with quote-focused sections. If the business wants to stay on Wix, these edits are realistic as copy/section changes plus a small layout cleanup.

## Offer fit
Strong fit for a $99 Page Profit Check. The prototype demonstrates a low-risk cleanup that could make the site feel more credible quickly: clearer hero, trustworthy service paths, visible local proof, and a specific quote CTA. A follow-on implementation sprint could be positioned only after they see the mini-audit and decide whether they want the Wix page updated.
