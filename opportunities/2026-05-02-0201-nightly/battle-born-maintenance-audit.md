# Battle Born Maintenance LLC — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Battle Born Maintenance already has a memorable local personality, real owner story, phone numbers, services, and customer quotes. The biggest conversion leak is trust/clarity: the live URL is a mismatched Wix account domain (`kingdomcoachinglv.wixsite.com/bbmllc`), the Wix banner is visible, the hero is long and shouty, and the first screen asks visitors to work too hard before they know what to do next.

## What is already working
- Strong local name and recognizable Battle Born visual identity.
- Phone number is visible near the top and repeated throughout the page.
- Clear owner-led story: Nate, Las Vegas native, 20+ years of experience, licensed and bonded.
- Broad service coverage for residential and commercial handyman/property maintenance needs.
- Real customer quotes from Paradise and Summerlin that can support trust.
- Distinctive design DNA: gray header, black/red Battle Born watermark hero, red stencil-style headings, blue service section, dark service cards, and bold call-now language.

## Biggest conversion issues
### Critical
1. **Trust damage from mismatched Wix account URL and Wix ad banner.** A handyman site hosted under `kingdomcoachinglv` with the Wix promotional banner can make prospects question whether the business is active, legitimate, or professionally maintained.
2. **Hero message is too dense for first-time visitors.** The homepage opens with a long all-caps paragraph instead of a fast promise, service scope, trust proof, and next step.

### High
3. **CTA path is not specific enough.** “Call Battle Born” is frequent, but the page should clearly separate urgent calls from quote/project-detail requests.
4. **Trust proof is buried.** Licensed/bonded, 20+ years, owner identity, and service areas appear later instead of directly supporting the first call-to-action.
5. **Services are present but hard to scan by task.** The list is useful, but the visual hierarchy makes it easy to miss what jobs are covered.

### Medium
6. Several typos and rough phrases reduce polish: “YOUR IN GOOD HANDS,” “recomendation,” “Instal,” inconsistent capitalization, and heavy spacing.
7. SEO basics need tightening around “Las Vegas handyman,” “property maintenance,” services, and local areas.
8. The contact form section could better explain what happens after someone sends project details.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Remove/replace trust-damaging template residue and move the business onto a clean branded domain or at minimum a branded Wix domain path.
- Keep the same Battle Born look, but shorten the hero to: who they help, what they do, why to trust Nate, and how to request help.
- Add a top trust strip: Las Vegas native owner, 20+ years experience, licensed & bonded, residential/commercial.
- Add two clear CTAs: “Call for a free quote” and “Send project details.”
- Reorganize services into quick cards plus a scannable task list.
- Put testimonials and contact details into intentional sections instead of letting them feel like leftover page blocks.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Suggested title: `Battle Born Maintenance LLC | Las Vegas Handyman & Property Maintenance`.
- Suggested meta description: `Battle Born Maintenance LLC helps Las Vegas homeowners and offices with repairs, painting, carpentry, low-voltage electrical, maintenance, and small remodel projects.`
- Use one clean H1 focused on Las Vegas handyman/property maintenance.
- Add internal anchor labels for Services, About, Testimonials, and Contact.
- Include city/service phrases naturally: Las Vegas handyman, property maintenance, home repairs, office repairs, painting, carpentry, drywall, TV mounting, Summerlin, Henderson, North Las Vegas.
- Correct spelling/grammar without removing Nate’s owner-led voice.

## Realistic implementation angle
This does not need a full redesign. The fastest useful sprint is a surgical cleanup: keep the gray header, logo, stencil/Impact personality, red/black/blue color system, watermark backgrounds, service-card rhythm, customer quotes, and owner story. Then remove the Wix/template trust issues, tighten first-screen copy, add a clean CTA flow, and make the service/contact sections easier to scan on mobile.

## Offer fit
Strong fit for a $99 Page Profit Check because the issues are visible, practical, and easy to demonstrate in a one-page prototype. An optional implementation cleanup sprint would likely be a small branded-domain/Wix cleanup or static landing page rebuild in the rough range of $650–$1,500 depending on whether the existing Wix account/domain/form setup is reused.
