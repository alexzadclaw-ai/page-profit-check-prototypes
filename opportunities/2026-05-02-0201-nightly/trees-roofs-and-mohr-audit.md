# Trees, Roofs and Mohr LLC — Page Profit Check

## Quick take
Trees/Roofs and Mohr already has the core ingredients of a trustworthy local contractor page: real phone/email, Cedar Rapids location, a personal origin story, broad practical services, and “licensed and insured” messaging. The biggest issue is not the business offer; it is that the Wix template makes the page feel unfinished and harder to act on than it should.

## What is already working
- Clear local contact info: `treesroofsandmohr@gmail.com` and `319-826-0003` appear on-page.
- The page communicates Cedar Rapids, IA service area.
- The owner story feels human and specific: opened in 2020 after seeing people get taken advantage of.
- The service range is valuable for homeowners who want one reliable contractor.
- The existing serif headings and simple Wix section rhythm create a recognizable, modest local-business feel.

## Biggest conversion issues
| Severity | Finding | Why it matters | Prototype fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| Critical | The primary CTA is generic (“Learn More”) instead of job-focused. | A homeowner with a leak, tree issue, or siding problem needs an estimate/call path immediately. | Hero CTA changed to “Call for an Estimate” with phone link and repeated contact path. |
| Critical | Template leftovers and dead-end navigation (“Blog,” “Book Online,” “Shop,” subscribe form, Wix banner) lower trust. | Unused Wix sections make a contractor look less current and less established. | Prototype removes unrelated nav/subscription clutter and keeps business-specific paths. |

| High | Trust signal is present but buried/awkwardly worded (“Licensed and Insurance”). | Contractor customers look for license/insurance, local area, and fair pricing before they call. | Trust strip added immediately below hero: opened 2020, licensed/insured, fair rates, phone. |

| Medium | Copy has typos and repetitive generic template language. | Minor writing issues can make a real local business feel less professional. | Kept the same voice while tightening grammar and scanning. |

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Keep the same Wix-style structure, serif headings, and simple local-contractor tone.
2. Replace vague CTAs with phone/email estimate paths.
3. Remove trust-damaging unfinished template items.
4. Reframe the broad service list into homeowner task paths.
5. Move trust details above the fold and clarify “Licensed and Insured.”
6. Add a simple next-step section so visitors know what happens after they call.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Page title should include service + city: “Trees/Roofs and Mohr LLC | Cedar Rapids Roofing, Tree Service & Remodeling.”
- Meta description should mention Cedar Rapids, roofing, siding, tree work, remodeling, fences and snow removal.
- Add separate service headings for roofing, tree service, siding, remodeling and snow removal.
- Use consistent business name formatting: “Trees/Roofs and Mohr LLC.”
- Remove or noindex unused Blog/Shop/Book pages if they are empty.
- Add alt text to photos when editing in Wix.

## Realistic implementation angle
This does not require a full rebrand. It is a surgical cleanup inside the existing Wix site: update hero copy, edit nav, remove unused template blocks, reorganize service cards, add a trust strip, and add a simple “how it works” section. A lightweight implementation sprint could be enough if the site owner has Wix access.

## Offer fit
Strong fit for a $99 Page Profit Check because the site is already public and credible, but the conversion problems are visible and fixable quickly. The prototype demonstrates a cleanup the owner can recognize as the same site, just clearer and more trustworthy.
