# Atlas Pro Clean Air LLC — Page Profit Check

## Quick take
Atlas Pro Clean Air has the right ingredients for a strong local home-service site: a visible phone number, broad Utah/Salt Lake County service coverage, emergency availability, and clear air duct / dryer vent services. The current homepage, though, acts mostly like a header plus a large equipment photo and contact form. A visitor can call, but they do not quickly see what is cleaned, why it matters, what happens after contacting the company, or enough proof to feel safe booking.

## What is already working
- Phone number is prominent in the header and repeated near the form.
- Hours and 24-hour emergency availability are visible above the fold.
- Navigation includes service-specific pages for air duct cleaning, dryer vent cleaning, commercial, industrial, retail, coil cleaning, and sanitizing.
- The blue-and-white color direction, bold logo area, badge graphics, and deep-blue contact block create a recognizable local contractor look.
- Existing service pages contain useful copy that can be pulled forward to the homepage.

## Biggest conversion issues
### Critical
1. **Homepage does not explain the service before asking for contact.** The hero area is mostly a giant coil image; there is no clear headline, benefit statement, or task-specific CTA above the fold.
   - Prototype fix: added a same-style hero card over the equipment image with a clear local service headline, benefit copy, and two CTAs: call for a free quote / request service online.
2. **Trust is weakened by site-builder/template residue.** The visible Wix promotional banner makes the business look less established at the exact moment visitors are deciding whether to invite a contractor into their home.
   - Prototype fix: removed the Wix ad/banner while preserving the site's header rhythm, logo area, badges, nav bar, and blue contact section.
3. **Phone-link mismatch risk.** The visible number is `(385) 497 6505`, while one captured homepage `tel:` link pointed to `385 479 6505`; this can leak calls if repeated elsewhere.
   - Prototype fix: standardized all call CTAs to `(385) 497-6505`.

### High
1. **Services are hidden behind navigation.** The homepage does not quickly route homeowners to air duct cleaning, dryer vent cleaning, or commercial duct cleaning.
   - Prototype fix: added three concise service cards using the existing service language and buyer intent paths.
2. **No “what happens next” section.** Visitors do not know whether they are requesting a quote, inspection, appointment, or emergency call.
   - Prototype fix: added a 3-step process: call/send form, get a free quote, service/cleaning.
3. **Proof is present but not contextual.** Badges and a Google rating widget appear visually, but there is little nearby copy tying trust to service area, availability, or specific outcomes.
   - Prototype fix: added a trust strip immediately under the hero with service area, hours, emergency availability, and direct contact.

### Medium
- Footer copyright is dated 2022, which can make the business appear inactive.
- The contact form has a CAPTCHA/form look, but no explanation of response time.
- The Google map/contact area is useful but visually arrives without service reassurance.
- SEO title is service-focused, but homepage body copy is too thin for local air duct / dryer vent intent.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. **Clarify the above-fold offer:** “Cleaner ducts, safer dryer vents, and a healthier home” with Utah/Salt Lake County service context.
2. **Make phone and form CTAs specific:** “Call for a free quote” and “Request service online,” not generic “Submit.”
3. **Pull service paths onto the homepage:** air duct cleaning, dryer vent cleaning, and commercial/industrial/retail duct cleaning.
4. **Add what-happens-next expectations:** simple quote and scheduling steps reduce hesitation.
5. **Remove trust-damaging leftovers:** especially the Wix promotional banner and inconsistent phone link.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Add visible homepage copy for: `Utah air duct cleaning`, `dryer vent cleaning`, `Utah County`, `Salt Lake County`, `commercial duct cleaning`.
- Use one H1 with the primary service/location promise.
- Give each service card a crawlable heading and short description.
- Update copyright/current-year signals.
- If a real business address or service-area schema is available, add LocalBusiness/Service schema later.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a lightweight homepage cleanup rather than a full rebuild. The business could keep Wix, keep the same logo/header/nav/contact section, and add a more useful hero overlay, service-card section, trust strip, and process section. The highest-value technical fix is auditing every `tel:` link so the displayed phone number and clickable phone number match.
