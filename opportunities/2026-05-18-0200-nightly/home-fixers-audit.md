# Home Fixers LLC Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take

Home Fixers already has the bones of a useful San Antonio handyman site: a clear local phone number, real service categories, service-area coverage, certification badges, a contact form, and recognizable Home Fixers branding. The biggest opportunity is first-screen clarity. Right now the hero looks polished, but visitors have to scroll before they see proof, service details, and the real estimate path.

## What is already working

- The page has a memorable Home Fixers logo and a consistent navy, burnt-orange, white, and light-gray palette.
- The hero image and large headline create an immediate home-improvement feel.
- The site lists many high-intent services, including plumbing repairs, drywall repairs, cabinets, fixture installs, appliance installation, painting, pressure washing, TV mounting, and make-ready work.
- Phone and email details are present, including the main Home Fixers email and Decor Team email.
- The bottom contact section includes a form, service-area list, phone number, and email links.
- The page already includes trust language such as locally owned, highest quality service, licensed and insured, and certification badges.
- The current Wix layout is simple and recognizable, so this can be improved without turning it into a generic contractor template.

## Biggest conversion issues

- The hero headline says home repairs and decor services, but it does not quickly explain San Antonio handyman work, make-ready help, or what action to take next.
- The main hero CTA is small and points back to the same page instead of clearly pushing call, email, or estimate request action.
- The phone number is visible in the header but not treated as a primary click-to-call CTA.
- Trust cues are too low on the page. Licensed and insured, badges, service area, and service details should support the first contact decision sooner.
- The intro copy is dense and formal, so it is hard to scan on a first visit.
- The service list is useful but buried below several tall sections.
- The top Wix banner and the header Login link make the site feel more template-like than local-service-ready.
- The About and Our Decor Team pages still show template/title leftovers such as "M&B Remodeling," which can hurt trust and SEO polish.
- Footer social links include Wix-owned destinations for Facebook and YouTube rather than clearly owned Home Fixers profiles.
- The contact form is strong in concept, but the visitor only reaches it after a long scroll.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements

- Replace the top Wix promo space with a useful local trust/action strip: San Antonio handyman repairs, phone, and estimate link.
- Make the hero CTA split into two clear actions: call 210-777-1123 and email project details.
- Add a short hero subheadline that names the core work: repairs, installations, small upgrades, apartments, AirBnB make-ready, and decor help.
- Move concise trust proof into the first screen: licensed and insured, residential and commercial, and easy estimate options.
- Keep the existing hero photo, logo, header geometry, navy/brown palette, and Wix-style spacious layout.
- Convert the long intro copy into shorter paragraphs plus a scannable common-services list.
- Keep the current service-card section but make each card support the quote path.
- Keep the bottom navy contact section while clarifying labels around project details, address/service area, and photos.

## SEO basics to tighten

- Use a home-page title focused on service and location, for example: "San Antonio Handyman and Home Repair | Home Fixers LLC."
- Use one clear H1 that includes home repair or handyman services and San Antonio context.
- Fix page titles that still reference "M&B Remodeling" on About and Work pages.
- Add concise copy mentioning handyman services, home repairs, fixture installation, drywall repairs, plumbing repairs, cabinet repair, make-ready services, and San Antonio/Bexar County.
- Add descriptive alt text for the logo, hero image, service cards, badges, and project photos.
- Confirm social links point to Home Fixers-owned profiles, not Wix defaults.
- Make phone, email, service areas, hours, and business name consistent across every page.
- Consider LocalBusiness schema later if Wix allows it.

## Realistic implementation angle

This should be a surgical Wix page update rather than a full rebuild. The practical path is:

1. Keep the Home Fixers logo, hero image, navy/brown palette, service cards, badge row, and bottom contact section.
2. Replace template distractions near the top with local trust and stronger contact actions.
3. Rework the hero and intro copy for scan speed.
4. Pull licensed and insured, service-area, and common-services proof closer to the first screen.
5. Fix page titles, social links, button destinations, alt text, and footer/contact consistency.
