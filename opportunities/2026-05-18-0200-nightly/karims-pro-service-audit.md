# Karim's Pro Service Etc Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take

Karim's Pro Service Etc already has the raw ingredients of a useful local-service page: a real phone number, direct email, service categories, family-owned positioning, testimonials, and a strong worker hero image. The main opportunity is to turn the broad Wix homepage into a quote-first page that quickly says what jobs Karim wants, where the business works, and whether visitors should call or email project details.

## What is already working

- The phone number is visible in the header, hero copy, and contact area.
- The page has clear service categories: carpentry, plumbing, pressure washing, landscaping, and car washing.
- The existing visual identity is recognizable: cream header, dark olive services area, burnt-orange CTA, blue contact section, tall service icons, and full-width Wix sections.
- The family-owned and operated message is useful for trust.
- The testimonials section provides social proof, even if the quotes are generic.
- The contact section includes phone, email, service areas, and a form.

## Biggest conversion issues

- The top Vista x Wix banner and generic Wix social links make the page feel unfinished.
- The hero headline is long and general. It says the business does quality work, but it does not quickly sort visitors into the highest-value services.
- The main `GET A FREE QUOTE` button points back to the same page instead of directly opening a call, email, or form path.
- The page tries to cover handyman work, pressure washing, landscaping, plumbing, and vehicle care with equal weight. That breadth can make the offer feel less focused.
- Phone and email are present, but the page does not clearly tell visitors what to send for a quote: city, job type, timeline, and photos.
- The service-area list is confusing and needs cleanup. `Waynesbor` appears misspelled, and the mix of Georgia cities plus Tennessee and Alabama needs clearer framing.
- The form button says `SCHEDULE SERVICE`, while the rest of the page says `GET A FREE QUOTE`.
- The email wraps awkwardly in the contact area, which hurts polish.
- The page title is still `HOME | My Site`, so SEO and professionalism are both undercut.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements

- Replace the template top banner with a small trust/quote strip while keeping the same geometry.
- Rewrite the first screen around a direct quote path: handyman and exterior services in Georgia, jobs big or small, call or email job details.
- Make the primary actions explicit: call the phone number and email project details.
- Keep the five service icons but tighten the descriptions around scannable quote categories.
- Add a small trust strip near the hero: family-owned, timely quality work, home/business/vehicle requests.
- Add quote instructions close to the services and form: city, service needed, timeline, and photos.
- Remove or replace generic Wix social links with real business profiles only.
- Clean up the areas list and fix spelling.
- Align the form CTA with the page goal: request quote, not schedule service.

## SEO basics to tighten

- Change the title from `HOME | My Site` to something like `Karim's Pro Service Etc | Georgia Handyman & Exterior Services`.
- Use one clear H1 that includes the core services and Georgia/location intent.
- Add a short meta description mentioning handyman, pressure washing, landscaping, plumbing, carpentry, and Georgia.
- Add descriptive alt text to the hero image and service icons.
- Make phone, email, services, and areas consistent across the page.
- If Wix allows it later, add LocalBusiness schema with phone, email, area served, and service categories.
- Replace template social links with actual business profiles or remove the icons.

## Realistic implementation angle

This does not need a rebuild. The practical path is a focused Wix page edit:

1. Keep the existing header, hero photo, service icons, color palette, and section order.
2. Replace the template banner and generic social links.
3. Rewrite the hero and service copy for a quote-first path.
4. Add phone and email CTAs above the fold.
5. Tighten service-area wording and form labels.
6. Update the title, H1, meta description, and image alt text.
