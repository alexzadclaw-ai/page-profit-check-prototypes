# Home Hero Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take

Home Hero already has a memorable personality: the red/yellow superhero palette, bold script logo, gutter-service hero photo, and direct quote form make it feel local and owner-operated. The biggest opportunity is not a redesign. It is a tighter conversion path: make the services, Storrs/Mansfield availability, and call/text/email next steps easier to understand without losing the playful Home Hero feel.

## What is already working

- The page has a distinctive visual identity with bright red, yellow CTA accents, oversized script typography, and a real home-service photo.
- The primary service categories are already present: gutter cleaning, window cleaning, leaf cleanup, holiday lighting, interior home cleaning, and other home help.
- The page includes a quote request form with useful fields for contact preference, best time, address, service type, scheduling, and extra job notes.
- Contact details are visible in the footer: Nico Gamarra, cthomehero@gmail.com, and 954-299-2229.
- The site has direct social links to Facebook, Instagram, and Nextdoor.
- The existing tone feels personal and local rather than corporate, which is a good fit for homeowners hiring help around the house.

## Biggest conversion issues

- The first screen says "High-Quality Home Services," but it does not clearly say the service area or the most common services until visitors keep reading.
- The hero paragraph on the live page is visually hard to read and starts partially off the left edge, so important service copy is easy to miss.
- The quote form is helpful, but the surrounding copy has a typo: "Fill out the from to the right." That hurts trust right before the visitor is asked to submit details.
- The call path is underused. The header has "Call Today," but the page could also make call/text/email options clearer near the form.
- Trust cues are present but thin. "Military taught Attention to Detail," custom quotes, and satisfaction guarantee are useful, but they need clearer context.
- The free Wix banner and "© 2035" footer date make the site feel less finished than the service likely is.
- SEO basics are close, but the page should more consistently connect Home Hero with home services, Storrs, Mansfield, CT, and the specific service categories.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements

- Keep the existing superhero-style identity, but make the hero copy more readable and local: Storrs, Mansfield, nearby CT homeowners.
- Keep the same hero photo and page geometry, but add a clearer "Free Quote" or call/text cue above the fold.
- Rewrite the quote-section intro so it explains what homeowners should send: service needed, address, best contact method, timing, and photos/details if helpful.
- Add a small "simple next steps" cue beside the form so the quote request feels quick instead of heavy.
- Keep the same form fields, but tighten labels and make the service options more homeowner-friendly.
- Update the footer date and contact block while keeping the red footer and social icons.
- Add compact trust language around reliability, communication, custom quotes, and military-taught attention to detail.

## SEO basics to tighten

- Use a location/service page title such as "Home Hero | Home Services in Storrs and Mansfield CT."
- Make the visible H1 include the core service category, for example "High-Quality Home Services in Storrs CT" if the design can support the extra words.
- Add concise copy mentioning gutter cleaning, window cleaning, leaf cleanup, holiday lighting, interior cleaning, and handyman/home help.
- Add clear local phrasing for Storrs, Mansfield, UConn area, and nearby Connecticut homeowners where accurate.
- Add alt text for the gutter/roof hero image and social icons.
- Fix small copy issues like "from" instead of "form" and "Let me what I can do."
- If Wix allows it later, add LocalBusiness schema with phone, email, service area, and same-as social links.

## Realistic implementation angle

This is a surgical Wix page refresh, not a rebuild:

1. Keep the logo, red/yellow palette, brush fonts, hero photo, nav, form, and red footer.
2. Improve the hero copy, quote explanation, and CTA language.
3. Add local service-area clarity around Storrs and Mansfield.
4. Clean up the form labels, footer date, and typo-level polish.
5. Add a few trust cues near the quote path so the visitor feels confident before submitting.
