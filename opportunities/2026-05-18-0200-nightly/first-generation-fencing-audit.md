# First Generation Fencing Inc - Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
First Generation Fencing Inc is a strong Page Profit Check fit because the live Wix page already has real local-contractor assets: a clear fence-installation offer, Sacramento-area targeting, phone number, email, license number, free-estimate language, warranty language, project photos, a customer testimonial, and recognizable logo/brand images.

The opportunity is not a full redesign. It is a brand-faithful Wix homepage cleanup that keeps the tan/navy palette, symmetric logo header, boxed nav, large project-photo backgrounds, big serif headings, highlighted text strips, testimonial carousel feel, and roomy footer, while making the free-estimate path, fence services, service areas, and trust cues easier to act on.

## What is already working
- The live page title is focused: `Fence Installation Sacramento | First Generation Fencing Inc`.
- The meta description already mentions a licensed Sacramento fence contractor, wood/vinyl/chain-link fences, and free quotes.
- The phone number is visible near the top and in the footer: `916-450-8016`.
- The email address is visible and linked: `Firstgenerationfencing.inc@gmail.com`.
- The license number appears repeatedly: `Lic# 1135897` / `CA Lic #1135897`.
- The hero quickly communicates fence installation in Sacramento, licensed/insured, reliable, affordable, and free estimates.
- Real fence project photos and branded logo images are present, which is stronger than generic stock imagery.
- The page lists important trust cues: over 9 years of experience, licensed and insured, transparent pricing, craftsmanship, clean job sites, quality wood, growing small business, and family owned/operated.
- The live footer names Sacramento, Elk Grove, Davis, and nearby areas.
- A testimonial from Akilah D in Sacramento is present in the page content.

## Biggest conversion issues
1. **Critical: several sections look unfinished or broken.** The beige section after the warranty area contains a large empty white card and a black rectangle, and the testimonial carousel hides or loses the quote body visually. This makes the site feel less reliable than the business likely is.

2. **Critical: the estimate path is present but not strong enough.** The page says free estimates and has a phone CTA, but it does not clearly tell visitors what to do next, such as calling, emailing photos, sharing the project address, or choosing a fence type.

3. **High: service areas are not prominent enough above the fold.** Sacramento is in the hero headline, but Elk Grove, Davis, Yolo County, and nearby areas matter for local-fit scanning and should be easier to see before the footer.

4. **High: services are implied instead of scannable.** The live page says wood, vinyl, metal, and more, while the meta description mentions chain link. Visitors should be able to scan fence types and gate/hardware work quickly.

5. **High: trust cues are useful but visually noisy.** The Why Choose Us list uses default bullets plus checkmarks and white text strips. It is recognizable, but it could be cleaner without losing the original Wix look.

6. **Medium: the header has contact information but no click-to-call style action.** The phone number is visible, but mobile visitors and urgent quote shoppers benefit from a clearer call button and repeated CTA near the bottom.

7. **Medium: template residue remains.** The Wix promo strip, Wix footer credit, and placeholder `mailto:info@mysite.com` link reduce polish.

8. **Medium: SEO basics are partly done but still need cleanup.** Title and description are good, but the page uses multiple H1s, social/image alt text is inconsistent, and local service area/service-type language could be structured better.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Keep the original business identity, logo images, project photos, tan/navy palette, serif display typography, boxed nav, and Wix-style section rhythm.
- Replace the top Wix promo strip with a business-focused notice that keeps the same 50px visual slot while adding a call action.
- Add a stronger hero CTA pair: **Get a Free Quote** and **Email Photos**.
- Bring Sacramento, Elk Grove, Davis, Yolo County, and nearby areas into the estimate/service copy instead of leaving them mainly in the footer.
- Convert the empty white-card area into a simple **Free estimate path** section that tells visitors to call or email photos, address, and fence style.
- Make wood, vinyl, metal, chain link, and gates/hardware scannable without inventing a completely new service catalog.
- Make the customer testimonial visible, with star context, reviewer name, and carousel dots retained.
- Improve the footer into a practical contact block with the original company image, email, social/review icons, phone, license, service areas, and repeated CTAs.
- Remove or replace visible placeholder/template residue where possible.
- Clean heading hierarchy to one H1 and semantic H2 sections.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Keep the current focused title, but consider adding Elk Grove/Davis/Yolo County in on-page copy or a longer meta description where appropriate.
- Use one clear H1 for the primary offer and location.
- Convert secondary large statements into H2/H3 or styled text instead of additional H1s.
- Add descriptive alt text to project photos and logo images.
- Ensure the email is consistent everywhere and remove `info@mysite.com` from the live page.
- Add a LocalBusiness/HomeAndConstructionBusiness schema block later after confirming the exact business address, service area, social links, and preferred contact email.
- Add internal service-page links from service-type phrases such as wood fencing, vinyl fencing, chain-link fencing, gates, and recent jobs if those pages have useful content.

## Realistic implementation angle
This should be positioned as a focused Wix homepage clarity pass, not a rebrand. The current page already has the selling points: licensed local contractor, real work photos, free estimates, warranty, phone/email, service areas, and a testimonial. The best implementation would keep the current visual personality while cleaning up the broken-looking sections and making the quote path clearer.

A practical implementation sprint would likely include hero CTA improvements, service-area copy placement, a rebuilt estimate/contact section, a readable testimonial section, footer/contact cleanup, mobile CTA behavior, placeholder-link removal, image alt text, and heading/SEO cleanup.
