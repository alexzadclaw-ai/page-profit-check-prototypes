# Brandon Construction - Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Brandon Construction is a good fit for a focused Page Profit Check because the site already has useful raw material: a service list, New Jersey service area, phone number, email, license number, contact form, and a recognizable construction-template look.

The main opportunity is not a full redesign. It is a brand-faithful Wix homepage improvement that keeps the dark navy header, large construction hero, yellow accent system, three service cards, about section, yellow proof band, project gallery, snow-removal/contact section, and simple footer while making the business feel more real, local, and ready for estimates immediately.

## What is already working
- The business name is visible in the header: Brandon Construction.
- The site has a direct phone number and quote link: `848-223-1770`.
- The site has a direct email link: `br4nd0nconstruction@gmail.com`.
- The page lists broad service categories that match local remodeling, hardscape, handyman, and seasonal property-service intent.
- The contact section says the company services all New Jersey.
- The New Jersey license number is visible: `Lic# 13VH13023200`.
- The page already follows a familiar contractor-site flow: hero, services, about, stats, projects, contact.
- The dark navy, white, and construction-yellow palette is appropriate for a construction/remodeling company.
- The large hero and service-card layout create a credible template foundation that can be improved without changing the entire identity.

## Biggest conversion issues
1. **Critical: the first screen has no direct estimate CTA.** The hero has a strong headline, but visitors do not get an immediate phone or email action until much later.

2. **Critical: trust cues are buried.** The New Jersey service area, phone, email, and license number are all useful, but they appear near the bottom instead of supporting the first impression.

3. **High: visible template leftovers weaken credibility.** The top Wix banner, `HOME | My Site` title, empty meta description, `© 2035 by Sphere Constructions`, and generic Wix social links make the page feel unfinished.

4. **High: the service list is broad but messy.** Asterisk lists, inconsistent capitalization, typos like `titles` instead of likely `tiles`, and mixed categories make it harder for homeowners to understand what Brandon Construction actually wants to quote.

5. **High: the stats strip includes a damaging placeholder.** `0 Contractors Appointed` looks accidental and undercuts trust.

6. **Medium: project images have no context.** The project grid is visually useful, but there are no captions, job types, locations, before/after framing, or proof that ties the images to the business.

7. **Medium: contact flow is too late and too generic.** The form exists, but the page does not clearly tell visitors what to send, whether to call or text, or how to request an estimate efficiently.

8. **Medium: stock-like imagery is doing most of the visual work.** The source site uses Wix/Unsplash-style images. Since those are already on the site, they can be reused, but the page should avoid implying they are completed local projects unless confirmed.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Replace the Wix promo strip with a small local estimate strip that preserves the same top-bar geometry while improving conversion.
- Keep the existing header/nav rhythm but add a clear quote action near the navigation.
- Keep the same hero headline style and construction image, then add direct `Call for an Estimate` and `Email Project Details` CTAs.
- Add a New Jersey/remodeling subline in the hero so visitors understand the local residential/commercial angle immediately.
- Reorganize service copy into three clearer categories: home renovations, landscape/hardscape, and handyman/general services.
- Preserve the three-card services layout and original images, but improve capitalization, scannability, and wording.
- Move license and service-area credibility into the about/stats area instead of hiding it only in contact.
- Replace the `0 Contractors Appointed` stat with the New Jersey license cue.
- Add light project captions so the gallery supports the sales story without overclaiming real completed-project proof.
- Strengthen the contact section with call/email links, what-to-send guidance, and a clearer estimate path.
- Correct the footer business name and current-year professionalism.
- Add basic SEO title, meta description, semantic H1/H2s, and descriptive alt text.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Replace the title `HOME | My Site` with a focused title such as `Brandon Construction | New Jersey Remodeling & Construction`.
- Add a meta description mentioning New Jersey, remodeling, construction, hardscape, handyman work, snow removal, and the estimate phone number.
- Use one clear H1 in the hero and H2s for Services, About, Projects, and Contact.
- Improve image alt text so service and construction relevance is clearer.
- Make the phone number a consistent `tel:` link anywhere it appears.
- Make the email a consistent `mailto:` link anywhere it appears.
- Use the license number consistently and consider adding LocalBusiness/HomeAndConstructionBusiness schema later after confirming exact address, service areas, insurance language, and real project photos.
- Add location specificity beyond `all New Jersey` if Brandon Construction has preferred counties, towns, or service radius.

## Realistic implementation angle
This should be positioned as a focused Wix homepage improvement, not a full rebuild. The existing site has a recognizable contractor template and enough business basics to work with. A realistic implementation would keep the current visual identity, remove or replace obvious template residue, clarify the service list, make estimate actions visible above the fold, and improve SEO fields.

The owner-facing value is simple: make visitors understand that Brandon Construction is a New Jersey remodeling/construction option with a license number and a fast phone/email estimate path before they scroll.
