# Six Paws Pet Care Inc. — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Six Paws Pet Care Inc. is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current site already has recognizable brand material and real service proof, but the first-screen path can be made clearer without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current site family and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Request Pet Care**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Current business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #6a4c93, #4db6ac, #fffaf2, #2f2440.
- **Typography feel:** Nunito, Arial, sans-serif heading direction with Inter, Arial, sans-serif body copy direction.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Local-service navigation with phone/contact emphasis.
- **Hero/image treatment:** Open paws, clearer details.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:**
- minimal Wix one-page structure
- warm “Open Paws” and “Claws or Paws” language
- purple/teal friendly pet-care direction
- contact form and call/text emphasized
- external social/profile links instead of detailed service sections

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with clear service intent in Western suburbs of Chicago, IL.
- The site already contains useful proof: Call or text contact, Western suburbs coverage, Pet-sitter profile link, Yelp/social references.
- The existing visual direction is recognizable enough for Solution A instead of a full redesign.
- There is a direct contact, quote, estimate, or booking path that can be made easier to notice.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The homepage is so minimal that visitors do not get enough service detail or reassurance before the contact form.
   - Prototype response: Keep the friendly Wix pet-care personality and “Open Paws” tone.
2. **High:** “Claws or Paws, We Watch Them All” is memorable but needs service examples and care expectations nearby.
   - Prototype response: Add a clear hero explaining pet sitting, dog walking, and flexible pet care.
3. **High:** The call/text, email, service area, and external proof links should be organized into one trustworthy first-screen block.
   - Prototype response: Move contact details and service-area proof near the top.
4. **High:** The form does not tell pet owners what information to send or what happens after they submit.
   - Prototype response: Add a short “what to send” process before the form.
5. **Medium:** Wix footer/template residue makes the business feel smaller than the service likely is.
   - Prototype response: Clean template residue and make the contact section feel intentional.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Keep the friendly Wix pet-care personality and “Open Paws” tone.
2. Add a clear hero explaining pet sitting, dog walking, and flexible pet care.
3. Move contact details and service-area proof near the top.
4. Add a short “what to send” process before the form.
5. Clean template residue and make the contact section feel intentional.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
