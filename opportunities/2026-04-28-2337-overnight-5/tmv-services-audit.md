# TMV Services LLC — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
TMV Services LLC is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current site already has recognizable brand material and real service proof, but the first-screen path can be made clearer without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current site family and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Get a Free Estimate**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Current business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #101010, #f7c948, #fffdf7, #0b0b0b.
- **Typography feel:** Inter, Arial Black, sans-serif heading direction with Inter, Arial, sans-serif body copy direction.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Local-service navigation with phone/contact emphasis.
- **Hero/image treatment:** Transform your space with the estimate flow tightened.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:**
- modern black/yellow contractor branding
- “We Transform Your Space Right” hero
- free-estimate form repeated on the page
- licensed/insured, same-day, satisfaction, and project-count proof
- homeowner/commercial/government audience sections

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with clear service intent in Chicagoland / Naperville, IL.
- The site already contains useful proof: Licensed & insured, IL License #TGC139780, 300+ projects done, Same-day estimate response.
- The existing visual direction is recognizable enough for Solution A instead of a full redesign.
- There is a direct contact, quote, estimate, or booking path that can be made easier to notice.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The homepage is strong but asks visitors to process homeowners, commercial, government, portfolio, reviews, service areas, and repeated forms all at once.
   - Prototype response: Keep the modern black/yellow TMV contractor identity.
2. **High:** The estimate form appears multiple times; the page needs one clearer primary path with phone as secondary.
   - Prototype response: Make “Get a Free Estimate” the single dominant CTA.
3. **High:** License, insurance, 300+ projects, reviews, and same-day response should be summarized before audience segmentation.
   - Prototype response: Summarize license, insurance, project count, and response proof in a compact trust strip.
4. **High:** Service categories should be grouped around common buyer needs instead of a broad remodeling menu plus audience blocks.
   - Prototype response: Group services into remodels, finish/repair work, and commercial/institutional projects.
5. **Medium:** Some location/service links and repeated CTAs can be lowered so the hero remains focused.
   - Prototype response: Reduce duplicated forms by adding one clear estimate process and one contact block.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Keep the modern black/yellow TMV contractor identity.
2. Make “Get a Free Estimate” the single dominant CTA.
3. Summarize license, insurance, project count, and response proof in a compact trust strip.
4. Group services into remodels, finish/repair work, and commercial/institutional projects.
5. Reduce duplicated forms by adding one clear estimate process and one contact block.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
