# WoofBeach — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
WoofBeach is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current site already has recognizable brand material and real service proof, but the first-screen path can be made clearer without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current site family and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Book Online**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Current business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #00a6b2, #ff8a3d, #fff7e7, #12323a.
- **Typography feel:** Poppins, Arial Black, sans-serif heading direction with Inter, Arial, sans-serif body copy direction.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Local-service navigation with phone/contact emphasis.
- **Hero/image treatment:** Tropical dog care with calmer booking choices.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:**
- tropical/beach dog-care personality
- large WordPress navigation with many dog service pages
- online booking link and phone prominent
- CPR/First Aid and professional team proof
- testimonials and playful “your dog is in good hands” copy

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with clear service intent in Batavia / Fox Valley, IL.
- The site already contains useful proof: CPR + First Aid certified, Professional groomers and trainers, Booking available online, Grooming, training, daycare.
- The existing visual direction is recognizable enough for Solution A instead of a full redesign.
- There is a direct contact, quote, estimate, or booking path that can be made easier to notice.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The site has too many service and class links in the main navigation before the visitor can simply choose grooming, training, or daycare.
   - Prototype response: Keep the bright tropical WoofBeach personality.
2. **High:** CPR/First Aid, professional groomers/trainers, and booking proof should support the hero immediately.
   - Prototype response: Make “Book Online” the primary action and phone the secondary action.
3. **High:** Grooming, training, daycare, and walk-in services need a cleaner buyer path for first-time dog owners.
   - Prototype response: Move CPR/First Aid and professional team proof near the top.
4. **High:** The tropical brand is memorable, but the CTA path alternates between booking, contact, location, and service links.
   - Prototype response: Group the service universe into grooming, training, and daycare/enrichment.
5. **Medium:** Some footer and sitemap-style links dilute the fun brand experience.
   - Prototype response: Add a simple booking process before contact/location details.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Keep the bright tropical WoofBeach personality.
2. Make “Book Online” the primary action and phone the secondary action.
3. Move CPR/First Aid and professional team proof near the top.
4. Group the service universe into grooming, training, and daycare/enrichment.
5. Add a simple booking process before contact/location details.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
