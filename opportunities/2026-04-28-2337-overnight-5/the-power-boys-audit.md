# The Power Boys Pressure Washing — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
The Power Boys Pressure Washing is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current site already has recognizable brand material and real service proof, but the first-screen path can be made clearer without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current site family and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Request a Same Day Estimate**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Current business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #101820, #25a9e0, #f5fbff, #071018.
- **Typography feel:** Poppins, Arial Black, sans-serif heading direction with Inter, Arial, sans-serif body copy direction.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Local-service navigation with phone/contact emphasis.
- **Hero/image treatment:** Proven pressure washing with a cleaner first step.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:**
- black/blue high-energy pressure-washing brand
- slider-style hero with “Request an Estimate” actions
- service cards for residential, commercial, roof, soft washing, process
- photo gallery and social proof emphasis
- 5.0 rating and many Google reviews

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with clear service intent in Naperville / Hinsdale, IL.
- The site already contains useful proof: Licensed & insured, 5.0 Google rating, 182 reviews, Residential + commercial.
- The existing visual direction is recognizable enough for Solution A instead of a full redesign.
- There is a direct contact, quote, estimate, or booking path that can be made easier to notice.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The homepage has strong proof but the hero slider, large nav, gallery/social links, and many service cards create avoidable choice overload.
   - Prototype response: Keep the bold black/blue Power Boys energy and exterior-cleaning photo feel.
2. **High:** 5.0 rating and 182-review proof should be visible as a top trust strip before visitors scroll into long reviews.
   - Prototype response: Move 5.0/182-review, licensed/insured, and residential/commercial proof to the top.
3. **High:** Same-day estimate should be the primary action instead of competing with gallery, services, and social links.
   - Prototype response: Make “Request a Same Day Estimate” the primary CTA.
4. **High:** Residential, roof/soft washing, and commercial cleaning need a cleaner buyer path.
   - Prototype response: Group services around residential, soft/roof, and commercial cleaning.
5. **Medium:** Some proof and process language is repeated across sections instead of becoming a concise next-step flow.
   - Prototype response: Add a short surface/method/process explanation before the estimate block.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Keep the bold black/blue Power Boys energy and exterior-cleaning photo feel.
2. Move 5.0/182-review, licensed/insured, and residential/commercial proof to the top.
3. Make “Request a Same Day Estimate” the primary CTA.
4. Group services around residential, soft/roof, and commercial cleaning.
5. Add a short surface/method/process explanation before the estimate block.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
