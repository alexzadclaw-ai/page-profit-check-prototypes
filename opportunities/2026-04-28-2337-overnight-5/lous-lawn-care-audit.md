# Lou's Lawn Care — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Lou's Lawn Care is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current site already has recognizable brand material and real service proof, but the first-screen path can be made clearer without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current site family and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Get a Free Estimate**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Current business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #214b25, #9f7b2d, #f6f2e8, #102714.
- **Typography feel:** Merriweather, Georgia, serif heading direction with Inter, Arial, sans-serif body copy direction.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Local-service navigation with phone/contact emphasis.
- **Hero/image treatment:** More than a lawn — with the estimate path made simple.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:**
- green local lawn-care palette
- family-owned story and quote “It’s more than a lawn, it’s art.”
- one-page service/gallery/review/contact flow
- insurance explanation section
- residential and commercial DuPage service area

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with clear service intent in Lisle / Naperville / DuPage County, IL.
- The site already contains useful proof: Family owned since 2017, Fully insured, Residential + commercial, Same-day style local responsiveness.
- The existing visual direction is recognizable enough for Solution A instead of a full redesign.
- There is a direct contact, quote, estimate, or booking path that can be made easier to notice.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The first screen has a friendly local feel but does not quickly separate recurring maintenance, landscape projects, and seasonal work.
   - Prototype response: Preserve the local green/family-owned lawn-care feel.
2. **High:** Fully insured proof is strong but appears as a lower section instead of reducing risk near the first estimate CTA.
   - Prototype response: Make “Get a Free Estimate” the dominant action in hero and contact.
3. **High:** The service list is useful but broad enough that customers may not know whether to ask for maintenance, landscape installation, or seasonal help.
   - Prototype response: Move fully insured, family-owned, service-area, and review proof near the top.
4. **High:** Reviews and the owner story are persuasive but should support the estimate path sooner.
   - Prototype response: Group services into weekly care, landscape/hardscape, and seasonal property help.
5. **Medium:** Contact details and schedule expectations could be easier to scan on mobile.
   - Prototype response: Add a property-care process that explains what happens after the estimate request.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Preserve the local green/family-owned lawn-care feel.
2. Make “Get a Free Estimate” the dominant action in hero and contact.
3. Move fully insured, family-owned, service-area, and review proof near the top.
4. Group services into weekly care, landscape/hardscape, and seasonal property help.
5. Add a property-care process that explains what happens after the estimate request.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
