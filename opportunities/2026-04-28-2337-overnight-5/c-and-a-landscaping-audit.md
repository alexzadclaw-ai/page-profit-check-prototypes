# C&A Landscaping — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
C&A Landscaping is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current site already has recognizable brand material and real service proof, but the first-screen path can be made clearer without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current site family and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Request a Free Quote**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Current business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #2f5d2e, #b68d40, #f8f5ec, #1f2b1d.
- **Typography feel:** Georgia, Times New Roman, serif heading direction with Arial, sans-serif body copy direction.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Local-service navigation with phone/contact emphasis.
- **Hero/image treatment:** Quality landscaping since 1983, with the quote path brought forward.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:**
- classic green landscaping site
- one-page anchor navigation
- Since 1983 family-owned story
- large project gallery with many category filters
- Unilock authorized contractor proof and free-quote contact

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with clear service intent in Naperville, IL.
- The site already contains useful proof: Since 1983, Family owned and operated, Unilock authorized contractor, Fully bonded/insured.
- The existing visual direction is recognizable enough for Solution A instead of a full redesign.
- There is a direct contact, quote, estimate, or booking path that can be made easier to notice.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The page has strong credibility but no clear modern hero/quote path before the about and service copy.
   - Prototype response: Preserve the classic green local landscaping identity.
2. **High:** Since 1983, family-owned, bonded/insured, and Unilock proof should be visible together near the top.
   - Prototype response: Make “Request a Free Quote” the dominant action in the hero and contact section.
3. **High:** The many services and gallery links need to be grouped into maintenance, landscape/hardscape, and irrigation/lighting paths.
   - Prototype response: Move 1983, family-owned, Unilock, and bonded/insured proof into a top trust strip.
4. **High:** Old gallery anchors, # links, and long image lists create clutter before the contact action.
   - Prototype response: Group services by maintenance, landscape/hardscape, and irrigation/lighting.
5. **Medium:** The contact section needs a clearer expectation of what to include in a free quote request.
   - Prototype response: Compress the gallery into a curated proof band so the page no longer feels overloaded.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Preserve the classic green local landscaping identity.
2. Make “Request a Free Quote” the dominant action in the hero and contact section.
3. Move 1983, family-owned, Unilock, and bonded/insured proof into a top trust strip.
4. Group services by maintenance, landscape/hardscape, and irrigation/lighting.
5. Compress the gallery into a curated proof band so the page no longer feels overloaded.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
