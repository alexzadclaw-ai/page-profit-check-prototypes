# Anderson Window & Gutter Cleaning — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Anderson Window & Gutter Cleaning is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current site already has recognizable brand material and real service proof, but the first-screen path can be made clearer without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current site family and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Request a Free Estimate**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Current business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #173b63, #c82127, #f8fbff, #0a1b2b.
- **Typography feel:** Roboto Slab, Georgia, serif heading direction with Arial, sans-serif body copy direction.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Local-service navigation with phone/contact emphasis.
- **Hero/image treatment:** Chicagoland gutter experts with the proof cleaned up.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:**
- blue/red veteran-service visual direction
- large uppercase claims around Chicagoland gutter expertise
- very broad navigation with gutter subservices
- insurance/guarantee/testimonial sections
- old-school WordPress/Divi section density

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with clear service intent in Naperville / Chicagoland, IL.
- The site already contains useful proof: Veteran fireman owned, Started in 1997, Workman’s comp + liability insurance, 30-day gutter guarantee.
- The existing visual direction is recognizable enough for Solution A instead of a full redesign.
- There is a direct contact, quote, estimate, or booking path that can be made easier to notice.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The homepage has excellent trust proof but buries it inside dense uppercase copy, duplicated service card headings, and a large navigation.
   - Prototype response: Keep the blue/red veteran-owned home-service feel.
2. **High:** Veteran fireman ownership, 1997 history, insurance, and the 30-day guarantee should appear together near the first CTA.
   - Prototype response: Make “Request a Free Estimate” the clear primary CTA with phone secondary.
3. **High:** Window, gutter, power washing, and drainage services need cleaner grouping than repeated “Read More” cards.
   - Prototype response: Move 1997, insurance, guarantee, and veteran-owner proof into a top strip.
4. **High:** Typos and old formatting reduce trust for a business with otherwise strong credentials.
   - Prototype response: Group services into gutter, window, and exterior/seasonal work.
5. **Medium:** The contact form and estimate path should feel more intentional and less like a generic page section.
   - Prototype response: Clean duplicated headings and typos while preserving the established local-company voice.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Keep the blue/red veteran-owned home-service feel.
2. Make “Request a Free Estimate” the clear primary CTA with phone secondary.
3. Move 1997, insurance, guarantee, and veteran-owner proof into a top strip.
4. Group services into gutter, window, and exterior/seasonal work.
5. Clean duplicated headings and typos while preserving the established local-company voice.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
