# Pressure Soft Pros LLC — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Pressure Soft Pros LLC is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current site already has recognizable brand material and real service proof, but the first-screen path can be made clearer without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current site family and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Get Free Estimate**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Current business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #073b67, #f59c22, #eef7ff, #061b2d.
- **Typography feel:** Montserrat, Arial, sans-serif heading direction with Inter, Arial, sans-serif body copy direction.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Local-service navigation with phone/contact emphasis.
- **Hero/image treatment:** Making old things new — with proof closer to the top.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:**
- blue/orange pressure-washing color system
- “Making Old Things New” messaging
- large service menus for residential/commercial
- photo/results section
- very long service-area navigation

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with clear service intent in Naperville, IL.
- The site already contains useful proof: Naperville pressure washing, Residential services, Commercial services, Photo/results emphasis.
- The existing visual direction is recognizable enough for Solution A instead of a full redesign.
- There is a direct contact, quote, estimate, or booking path that can be made easier to notice.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** Residential and commercial buyers have to scan too many service links before seeing the cleanest decision path.
   - Prototype response: Preserve the blue/orange Pressure Soft Pros look and “Making Old Things New” phrase.
2. **High:** The “reviews coming soon” area weakens trust and should not be the main proof moment for a first-time visitor.
   - Prototype response: Make residential vs commercial the primary service decision.
3. **High:** Service-area links create local SEO value but visually overwhelm the conversion path.
   - Prototype response: De-emphasize “reviews coming soon” and elevate results/photo proof instead.
4. **High:** The strongest promise — restoring property appearance — should be paired with estimate and phone actions above the fold.
   - Prototype response: Keep service-area SEO lower on the page so it does not compete with the estimate CTA.
5. **Medium:** Some copy is generic pressure-washing explanation rather than owner-ready decision support.
   - Prototype response: Add a short three-step estimate flow before contact.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Preserve the blue/orange Pressure Soft Pros look and “Making Old Things New” phrase.
2. Make residential vs commercial the primary service decision.
3. De-emphasize “reviews coming soon” and elevate results/photo proof instead.
4. Keep service-area SEO lower on the page so it does not compete with the estimate CTA.
5. Add a short three-step estimate flow before contact.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
