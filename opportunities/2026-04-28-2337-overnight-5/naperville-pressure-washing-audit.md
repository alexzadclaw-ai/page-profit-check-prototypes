# Naperville Pressure Washing — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Naperville Pressure Washing is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current site already has recognizable brand material and real service proof, but the first-screen path can be made clearer without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current site family and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Get a Free Estimate**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Current business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #0d5f9f, #78b82a, #f7fbff, #0b263c.
- **Typography feel:** Oswald, Impact, Arial Narrow, sans-serif heading direction with Arial, sans-serif body copy direction.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Local-service navigation with phone/contact emphasis.
- **Hero/image treatment:** Pressure washing choices made easier.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:**
- blue/green pressure-washing palette
- phone and free-estimate CTA repeated across the page
- residential/commercial split
- long SEO-style service explanations
- blog/service links and “Need Pressure Washing?” bands

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with clear service intent in Naperville, IL.
- The site already contains useful proof: Free estimates, Eco-friendly cleaners, Residential + commercial, PWRA/PWNA training language.
- The existing visual direction is recognizable enough for Solution A instead of a full redesign.
- There is a direct contact, quote, estimate, or booking path that can be made easier to notice.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The homepage is dominated by long SEO copy before the visitor can quickly choose a service path.
   - Prototype response: Keep the blue/green local pressure-washing look while cutting first-screen copy volume.
2. **High:** “Get Free Estimate” appears repeatedly, but it competes with many generic links and popup anchors instead of anchoring one clean flow.
   - Prototype response: Make “Get a Free Estimate” the single dominant primary CTA.
3. **High:** Residential, commercial, roof, deck, driveway, and patio services need to be grouped by buyer intent near the top.
   - Prototype response: Group services into house/roof, deck/concrete, and commercial exterior cleaning.
4. **High:** Useful trust points like training, eco-friendly cleaners, and free estimates are present but not packaged into a quick proof strip.
   - Prototype response: Move training, eco-friendly cleaners, and free-estimate proof near the hero.
5. **Medium:** Repeated claims and older copy patterns make the site feel less local and less current than it could.
   - Prototype response: Replace repeated “Need Pressure Washing?” sections with a short process and direct contact block.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Keep the blue/green local pressure-washing look while cutting first-screen copy volume.
2. Make “Get a Free Estimate” the single dominant primary CTA.
3. Group services into house/roof, deck/concrete, and commercial exterior cleaning.
4. Move training, eco-friendly cleaners, and free-estimate proof near the hero.
5. Replace repeated “Need Pressure Washing?” sections with a short process and direct contact block.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
