# Page Profit Check Audit - Handyman Experts

## Quick take

Handyman Experts is a strong fit for a Page Profit Check because the site already has real local-service intent, clear contact information, and a buyer who is likely deciding quickly. The main opportunity is not a full redesign. It is a tighter first screen, clearer service grouping, and a faster quote or appointment path.

## What is already working

- The business category is clear: Handyman and home repair.
- The service area is usable: Naperville, IL.
- The current site has enough real brand and service context to improve without replacing the identity.
- Contact information is available: handymanexperts@yahoo.com.

## Biggest conversion issues

- The first screen does not make the highest-value next action obvious enough.
- Trust proof and service scope are present but need to be closer to the top of the page.
- Visitors have to scan too much before knowing which service path applies to them.
- The contact path would benefit from a specific quote or appointment prompt instead of a generic contact action.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements

- Keep the recognizable visual direction and tighten the hero around one primary action.
- Add a short trust bar near the top with location, service type, and owner/local proof.
- Group services by buyer intent so a rushed visitor can choose quickly.
- Add a simple what-happens-next section before the final contact block.
- Make phone/email actions persistent and specific.

## SEO basics to tighten

- Page title should combine the business name, primary service, and service area.
- H1 should clearly state the service and location.
- Add concise service-section headings that match common local search intent.
- Use descriptive alt text on reused source images.
- Keep NAP details consistent across footer and contact sections.

## Realistic implementation angle

Tighten a long, service-rich homepage into a faster estimate path while preserving the friendly local handyman identity.

This is a small implementation, not a rebuild. A practical sprint would focus on the homepage or service page hierarchy, CTA labels, trust copy, mobile scanability, and contact flow.

## Offer fit

The $99 Page Profit Check fits as an audit and diagnosis only. It would give Handyman Experts a prioritized punch list, a focused before/after direction, and a clear implementation scope. Implementation would be separate from the $99 audit.
