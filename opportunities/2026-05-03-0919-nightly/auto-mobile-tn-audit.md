# Auto-Mobile — Page Profit Check

## Quick take
Auto-Mobile has a very clear local-service idea: mobile oil changes in the Cleveland, TN area, with a strong “we come to you” convenience promise. The indexed site content suggests useful trust details — disabled U.S. Army Veteran owned, licensed/insured, CARFAX service reporting, and complimentary fluid checks — but the live custom domain currently resolves to a Wix domain-connection error. That creates an immediate conversion blocker before any copy or design improvements matter.

## Current access note
- Target URL checked: https://www.automobiletn.com/
- Result on May 3, 2026: Wix error page, “domain isn't connected to a website yet.”
- Because the real page was not accessible, the prototype uses the visible search-indexed content and Wix static assets found for this exact domain: black Auto-Mobile logo, American flag image, and oil-change hero image.

## Visual fingerprint
- Site-builder/Wix feel with a simple centered layout and large image blocks.
- Black square logo with white gear/wrench icon, serif “Auto-Mobile” wordmark, and small “We Come To You” tagline.
- Patriotic/veteran cue from a square American flag image.
- Automotive hero photography: oil being poured into an engine bay with warm orange highlight.
- Minimal black/white/cream palette, serif headings, simple top navigation, and straightforward contact cues.

## What is already working
- The business concept is easy to understand: oil changes without visiting a shop.
- Contact information is available from the source: 423-740-3516 and mr_rose1987@yahoo.com.
- The owner story/trust angle is valuable: disabled U.S. Army Veteran founded, licensed and insured.
- CARFAX service reporting is a strong reassurance for people worried about warranty records.
- Prices are simple and easy to explain: conventional/synthetic at $99 and heavy-duty pickup at $199.
- The “complimentary engine-bay fluid check” is a useful value-add.

## Biggest conversion issues
### Critical
1. **The custom domain is disconnected.** Visitors who reach the site see a Wix setup/error page instead of Auto-Mobile’s homepage.
   - Prototype fix: created a working static homepage concept so the offer can show the intended experience, but the real implementation must reconnect/publish the domain first.
2. **The above-fold offer needs to state the service, location, availability, and booking action immediately.** Indexed snippets show the service, but Saturday-only availability and key proof can easily be missed if buried lower on the page.
   - Prototype fix: hero now says “Oil changes at your home or office,” names Cleveland, TN, mentions Saturday appointments, and has call/email CTAs.

### High
1. **Trust signals are too sparse or not grouped near the buying decision.** Veteran-founded, licensed/insured, CARFAX reporting, and fluid checks should be visible before a visitor decides whether to call.
   - Prototype fix: added a four-item trust strip directly below the hero.
2. **Service selection can scan faster.** A visitor should not have to read paragraphs to determine whether their vehicle is conventional, synthetic, or heavy-duty pickup.
   - Prototype fix: added three clear service cards with price, plain-language description, and service-specific call actions.
3. **Saturday-only availability should be framed as useful clarity, not a surprise.** If availability is limited, hiding it creates friction and wasted calls.
   - Prototype fix: repeated Saturday availability in the hero, “Before booking” card, and contact section.

### Medium
- Email-only request flow could feel informal unless paired with simple vehicle/location instructions.
- The original site likely needs more “how it works” guidance to reduce hesitation about driveway/office service.
- Local SEO body copy should include Cleveland, TN, mobile oil change, synthetic oil change, heavy-duty pickup oil change, and service record/CARFAX language.
- If the business is only available Saturdays, Google Business Profile, site hours, and homepage copy should match.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. **Fix domain connection first:** make the real site load at automobiletn.com and www.automobiletn.com.
2. **Rewrite the hero for buyer clarity:** mobile oil changes at home/office, Cleveland, TN, Saturday availability, call to book.
3. **Put proof near the top:** veteran founded, licensed/insured, CARFAX reporting, and complimentary fluid check.
4. **Make service cards scannable:** conventional, synthetic, and heavy-duty pickup with prices.
5. **Add a simple appointment flow:** call/email, confirm vehicle/oil type, service comes to you.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use one visible H1 around “Mobile Oil Change in Cleveland, TN.”
- Add crawlable headings for conventional oil change, synthetic oil change, and heavy-duty pickup oil change.
- Add service-area copy for Cleveland, TN and nearby areas if applicable.
- Add LocalBusiness/AutomotiveBusiness schema once the final business name, address/service area, and hours are confirmed.
- Keep phone, email, hours, and availability consistent across the website and Google Business Profile.

## Realistic implementation angle
This does not require a full redesign. The fastest path is a Wix/domain cleanup plus a focused homepage refresh: fix the domain, keep the recognizable black logo/flag/oil-photo look, replace generic or buried copy with a clear service promise, add trust proof, add service cards, and make the phone/email booking path obvious.
