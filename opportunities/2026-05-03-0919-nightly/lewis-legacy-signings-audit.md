# Lewis Legacy Signings — Page Profit Check Audit

## Visual fingerprint
- Black header with a gold/cream Lewis Legacy Signings logo and elegant serif wordmark.
- Hero uses a close-up notary/document image, followed by a muted gray-green banner with white “Notarize with Ease” and “On-Demand Notary Services” text.
- Brand palette is mostly black, gold/mustard, off-white, gray-green, and dark navy icon accents.
- Typography mixes large serif section titles in gold with simple rounded sans-serif body copy.
- Mobile layout is highly vertical: logo/header, hero image, slogan, About story, a single Wix booking card, long pricing/legal sections, contact, feedback form.
- The original has a personal, trustworthy founder story and useful fee transparency, but the information appears as long blocks rather than quick service paths.

## Quick take
Lewis Legacy Signings has a strong foundation: professional branding, a veteran-owned story, clear Orlando mobile service area, phone/email links, transparent Florida notary fees, I-9/witness details, 24-hour weekday phone availability, and a testimonial. The main opportunity is not a new brand direction. It is a conversion cleanup so a rushed visitor can immediately pick the right notary path, trust the provider, understand the next step, and call or email without scrolling through a very long page.

## What is already working
- Clear brand identity: black/gold logo, elegant wordmark, and consistent gold accents.
- Strong trust story: veteran-owned, United States Marine Corps background, founder named Sandrea, reliability/integrity/compassion positioning.
- Direct phone and email links exist: `(407) 900 - 1184` and `info@lewislegacysignings.com`.
- Service breadth is useful: acknowledgments, jurats, powers of attorney, wills, I-9, witness services, marriage officiant, travel, weekend/holiday options.
- Pricing and policy details are transparent compared with many local service sites.
- Local relevance is clear: Orlando and surrounding areas.

## Biggest conversion issues
- Urgent mobile notary visitors do not get a clear call/request action immediately enough.
- The broad service set is buried inside a long single-page flow instead of being organized by buyer intent.
- Contact, trust cues, fee reassurance, and the next-step explanation need to appear earlier for mobile users.

## Severity-ranked findings

### Critical
1. **Above-the-fold CTA does not match urgent mobile notary intent.**  
   The first screen says “Notarize with Ease” / “On-Demand Notary Services,” but there is no immediate “Call now,” “Request mobile notary,” or “Urgent signing” action in the hero.
   - Concrete fix: add two hero CTAs: **Call for a Notary Now** and **Request Appointment**.
   - Prototype coverage: hero CTAs added directly below the tagline.

2. **Service routing is buried inside one long page.**  
   The live mobile page has a single visible booking card for “Acknowledgments,” then extensive fee/legal text. Visitors with loan signing, I-9, witness, or wedding needs must read too much to know they are in the right place.
   - Concrete fix: add three service-path cards near the top: General Notary, Loan/Urgent Signings, and I-9/Witness/Wedding.
   - Prototype coverage: service-path section added immediately after About/trust.

3. **Mobile page length creates friction before contact.**  
   The captured full mobile screenshot is extremely long, with large blank gold spacing around the services area. Contact information appears far down the page.
   - Concrete fix: reduce spacer blocks, move contact/trust cues up, and use condensed cards/accordions for fees and policies.
   - Prototype coverage: compact sections and sticky mobile call/email bar.

### High
4. **Trust cues are strong but not surfaced early enough.**  
   Veteran-owned, Florida-commissioned, Orlando mobile service, and 24-hour weekday availability are persuasive but mainly appear in paragraph form.
   - Concrete fix: place a top trust strip directly below the hero.
   - Prototype coverage: four-part trust strip under the hero.

5. **No quick “what happens next” explanation.**  
   A notary prospect may hesitate if they do not know what to provide, what fees apply, or what to bring.
   - Concrete fix: add a 3-step process: share basics, confirm price/prep, meet and sign.
   - Prototype coverage: “What happens next” section added.

6. **Fee transparency is useful but visually heavy.**  
   The live fee section is long and dense, especially on mobile. Important details get lost.
   - Concrete fix: summarize key fees in a scannable guide and keep detailed terms lower on the page.
   - Prototype coverage: fee guide cards for notarization, travel, weekend/holiday, and extras.

### Medium
7. **Book Online flow appears underpowered for the full service set.**  
   Only “Acknowledgments” is presented as a booking item in the captured homepage text, which undersells the broader service list.
   - Concrete fix: either add booking items for major service categories or route the homepage CTA to a quick appointment request/call.

8. **SEO title is too broad.**  
   Current title: `Lewis Legacy Signings | Notary Public`. It misses Orlando, mobile notary, veteran-owned, and service-specific intent.
   - Concrete fix: use a title like `Lewis Legacy Signings | Orlando Mobile Notary & Loan Signing Services`.

9. **Legal, terms, privacy, and feedback content compete with the sales path.**  
   These are useful, but they should be lower-priority sections after service choice and contact.
   - Concrete fix: move legal/terms/privacy to compact footer or dedicated pages/accordions.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Keep the black/gold Lewis Legacy identity, logo, hero image, and personal founder story.
- Add a hero CTA pair for urgent call and appointment request.
- Add a top trust strip for veteran-owned, Florida-commissioned, Orlando mobile, and 24-hour weekday availability.
- Turn the long service/fee content into clear visitor paths.
- Add a simple “what happens next” section to reduce uncertainty.
- Put phone/email access closer to the top and keep it sticky on mobile.
- Tighten SEO title/meta around Orlando mobile notary, loan signing, I-9, and witness services.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Suggested title: `Lewis Legacy Signings | Orlando Mobile Notary & Loan Signing Services`.
- Suggested meta description: `Veteran-owned Orlando mobile notary for general notarizations, loan signings, I-9 verification, witnesses, and wedding officiant services. Call (407) 900-1184.`
- Use one clear H1/Hero message that includes “Orlando mobile notary.”
- Add descriptive H2s for Services, What Happens Next, Fees, and Contact.
- Keep NAP consistent: Lewis Legacy Signings; Orlando, FL; `(407) 900-1184`; `info@lewislegacysignings.com`.
- Add service/location language naturally, not as keyword stuffing: Orlando mobile notary, loan signing, I-9 verification, witness service, wedding officiant.

The highest-impact work is rearranging existing content, adding service routing, improving mobile spacing, adding CTA buttons, and making the contact path obvious.
