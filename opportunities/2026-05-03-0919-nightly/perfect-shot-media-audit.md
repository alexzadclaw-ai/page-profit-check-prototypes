## Quick take
Perfect Shot Media has real services, transparent package prices, direct contact information, and portfolio imagery already on the site. The biggest issue is not lack of offer — it is that the homepage makes visitors decode a long booking catalog before they understand which path fits them.

**Recommendation:** use a Solution A homepage cleanup that keeps the Wix media/portfolio identity but turns the first screen into a clear “choose your session” path for milestones, portraits, headshots, business content, and events.

## What is already working
- The business has clear photography/videography intent and a memorable name.
- Package names, durations, and prices are visible, which reduces buyer uncertainty.
- There are many bookable services: graduation, birthday, maternity, headshots, family, engagement, events, business marketing, lifestyle, newborn, sports, and studio sessions.

- Portfolio imagery is present and can support trust if it is moved into a stronger position.
- The black/white/red visual direction is simple and recognizable.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The first screen does not quickly explain who Perfect Shot Media serves or which booking path a visitor should choose.
   - Current impact: visitors land on “Captured with intention / Perfect Shot Media” and then immediately face a long list of packages without buyer guidance.
   - Prototype response: add a hero promise, “Choose Your Session” CTA, and three clear buyer paths: Milestones, Studio + Portraits, Business + Events.

2. **Critical:** The package list is too long and flat for first-time buyers.
   - Current impact: more than 20 service options appear with similar “Book Now” actions, making it easy to hesitate or pick the wrong package.
   - Prototype response: group packages by occasion and highlight a few common starting points while preserving the detailed pricing idea.

3. **High:** Portfolio proof is not positioned early enough to support booking confidence.
   - Current impact: a media business needs visitors to see style/quality before committing, but the booking catalog dominates.
   - Prototype response: place a portfolio preview and trust strip near the top, then a larger gallery section before final contact.

4. **High:** Booking instruction copy and contact area feel unpolished.
   - Current impact: copy like “please book ur session at the top of the page click book online to select ur session” reduces professionalism and clarity.
   - Prototype response: replace it with a simple three-step booking explanation and a specific “Need help choosing a package?” contact block.

5. **Medium:** Header/footer residue creates trust drag.
   - Current impact: login/cart/More links and “© 2035 by Z-Photography. Powered and secured by Wix” feel unfinished or unrelated to Perfect Shot Media.
   - Prototype response: simplify navigation around Home, Packages, Portfolio, Next Steps, Contact, and use a branded footer.

6. **Medium:** Service naming has spelling/format issues.
   - Current impact: terms like “LIFESTLYE,” “SESSSION,” “EVENTS/PARTYS,” and inconsistent capitalization make the brand feel less polished.
   - Prototype response: clean the visible homepage labels while leaving exact package implementation for the booking system.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Replace the vague first screen with a specific promise: photography and videography for milestones, portraits, events, and business marketing.
2. Use one primary above-fold CTA: **Choose Your Session**.
3. Add a trust strip near the top: portraits + video, business-ready content, transparent pricing, direct contact.
4. Group services by buyer intent rather than listing every session equally.
5. Move portfolio imagery close enough to the top that visitors can judge style before booking.
6. Add a three-step next-action block: pick a session lane, confirm details, arrive ready.
7. Rewrite contact copy around helping buyers choose the right package.
8. Remove visible template/footer residue and casual instruction copy.
