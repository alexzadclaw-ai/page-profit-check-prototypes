# The New Era of Me — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
The New Era of Me already has a warm, recognizable Wix presence: soft beige background, serif typography, a distinctive logo, women’s wellness positioning, visible service booking paths, testimonials, email, and phone. The biggest opportunity is not a full redesign. It is a surgical clarity pass that helps a first-time visitor immediately choose the right path: menopause coaching, prenatal/doula/postpartum support, or broader wellness coaching.

## Biggest conversion issues
### Critical
1. **The hero does not help visitors choose the right service.** “Empower Your Wellness Journey” is warm but broad; the page serves very different buyers, from menopause coaching to doula support, so visitors may not know where to click first.
   - Prototype fix: hero rewritten around choosing support for the visitor’s current life stage, with explicit mention of menopause, pregnancy, birth, postpartum, fitness, and lifestyle change.
2. **CTA hierarchy is inconsistent.** The live page has Explore More, Learn More, Discover More, Book a Session, Book Now, service-level Book Now buttons, and a free consultation form. These all compete.
   - Prototype fix: make “Book a Free Consultation” the primary CTA and “View Services” the secondary CTA, while preserving service-specific booking paths lower on the page.

### High
3. **Service offers are listed, but not grouped by buyer intent.** A visitor has to mentally connect “Peri and Post Menopause Coaching,” “Prenatal Class,” “Doula Services,” “Wellness Coaching,” and “Postpartum Doula Support.”
   - Prototype fix: group offers into three scannable paths: Menopause Coaching, Baby in Bloom, and Wellness Coaching.
4. **Trust signals are not visible soon enough.** Testimonials exist, but the top of the page waits too long to reassure a new visitor.
   - Prototype fix: add a trust strip immediately below the hero with certification, women’s health focus, personalized support, and free consult reassurance.
5. **The first consultation path is buried below service listings and a large booking widget.** This can create friction for people who are unsure which offer fits.
   - Prototype fix: explain the free consultation as the safe first step and add a simple “What Happens Next” section.
6. **Footer/social links show Wix/template residue.** The live page includes Wix social URLs in the footer area, which can weaken trust if clicked.
   - Prototype fix: remove obvious Wix social residue in the prototype footer and keep clean About / Services / Contact navigation.

### Medium
7. The SEO title is decent but could include the main service buckets more clearly.
8. The contact form copy has small grammar issues such as “lets talk.”
9. Some image alt text and file names are generic or template-like.
10. Service pricing/plan links may need consistent formatting across booking and plan pages.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Rewrite the hero so visitors immediately understand which life stages are supported.
- Choose one main action above the fold: free consultation first, service booking second.
- Add a small trust strip near the top instead of making visitors scroll to testimonials.
- Organize services into three buyer paths: menopause, birth/postpartum, and wellness coaching.
- Add a “What Happens Next” section so the free consultation feels low-risk and useful.
- Clean up contact/footer residue and small grammar issues while preserving the Wix style.
- Tighten title/meta/H1/H2 structure around women’s wellness coaching, menopause coaching, doula care, and postpartum support.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a good Wix cleanup, not a rebuild. The existing brand system should stay: beige background, serif fonts, centered logo band, right-side hero copy, service booking structure, testimonials, and contact form. Most improvements can be implemented by editing copy, reordering/duplicating existing sections, adding a small trust strip, grouping services into three cards, and cleaning footer links. The prototype intentionally keeps the Wix/site-builder rhythm so the owner can imagine the changes inside the current site.
