# Sauna Suites — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Sauna Suites is a strong fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current site already has a recognizable warm wellness look, clear session pricing, online booking, gift cards, contact information, hours, and a local Martinsville address. The opportunity is not to redesign the brand. The opportunity is to make the first screen and booking path easier to understand, add trust and location context sooner, reduce repeated generic booking blocks, and keep wellness language safer and more customer-friendly.

**Recommendation:** preserve the calm sauna/wellness design language, large image-led sections, white/cream background rhythm, gold CTA buttons, simple Arial/Wix typography, and “Sweat. Breathe. Heal.” identity while rebuilding the homepage around one clear action: **Book Your Session**.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The first screen says “Welcome” and repeats booking, but does not quickly state the specific local offer, who it is for, where it is, and why to book.
   - Prototype response: replace the generic welcome with a specific hero: “Private dry-heat sauna suites in Martinsville, Indiana,” a clear customer promise, and a primary **Book Your Session** CTA.

2. **Critical:** Pricing is present, but it is a long text list that forces visitors to parse session paths before acting.
   - Prototype response: convert pricing into three scannable paths: Solo Reset, Couples Session, and Small Group, while preserving the original prices and package/discount notes.

3. **High:** The page repeats generic “Book Now” sections without explaining what happens after the visitor clicks or arrives.
   - Prototype response: add a “What happens next” section with three steps: pick a session, arrive hydrated, sweat/cool down/reset.

4. **High:** Trust cues exist but are buried in long About/FAQ copy.
   - Prototype response: add a top trust strip for traditional Finnish barrel sauna, private shower, optional aromatherapy, and beginner-friendly staff guidance.

5. **High:** The site leans heavily on health-benefit claims, some of which sound medical or over-specific for a conversion page.
   - Prototype response: keep safer generic wellness phrasing around relaxation, private time, recovery routines, calming sensory elements, and checking with a healthcare provider when appropriate.

6. **High:** Location context is too easy to miss even though local intent matters for booking.
   - Prototype response: put Martinsville, Indiana and the Washington Street suite address in the hero/supporting details and final booking block.

7. **Medium:** Wix/social/template residue and old footer copy make the site feel less polished than the private-suite experience.
   - Prototype response: remove builder/social residue in the prototype footer and use clean business-specific footer links.

8. **Medium:** FAQ is valuable but long; first-time visitors need a shorter pre-booking version before the full FAQ.
   - Prototype response: add three first-visit cards covering heat, session length/listening to your body, and who should check with a healthcare provider.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Change the first screen from generic “Welcome” to a specific local booking hero.
- Make **Book Your Session** the primary CTA and keep **Gift Cards** as a secondary CTA.
- Group session pricing into buyer paths instead of one dense list.
- Add a compact trust strip near the top with the most useful experience details.
- Explain the booking/arrival/session flow before the final CTA.
- Keep the warm spa palette, sauna images, gold buttons, and “Sweat. Breathe. Heal.” brand phrase.
- Reduce medical-sounding benefit language and use safer wellness/relaxation language.
- Make hours, address, phone, email, discounts, and appointment guidance easier to scan.
- Remove obvious Wix/template residue from the customer-facing footer.

## SEO basics to tighten
