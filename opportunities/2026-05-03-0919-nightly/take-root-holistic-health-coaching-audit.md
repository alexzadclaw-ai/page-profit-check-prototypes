# Take Root Holistic Health Coaching — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Take Root already has a warm, recognizable Wix wellness aesthetic: orange/rust accents, a centered logo, a root/tree motif, a food hero image, rounded pill navigation, serif service names, and generous white space. The main conversion opportunity is not a full redesign; it is a surgical homepage cleanup that keeps the same visual family while making the first screen, trust cues, service choices, and next step much clearer.

Visual fingerprint evidence:
- Source screenshot: `screenshots/2026-05-03-0919-nightly/take-root-holistic-health-coaching-target.png`
- Logo asset: Wix static floral logo image used in the header.
- Hero/background asset: plate-of-food image used behind the top content block.
- Dominant colors: white, pale gray, rust/orange `#f05918`, muted brown/rust, black text.
- Typography feel: Montserrat-style clean sans for large headings/navigation, Caudex-style serif for service titles.
- Header/nav pattern: pale gray top band, centered logo, tree-root image on the right, partial value words on the left, rounded rust outline nav pills.
- Recognizable cue: the offset white hero panel over the large food photograph is the site’s strongest structural cue.
- Upgrade direction: preserve the Wix-like spacing and assets, but replace the mostly blank/top-heavy hero with a clear promise, specific booking CTA, trust strip, and simple “what happens next” flow.

## What is already working
- Warm and coherent holistic wellness direction; the root/tree and food imagery match the business well.
- The logo and orange/rust accent color are memorable.
- Services already exist with booking links, so the conversion path is technically present.
- Contact email and location are visible near the bottom.
- Blog/recipe content gives the site personality and can support trust if organized better.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Clarify the hero without changing the brand: keep the food photo, logo, root art, nav pills, and white overlay, but fill the hero with a direct promise and CTA.
2. Make the first action obvious: “Book Initial Consult” should be the primary homepage action.
3. Move trust higher: put credentials and delivery modes near the top instead of relying on paragraph copy.
4. Reframe services by visitor need: initial consultation, follow-up online session, local yoga classes.
5. Add a short consultation flow so visitors know what happens after they click.
6. Tighten the contact/footer so it feels business-specific and not like a default Wix ending.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Page title should include the business name plus service/location keywords, e.g. “Take Root Holistic Health Coaching | Nutrition, Yoga & Wellness Coaching.”
- Meta description should mention holistic nutrition coaching, wellness planning, yoga, Lone Rock/WI, and online sessions.
- Use one clear H1 on the homepage that describes the service rather than only an inspirational phrase.
- Add descriptive alt text for key images where missing, especially the hero/food and root imagery.
- Consider a dedicated local service page or section for “holistic health coach in Lone Rock, WI / Spring Green area” if local discovery matters.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a small Wix cleanup, not a rebuild. A practical sprint would update the homepage hero, intro paragraph, trust strip, service rows, consultation-flow section, contact footer, title/meta, and mobile CTA. The existing booking links, brand assets, blog cards, and overall Wix layout can stay.
