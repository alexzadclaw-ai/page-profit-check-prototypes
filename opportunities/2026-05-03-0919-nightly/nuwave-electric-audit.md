# NuWave Electric — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
NuWave Electric is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current site already has a recognizable local contractor identity, long operating history, real service breadth, and customer reviews. The opportunity is to simplify the path from “I need electrical/HVAC/generator help” to “request an estimate” without making the business look like a different company.

**Recommendation:** preserve the blue/yellow NuWave site family and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Request an Estimate**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** NuWave Electric logo with blue/yellow wave mark and contractor-style wordmark.
- **Dominant colors:** #181818 / #333333 dark sections, #007cf9 bright blue CTA, #fdee29 yellow accent, white content blocks.
- **Typography feel:** Oswald/Poppins-style headings with simple sans-serif body copy.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Dark sticky-style Wix header, centered navigation, social icons, and a blue “REQUEST AN ESTIMATE” button.
- **Hero/image treatment:** Split intro with contractor imagery, service promise, and supporting text; strong use of dark overlays and blue/yellow accents.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, dense intro/service blocks, service tiles, contractor capability lists, review quotes, contact form/footer.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:** “Residential • Commercial • Industrial,” Mitsubishi Preferred Diamond Contractor, Generator Headquarters, excavation/underground utilities, Lakes Region/Central/Southern NH service area.

## What is already working
- The business has a clear market: electrical contractor serving homeowners and businesses in the Lakes Region, central NH, and southern New Hampshire.
- The site communicates meaningful credibility: over 35 years of experience, family-owned and operated, licensed/insured language, and real customer reviews.
- The service breadth is strong: residential, commercial, industrial, generators, HVAC/ductless mini-splits, EV charging, excavation, security/alarm systems, pool/spa controls, and high-voltage work.
- There is a direct estimate CTA and visible phone number: 603-524-4400.
- The current visual direction is recognizable enough for a cleanup instead of a full redesign.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The homepage asks visitors to parse a lot of service breadth before the right estimate path feels simple.
   - Prototype response: Lead with one clear “Request an Estimate” CTA and route visitors into four practical service paths: Electrical, Ductless/HVAC, Generators, and Excavation/Underground.
2. **High:** Strong trust signals exist, but they are buried inside paragraph copy and reviews instead of supporting the first decision.
   - Prototype response: Move 35+ years, family-owned, licensed/insured, and NH service-area proof directly below the hero.
3. **High:** Electrical, HVAC, generator, and excavation services appear as equal blocks, but the page does not quickly explain which customer should choose which path.
   - Prototype response: Add buyer-intent cards with short “best for” guidance and direct CTAs.
4. **High:** The estimate action is inconsistent: the nav CTA points to the site estimate page, while the hero “GET A FREE ESTIMATE” link appears to point to an old EditorX/Wix build URL.
   - Prototype response: Standardize all estimate CTAs around the current `/request-an-estimate` path and phone fallback.
5. **Medium:** The page has useful review proof, but the reviews arrive late after long service lists.
   - Prototype response: Bring one review snippet and a proof summary higher on the page, then keep more reviews as a secondary path.
6. **Medium:** Footer/source details include visible Wix/Sprout implementation residue and schema/contact details should be checked before implementation.
   - Prototype response: Make the contact block intentional and note schema/phone consistency as an implementation QA item.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Make **Request an Estimate** the dominant CTA in the header, hero, service cards, and contact section.
2. Place the strongest trust proof immediately after the hero: 35+ years, family-owned, licensed/insured, Lakes Region/Central/Southern NH.
3. Reorganize dense service content into four clear buyer paths: Electrical, Mitsubishi ductless/HVAC, Generators, and Excavation/underground utilities.
4. Add “what happens next” clarity so visitors understand the estimate process before calling or filling out the form.
5. Standardize estimate links and clean minor Wix/old-build residue during implementation.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use one focused homepage title such as “Electricians, Generators & Ductless HVAC in Laconia, NH | NuWave Electric.”
- Write a concise meta description around licensed electrical, generator, ductless/HVAC, and excavation service in Lakes Region/Central/Southern NH.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for logo, electrical work, generator, ductless/HVAC, and excavation images.
- QA LocalBusiness/Contractor schema before launch, especially phone number consistency and service-area details.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. Keep the current NuWave colors, logo, contractor feel, service breadth, and review proof, but simplify the first-screen decision, route services more clearly, and remove any confusing old estimate-link behavior.
