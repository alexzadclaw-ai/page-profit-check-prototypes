# Fix It Quick: Handyman Services LLC Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Fix It Quick: Handyman Services LLC is a good fit for a focused homepage cleanup. The live Wix site already has useful conversion material: direct phone and email, a clear Richmond and Tri-Cities service area, a practical services list, pricing/rate guidance, photo-gallery proof, and a simple promise around keeping handyman work short, sweet, and simple.

The gap is that the best buying information is spread across several pages. A first-time visitor should not have to click into Services and Pricing / Rates to understand what Fix It Quick does, where it works, and how to request an estimate.

Recommendation: keep the recognizable Wix structure, white header, uppercase business name, tools-photo hero, direct contact details, and plain local-service tone. Clean up the first screen, group the high-demand jobs, and make the call/email estimate path the dominant action.

## What is already working
- The site has direct contact paths: `fixitquickrva@gmail.com` and `(804)393-1391`.
- The homepage already states Richmond and the Tri-Cities area of VA.
- The Services page has a useful list of specific jobs, including furniture assembly, TV wall mounts, play-set installation, pressure washing, dryer duct cleaning, gutter cleaning, junk removal, weatherproofing, Ring doorbell installation, pictures, faucets, lights, fans, filters, knobs, and locks.
- The Pricing / Rates page gives visitors useful expectations: free estimates, phone or on-site estimates, $20 pricing increments, and pricing based on complexity, size, and quantity.
- The Photo Gallery and About pages add proof that a real local operator is behind the site.
- The visual identity is simple and recognizable: Wix banner, white header/nav, uppercase business name, full-width tools photo, centered hero type, and repeated footer contact details.

## Biggest conversion issues
- **Critical: The first screen is too generic.** The homepage says Fix It Quick is professional and reliable, but it does not immediately show the most common services or the full service area.
- **Critical: The estimate path is underdeveloped.** `Want it fixed now? Just pick up the phone and call!` is useful, but visitors also need a clear email path and what to send.
- **High: Strong service and pricing details live on separate pages.** The most persuasive details should be previewed on the homepage before the visitor clicks away.
- **High: The Services page has many useful specifics, but they are hard to scan quickly.** Grouping them by assembly, installation, exterior help, and small repairs would make the offer easier to understand.
- **Medium: Wix/template residue distracts from the business.** The promotional banner and `Proudly created with Wix.com` language make the page feel less polished.
- **Medium: SEO structure could be clearer.** The site would benefit from a stronger local handyman title, one focused H1, and page copy that names Richmond/Tri-Cities handyman services in a natural way.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Rewrite the hero into a clear local promise: Richmond handyman help, fixed quick.
- Add primary call and email CTAs near the top.
- Bring the Richmond/Tri-Cities service area and estimate instructions into the homepage body.
- Group the services into scannable categories while preserving the existing service list.
- Bring the pricing/free-estimate expectations forward so visitors know how to start.
- Reuse original Wix-hosted images, including the hero tools photo, service images, owner image, and Facebook icon.
- Keep the same overall page DNA while removing friction and template residue from the customer-facing message.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a focused homepage title such as `Fix It Quick: Handyman Services LLC | Richmond Handyman Services`.
- Use one primary H1 that includes local handyman intent.
- Add a concise meta description naming Richmond, Tri-Cities, handyman services, assembly, repair, pressure washing, dryer duct cleaning, and small home improvement help.
- Use descriptive alt text for the tools hero, service photos, and owner image.
- Add LocalBusiness or HomeAndConstructionBusiness schema after confirming preferred public service area, phone, email, and owner details.
- Make sure the Contact page repeats the same estimate instructions as the homepage.

## Realistic implementation angle
This does not need a full redesign. The realistic move is a surgical Wix cleanup that keeps the same site identity but reorganizes the homepage around action: call, email, common services, service area, and estimate instructions.

Likely implementation scope: homepage copy cleanup, CTA wording, service grouping, contact/estimate guidance, mobile order, footer cleanup, basic SEO fields, and optional contact form improvements.

Implementation should stay separate from the $99 diagnosis. A practical follow-up cleanup sprint would likely be a modest Wix improvement project, not a new brand or custom build.
