# Eco Cleaning Services — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Eco Cleaning Services is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current site has recognizable brand value and real local-business proof, but the first-screen clarity, trust hierarchy, service grouping, and action path can be tightened without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current site family and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Get Your Free Estimate**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Text/wordmark identity and current business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #217a46, #8cc63e, #1aa8a8, #153324.
- **Typography feel:** Poppins, Arial, sans-serif heading feel with Poppins, Arial, sans-serif body copy direction.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Local-service navigation with phone/contact emphasis.
- **Hero/image treatment:** eco leaves style visual cue with strong service promise.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:** modern WordPress cleaning site; green eco palette; hero quote form; health/well-being promise; large service-area navigation and review links.

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with real local-service intent in Naperville, IL.
- The site already contains useful proof: Non-toxic products, HEPA + microfiber process, Recurring + deep cleaning.
- The existing visual direction is recognizable enough for Solution A instead of a full redesign.
- There is a direct contact or booking path that can be made easier to notice.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The free-estimate path is present but the hero/form area is surrounded by navigation and repeated elements that dilute action.
   - Prototype response: Make one clean estimate form/CTA the focal point.
2. **High:** Visible credibility issues such as zero-style stats and typo-prone headings can make the site feel unfinished.
   - Prototype response: Replace counters with concrete trust/process proof and tighten headings.
3. **High:** Service breadth and service-area links crowd the primary house-cleaning message.
   - Prototype response: Group services into Regular, Deep, and Specialty paths.
4. **High:** The health/ecological differentiator is strong but too paragraph-heavy.
   - Prototype response: Move non-toxic products, HEPA, microfiber, and healthy-home proof into a trust strip.
5. **Medium:** Multiple forms repeat before expectations are clear.
   - Prototype response: Add a three-step estimate flow before the form.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Replace scattered first-screen messaging with a clear local-service promise: “Eco-friendly house cleaning with a cleaner quote path.”
2. Make **Get Your Free Estimate** the primary action in the hero, process section, and contact block.
3. Move trust proof near the top: Non-toxic products, HEPA + microfiber process, Recurring + deep cleaning, Naperville-area service.
4. Organize services by buyer intent instead of making visitors decode every page/link.
5. Add a simple “what happens next” flow before the form or contact action.
6. Clean template residue, duplicate text, stale content, or technical form clutter where present.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
