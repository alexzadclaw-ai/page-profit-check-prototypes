# Handyman Experts Inc. — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Handyman Experts Inc. is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current site has recognizable brand value and real local-business proof, but the first-screen clarity, trust hierarchy, service grouping, and action path can be tightened without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current site family and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Get a Free Quote**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Text/wordmark identity and current business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #274c77, #f2c94c, #d9534f, #182332.
- **Typography feel:** Arial Black, Arial, sans-serif heading feel with Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif body copy direction.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Local-service navigation with phone/contact emphasis.
- **Hero/image treatment:** tools grid style visual cue with strong service promise.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:** older static handyman website; blue/yellow practical palette; phone/email-forward header; long testimonial proof; interior/exterior service links.

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with real local-service intent in Naperville, IL.
- The site already contains useful proof: Family owned since 2002, Interior + exterior repairs, Many local testimonials.
- The existing visual direction is recognizable enough for Solution A instead of a full redesign.
- There is a direct contact or booking path that can be made easier to notice.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The quote path and core services are less prominent than long testimonial content, slowing urgent home-repair visitors.
   - Prototype response: Lead with Get a Free Quote, phone/text, and service categories.
2. **High:** The opening paragraph tries to explain everything at once instead of segmenting repair needs.
   - Prototype response: Group services into interior, exterior, and remodeling/project paths.
3. **High:** Two phone numbers and email are useful but visually scattered.
   - Prototype response: Present office/cell/email as a clean contact panel.
4. **High:** Testimonials are strong but dominate before visitors know whether the company handles their issue.
   - Prototype response: Move testimonial proof into a supporting trust block after service routing.
5. **Medium:** Footer date and older design cues could make the site feel stale despite active proof.
   - Prototype response: Refresh hierarchy while preserving the established-business feel.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Replace scattered first-screen messaging with a clear local-service promise: “Naperville handyman help with a clearer free-estimate path.”
2. Make **Get a Free Quote** the primary action in the hero, process section, and contact block.
3. Move trust proof near the top: Family owned since 2002, Interior + exterior repairs, Many local testimonials, No job too big or too small.
4. Organize services by buyer intent instead of making visitors decode every page/link.
5. Add a simple “what happens next” flow before the form or contact action.
6. Clean template residue, duplicate text, stale content, or technical form clutter where present.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
