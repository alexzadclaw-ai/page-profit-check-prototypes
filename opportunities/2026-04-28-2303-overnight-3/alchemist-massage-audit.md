# Alchemist Massage, PLLC — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Alchemist Massage, PLLC is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current site has recognizable brand value and real local-business proof, but the first-screen clarity, trust hierarchy, service grouping, and action path can be tightened without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current site family and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Request an Appointment**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Text/wordmark identity and current business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #6f4aa3, #bca3df, #d8b46a, #231f2c.
- **Typography feel:** Georgia, Times New Roman, serif heading feel with Georgia, Times New Roman, serif body copy direction.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Local-service navigation with phone/contact emphasis.
- **Hero/image treatment:** amethyst calm style visual cue with strong service promise.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:** Wix wellness site; soft spiritual/massage positioning; purple-lavender calm palette; personal practitioner voice; simple nav with About / Services / Testimonials / Connect.

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with real local-service intent in Naperville, IL.
- The site already contains useful proof: Licensed massage therapist, Naperville + ~15 mile radius, Mobile sessions in your space.
- The existing visual direction is recognizable enough for Solution A instead of a full redesign.
- There is a direct contact or booking path that can be made easier to notice.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The first screen emphasizes reflective wellness language before clearly stating mobile massage, service radius, and appointment action.
   - Prototype response: Lead with mobile massage in Naperville, then support the holistic promise.
2. **High:** Booking is split between contact links, form copy, services/pricing, and email, so the next step is not obvious.
   - Prototype response: Use “Request an Appointment” as the consistent primary CTA and explain the three-step booking flow.
3. **High:** Practical trust signals such as license number, phone, and service radius are too low or easy to miss.
   - Prototype response: Add a trust strip with license/radius/mobile/personalized care.
4. **High:** The testimonial path says “coming soon,” which weakens confidence if left as a main nav promise.
   - Prototype response: Reframe proof around license, service radius, and clear consultation until testimonials are ready.
5. **Medium:** Contact/email details include inconsistent mailto behavior on the source page.
   - Prototype response: Make one visible email and phone path consistent in the contact section.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Replace scattered first-screen messaging with a clear local-service promise: “Mobile massage therapy brought to your space in Naperville.”
2. Make **Request an Appointment** the primary action in the hero, process section, and contact block.
3. Move trust proof near the top: Licensed massage therapist, Naperville + ~15 mile radius, Mobile sessions in your space, Personalized bodywork + care.
4. Organize services by buyer intent instead of making visitors decode every page/link.
5. Add a simple “what happens next” flow before the form or contact action.
6. Clean template residue, duplicate text, stale content, or technical form clutter where present.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
