# EcoClean Carpet Cleaning — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
EcoClean Carpet Cleaning is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current site has recognizable brand value and real local-business proof, but the first-screen clarity, trust hierarchy, service grouping, and action path can be tightened without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current site family and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Request an Estimate**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Text/wordmark identity and current business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #0b6e4f, #1f7ecb, #f05a28, #0f2530.
- **Typography feel:** Montserrat, Arial, sans-serif heading feel with Open Sans, Arial, sans-serif body copy direction.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Local-service navigation with phone/contact emphasis.
- **Hero/image treatment:** fresh carpet style visual cue with strong service promise.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:** WordPress service/restoration site; green/blue/orange cleaning palette; large mega-menu duplication; review and emergency bars; healthy-home headline and many service links.

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with real local-service intent in Naperville, IL.
- The site already contains useful proof: 1000+ positive reviews, 24/7 water + mold services, Price quotes by phone.
- The existing visual direction is recognizable enough for Solution A instead of a full redesign.
- There is a direct contact or booking path that can be made easier to notice.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** Routine carpet-cleaning visitors and urgent water/mold visitors are mixed into the same crowded hero/navigation path.
   - Prototype response: Separate routine estimate and emergency call paths in the hero and process.
2. **High:** Duplicated mega-navigation and repeated service lists overload first-time visitors.
   - Prototype response: Condense services into three clear categories.
3. **High:** 1000+ reviews and 24/7 proof are strong but compete with too many links.
   - Prototype response: Move proof into a concise trust strip.
4. **High:** The estimate CTA appears as a generic anchor/popup path without explaining what information is needed.
   - Prototype response: Create a clear “Request an Estimate” path and three-step flow.
5. **Medium:** Service scope is impressive but can blur the primary carpet-cleaning offer.
   - Prototype response: Lead with carpet/healthy-home promise, then route to restoration.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Replace scattered first-screen messaging with a clear local-service promise: “Healthy-home carpet cleaning with separate paths for routine and emergency needs.”
2. Make **Request an Estimate** the primary action in the hero, process section, and contact block.
3. Move trust proof near the top: 1000+ positive reviews, 24/7 water + mold services, Price quotes by phone, Naperville healthy-home experts.
4. Organize services by buyer intent instead of making visitors decode every page/link.
5. Add a simple “what happens next” flow before the form or contact action.
6. Clean template residue, duplicate text, stale content, or technical form clutter where present.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
