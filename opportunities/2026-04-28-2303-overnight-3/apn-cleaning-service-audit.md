# APN Cleaning Service LLC — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
APN Cleaning Service LLC is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current site has recognizable brand value and real local-business proof, but the first-screen clarity, trust hierarchy, service grouping, and action path can be tightened without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current site family and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Request a Free Estimate**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Text/wordmark identity and current business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #195f93, #7dcff4, #ffd36a, #172b3a.
- **Typography feel:** Trebuchet MS, Arial, sans-serif heading feel with Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif body copy direction.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Local-service navigation with phone/contact emphasis.
- **Hero/image treatment:** cleaning sparkles style visual cue with strong service promise.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:** Wix-style local cleaning homepage; blue/white clean-service palette; licensed/bonded/insured badge emphasis; family-owned proof and flexible scheduling; simple service tiles and estimate CTA.

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with real local-service intent in Romeoville / Naperville, IL.
- The site already contains useful proof: Serving since 1993, Licensed, bonded & insured, Independent local family business.
- The existing visual direction is recognizable enough for Solution A instead of a full redesign.
- There is a direct contact or booking path that can be made easier to notice.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The first screen does not quickly connect the slogan to the exact cleaning services and free-estimate action.
   - Prototype response: Rewrite the hero around recurring/deep/move/commercial cleaning and make “Request a Free Estimate” the primary CTA.
2. **High:** Strong proof such as since 1993, independent family ownership, bonded/insured service, and guarantee is buried in paragraphs.
   - Prototype response: Move four trust signals directly below the hero.
3. **High:** Service links and repeated URL/card text make the page harder to scan than the actual offering.
   - Prototype response: Group services into three buyer-intent cards.
4. **High:** The quote path competes with Special Offers, FAQ, Reviews, Supplies List, and other links before the visitor knows what to do.
   - Prototype response: Use one consistent estimate CTA in hero and contact block, with secondary phone option.
5. **Medium:** Footer/service-area content has enough detail but needs cleaner hierarchy and fewer empty/link-only fragments.
   - Prototype response: Keep local service area and contact facts while simplifying the footer.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Replace scattered first-screen messaging with a clear local-service promise: “Reliable house cleaning for Naperville-area families who want their time back.”
2. Make **Request a Free Estimate** the primary action in the hero, process section, and contact block.
3. Move trust proof near the top: Serving since 1993, Licensed, bonded & insured, Independent local family business, 100% guaranteed work.
4. Organize services by buyer intent instead of making visitors decode every page/link.
5. Add a simple “what happens next” flow before the form or contact action.
6. Clean template residue, duplicate text, stale content, or technical form clutter where present.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
