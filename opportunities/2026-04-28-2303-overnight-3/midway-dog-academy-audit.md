# Midway Dog Academy — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Midway Dog Academy is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current site has recognizable brand value and real local-business proof, but the first-screen clarity, trust hierarchy, service grouping, and action path can be tightened without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current site family and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Discuss Your Dog**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Text/wordmark identity and current business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #17385f, #f0a232, #59a96a, #142133.
- **Typography feel:** Arial Black, Roboto, Arial, sans-serif heading feel with Roboto, Arial, sans-serif body copy direction.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Local-service navigation with phone/contact emphasis.
- **Hero/image treatment:** training leash style visual cue with strong service promise.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:** WordPress-style dog academy site; dark blue/gold professional palette; top phone/social bar; large dropdown navigation; free evaluation and testimonial emphasis.

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with real local-service intent in Naperville / Chicago, IL.
- The site already contains useful proof: Free evaluation path, 7am–9pm availability, Puppy + adult dog programs.
- The existing visual direction is recognizable enough for Solution A instead of a full redesign.
- There is a direct contact or booking path that can be made easier to notice.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The homepage repeats generic training copy instead of quickly routing owners by problem and program type.
   - Prototype response: Lead with “Discuss Your Dog” and group services by owner need.
2. **High:** The large menu and many program links make visitors work before choosing puppy, behavior, board-and-train, or private lessons.
   - Prototype response: Use three clear service cards and a free-evaluation process.
3. **High:** Free evaluation is present but not strong enough as the central conversion path.
   - Prototype response: Make free evaluation the main hero/contact CTA.
4. **High:** Testimonials are valuable but appear after a lot of copy.
   - Prototype response: Move proof language into an early trust strip and supporting proof block.
5. **Medium:** The title/SEO direction is location-rich but copy density harms scannability.
   - Prototype response: Tighten headings around dog behavior outcomes and local service.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Replace scattered first-screen messaging with a clear local-service promise: “Start with a free evaluation for the dog behavior you need fixed.”
2. Make **Discuss Your Dog** the primary action in the hero, process section, and contact block.
3. Move trust proof near the top: Free evaluation path, 7am–9pm availability, Puppy + adult dog programs, Client success stories.
4. Organize services by buyer intent instead of making visitors decode every page/link.
5. Add a simple “what happens next” flow before the form or contact action.
6. Clean template residue, duplicate text, stale content, or technical form clutter where present.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
