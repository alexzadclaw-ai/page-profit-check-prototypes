## Quick take

Capps Lawn and Landscaping already has the bones of a solid local service site: clear business name, calm green palette, relevant lawn and irrigation imagery, service categories, a real testimonial, and direct contact info. The biggest issue is that the current homepage looks more like a simple brochure than a quote-generating page. A few surgical changes can keep the same Wix-style site identity while making it much easier for homeowners to call, email, or request a quote.

## What is already working

- The business name is repeated clearly and consistently.
- The teal and white palette feels appropriate for lawn care and landscaping.
- The hero grass photo, sprinkler photo, garden image, and trimming image all support the service category.
- The page already has service categories: Garden Design, Lawn Care, and Irrigation.
- The testimonial is strong because it specifically mentions showing up, professionalism, and expertise.
- Contact details are present: `Cappsc40@gmail.com` and `8506243064`.
- The existing layout has a recognizable Wix/site-builder rhythm: large centered hero, split about block, three service cards, testimonial band, contact panel, and footer.

## Biggest conversion issues

- The Wix promotional banner at the top makes the business feel less polished.
- The hero has no clear customer action. There is no above-the-fold quote button, call button, or service-area cue.
- The phone number appears far down the page and is not formatted as a tappable `(850) 624-3064` link.
- The email is present but could be lowercase and clickable for trust and usability.
- The service cards do not explain what the visitor gets or offer a next step.
- The testimonial section has a lot of unused space and only one visible review, so the trust signal is underused.
- The contact section is strong visually but vertically loose, making the form feel farther away than it needs to be.
- The footer keeps the Wix-created language, which can make the site look unfinished.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements

- Remove visible Wix promotional branding where the business can control it.
- Add a small hero service-area line: `Panama City, FL and Bay County`.
- Add two hero CTAs: `Request a Free Quote` and `Call (850) 624-3064`.
- Preserve the large all-caps hero identity, but support it with a plain-English value line.
- Keep the original split about section, but add simple trust/expectation bullets.
- Keep the original three service cards and original photos, but add one sentence and a quote prompt to each card.
- Tighten the testimonial section by keeping the existing quote and using the empty side for proof points.
- Format contact info as clickable mail and phone links.
- Keep the contact form, but make the surrounding copy answer what a visitor should send.
- Add the source logo from the live site's Open Graph image as a small header brand mark.

## SEO basics to tighten

- Use one focused title tag, for example: `Capps Lawn and Landscaping LLC | Panama City Lawn Care and Irrigation`.
- Improve the meta description beyond the current short phrase so it mentions lawn care, landscaping, irrigation, Bay County, and Panama City.
- Keep one visible H1 and avoid repeating the exact business name as the only headline in multiple sections.
- Add local service-area language in visible copy, not just metadata.
- Format the phone number as a clickable `tel:` link.
- Add descriptive alt text to every image.
- Consider adding LocalBusiness or HomeAndConstructionBusiness schema if Wix allows it.
- If the business has a Google Business Profile, link the website from it and keep NAP details consistent.

## Realistic implementation angle

This does not need a full rebuild. The prototype is intentionally close to the current Wix homepage: same section order, same green and white direction, same service card pattern, same contact block, and same source imagery. The realistic implementation path is a focused Wix edit:

- Hide or remove Wix ad/banner if the plan supports it.
- Update hero copy and add two buttons.
- Format phone and email as links.
- Add short service descriptions under the existing cards.
- Tighten the testimonial and contact sections.
- Update title, meta description, image alt text, and footer language.

Estimated ROM: 3 to 5 hours in Wix for copy, layout, link, and SEO updates if the current site owner has editor access. Add extra time only if the Wix plan/domain setup needs upgrading or if form automation needs to be connected.
