# Pro Garden Landscaping LLC Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take

Pro Garden Landscaping LLC already has the essentials customers need: a real local business name, service list, phone, email, address, Facebook link, and original project photos. The biggest opportunity is not a full redesign. It is a cleaner version of the same Wix site with clearer estimate calls, better phone formatting, tighter spacing, service context, and basic local SEO cleanup.

## What is already working

- The site feels local and real because it uses original landscaping photos instead of obvious stock imagery.
- The black, white, gray, and beige palette is simple and recognizable.
- The page has a clear section order: header/nav, hero, About, Services, free estimate/contact, footer.
- Phone, email, address, and Facebook are all present.
- The service list covers high-intent landscaping needs: mulch, sod, cleanups, weekly lawn care, edging, patios, walls, pruning, and drainage.
- The free estimate message is already aligned with how a landscaping customer wants to start.

## Biggest conversion issues

- The Wix promotional banner at the top makes the business feel less professional before visitors even reach the hero.
- The hero has two side-by-side buttons with the same label, `Get in Touch`, but one is phone and one is email. That creates friction.
- Phone number formatting is hard to scan on the live site: `8152711022` instead of `(815) 271-1022`.
- The hero and service areas use very large empty spacing, which pushes useful details and contact options far down the page.
- The service list is just names, so customers do not get enough confidence about what each service includes.
- The `Book Online` nav item may be confusing if the experience is really an estimate request rather than instant booking.
- The contact form area is visually present, but the submit path should be clearly connected to email, phone follow-up, or Wix form handling.
- The live page has no meta description, generic image alt text such as `Untitled`, and little location/service SEO context.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements

- Keep the same site structure and original photos, but tighten the vertical rhythm so the CTA and services are easier to reach.
- Rename duplicate hero CTAs into clear actions: `Call for Free Estimate` and `Email Project Details`.
- Format the phone number everywhere and keep it clickable with `tel:` links.
- Add a concise local/value line in the meta description and hero copy.
- Add short descriptions under each service so the list sells the work instead of only naming it.
- Keep the About split section, but make the proof points easier to scan with small trust chips like free estimates, open communication, and local service.
- Preserve the contact/free estimate section, but make the form fields feel more intentional and add phone/email backup paths.
- Remove or reduce distractions from Wix branding where possible through a paid Wix plan and domain setup.

## SEO basics to tighten

- Change the title from `Home | Pro Garden Landscaping LLC` to include service and locality intent, for example `Pro Garden Landscaping LLC | Landscaping in Harvard, IL`.
- Add a meta description that mentions landscaping, lawn care, cleanups, mulch, sod, patios, drainage, Harvard, McHenry County, and free estimates.
- Replace generic image alt text like `Untitled` with descriptive alt text for the logo and project photos.
- Add the formatted phone number and address consistently in the header, contact section, and footer.
- Add local business schema if Wix allows it through custom code or SEO settings.
- Consider adding separate service pages or expandable sections later for high-intent terms like drainage issues, retaining walls, sod installation, and spring/fall cleanup.

## Realistic implementation angle

This is a practical Wix cleanup, not a rebuild. Most improvements can be done in the Wix editor by changing text, button labels, section spacing, image alt text, SEO settings, and contact form behavior. Removing the Wix promo banner and connecting a custom domain likely requires a paid Wix plan. A realistic implementation ROM is 2 to 4 hours for the page cleanup, plus any Wix plan/domain setup time.

## Offer fit

This site is a strong fit for a $99 Page Profit Check because the business does not need a brand-new visual identity. It needs the existing page tightened so more visitors understand the services and contact the owner for a free estimate.
