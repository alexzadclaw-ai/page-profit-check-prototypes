# T the Lawn Care, LLC - Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
T the Lawn Care is a good fit for a $99 Page Profit Check because the live Wix site already has the pieces a local buyer needs: real photos, a visible phone number, service categories, an about story, a quote CTA, and contact details. The issue is not that the site needs a new brand. It needs a cleaner first screen, stronger local proof, more specific quote language, and less generic Wix/Book Now friction.

**Recommendation:** keep the same Wix-style lawn-care site family, same header/nav pattern, same blue/gray palette, and same original photos, but rebuild the homepage hierarchy around one action: **Get a Free Lawn Care Quote**.

- **Visual resemblance goal:** High
- **Audit coverage goal:** High
- **Primary conversion action:** Get a Free Lawn Care Quote
- **Location angle:** Pompano Beach, Broward County, and surrounding communities
- **Implementation type:** Surgical Wix homepage cleanup, not a rebrand

## What is already working
- The site has a real local-service identity with the business name, phone number, email, address, and Facebook link visible.
- The header/nav is simple and recognizable: Home, Services, About, Gallery, Contact, plus Book Now.
- The page uses original lawn-care photos, not generic stock imagery.
- The service range is clear enough to qualify buyers: lawn and garden care, fountains and ponds, landscaping, docks/decks/fencing, patios/driveways, tree service, and pool coping.
- The about copy includes useful trust details: founded in 2004, Broward County service area, residential and commercial work, quality workmanship, and customer-service focus.
- The existing visual direction is consistent: dark navy/blue blocks, white cards, gray section backgrounds, bold Wix heading typography, and a dark contact footer.

## Biggest conversion issues
1. **Critical:** The first screen looks like a template page before it feels like a local quote path. The Wix banner, split H1, and generic Book Now button make the business look less polished than the work likely is.
   - Prototype response: remove the Wix-ad distraction in the cleaned-up view, keep the same header/nav, and make the hero headline one clear local service promise.

2. **High:** The main CTA says Book Now, but most lawn-care visitors probably need a quote or callback before booking.
   - Prototype response: change the primary action to Get a Free Lawn Care Quote, with phone as the secondary action.

3. **High:** Good proof is present but buried. Founded in 2004, Broward County, residential/commercial, and customer satisfaction should appear near the top.
   - Prototype response: add a proof strip immediately after the hero using the same blue/white visual language.

4. **High:** The service cards list many jobs, but they do not help visitors choose the right path or understand what happens next.
   - Prototype response: keep the same services and card grid, then add short buyer-focused descriptions and one consistent quote CTA.

5. **Medium:** The about copy has grammar/capitalization issues that can quietly reduce trust.
   - Prototype response: preserve the story but clean the wording so it sounds professional and local.

6. **Medium:** The contact form is visible, but it does not set expectations for response, estimate, or service-area fit.
   - Prototype response: rewrite the contact section around a simple quote request and add next-step expectations.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Replace generic Book Now emphasis with **Get a Free Lawn Care Quote**.
2. Keep the same hero geometry but turn the split headline into one stronger local H1.
3. Move top proof closer to the hero: founded in 2004, Broward County, residential/commercial, free quotes.
4. Keep the original service categories but add short scannable descriptions.
5. Preserve the original photos and logo so the owner recognizes the site immediately.
6. Clean the about copy while keeping the same company story.
7. Add a simple “what happens next” quote process before the contact form.
8. Keep the dark Wix-style footer and contact layout, but make it more intentional.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use one clear H1, for example: `Full-Service Lawn Care and Landscaping in Broward County`.
- Update the homepage title to combine the service, location, and brand.
- Rewrite the meta description around Pompano Beach, Broward County, lawn care, landscaping, and free quotes.
- Add descriptive alt text to the real photos, especially the hero, about, quote, and gallery images.
- Use H2s for Services, About, Latest Work, and Contact instead of relying on visual text only.
- Add LocalBusiness or LandscapeBusiness schema after the final copy is approved.
- If this remains on Wix, connect a custom domain and remove the Wix promotional banner if budget allows.

## Realistic implementation angle
This should be handled as a focused Wix homepage cleanup, not a full rebuild. The current site structure is serviceable. The practical implementation is to edit the existing Wix sections, reuse the same photos, tighten the wording, adjust spacing, rename CTAs, and add a top proof strip and quote-process block.

A realistic implementation range is **3 to 6 hours in Wix** if the current editor access is available and no booking-system rebuild is required. If the owner wants the Wix ad removed, that may require a paid Wix plan and domain connection.
