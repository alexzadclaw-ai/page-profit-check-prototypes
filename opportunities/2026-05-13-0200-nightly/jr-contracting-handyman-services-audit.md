## Quick take

The site has a memorable black and bright green look, visible phone and email, and a simple one-page Wix structure. The biggest opportunity is not a full redesign. It is tightening the first screen, giving the service section more substance, making the contact path clearer, and adding Vancouver, WA relevance so visitors immediately understand what JR does and how to request work.

## What is already working

- The business name, phone, email, and Facebook link are visible near the top.
- The black and green palette is distinctive and easy to recognize.
- The page has a simple flow: header, hero, about, services, contact, footer.
- The site already includes core services: Refurbishing, Repair, and Remodeling.
- The contact section has a form plus address, email, and phone.
- The Wix page is lightweight in concept and should be easy to edit without rebuilding the business identity.

## Biggest conversion issues

- The hero headline says welcome but does not quickly say what the business does, where it serves, or what to do next.
- The first call to action is small compared with the oversized headline.
- The services section is underdeveloped. It lists three services but gives no descriptions, use cases, or next-step links.
- The page has excessive vertical whitespace, making important information feel sparse and unfinished.
- The form button says only Submit. That is functional, but less persuasive than Request an Estimate or Ask About This Project.
- The Wix banner and Wix footer language reduce trust. If the business stays on Wix, upgrading to remove Wix branding would help.
- SEO basics are thin. The page needs location and service language in the title, H1 support text, service copy, and meta description.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements

- Keep the existing black and green visual identity, but make the hero communicate a clearer value proposition.
- Add Vancouver, WA to the hero and page metadata.
- Keep the split hero geometry with the original construction image on the left and the green panel on the right.
- Turn the three service words into three useful service cards using the original source images and short plain-English descriptions.
- Add stronger CTAs: Request an Estimate, Call (360) 600-4972, Ask about refurbishing, Schedule a repair, Start a remodel.
- Improve the contact section with a short lead-in, better field labels, and a clearer submit button.
- Preserve the centered logo area, top navigation, email, phone, footer contact details, and Wix-style texture so the owner recognizes the cleaned-up version.

## SEO basics to tighten

- Suggested title: `JR Contracting & Handyman Services LLC | Vancouver WA Home Improvement`
- Suggested meta description: `JR Contracting & Handyman Services LLC provides refurbishing, repair, and remodeling help in Vancouver, WA. Call or email for a clear estimate.`
- Keep one main H1 and support it with local service language.
- Add service descriptions for refurbishing, repair, and remodeling instead of only headings.
- Add descriptive image alt text for each reused source image.
- Use real internal anchors for Home, About, Services, and Contact.
- If possible, connect a custom domain and remove Wix promotional branding.
