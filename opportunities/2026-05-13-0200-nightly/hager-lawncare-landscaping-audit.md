# Page Profit Check Audit: Hager lawncare & landscaping LLC

Batch: 2026-05-13-0200-nightly  
Prototype: `page-profit-check-prototypes/prototypes/2026-05-13-0200-nightly/hager-lawncare-landscaping/index.html`

## Quick take

This is a recognizable, simple Wix lawn care site with a strong black-and-white visual direction, useful contact form, real phone number, and service-area copy. The main conversion problem is not the look. It is that the site asks visitors to work too hard before they can request a quote, and a few small trust and clarity issues make it feel unfinished.

The prototype keeps the same site structure and visual personality, then makes surgical improvements: stronger quote CTA, consistent contact details, cleaned copy, better service clarity, improved seasonal readability, lighter whitespace, and basic local SEO framing.

## What is already working

- Clear local business name in the header: Hager lawncare & landscaping LLC.
- Real contact paths are present: phone, email, Facebook, and a contact form.
- The black background, large white text, and simple Wix spacing create a memorable look.
- The hero image, About image, Seasonal banner, and circular autumn image already give the page visual rhythm.
- The site explains core services: lawn mowing, weed trimming, tree trimming, leaf blowing, and future pressure washing.
- The form fields are practical for a service quote: name, email, phone, address, subject, and message.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. The top Wix ad banner is visible and makes the business look less established.
2. The header email is `jaredhager15@gmail.com`, but the contact section uses `hagerlawncare@gmail.com`. That inconsistency can create hesitation.
3. The main CTA is a thin outline button. It fits the design, but it does not stand out enough for quote requests.
4. The hero copy has a typo spacing issue: `estimate.Be`.
5. The About copy has capitalization issues, especially `Danville wv`, which should read `Danville, WV`.
6. The Seasonal banner text sits directly on the image, so readability depends on the image area behind it.
7. There is no quick service scan near the top. Visitors have to read paragraph copy to confirm that mowing, trimming, tree work, and leaf cleanup are offered.
8. The page has very large vertical gaps, especially before the footer, which makes the site feel longer than it needs to be.
9. The page title and metadata are generic: `Home | Hager lawncare & landscaping LLC`. It should mention lawn care and Danville, WV.
10. The footer still says `Proudly created with Wix.com`, which competes with the business brand.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements

- Keep the same header layout, logo, nav, Facebook icon, and black background, but make the email consistent across the page.
- Keep the hero geometry, with the mower photo on the left and `Welcome` copy on the right, but add clearer conversion cues: `Free estimates`, `Danville, WV area`, and `Residential and commercial`.
- Convert the hero CTA into a stronger white button while keeping the original outlined-button style as a secondary phone CTA.
- Fix small copy issues without rewriting the business into a different voice.
- Preserve the About section and right-side photo, including the white offset image border.
- Preserve the full-width Seasonal banner and add a subtle dark overlay so the text stays readable.
- Preserve the circular autumn image and add a compact service grid below it for scanability.
- Keep the contact section layout and form fields, but add a small expectation-setting note for free estimates.
- Remove Wix-branded clutter from the cleaned prototype.
- Tighten footer spacing and make the phone number clickable.

## SEO basics to tighten

- Use a title like `Hager Lawn Care & Landscaping LLC | Danville, WV`.
- Add a meta description that includes lawn mowing, weed trimming, tree trimming, leaf cleanup, seasonal services, and Danville, WV.
- Use one clear H1. The current H1 is `Welcome`, which is visually consistent, but adding local service context nearby helps.
- Normalize business naming: `Hager Lawn Care and Landscaping LLC` in paragraph copy, while preserving the visible logo/header style if the owner prefers `lawncare`.
- Add local signals in copy: Danville, WV, West Virginia, residential and commercial, free estimates.
- Make phone and email links crawlable and clickable.
- Add descriptive alt text for the logo and photos instead of generic image names.

This is a small Wix cleanup, not a full rebuild. A practical implementation would be:

- 30 to 45 minutes: update header email, footer text, title, metadata, alt text, and copy fixes.
- 45 to 90 minutes: adjust hero CTA styling, add quick service pills, and add phone link.

Realistic ROM estimate: 2.5 to 4.5 hours in Wix if the owner has editor access and the existing site is editable cleanly.
