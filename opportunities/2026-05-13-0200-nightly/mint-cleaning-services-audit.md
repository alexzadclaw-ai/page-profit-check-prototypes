# Mint Cleaning Services LLC Page Profit Check

Batch: 2026-05-13-0200-nightly  
Slug: mint-cleaning-services  
Live site: https://ymmintcleaningsvc.wixsite.com/mintcleaningservices

## Quick take

Mint Cleaning Services LLC already has the bones of a good one-page Wix site: visible contact info, a real business name, a friendly tagline, veteran-owned positioning, bilingual service support, and two useful testimonials. The biggest opportunity is not a rebuild. It is a sharper first screen, clearer quote path, more useful service copy, and basic SEO cleanup so visitors know what Mint Cleaning does, why they should trust it, and how to request a quote without scrolling to the bottom.

## What is already working

- Email and phone are visible near the top and repeated in the footer.
- The brand name and tagline are memorable: “Rest and forget the mess, Mint Cleaning will leave it clean and fresh.”
- The site has a simple Wix one-page flow: header, hero, services, about, reviews, quote form, footer.
- Core services are already listed: Move-in/Move-out, Common Area Maintenance, Customized Cleaning Services, and Hablamos Español.
- The About section communicates a strong trust point: veteran-owned and operated.
- The testimonials are specific and credible, especially around move-in/move-out and preparing a house for sale or occupancy.
- The soft cream, mauve, and sage palette fits a calm cleaning-service brand.
- The source site has enough images to keep the prototype visually recognizable without adding outside stock.

## Biggest conversion issues

- The hero has no strong call to action. Visitors see text first, but there is no immediate “Request a Quote” or “Call Now” button above the fold.
- The phone number appears as plain text on the live site, not a clearly clickable call link.
- The navigation hides important sections behind “More,” which makes Testimonials and Get a Quote less obvious.
- Service cards only show headings and images. They do not explain what each service includes or who it is for.
- The quote form arrives very late after a long scroll, so motivated visitors may not reach it.
- The form is generic. It asks for comments, but not service type, property details, timing, or language preference.
- The site does not state a clear service area. The 505 phone number suggests New Mexico, but the site itself should confirm the city or region before using local SEO language.
- The page title is generic: `Home | Mint Cleaning Services LLC`.
- The meta description is empty.
- Wix/Vista branding at the top and Wix credit in the footer reduce polish and trust.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements

- Preserve the current Wix-style structure and visual identity, but make the first screen more action-oriented.
- Add two hero CTAs: Request a Cleaning Quote and Call (505) 226-2522.
- Keep the original headline and tagline, but tighten the hero copy around move-in, move-out, common-area, customized cleaning, veteran-owned reliability, and eco-conscious products.
- Keep the two-column service grid and original images, but add short service descriptions and service-specific quote links.
- Make phone and email links clickable in the header, form area, and footer.
- Keep the About section in the same mauve band with the veteran image, but add four quick trust points.
- Keep both testimonials and make the review cards easier to scan.
- Keep the large cleaning-materials image before the form to preserve the live page rhythm.
- Improve the quote form with a service selector and clearer submit language: Request My Cleaning Quote.
- Keep the footer content and Facebook icon, while noting that removing Wix branding would be a trust upgrade if the business uses a paid Wix plan.

- Add service copy for move-in/move-out cleaning, common-area maintenance, custom cleaning, and Spanish-language support.
