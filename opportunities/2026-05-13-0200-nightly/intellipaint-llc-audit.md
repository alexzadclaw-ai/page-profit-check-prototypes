# Intellipaint LLC Audit

## Quick take
Intellipaint LLC is a good Page Profit Check fit because the site already has real local business signals, a phone number, an email address, service content, and original visual assets. The main opportunity is not a full redesign. It is a surgical cleanup that keeps the same Wix/local-service feel while making the first screen, quote path, service hierarchy, and trust cues easier to understand.

Visual fingerprint observed: bright blue Wix-style header, centered text logo, social icons on the left, centered nav, cream and sage sections, peach color accents, large Questrial-like headings, circular service photos, paint-brush strip graphics, large interior/paint imagery, four-column service grid, project/Facebook callout, and contact form/footer.

Recognizable cues to preserve: Intellipaint LLC wordmark text, phone number, Licensed & Insured line, Color Consultants tagline, Home/Services/About/Contact navigation, warm cream/sage/peach palette, original image treatment, long section rhythm, and contact form placement.

Visual resemblance target: High. The prototype should feel like the same Wix site cleaned up, not a new agency/SaaS landing page.

## What is already working
- The business name, phone number, and Licensed & Insured message are visible in the header.
- The site has a clear service category: residential painting, cabinet painting/refacing, staining, drywall work, and color guidance.
- There is a real contact email: `intellipaint2696@gmail.com`.
- The live page uses original Wix-hosted visual assets that can be reused instead of generic stock.
- The color direction has a recognizable personality: bright blue header, cream backgrounds, sage copy/buttons, and peach paint/color accents.
- The contact form already exists, so conversion improvements can be framed as a cleanup rather than a rebuild.

## Biggest conversion issues
### Critical
1. The hero/header shows the phone and license status, but it does not give visitors a clear primary action.
   - Prototype fix: add `Get a Painting Quote` and `Call Now` buttons while preserving the centered header geometry.
2. Trust cues are present, but they are not structured for quick scanning.
   - Prototype fix: add a compact trust strip for licensed and insured, Middle Tennessee, color consulting, and quote-ready contact.

### High
3. The navigation has two About links, which makes the site feel unfinished.
   - Prototype fix: use one About link and add a Projects link to match the visible project section.
4. The first Services section has very long, dense paragraphs that slow down visitors.
   - Prototype fix: keep the three-column circular-image layout, but shorten the copy into buyer-friendly service summaries.
5. The Project section has a large empty gap and only a plain social text link.
   - Prototype fix: turn the Facebook path into a clean project callout and remove the excessive blank space.
6. Some social links appear to point to Wix defaults rather than Intellipaint-owned profiles.
   - Prototype fix: preserve the social icon look, keep the real Facebook URL, and avoid sending visitors to Wix default Instagram/Pinterest pages.

### Medium
7. The footer has template-looking details, including a future copyright year and unrelated brand text.
   - Prototype fix: use Intellipaint LLC footer language and current business contact details.
8. The contact form asks for information, but it does not tell visitors what to include or what happens next.
   - Prototype fix: add a simple three-step quote process and a form note.
9. SEO basics are present but rough. The title is URL-heavy and the H1 mixes business name, phone number, and trust text.
   - Prototype fix: use a clearer title, one focused H1, and service/location language in headings and body copy.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Keep the same recognizable site-builder structure and original imagery.
- Add a strong quote CTA in the first screen without changing the overall header shape.
- Move trust signals closer to the top.
- Remove duplicate navigation and obvious template rough edges.
- Shorten the service copy so homeowners can quickly identify their project type.
- Make the Facebook project link look intentional.
- Add a simple quote process section before the contact form.
- Keep phone, email, and location visible and clickable.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a title tag like `Intellipaint LLC | Residential Painting in Middle Tennessee` instead of a URL-heavy title.
- Use one H1 for the business name, then use service/location wording in the hero sentence and section headings.
- Add Murfreesboro and Middle Tennessee naturally in copy where accurate.
- Keep `tel:` and `mailto:` links crawlable and visible.
- Use descriptive headings for residential interior painting, residential exterior painting, cabinet refacing, staining, and drywall work.
- Fix spelling in `Middle Tenneesee` to `Middle Tennessee`.
- Replace default Wix social destinations with owned social profile links.
