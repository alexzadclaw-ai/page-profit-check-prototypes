# Devereux's Auto Detailing LLC - Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take

The site already has the right raw material: real vehicle photos, a recognizable logo, clear price ranges, and a simple mobile-detailing message. The main conversion problem is not credibility, it is scanability. Visitors have to work too hard to understand packages, compare prices, and choose whether to call, text, or email.

Best direction: keep the Wix/mobile-detailing look, preserve the dark palette and real photos, then make the hero CTA, pricing cards, service bullets, and contact path much easier to act on.

## What is already working

- Real business assets are present: logo, vehicle/detailing photos, social links, phone, and email.
- The hero image immediately communicates auto detailing.
- The site states that this is a mobile detailing service and says visitors can call or text.
- Pricing exists on the page, which removes a lot of buyer uncertainty.
- The black/dark photo-heavy style feels appropriate for auto detailing and should not be replaced with a generic SaaS/agency template.

## Biggest conversion issues

- The hero CTA is just a small `Contact` text link, so the next step is easy to miss.
- The phone number appears in the header, but it does not read like an urgent call/text booking action.
- Price ranges are buried as a small bullet list over a background photo instead of being easy-to-compare cards.
- The service section shows useful detail, but it behaves like a carousel and only one package is obvious at first glance.
- Contact options are split between form, email, phone, and chat without a simple recommended path.
- Wix/template branding and a generic site-builder feel reduce the professional impression.
- The page needs stronger local/mobile cues, such as Maryland mobile service area and quote expectations.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements

- Replace the tiny hero link with clear CTAs: Call, Text, and Email for quote.
- Add a short hero subhead: mobile auto detailing serving Maryland, call or text to schedule.
- Turn the pricing list into six package cards with the exact live price ranges.
- Add small “best for” cues under each package so visitors can self-select faster.
- Keep the original pricing background image, but add contrast and cards for readability.
- Move the full interior bullet list into a cleaner service detail panel using the original interior photo.
- Add a contact strip that repeats phone, text, and email before the form.
- Keep the logo, social icons, footer phone number, serif headings, dark header, and real photos so it still feels like the same business.

## SEO basics to tighten

- Use a more descriptive title, for example: `Mobile Auto Detailing in Maryland | Devereux's Auto Detailing LLC`.
- Add a meta description focused on mobile interior and exterior detailing, package pricing, and call/text booking.
- Use one H1 with business name plus service/location context.
- Rename vague sections like `Professional Services` to include auto detailing service terms.
- Add visible service area copy and internal anchors for packages/services/contact.
- Add alt text for photos that describes the service, not just file names.
- Consider local business schema if rebuilding or upgrading the Wix page.

## Realistic implementation angle

This does not need a complete brand reinvention. The realistic implementation is a focused one-page cleanup inside Wix or a simple static rebuild that mirrors the current page structure:

1. Keep the existing header, logo, dark palette, social links, and real photos.
2. Rework the hero copy and booking buttons.
3. Replace the pricing bullet list with cards.
4. Simplify the services carousel into visible service/package detail.
5. Tighten the contact section so call/text/email are obvious.
6. Remove or reduce template leftovers where possible.

## Offer fit

This is a good Page Profit Check candidate because the site is already live and credible, but the highest-impact fixes are very concrete. A $99 audit/diagnosis can show the business owner exactly where the page is leaking bookings and provide a visual before/after direction. Implementation should be scoped separately because the $99 offer should not imply that site editing or Wix rebuild work is included.
