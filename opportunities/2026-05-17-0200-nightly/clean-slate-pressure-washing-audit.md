# Clean Slate Pressure Washing Page Profit Check audit

## Quick take

Clean Slate already has the key ingredients for a stronger local-service page: a memorable black/yellow brand, real work photos, service cards, starting prices, an owner/operator story, and clear phone/email details. The biggest opportunity is not a full rebrand. It is a tighter first screen that immediately says who they serve, what they clean, why to trust them, and how to request a free quote.

Recommended angle: keep the Clean Slate language and Wix-built feel, but make the homepage act like a North Georgia exterior cleaning quote page.

## What is already working

- Brand name is clear and memorable: Clean Slate Pressure Washing.
- The black, white, and yellow palette is distinctive and appropriate for exterior cleaning.
- The homepage uses real business photos, not generic stock imagery.
- Services are already specific: house wash, surface cleaning, roof wash, rust/oil stain removal, and gutter cleaning.
- Contact details are present: Oakwood, Georgia, email, phone, and Facebook.
- The owner intro with Micheal Mitchell adds trust and makes the business feel personal.
- The page already has a quote/service structure that can be improved instead of replaced.

## Biggest conversion issues

- The first screen leads with a broad intro instead of a clear local service promise for Oakwood and North Georgia.
- The phone number is not prominent enough in the header or hero, even though phone/text is likely the fastest quote path.
- There are too many competing navigation items: Book Online, Contact, Recent Work, Get a Quote, Meet the Team, Terms of Use, plus Facebook and Get in Touch.
- The hero CTA says “Get in Touch” and routes to Facebook, which adds friction for homeowners who just want a quote.
- Recent work proof appears too low and does not support the quote decision early enough.
- The visible Wix banner and “Proudly created with Wix.com” reduce credibility for a local service provider.
- The service cards show starting prices but do not explain that many projects are quote-based by surface and condition.
- The contact form is buried far down the page.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements

- Rewrite the hero around “Oakwood, GA / North Georgia exterior cleaning” and “free quote.”
- Make one primary contact path obvious: call or text 770-714-9252.
- Keep the Clean Slate tagline, “Let’s start the year off with a Clean Slate,” but support it with service specifics.
- Move proof points into the first screen: local, owner-operated, free estimates, recent work.
- Turn the service cards into quote starters instead of separate booking decisions.
- Bring recent work photos higher and caption them by service type.
- Keep the owner/operator section, but place it before the final quote/contact area.
- Clean up footer/header credibility by removing unnecessary Wix/free-site cues when possible.

## SEO basics to tighten

- Homepage title should include the service and location, for example: “Pressure Washing & Soft Washing in Oakwood, GA | Clean Slate.”
- H1 should include “North Georgia exterior cleaning” or “Pressure washing in Oakwood, GA.”
- Add short service blurbs for house washing, roof washing, driveway/surface cleaning, gutter cleaning, and rust/oil stain removal.
- Add alt text to real work photos that mentions the service shown.
- Add local service area language for Oakwood, Gainesville, Flowery Branch, Buford, and nearby North Georgia communities if accurate.
- Add LocalBusiness schema if the site stays on Wix and allows custom code.
- Verify the spelling of “Micheal Mitchell” before making broad SEO updates, since the current site uses that spelling.

## Realistic implementation angle

This should be a focused Wix homepage/quote-path refresh, not a custom rebuild. Keep the existing brand, assets, fonts, colors, page sections, and service menu. The implementation should tighten copy, reorder the homepage, standardize CTAs, crop the existing photos more consistently, and make the phone/text quote path visible above the fold.

The current Wix site can likely support this without moving platforms. A separate project could later add local SEO service pages, reviews, before/after galleries, and a cleaner domain setup.

## Offer fit

Good fit for a $99 Page Profit Check as audit and diagnosis only. The deliverable can show the current conversion gaps, identify the top fixes, and provide a visual before/after direction.

Do not position the $99 offer as implementation. Actual Wix editing, copy updates, photo cleanup, contact form setup, and SEO work should be a separate implementation project. A realistic focused implementation ROM is roughly $450 to $900 depending on how much Wix page editing, mobile cleanup, and service-area content is included.
