# Loper Painting LLC Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take

Loper Painting already has a premium black-and-gold visual direction, real project photos, a clear Overland Park location, and useful service proof. The main conversion issue is that the live Wix page is very long, contact actions are scattered, some service labels read like placeholders, and the strongest project photos/testimonials are buried below the first impression.

## What is already working

- The business name is visible as Loper Painting LLC.
- The site has a distinctive dark, upscale painting-company feel with gold accents and large photography.
- Contact details are present: loperpainting@gmail.com and 913-927-2335.
- The live page includes service content for painting consultation, scheduling, customer service, gallery, exterior painting, projects, testimonials, and contact.
- Real Wix-hosted project photos are available and reusable, including kitchen, cabinet, exterior, and Kearney project images.
- Testimonials from Kelly Hudgins and Angie Hurt are present and can be cleaned up without inventing reviews.
- The owner story mentions 22+ years of industry experience and premium materials.

## Biggest conversion issues

- The Wix banner and Wix footer make the site feel unfinished.
- The first screen has a strong brand impression, but it does not make the estimate path obvious enough.
- Navigation and contact actions are present, but phone/email are not consistently framed as the primary next step.
- Service labels such as “Scheduled” and “Customer Service Call” do not tell a homeowner which painting service to choose.
- Several project photos are small, blurry, or narrow-cropped, even though the source assets include stronger images.
- Testimonials repeat and include inconsistent punctuation, plus one generic “Button” element.
- The form area appears very low on a 12,000+ pixel page, which makes visitors work too hard before acting.
- Social links point to Wix/default social pages rather than confirmed Loper Painting profiles.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements

- Add a clear hero CTA for “Get a Painting Estimate” plus a visible phone link.
- Keep the dark premium palette, gold accents, serif display type, and photo-led style so it still feels like Loper Painting.
- Rewrite the first-screen copy around Overland Park painting, interior/exterior work, cabinet finishes, and easy estimates.
- Replace generic service labels with homeowner-friendly service cards: Interior Painting, Cabinet Finishes, Exterior Painting.
- Use fewer, larger project photos from the live Wix assets so the craftsmanship is visible.
- Clean up testimonials into readable trust cards and remove the generic “Button” artifact.
- Move contact instructions higher and make phone/email links easy to act on.
- Ask visitors to include address and project photos to make estimate conversations smoother.

## SEO basics to tighten

- Add a focused meta description for Overland Park painting services.
- Use one descriptive H1 such as “Interior and Exterior Painting in Overland Park.”
- Add readable section headings for interior painting, cabinet painting, exterior painting, gallery, reviews, and estimate request.
- Give source images descriptive alt text instead of file-name-only labels.
- Add local service-area text if Loper serves nearby Kansas City/Johnson County areas.
- Replace or remove default Wix social links so search engines and visitors are not sent to Wix pages.
- Consider local business schema after address/service-area details are confirmed.

## Realistic implementation angle

This is a surgical Wix cleanup, not a full rebuild. The right implementation path is to keep the existing brand mood, dark background, gold accent, serif headlines, hero/photo rhythm, gallery, testimonials, and contact form concept. The work is mostly content hierarchy, clearer CTAs, service card rewrites, better image cropping, testimonial cleanup, link fixes, and contact-flow tightening.

A practical implementation ROM after diagnosis: 5 to 9 hours in Wix if account access is available and the existing project images can be reused. If new reviews, social links, or detailed service-area copy need to be gathered, add time for content collection.

## Offer fit

Strong fit for a $99 Page Profit Check because the business already has good assets and credibility, but visitors need a faster path from first impression to estimate request. The audit can diagnose the highest-impact fixes and show a visual direction before any implementation is scoped separately.
