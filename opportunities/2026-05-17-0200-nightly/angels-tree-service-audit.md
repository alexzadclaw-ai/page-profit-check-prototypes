# Angel's Tree Service - Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Angel's Tree Service is a strong fit for a focused Page Profit Check because the current site already has useful sales assets: a direct phone number, email address, state contractor license language, a long operating-history claim, real work photos, a service list, opening hours, a business-card graphic, and a local Orange County address.

The opportunity is not a full rebrand. It is a brand-faithful Wix homepage cleanup that keeps the sparse white layout, thin all-caps type, square work-photo carousel, pale gray service section, pale blue hours block, business-card image, and repeated contact footer, while making the first screen and service/contact path much easier to act on.

## What is already working
- The phone number is visible and repeated: `714-279-0669`.
- The page has a direct email link: `angelstreeservicec27@gmail.com`.
- The hero includes an important trust cue: `Contractor State License #C27-853986`.
- The copy says Angel's Tree Service has served Orange County and surrounding areas since 1980.
- The business positions itself for residential, commercial, and management/property clients.
- The page includes concrete services: pruning and thinning, crown reduction, palm trimming/skinning, tree removal, stump grinding, and landscape clean-up.
- The live site uses real work photos instead of generic stock imagery.
- The business-card graphic adds recognizable local-business texture and shows more service language such as tree trimming, pruning, removal, stump grinding, cutting, and topping.
- The hours and address are easy to find once visitors scroll.
- The older Wix look has recognizable visual DNA: white space, charcoal text, thin dividers, light gray/pale blue content blocks, large all-caps headings, and a simple footer.

## Biggest conversion issues
1. **Critical: the first screen mentions free estimates but does not make the next step obvious.** Phone and email are present, but there is no button-style call or quote path in the hero.

2. **Critical: trust signals are real but underused.** License number, insured/company language, since-1980 history, and residential/commercial/property work are scattered instead of packaged near the top.

3. **High: the service list is too plain.** Asterisks in a paragraph make the service coverage easy to miss, especially for mobile visitors scanning for tree removal, palms, stump grinding, or clean-up.

4. **High: the photo carousel communicates real capability, but it does not support conversion.** The photos are valuable, yet the section has no captions, no context, and no nearby call/email action.

5. **High: the contact path repeats information but does not clarify what to send.** A tree-service visitor often has photos and a location; the page should tell them to call or email photos/details for a free estimate.

6. **Medium: visible Wix/template residue lowers perceived polish.** The Wix promotional strip and Wix footer credit make the business feel less professional than the actual service offering.

7. **Medium: SEO basics are thin.** The live page title is just `Home`, the meta description is empty, the heading structure is sparse, and image alt text/service keywords can be more descriptive.

8. **Medium: contact details have a small source inconsistency.** The live page uses `angelstreeservicec27@gmail.com`, while the older business-card image appears to show `angelstreeservice27@gmail.com`. The live link should be treated as the source of truth unless the owner confirms otherwise.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Preserve the recognizable Wix-style structure while removing the top Wix ad and bottom Wix credit.
- Keep the exact phone, email, license language, since-1980 positioning, work photos, business-card image, and pale gray/pale blue section rhythm.
- Add a clear hero CTA pair: **Call for a Free Estimate** and **Email Photos for a Quote**.
- Package trust cues near the top: since 1980, licensed/insured, residential/commercial/property clients.
- Convert the service paragraph into scannable service cards using only services supported by the live page/business card.
- Add light captions to the work photos so the gallery helps establish capability instead of acting only as decoration.
- Make the opening-hours and contact sections more action-oriented with clickable phone, email, and map links.
- Add a mobile sticky call button so phone-based tree-service leads do not have to hunt for the number.
- Add basic SEO metadata, one clear H1, H2 section headings, and descriptive image alt text.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a focused title such as `Angel's Tree Service | Orange County Tree Service`.
- Add a meta description mentioning Orange County, licensed and insured tree service, trimming, pruning, palm trimming, tree removal, stump grinding, and free estimates.
- Use one clear H1: `Angel's Tree Service`.
- Use H2s for Services, Work Photos, Opening Hours, and Contact.
- Add descriptive alt text to the real work photos and business-card image.
- Use the confirmed live phone/email consistently across HTML links and visible copy.
- Add LocalBusiness/HomeAndConstructionBusiness schema later after confirming exact business name, service area, address, and preferred email.

## Realistic implementation angle
This should be positioned as a focused homepage cleanup, not a new brand or complex rebuild. The current page already has the key ingredients: local trust, licensed language, service list, real work photos, phone, email, address, and hours. A practical implementation sprint would reorganize the existing Wix page, improve above-the-fold CTAs, polish mobile scanning, clean visible builder residue where Wix allows it, and update SEO fields.

The biggest owner-facing value is clarity: visitors should instantly understand that Angel's Tree Service is licensed, experienced, local to Orange County, and ready to quote tree trimming/removal/stump/palm work by phone or email.
