# Harris Pest Management Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take

Harris Pest Management already has the bones of a conversion page: real phone/email links, service categories, a contact form, a truck photo that shows the brand, and reviews on a separate page. The biggest opportunity is not a full redesign. It is a focused Wix cleanup that keeps the direct pest-management look while making the next step obvious: call now or request a free inspection/inspection-based quote for a specific pest problem.

## What is already working

- The site clearly belongs to Harris Pest Management and uses a recognizable truck decal/photo, business name, and tagline: "Our Word Is Our Bond."
- Phone, email, Facebook, and contact form options are already present.
- The live site lists key services: mosquitoes, termites, rodent control, bed bugs, flea and tick control, roaches, and general pest control.
- The truck image advertises free inspections, residential/commercial service, and the main phone number.
- The Reviews page has useful proof from Marvin Carter, Rolly Khan, and Michael D.
- The site already mentions the San Antonio area and says the company has serviced the area since 2018.
- The gray and bright-pink Wix look is distinctive and memorable, even though it needs better hierarchy and contrast.

## Biggest conversion issues

- The first screen spends too much space on a general welcome message instead of saying what pest problems Harris handles and why someone should call today.
- The strongest offer, free inspection/inspection-based quote, appears on the truck photo and Services page but is not turned into the main CTA path.
- Service cards repeat "Pricing varies" without explaining that an inspection helps define the treatment plan and quote.
- Reviews are isolated on a separate page instead of supporting the quote/contact section on the home page.
- The contact form is useful but generic. It does not ask visitors to choose the pest issue or make phone/email the fastest options.
- The header is cluttered by a Wix ad, duplicated contact info, social icon, and a Log In button that does not help a local pest-control lead.
- Footer copy still says "Proudly created with Wix.com," which weakens professionalism.
- Copy has grammar issues and some lower-polish phrasing, especially in the About section.
- Location and service-area messaging are not strong enough above the fold.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements

- Keep the existing Harris identity, truck image, gray/pink palette, and Wix block structure, but make the hero headline more direct: pest control and free inspections for the San Antonio area.
- Replace generic "Get in Touch" emphasis with stronger actions: "Call (210) 318-1246" and "Request Free Inspection."
- Organize the pest categories into a scan-friendly grid with short inspection/quote language for each service.
- Add a simple quote path: call/email or form, identify pest and address, inspection, treatment recommendation.
- Bring three short review snippets onto the home page near the CTA.
- Remove/ignore low-value template leftovers in the conversion path: Log In, Wix promo language, repeated pricing placeholders.
- Tighten the contact form labels around pest issue, address, phone/email, and message.
- Keep both phone numbers visible, but make the primary number visually dominant.

## SEO basics to tighten

- Change the page title to include local service intent, for example: "San Antonio Pest Control | Harris Pest Management."
- Use one clear H1 that includes pest control and San Antonio rather than only "Welcome."
- Add short service/location copy for mosquito control, termite service, rodent control, bed bug treatment, roaches, flea/tick, and inspections.
- Add descriptive alt text to original truck, mosquito, bed bug, and pest-control images.
- Make name, address/service area, phone, and email consistent across header, contact, footer, and metadata.
- Add LocalBusiness or PestControl schema later if Wix allows custom structured data.
- Create or improve individual service sections/pages for high-intent terms like termite inspection, mosquito control, rodent control, and bed bug treatment in San Antonio.

## Realistic implementation angle

This should be a surgical Wix refresh, not a new agency-style rebuild:

1. Keep the existing logo/photo, truck hero, gray/pink brand direction, nav labels, footer contact pattern, and Wix-style stacked sections.
2. Rework the home page top section so it names the service, location, free inspection angle, and phone CTA immediately.
3. Rewrite the services area into clearer pest cards with inspection-based quote language.
4. Pull the best review snippets from the Reviews page into the home page.
5. Tighten the contact form and remove template leftovers that distract from calls and quote requests.
6. Clean up grammar, page title, headings, image alt text, and mobile spacing.

## Offer fit

Good fit for a $99 Page Profit Check because the site has real content and real assets, but the conversion path is blurry. The $99 should stay positioned as audit/diagnosis only: review the page, identify the highest-impact fixes, and show a visual before/after direction. Implementation would be a separate Wix cleanup project after Harris approves the direction.
