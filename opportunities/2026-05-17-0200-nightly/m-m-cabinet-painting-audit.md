# M + M Cabinet Painting LLC Page Profit Check audit

## Quick take

M + M Cabinet Painting has a real specialty, real project photos, and useful process detail already on the page. The biggest opportunity is not to make it look like a different company. It is to keep the focused cabinet-painting feel, remove the Wix/template friction, and make the first screen answer three questions faster: what they do, why the finish is better, and how to request a quote or call.

## What is already working

- Clear business focus: cabinet painting and kitchen cabinet refinishing, not generic interior painting.
- Real photos are the strongest trust asset. The site has a large hero kitchen photo, in-process/project shots, testimonial photos, and a substantial gallery.
- Contact details are visible in the header: mmqpainting@gmail.com and 980-292-3250.
- The site mentions differentiators that should be brought higher: high-end sprayers, authentic Italian wood coatings, offsite shop work, and a typical one-week process.
- Testimonials are specific and include one Charlotte, NC reference.
- The blue-on-white palette and simple Wix spacing are recognizable and can be improved without a full rebrand.

## Biggest conversion issues

- The free Wix banner is the first thing visitors see and weakens credibility immediately.
- The hero headline says the company name, but not the value proposition. It does not quickly say “cabinet painting in Charlotte” or “sprayed Italian coating finish.”
- The first clear action is a generic form section below the hero. There is no strong top CTA cluster with phone, email, and quote options.
- The page has multiple competing forms: Request a Quote, Contact Us, and Rate Our Service. New prospects should see one primary quote path.
- The strong finish/process information is buried below the first form and photo instead of being used as above-the-fold trust proof.
- Testimonials are useful but hard to scan and appear after a very large spacing stretch.
- The gallery is valuable but is presented as a large image dump with no project labels, finish cues, or before/after context.
- Mobile users likely have to scroll too far before the main value and contact options are clear.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements

- Rebuild the hero around a focused promise: “Cabinet painting and refinishing for Charlotte kitchens,” supported by Italian wood coatings and sprayed shop finish cues.
- Add visible CTAs near the top: Request a Cabinet Quote, Call 980-292-3250, and Email mmqpainting@gmail.com.
- Keep the original hero photo and logo, but tighten spacing and pair the photo with the quote form/trust bullets.
- Move the best differentiators into a short proof strip: Cabinet-only specialty, Italian wood coatings, high-end sprayers, typical one-week process.
- Replace the duplicate contact/rating sections with one clean quote form and a simple “what happens next” note.
- Turn the process copy into an easy Day 1 through Day 5 timeline.
- Reformat testimonials into cards with larger quote text and names.
- Add captions to gallery images so the work communicates finish quality instead of just volume.

## SEO basics to tighten

- Update the title tag from a generic home title to something like “Cabinet Painting Charlotte NC | M + M Cabinet Painting LLC.”
- Use one clear H1 that includes the service and location.
- Add concise section headings for “Kitchen Cabinet Refinishing,” “Spray Finish Process,” “Italian Wood Coatings,” and “Charlotte Cabinet Painting Quotes.”
- Add descriptive image alt text instead of raw file names or blank gallery alts.
- Add service-area copy for Charlotte and nearby areas if accurate.
- Add a meta description that mentions cabinet painting, refinishing, sprayed finishes, Italian wood coatings, and quote requests.
- Link phone and email consistently with tel: and mailto: links.

## Realistic implementation angle

This can be implemented as a surgical Wix cleanup rather than a full rebuild. Keep the current logo, photos, palette, and basic single-page structure. Then remove the Wix ad by upgrading/connecting a domain, tighten the header, rebuild the hero, consolidate the forms, add process/testimonial cards, and caption the gallery. If Wix editing limitations are too constraining, a simple static one-page rebuild could match the existing look while giving cleaner performance and layout control.
