## Quick take

E & J already has the most important raw ingredients: a local family-owned message, real before/after work photos, a clear San Diego service area, phone, email, hours, and a Google Reviews link. The page feels authentic, but it is hard to convert because the services are scattered, the primary quote action is not prominent, and the Wix template structure creates a lot of extra vertical space.

The best improvement is not a total rebrand. It is a tighter version of the same page: keep the black/yellow/blue look, keep the real job photos, keep the direct family-owned voice, and make the call/text/email quote path obvious from the top and repeated at the key decision points.

## What is already working

- The business name is visible at the top of the page.
- Phone number and email are present, and the email is already a mailto link.
- The page clearly says the company is local and family owned, serving San Diego since 2020.
- The services are present in copy: residential and commercial window cleaning, gutter cleaning, power washing, soft washing, and solar panel cleaning.
- Real job photos make the site feel credible and local instead of generic.
- The black background, yellow headings, and blue intro block create a recognizable DIY Wix service-business personality.
- A Google Reviews link is available near the final call-to-action area.

## Biggest conversion issues

- The Wix promo banner at the top immediately makes the site feel less professional.
- There is no strong above-the-fold primary CTA. Visitors see contact info, but not a styled “Call or Text for a Quote” action.
- The phone number is mostly plain text instead of consistently clickable.
- The service list is not scannable. Only “Clean Rain Gutter” gets a dedicated service card, while window cleaning, solar panel cleaning, power washing, and soft washing are buried in paragraphs.
- The contact form appears after a long scroll and does not clearly explain what information to send for a quote.
- Large fixed sections create a lot of empty space, especially on desktop.
- The floating chat/control overlaps content around the contact area.
- The image layout feels uneven, with before/after photos cropped differently and no consistent captions.
- The footer is minimal and misses a final email, service area, and quote CTA.

## SEO basics to tighten

- Change the page title from “Home | E & J Window and solar panel cleaning” to something like “Window Cleaning & Solar Panel Cleaning in San Diego | E & J.”
- Add a real meta description focused on San Diego window cleaning, solar panel cleaning, gutter cleaning, power washing, and soft washing.
- Remove template/meta leftovers. The inspected page includes keyword metadata like “BUSINESS, Locksmith, NAME., Reliable, Service.”
- Fix the Open Graph site name typo: “E & J Window and sola panel cleaning.”
- Use one strong H1 with the main services and location.
- Add H2s for Services, Before & After, Reviews, and Contact.
- Add descriptive image alt text instead of file names like “Polish_20240626_205447755.jpg” or “Untitled.”
- If the business has a Google Business Profile, link to the actual review/profile URL with normal anchor text.

## Realistic implementation angle

This can likely be improved inside Wix without a rebuild. The quickest path is to keep the current single-page structure, replace the oversized sections with tighter strips, update text blocks, add anchor navigation, style phone/email as buttons, and build a small services grid using the existing Wix editor.

A practical implementation ROM is 4 to 7 hours for a focused Wix cleanup if the account/assets are available. A standalone static rebuild with hosting and QA would be more like 6 to 10 hours, depending on whether the form needs real submission handling.
