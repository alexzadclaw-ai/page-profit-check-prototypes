## Quick take
FYR already has a distinctive visual direction: muted olive/tan, rust orange, black-green hero bands, compressed poster-like typography, and a memorable “what we do” service collage. The main issue is not lack of personality; it is that the first visit is interrupted by a modal, the CTA path competes with several sections, and the footer/contact area shows template leftovers that can damage trust.

## What is already working
- Strong niche promise for busy small-business owners who need strategy and breathing room.
- Recognizable brand palette and graphic service tiles.
- Testimonials and client examples are present.
- Booking and service links exist, so conversion infrastructure is already started.

## Biggest conversion issues
1. **Critical — intrusive first-screen popup:** the first screenshot is blocked by a consultation quiz modal, hiding the hero and CTA.
2. **Critical — trust damage from template leftovers:** footer/contact items include placeholder email/phone patterns rather than finished business details.
3. **High — too many headline fragments:** multiple large H1-style statements compete before the visitor understands the offer menu.
4. **High — service path is graphic-first, not decision-first:** the tiles have personality, but buyers need quick language about which service fits their situation.
5. **Medium — CTA specificity:** “schedule consultation” is useful, but the page can make the next step feel safer by naming a free strategy call and what happens after clicking.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Remove the entry-blocking modal and move the quiz idea into a calmer “Not sure where to start?” panel.
- Keep the FYR visual fingerprint while turning the first screen into a clearer coaching/strategy pitch.
- Bring client proof and service fit guidance closer to the top.
- Replace placeholder/template contact details with clean web/contact/social paths.
- Add a simple “how the consultation works” section so the next step feels low-friction.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use one primary H1 for the homepage; convert supporting slogans into H2/H3 sections.
- Add clearer service language for small business coaching, fractional CMO, growth planning, events, nonprofit fundraising, and website support.
- Ensure footer NAP/contact fields are complete before sending traffic.
- Add descriptive alt text to the service collage and client screenshots.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a surgical GoHighLevel/page-builder cleanup: suppress or delay the popup, reorder the top sections, add decision-focused copy around existing service artwork, and replace placeholder footer content. It should be feasible without rebuilding the whole site.

## Offer fit
Strong fit for the $99 Page Profit Check because the page has clear, visible fixes that are easy to explain in screenshots: popup friction, placeholder trust issues, CTA clarity, and service organization. A follow-up cleanup sprint could be positioned as a light page-builder implementation pass if FYR likes the prototype direction.
