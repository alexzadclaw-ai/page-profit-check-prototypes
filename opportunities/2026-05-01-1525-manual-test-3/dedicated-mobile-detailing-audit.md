## Quick take
Dedicated Mobile Detailing already has one of the stronger visual foundations in this test: black/yellow contrast, a clear Sacramento/Placer County service area, strong car photography, a phone CTA, and an above-fold quote form. The opportunity is to make the first screen less crowded, tighten the form promise, and convert the long service grid into a faster decision path.

## What is already working
- Clear local positioning for Sacramento and Placer County.
- Strong black/yellow brand system and premium automotive photography.
- Phone number and quote form are visible above the fold.
- Service range is broad and commercially useful: mobile detailing, interior/exterior, ceramic coating, paint correction, polishing, waxing, headlights, and steam cleaning.
- Footer includes real phone and email contact details.

## Biggest conversion issues
1. **High — hero has many competing actions:** Locations, phone, form, service bullets, nav dropdown, and call button all compete in one view.
2. **High — quote form lacks expectation-setting:** visitors are asked for appointment info before they know what they receive or how fast they will hear back.
3. **High — services become a long image grid:** the current cards are attractive, but buyers need quick groupings such as “maintenance,” “deep clean,” and “paint protection.”
4. **Medium — proof is lower than the first decision point:** reviews/satisfaction messaging exist, but could be moved closer to the top.
5. **Medium — mobile scanning risk:** the full grid and form can feel heavy on small screens without stronger hierarchy.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Keep the black/yellow/car-photo brand fingerprint but simplify the hero into two clear actions: call now or request a quote.
- Reframe the form as a free quote with a short response promise and service checklist.
- Add a top trust strip for mobile service, Sacramento area, phone, and proof.
- Group services into buyer-friendly packages while keeping the photo-card rhythm.
- Add a “how mobile detailing works” block before the visitor hits the full gallery.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Keep one primary H1 that includes “mobile detailing in Sacramento & Placer County.”
- Add internal anchors and headings for service categories people search for: ceramic coating, paint correction, interior detailing, exterior detailing.
- Add descriptive alt text to the car/detailing images.
- Consider a concise title/description for each service page and consistent service-area mentions.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a WordPress/Elementor-style cleanup: hero hierarchy, form microcopy, top trust strip, and service-card grouping can be improved without changing the brand or service pages. The prototype intentionally preserves the high-contrast visual style.

## Offer fit
Very strong $99 Page Profit Check fit because the site already has traffic/conversion components; the audit can show how a small hierarchy pass could help more visitors choose between calling, quoting, or selecting a service. A follow-up cleanup sprint could be a moderate homepage pass if they want implementation help.
