## Quick take

Body Wise already has real strengths on the page: a long-running business, clear training categories, a visible phone number, a free-consultation angle, and strong local service signals for Charlotte / Pineville. The opportunity is not inventing credibility — it is **organizing and presenting it better** so more visitors understand the offer fast and take the next step.

## What is already working

1. **Longevity and experience are visible** — the page says the business has 25 years of experience.
2. **Core services are broad enough to appeal to multiple buyer types** — personal training, sport-specific training, wellness, competition prep, VO2 max testing, and metabolic rate testing.
3. **Phone contact is easy to find** — (704) 614-1511 appears prominently.
4. **Free consultation language exists** — there is already a conversion path to build around.
5. **Local relevance is strong** — Charlotte, Pineville, and South Park are mentioned directly.

## Biggest conversion issues

### 1) The homepage is noisy before it is clear
Navigation repeats, multiple sections compete for attention, and the page asks visitors to work too hard before they understand the best next step.

### 2) CTA hierarchy is too generic
“Get Started Today” is usable, but it does not explain *what* starts. A stronger CTA like **Book Your Free Consultation** or **Call for a Free Fitness Consultation** would better match intent.

### 3) Long text blocks reduce scannability
The page has useful substance, but it is delivered in dense paragraphs. Mobile visitors will likely skim past details that should build trust.

### 4) The service story is too flat
Everything is listed, but not prioritized. First-time visitors likely need a cleaner path such as:
- weight loss / body toning
- personal training
- performance / sport-specific work
- testing / data-driven support

### 5) Template residue hurts trust
Observed issues include:
- duplicate navigation patterns
- form labels like **“New Field”**
- irrelevant footer links such as roofing/siding items
- awkward copy artifacts like “surrounding communitiesin” and spacing issues

These are high-value cleanup items because they quietly undermine an otherwise credible service business.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements

### 1) Rebuild the hero around one clear promise
A tighter above-the-fold section should answer:
- who this is for
- what outcomes Body Wise helps with
- how to start

**Suggested direction:**
> Personal training, weight loss coaching, and performance support in Charlotte for adults who want a plan that fits their body, goals, and schedule.

Follow that with one primary CTA and one secondary CTA.

### 2) Add a trust strip directly under the hero
Bring proof closer to the first action:
- 25 years of experience
- Charlotte / Pineville service area
- Free consultation
- One-on-one customized planning

### 3) Turn the services into clearer buyer paths
Instead of simply listing categories, frame them around visitor intent:
- **Lose weight & tone up**
- **Build strength & confidence**
- **Train for sport or competition**
- **Use testing to guide your plan**

### 4) Explain what happens in the consultation
A small 3-step block can reduce hesitation:
1. Talk about goals, injuries, and schedule
2. Get matched to the right training path
3. Start with a plan built around your needs

### 5) Trim and restructure the copy for mobile
Keep the same ideas, but present them as short sections, bullets, and cards instead of long generic paragraphs.

### 6) Clean the footer and form details
This looks like an easy trust win:
- remove irrelevant template links
- rename unfinished form labels
- tighten formatting/spacing
- make the contact section feel intentionally fitness-specific

## SEO basics to tighten
