# Men In Black Pest Control — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Men In Black Pest Control is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current site already has recognizable local-service proof, brand material, and a reachable contact path — but the first-screen decision flow can be made more direct without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current site family and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Request an Estimate**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #111827, #2f80ed, #f4f6f9, #060708.
- **Typography feel:** Practical local-service sans-serif body copy with stronger display headings for hierarchy.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Contact-forward local service navigation with fewer competing choices.
- **Hero/image treatment:** Same service-business positioning, but with the main action visible immediately.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:**
  - black “suited solution” identity
  - 4.9 rating proof
  - safe/discreet/professional value language
  - Naperville and Chicagoland service area
  - clear process structure

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with clear service intent in Naperville / Chicagoland, IL.
- The homepage already communicates a real local service category: residential and commercial pest control and exclusion services.
- The site includes direct phone, contact, quote, estimate, appointment, or booking paths.
- The brand has proof points worth preserving: 4.9/5.0 rating, Family-owned since 2012, Discreet vehicles on request.
- The service range is broad enough to support multiple buyer-intent paths.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** Repeated “Call Now” and “Request an Estimate” sections make the page feel longer than the actual decision path.
   - Prototype response: Keep the memorable black/blue brand but tighten the first-screen estimate flow.

2. **High:** The 4.9 rating, safe/discreet proof, and family-owned story should be grouped closer to the primary CTA.
   - Prototype response: Group rating, safety, discretion, and custom-plan proof near the hero.

3. **High:** Residential, commercial, exclusion, and insulation services need clearer buyer paths before the long pest list.
   - Prototype response: Separate the main buyer paths: residential, commercial, exclusion, insulation.

4. **High:** The local SEO section is useful for search but should sit behind the conversion path rather than dominating the homepage flow.
   - Prototype response: Move local SEO text below service and process sections.

5. **Medium:** The form can be made easier to understand with a short “what happens next” sequence.
   - Prototype response: Make the form expectations clearer before the visitor submits.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Keep the memorable black/blue brand but tighten the first-screen estimate flow.
2. Group rating, safety, discretion, and custom-plan proof near the hero.
3. Separate the main buyer paths: residential, commercial, exclusion, insulation.
4. Move local SEO text below service and process sections.
5. Make the form expectations clearer before the visitor submits.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
