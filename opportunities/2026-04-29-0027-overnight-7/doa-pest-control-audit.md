# D.O.A. Pest Control — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
D.O.A. Pest Control is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current site already has recognizable local-service proof, brand material, and a reachable contact path — but the first-screen decision flow can be made more direct without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current site family and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Schedule Pest Service**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #202020, #d83a22, #fff5ec, #111111.
- **Typography feel:** Practical local-service sans-serif body copy with stronger display headings for hierarchy.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Contact-forward local service navigation with fewer competing choices.
- **Hero/image treatment:** Same service-business positioning, but with the main action visible immediately.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:**
  - dark pest-control identity
  - Yorkville local specialist positioning
  - 31+ years of experience
  - family-owned proof
  - broad pest category coverage

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with clear service intent in Yorkville, IL.
- The homepage already communicates a real local service category: residential and commercial pest control.
- The site includes direct phone, contact, quote, estimate, appointment, or booking paths.
- The brand has proof points worth preserving: 31+ years of experience, 100% satisfaction guarantee, Family-owned local company.
- The service range is broad enough to support multiple buyer-intent paths.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** Duplicated residential/commercial and pest sections make the page longer without helping urgent visitors choose a next step.
   - Prototype response: Make “Schedule Pest Service” the primary action.

2. **High:** Important proof — 31+ years, local ownership, 100% satisfaction guarantee, and same-day service — should appear together earlier.
   - Prototype response: Move years of experience, guarantee, and family-owned proof into a top trust strip.

3. **High:** Some “View More” and repeated pest links feel less polished than the quality claims.
   - Prototype response: Condense duplicated service blocks into clear urgent-pest categories.

4. **High:** The page needs a simple path for high-urgency problems like termites, bed bugs, rodents, and wasps.
   - Prototype response: Add a simple three-step treatment flow.

5. **Medium:** Commercial and residential buyers need distinct but compact choices.
   - Prototype response: Keep the Yorkville/local positioning visible throughout the page.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Make “Schedule Pest Service” the primary action.
2. Move years of experience, guarantee, and family-owned proof into a top trust strip.
3. Condense duplicated service blocks into clear urgent-pest categories.
4. Add a simple three-step treatment flow.
5. Keep the Yorkville/local positioning visible throughout the page.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
