# Koshgarian Rug Cleaners — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Koshgarian Rug Cleaners is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current site already has recognizable local-service proof, brand material, and a reachable contact path — but the first-screen decision flow can be made more direct without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current site family and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Request Service**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #29406d, #c4933e, #fbf6ec, #1f2b3d.
- **Typography feel:** Practical local-service sans-serif body copy with stronger display headings for hierarchy.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Contact-forward local service navigation with fewer competing choices.
- **Hero/image treatment:** Same service-business positioning, but with the main action visible immediately.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:**
  - classic navy/gold family-business tone
  - Koshgarian history since 1906
  - Hinsdale and Naperville location details
  - request-service emphasis
  - rug cleaning/repair specialty

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with clear service intent in Hinsdale and Naperville, IL.
- The homepage already communicates a real local service category: rug, carpet, upholstery, floor, and water-damage cleaning.
- The site includes direct phone, contact, quote, estimate, appointment, or booking paths.
- The brand has proof points worth preserving: Family-owned for over 100 years, Naperville + Hinsdale locations, Pickup and delivery available.
- The service range is broad enough to support multiple buyer-intent paths.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The page repeats the same service-card set, making a strong family brand feel more cluttered than needed.
   - Prototype response: Make “Request Service” the consistent primary action.

2. **High:** The request-service action competes with many location, waiver, team, review, service-area, and nav links.
   - Prototype response: Feature the two locations and pickup/delivery options near the top.

3. **High:** The Naperville location is a major proof point but should connect more clearly to pickup/drop-off and service requests.
   - Prototype response: Condense repeated residential services into four buyer-friendly paths.

4. **High:** The 1906 family legacy is excellent but needs to support immediate service selection earlier.
   - Prototype response: Pair the 100+ year story with practical service guidance.

5. **Medium:** Commercial services and water damage can be included without crowding the main residential path.
   - Prototype response: Add a simple service flow before the contact block.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Make “Request Service” the consistent primary action.
2. Feature the two locations and pickup/delivery options near the top.
3. Condense repeated residential services into four buyer-friendly paths.
4. Pair the 100+ year story with practical service guidance.
5. Add a simple service flow before the contact block.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
