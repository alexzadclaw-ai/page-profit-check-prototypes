# Executive Green Carpet Cleaning Services — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Executive Green Carpet Cleaning Services is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current site already has recognizable local-service proof, brand material, and a reachable contact path — but the first-screen decision flow can be made more direct without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current site family and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Get Your Free Quote**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #1d7354, #7dbb45, #eff8f0, #15362d.
- **Typography feel:** Practical local-service sans-serif body copy with stronger display headings for hierarchy.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Contact-forward local service navigation with fewer competing choices.
- **Hero/image treatment:** Same service-business positioning, but with the main action visible immediately.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:**
  - green-cleaning identity
  - since-1991 proof
  - online scheduling focus
  - broad carpet/upholstery/rug/floor service mix
  - review-led trust story

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with clear service intent in Naperville / Chicagoland suburbs, IL.
- The homepage already communicates a real local service category: carpet, upholstery, rug, tile, hardwood, and commercial cleaning.
- The site includes direct phone, contact, quote, estimate, appointment, or booking paths.
- The brand has proof points worth preserving: Since 1991, Schedule online 24/7, Master Textile Cleaners.
- The service range is broad enough to support multiple buyer-intent paths.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** Malformed phone-link text and repeated menu labels can make an otherwise trusted cleaning brand feel less polished.
   - Prototype response: Use clean phone and quote actions throughout the top section.

2. **High:** Schedule-online and free-quote actions compete with a deep menu instead of anchoring the first screen.
   - Prototype response: Group since-1991, scheduling, and textile expertise into a trust strip.

3. **High:** Master Textile Cleaner and since-1991 proof should appear nearer the CTA.
   - Prototype response: Present the main cleaning services in four scannable paths.

4. **High:** Reviews are strong but need a compact summary before long testimonial blocks.
   - Prototype response: Summarize review proof before linking to more testimonials.

5. **Medium:** Area pages and old blog archives can sit lower than primary cleaning choices.
   - Prototype response: Keep area/SEO content below the service and quote flow.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Use clean phone and quote actions throughout the top section.
2. Group since-1991, scheduling, and textile expertise into a trust strip.
3. Present the main cleaning services in four scannable paths.
4. Summarize review proof before linking to more testimonials.
5. Keep area/SEO content below the service and quote flow.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
