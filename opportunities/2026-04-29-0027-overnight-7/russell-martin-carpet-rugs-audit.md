# Russell Martin Carpet & Rugs — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Russell Martin Carpet & Rugs is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current site already has recognizable local-service proof, brand material, and a reachable contact path — but the first-screen decision flow can be made more direct without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current site family and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Request an Estimate**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #5a3e2b, #c29a5b, #fbf7f0, #2c2118.
- **Typography feel:** Practical local-service sans-serif body copy with stronger display headings for hierarchy.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Contact-forward local service navigation with fewer competing choices.
- **Hero/image treatment:** Same service-business positioning, but with the main action visible immediately.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:**
  - warm carpet/rug showroom feel
  - Naperville address
  - since-1957 local trust
  - free estimate positioning
  - reviews, specials, and financing proof

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with clear service intent in Naperville, IL.
- The homepage already communicates a real local service category: carpet, rugs, installation, cleaning, and flooring services.
- The site includes direct phone, contact, quote, estimate, appointment, or booking paths.
- The brand has proof points worth preserving: Serving since 1957, Free in-home estimates, Financing available.
- The service range is broad enough to support multiple buyer-intent paths.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** Some homepage service links and labels appear mismatched, which can confuse visitors choosing between carpet, rugs, cleaning, and installation.
   - Prototype response: Make “Request an Estimate” the main first-screen action.

2. **High:** Decades of local trust and reviews are strong but should support the estimate path sooner.
   - Prototype response: Align service cards with the correct destination and customer intent.

3. **High:** The service breadth is valuable but needs cleaner categories before the visitor reaches the long story and reviews.
   - Prototype response: Group carpet, rugs, cleaning, and installation into four clear paths.

4. **High:** A typo-level polish issue in a closing CTA can reduce confidence for detail-sensitive flooring shoppers.
   - Prototype response: Move decades-of-trust and review proof near the top.

5. **Medium:** Testimonials are persuasive but can be summarized before linking to the full review page.
   - Prototype response: Use a short planning flow before the final contact step.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Make “Request an Estimate” the main first-screen action.
2. Align service cards with the correct destination and customer intent.
3. Group carpet, rugs, cleaning, and installation into four clear paths.
4. Move decades-of-trust and review proof near the top.
5. Use a short planning flow before the final contact step.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
