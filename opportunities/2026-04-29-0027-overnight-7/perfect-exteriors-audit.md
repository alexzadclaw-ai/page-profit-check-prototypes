# Perfect Exteriors — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Perfect Exteriors is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current site already has recognizable local-service proof, brand material, and a reachable contact path — but the first-screen decision flow can be made more direct without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current site family and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Book Inspection**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #1b2d4a, #e87c25, #f5f8fb, #111a25.
- **Typography feel:** Practical local-service sans-serif body copy with stronger display headings for hierarchy.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Contact-forward local service navigation with fewer competing choices.
- **Hero/image treatment:** Same service-business positioning, but with the main action visible immediately.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:**
  - dark blue/orange exterior-contractor feel
  - Perfect Exteriors name and “Exterior Detectives” positioning
  - family-owned/BBB/licensed proof
  - Book Inspection action
  - broad roofing and siding service coverage

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with clear service intent in Naperville / Chicagoland, IL.
- The homepage already communicates a real local service category: roofing, siding, gutters, windows, doors, skylights, and exterior repair.
- The site includes direct phone, contact, quote, estimate, appointment, or booking paths.
- The brand has proof points worth preserving: Family owned business, BBB accredited, Licensed & insured.
- The service range is broad enough to support multiple buyer-intent paths.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** Spacing and copy-flow issues in the hero and section transitions make the page feel less polished than the trust claims.
   - Prototype response: Make “Book Inspection” the main first-screen action.

2. **High:** Family-owned, BBB, licensed, insured, and clear-pricing proof should sit beside the inspection CTA.
   - Prototype response: Group family-owned, BBB, licensed, insured, and pricing proof at the top.

3. **High:** The long service catalog needs a short first-pass grouping before detailed pages.
   - Prototype response: Condense the service catalog into roofing, siding, gutters, and windows/doors paths.

4. **High:** The “Exterior Detectives” positioning is memorable but needs a more direct connection to booking an inspection.
   - Prototype response: Tie the “Exterior Detectives” positioning to the inspection process.

5. **Medium:** Material-brand proof can support service pages after the homepage path is clear.
   - Prototype response: Use a simple three-step inspection flow before the contact section.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Make “Book Inspection” the main first-screen action.
2. Group family-owned, BBB, licensed, insured, and pricing proof at the top.
3. Condense the service catalog into roofing, siding, gutters, and windows/doors paths.
4. Tie the “Exterior Detectives” positioning to the inspection process.
5. Use a simple three-step inspection flow before the contact section.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
