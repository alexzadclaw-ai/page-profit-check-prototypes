# Stec Roofing S&G, Inc. — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Stec Roofing S&G, Inc. is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current site already has recognizable local-service proof, brand material, and a reachable contact path — but the first-screen decision flow can be made more direct without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current site family and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Request a Free Estimate**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #a71920, #f4b53f, #fff7f4, #272424.
- **Typography feel:** Practical local-service sans-serif body copy with stronger display headings for hierarchy.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Contact-forward local service navigation with fewer competing choices.
- **Hero/image treatment:** Same service-business positioning, but with the main action visible immediately.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:**
  - red-and-dark local roofing identity
  - Naperville address and phone
  - licensed and insured proof
  - Se Habla Español message
  - residential and commercial roofing breadth

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with clear service intent in Naperville, IL.
- The homepage already communicates a real local service category: residential, commercial, industrial, and institutional roofing.
- The site includes direct phone, contact, quote, estimate, appointment, or booking paths.
- The brand has proof points worth preserving: Serving since 1966, Licensed and insured, Se Habla Español.
- The service range is broad enough to support multiple buyer-intent paths.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The “HOA & Multi-Family Roofer” homepage link points to an unrelated HVAC domain, which can create immediate trust damage.
   - Prototype response: Replace any confusing off-domain or placeholder-style links with direct roofing paths.

2. **High:** The page alternates between 58 and 59 years of experience; one consistent “since 1966” proof line is clearer.
   - Prototype response: Use “serving since 1966” consistently to avoid year-count mismatch.

3. **High:** The free-estimate CTA is useful but should be supported by service cards and process expectations earlier.
   - Prototype response: Put the estimate action beside the key trust proof.

4. **High:** Residential and commercial service menus are deep, but first-time visitors need a shorter decision path.
   - Prototype response: Group roof repair, new roof, storm damage, and commercial roofing into four clear options.

5. **Medium:** Brand/testimonial areas appear without much supporting context on the homepage.
   - Prototype response: Add a practical estimate timeline section before the contact block.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Replace any confusing off-domain or placeholder-style links with direct roofing paths.
2. Use “serving since 1966” consistently to avoid year-count mismatch.
3. Put the estimate action beside the key trust proof.
4. Group roof repair, new roof, storm damage, and commercial roofing into four clear options.
5. Add a practical estimate timeline section before the contact block.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
