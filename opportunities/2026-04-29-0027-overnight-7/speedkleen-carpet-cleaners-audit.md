# SpeedKleen Carpet Cleaners — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
SpeedKleen Carpet Cleaners is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current site already has recognizable local-service proof, brand material, and a reachable contact path — but the first-screen decision flow can be made more direct without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current site family and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Get a Quick Quote**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #0f766e, #7bbf43, #effaf5, #123b3a.
- **Typography feel:** Practical local-service sans-serif body copy with stronger display headings for hierarchy.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Contact-forward local service navigation with fewer competing choices.
- **Hero/image treatment:** Same service-business positioning, but with the main action visible immediately.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:**
  - green eco-friendly positioning
  - SpeedKleen name treatment
  - family/pet-safe proof
  - broad home and office service list
  - Naperville phone-first booking

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with clear service intent in Naperville, IL.
- The homepage already communicates a real local service category: eco-friendly carpet, rug, floor, and upholstery cleaning.
- The site includes direct phone, contact, quote, estimate, appointment, or booking paths.
- The brand has proof points worth preserving: Non-toxic green products, Safe for kids and pets, Residential and commercial.
- The service range is broad enough to support multiple buyer-intent paths.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** A broken or raw Google/share link and login/menu clutter interrupt trust before the visitor reaches the quote action.
   - Prototype response: Put the green-cleaning promise, phone number, and quote action at the top.

2. **High:** The eco-friendly promise is compelling but needs a cleaner hierarchy with phone and quote CTA visible sooner.
   - Prototype response: Group services into four high-demand categories.

3. **High:** The service list is broad, but it reads like a wall of items instead of grouped customer choices.
   - Prototype response: Move conditions and pricing notes below the main appointment path.

4. **High:** Pricing fine print is useful but appears as a dense block that can distract before booking.
   - Prototype response: Remove raw/duplicate utility links from the first impression.

5. **Medium:** Residential and commercial audiences need clearer paths.
   - Prototype response: Add a short appointment flow so the visitor knows what to share.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Put the green-cleaning promise, phone number, and quote action at the top.
2. Group services into four high-demand categories.
3. Move conditions and pricing notes below the main appointment path.
4. Remove raw/duplicate utility links from the first impression.
5. Add a short appointment flow so the visitor knows what to share.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
