# Everlast Exteriors of Illinois Inc — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Everlast Exteriors of Illinois Inc is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current site already has recognizable local-service proof, brand material, and a reachable contact path — but the first-screen decision flow can be made more direct without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current site family and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Request Estimate**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #183f55, #83a844, #f3f8f1, #12252f.
- **Typography feel:** Practical local-service sans-serif body copy with stronger display headings for hierarchy.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Contact-forward local service navigation with fewer competing choices.
- **Hero/image treatment:** Same service-business positioning, but with the main action visible immediately.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:**
  - blue/green exterior brand feel
  - Naperville service-area focus
  - 39+ years of experience
  - same-day consultation and free inspection proof
  - windows/siding/roofing/storm-damage breadth

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with clear service intent in Naperville / Chicagoland suburbs, IL.
- The homepage already communicates a real local service category: windows, siding, roofing, and storm-damage exterior contractor.
- The site includes direct phone, contact, quote, estimate, appointment, or booking paths.
- The brand has proof points worth preserving: 39+ years of experience, Lifetime warranty, Free inspections.
- The service range is broad enough to support multiple buyer-intent paths.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** Duplicated navigation and repeated “Serving” lines make the top of the page feel busy before the owner story lands.
   - Prototype response: Keep the exterior-contractor look while simplifying the header and hero flow.

2. **High:** The estimate form is prominent, but trust proof should frame it more clearly.
   - Prototype response: Bring 39+ years, lifetime warranty, free inspections, and premium brands into one trust strip.

3. **High:** Windows, siding, roofing, and storm-damage services need a simpler scan path.
   - Prototype response: Present four service paths before long supporting content.

4. **High:** Premium brands and warranty proof are strong but are scattered across the page.
   - Prototype response: Use the estimate action consistently instead of competing header/menu actions.

5. **Medium:** Recent blog and review requests can move lower after the homeowner decision path.
   - Prototype response: Add a concise consultation process before the contact section.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Keep the exterior-contractor look while simplifying the header and hero flow.
2. Bring 39+ years, lifetime warranty, free inspections, and premium brands into one trust strip.
3. Present four service paths before long supporting content.
4. Use the estimate action consistently instead of competing header/menu actions.
5. Add a concise consultation process before the contact section.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
