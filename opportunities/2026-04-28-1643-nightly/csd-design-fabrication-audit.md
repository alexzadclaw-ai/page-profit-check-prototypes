# CSD Design & Fabrication — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
CSD Design & Fabrication has a usable brand shell already: logo, dark industrial photo background, a simple Squarespace nav, and direct contact details. The problem is that the homepage is effectively a placeholder. The first thing a buyer sees is an under-construction warning instead of a clear explanation of what CSD makes, who it serves, or how to request a quote. That creates immediate trust and conversion loss.

**Visual fingerprint:**
- Squarespace-built layout with centered logo and simple top nav
- Industrial dark/steel color direction with white text
- Lato-style sans serif typography
- Large fabrication shop hero image background
- Sparse section rhythm with big bands and minimal ornament
- Small-business, builder-template feel that the owner would likely recognize instantly

## What is already working
- The business name and logo are visible.
- Contact details are public and easy to find.
- The industrial background image supports the fabrication positioning.
- Navigation labels suggest real capabilities: what they do, products, gallery, contact, location.
- Social profiles are already referenced, which hints that proof assets likely exist off-site.

## Biggest conversion issues

### Critical
1. **Homepage headline is an under-construction notice**  
   Buyers get no service clarity, no offer, and no reason to continue.
2. **No primary quote CTA or next-step explanation**  
   A visitor can find contact details, but there is no guided path for RFQ, fabrication inquiry, or custom work request.
3. **Trust signals are missing near the top**  
   No quick proof of capabilities, process, materials, or project types appears before the visitor has to hunt around.
4. **Template residue damages credibility**  
   The live homepage surfaces stray content like `AVIATOR / LONDON`, which looks unfinished and undermines confidence.

### High
1. **Services are not organized for first-time buyers**  
   Nav labels exist, but the homepage does not explain whether CSD handles cutting, welding, formed parts, fixtures, custom runs, or product fabrication.
2. **Location and service-area context are buried**  
   The Pennsylvania address exists, but there is no quick statement about local/regional fabrication support.
3. **Photo proof is disconnected from the main sales story**  
   Gallery and social links exist, but the homepage does not bridge them into a credibility story.
4. **Contact page naming and path feel generic**  
   A “new-page” contact route and generic page structure feel unfinished.

### Medium
1. Heading structure is weak and not search-friendly.
2. Meta positioning is likely too thin for homepage SEO.
3. Mobile scan flow is sparse but not helpful.
4. Hours and contact formatting are inconsistent across pages.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Replace the placeholder hero copy with a direct fabrication value proposition.
- Add a top trust strip with capabilities, response expectation, and location.
- Turn the homepage into a buyer-intent page: custom fabrication, production help, and product work.
- Add a clear “Request a Quote” CTA and a simple “what happens next” sequence.
- Pull visible proof forward: product categories, process capabilities, and links to gallery/social work.
- Remove obvious unfinished/template leftovers.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Add a clear homepage title focused on metal fabrication / CNC / custom fabrication in the Huntingdon Valley / Philadelphia area.
- Add a meta description that explains services and quote action.
- Use one real H1 instead of placeholder text.
- Add descriptive section headings for fabrication capabilities, products, process, and contact.
- Make phone/email/location text consistent and machine-readable.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a strong **Solution A** fit because the site does not need a reinvention to look credible. It needs a surgical homepage cleanup that preserves the existing dark industrial/Squarespace feel while replacing placeholder content with a real lead-gen structure. The fastest win is a cleaned-up homepage using the current brand assets, background image direction, and simple navigation style.
