## Quick take
CLT Wellness already has a credible niche, a soft approachable brand, and a strong pre/postnatal positioning angle. The problem is that the homepage currently looks unfinished in key places and makes the next step too vague. Placeholder business info, header filler text, a buried consultation path, and weak service organization create avoidable trust loss right at first touch.

This is a strong Solution A candidate because the site does not need a redesign. It needs a cleaner version of the same site family: same soft wellness feel, same centered Squarespace rhythm, same logo-forward layout — but with clearer messaging, visible trust, and a specific consultation path.

## What is already working
- The core niche is valuable and specific: preconception through postpartum support.
- The logo, airy spacing, and centered layout already create a gentle wellness tone.
- Carolyn’s credentials and founder story are real trust assets.
- The business has multiple service types, which supports both 1:1 and education-led offers.
- The existing copy hints at a holistic model that combines movement, education, postpartum support, and practical coaching.

## Site fingerprint
- **Platform feel:** older Squarespace template with centered logo, top info rail, thin rules, narrow content width, and stacked content blocks.
- **Color direction:** white background, charcoal text, muted gray dividers, soft blue links, restrained accent usage.
- **Typography feel:** clean sans serif with italic emphasis in the hero and generous line spacing.
- **Layout rhythm:** centered sections, thin horizontal separators, light content density, modest image use, large white space.
- **Recognizable elements to preserve:** centered logo header, simple navigation, calm white canvas, serif-free typography, thin divider lines, testimonial/gallery feel, footer social/contact pattern.

## Biggest conversion issues
### Critical
1. **Placeholder header content damages trust immediately**  
   The live homepage still shows “Street Address,” “City, State, Zip,” “Phone Number,” and “Your Custom Text Here.” That reads like an unfinished template.

2. **Primary CTA path is unclear**  
   The page talks about support broadly, but the main next step is not obvious or repeated with enough specificity. Visitors should instantly know how to book a consultation.

3. **Hero is broad but not action-oriented**  
   The headline is warm, but the opening section does not quickly translate into a concrete path for a trying-to-conceive, pregnant, or postpartum visitor.

### High
1. **Top trust signals are buried too low**  
   Carolyn’s certifications and founder context live on interior pages, not near the top of the homepage.

2. **Service list is not organized by buyer intent**  
   The homepage presents capabilities, but not a clearer “I’m pregnant / newly postpartum / wanting ongoing support” path.

3. **What happens next is vague**  
   The contact page mentions a free consultation, but the homepage does not clearly explain what a consultation includes or what to expect after reaching out.

4. **Contact and footer areas feel generic rather than business-specific**  
   Newsletter and social/footer blocks exist, but the conversion story is not tied together.

### Medium
1. **Homepage title structure is thin** — strong positioning exists, but heading hierarchy can be cleaner.
2. **SEO basics could better target Charlotte-area prenatal/postpartum intent** if local service area is relevant.
3. **Mobile scanning can improve** through shorter sections, grouped benefit bullets, and tighter CTA repetition.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Replace all placeholder header/template residue with real business-specific language.
- Keep the same soft centered aesthetic, but rebuild the hero around a more specific promise and consultation CTA.
- Add a top trust strip with credentials, service span, and support phases.
- Reorganize services into clearer decision paths: ongoing support, postpartum doula care, and movement/education.
- Add a “how to get started” section so the next step feels safe and easy.
- Pull founder credibility higher on the page.
- Keep testimonials and social proof visible without overcomplicating the page.

## SEO basics to tighten
- **Title tag:** make it more specific than just the brand name. Example direction: Pre & Postnatal Wellness Support in Charlotte | CLT Wellness.
- **Meta description:** clarify who it is for and what support includes.
- **H1/H2 structure:** keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service pathways and consultation flow.
- **Internal linking:** give direct links from homepage sections to Carolyn, Programs & Services, and Contact.
- **Local relevance:** if Charlotte is the service area, mention it naturally in hero/support sections.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a straightforward homepage cleanup, not a platform migration. The win is preserving the current visual fingerprint while fixing trust leaks and making the consultation path obvious. Most of the improvement can be accomplished through copy cleanup, section restructuring, and selective trust placement inside the current Squarespace style.
