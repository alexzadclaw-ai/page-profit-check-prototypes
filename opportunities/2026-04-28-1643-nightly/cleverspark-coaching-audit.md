# CleverSpark Coaching — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
CleverSpark Coaching already feels warm, personal, and approachable. The biggest opportunity is not a brand reset — it is a credibility and conversion cleanup. The current homepage communicates heart, but it undersells the offer by hiding trust cues, repeating generic CTAs, leaving proofreading issues live, and making package decisions harder than they need to be.

**Best-fit verdict:** Audit-Driven Solution A. Keep the Squarespace feel, centered navigation, friendly black/white palette, personal portrait, and section rhythm — then tighten clarity, proof, package scanning, and next-step flow.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## What is already working
- Kimberly is visibly present, which helps the brand feel human immediately.
- The offer is naturally empathetic and accessible, especially for career transition, neurodivergence, and education support.
- The "Spark" naming system gives the services a memorable personality.
- Pricing is comparatively approachable, which is a real advantage once it is presented more clearly.
- The site already has enough visual structure to improve surgically instead of redesigning from scratch.

## Site fingerprint
- **Platform feel:** Squarespace template with centered nav, airy spacing, and simple stacked sections.
- **Color direction:** crisp white sections, black contrast band, soft neutrals, minimal accent use.
- **Typography feel:** modern sans-serif with clean headings and light editorial spacing.
- **Imagery:** friendly founder portrait plus lifestyle imagery in package sections.
- **Layout rhythm:** intro hero, brand story, service overview, contact CTA, footer.
- **Recognition elements to preserve:** centered header/nav, black-and-white section contrast, founder image prominence, approachable tone, "Spark" package naming.

## Biggest conversion issues

### Critical
1. **Trust-damaging proofreading and cleanup issues are live.**
   - "CleverSpark Coacghing" appears in the footer.
   - "Caoching Bundle" and "Resume Wtiring" appear in services.
   - "Powered by Squarespace Circle" reads like template residue, not intentional business footer copy.
   - These errors directly weaken professionalism for a coaching business selling confidence and guidance.

2. **The homepage does not show enough proof or qualification framing near the top.**
   - Kimberly's 15+ years of experience and counseling/education background are not surfaced early enough.
   - Visitors have to work too hard to answer "Why should I trust this coach with my next step?"

### High
1. **Hero CTA sequence is too generic and repetitive.**
   - "Work with Me" appears twice without helping the visitor choose a path.
   - The CTA does not explain what happens after clicking or why the consult matters.

2. **Service paths are not organized for quick buyer recognition.**
   - The homepage lists broad categories, but not the most likely intent paths a visitor will self-identify with.
   - Pricing and packages live on a separate page with too much copy density and low scannability.

3. **Package page clarity is dragged down by formatting and typo noise.**
   - Pricing is there, but the page makes comparison harder than necessary.
   - Formatting inconsistencies make the offer feel less polished than it actually is.

4. **Next-step clarity is weak.**
   - There is no strong, reassuring "here is what happens after you book" explanation.
   - Coaching can feel vulnerable; process clarity reduces hesitation.

### Medium
1. **SEO structure is thin.**
   - Homepage heading hierarchy is awkward (`H2`, `H4`, then `H1` for Services).
   - The services page title `Services 4` is weak and likely accidental.

2. **Footer and contact area feel under-finished.**
   - The footer is more template than brand.
   - Contact invitation is kind, but not especially specific.

3. **Homepage copy blocks are long for mobile scanning.**
   - The warmth is good; the density is harder on first-touch readers.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. **Keep the visual family, but rewrite the hero for clarity.**
   - State who Kimberly helps and what outcomes people come for.
   - Replace generic CTA with a specific consult CTA plus a secondary package CTA.

2. **Add a trust strip directly under the hero.**
   - Surface 15+ years of experience, counseling/education background, and neurodivergence-aware support.

3. **Turn services into scan-friendly paths.**
   - Present the main pathways as clear cards: career direction, job search, neurodivergent support, education planning.
   - Keep the original categories and Spark naming language nearby so it still feels like the same brand.

4. **Bring package examples onto the homepage.**
   - Feature a few recognizable Spark packages with session counts, best-fit use cases, and investment ranges.
   - This reduces pogo-sticking to a dense services page before trust is established.

5. **Add a "what happens next" block.**
   - Free 30-minute consult → recommend best-fit package → start with a clear next step.

6. **Clean the footer and typo issues.**
   - This is a light lift with outsized trust payoff.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Change homepage title only lightly if desired, but the current version is usable.
- Improve heading hierarchy so the page has one clear `H1` and sensible supporting headings.
- Rename the services page title from **Services 4** to something intentional, such as **Coaching Packages & Career Support Services | CleverSpark Coaching**.
- Add internal links between homepage, about, services, and contact using descriptive anchor text.
- Make package headings and service paths more keyword-legible: career coaching, ADHD/neurodivergence coaching, college and education planning, interview and resume help.

## Realistic implementation angle
This should be sold as a surgical homepage cleanup, not a redesign. The owner likely wants continuity and recognizability. The win is preserving the founder-led feel while removing accidental friction.

Practical implementation path:
- tighten homepage messaging and CTA hierarchy
- add a small trust/proof strip near the top
- feature clearer service and package previews
- fix proofreading and footer residue site-wide
- lightly clean service page labeling and structure

## Offer fit
Strong fit for the **$99 Page Profit Check** because the opportunity is visible, concrete, and fixable without changing the whole brand. An optional cleanup sprint is also credible here because the improvements are mostly copy/layout refinement, service-page cleanup, and CTA flow work.
