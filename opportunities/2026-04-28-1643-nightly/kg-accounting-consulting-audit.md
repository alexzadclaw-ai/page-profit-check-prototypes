# KG Accounting & Consulting — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
KG Accounting & Consulting already presents as a credible small-business finance firm. The positioning is solid, the service mix is relevant, and Kristine’s expertise is real. The homepage issue is mostly conversion flow: the hero is broad, the first CTA is a little abstract, trust proof arrives later than it should, and the page asks visitors to scroll through several broad sections before they know which path fits them.

**Recommendation:** keep the same Wix-built visual family, dark-blue/white color direction, polished small-business tone, and centered section rhythm — but surgically sharpen the hero, move trust higher, organize services by buyer intent, and make the right-fit call feel more concrete.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Platform / feel:** modern Wix brochure-site build with centered copy blocks, rounded CTA buttons, icon-supported service cards, and long single-page section flow.
- **Color direction:** deep navy, white, light gray backgrounds, and muted slate text with small gold/yellow accents in imagery.
- **Typography feel:** polished serif-style display heading feel paired with clean sans-serif body copy.
- **Header pattern:** compact top nav, logo at left, prominent CTA on the right.
- **Section rhythm:** hero → brand promise → services grid → lead magnet → founder intro → blog preview → final CTA/footer.
- **Image treatment:** clean professional brand graphics, iconography, and founder-led credibility.
- **Builder quirks worth preserving:** Wix spacing rhythm, centered section headings, simple card grids, button style, and polished-but-approachable small-business consulting feel.

## What is already working
- The core offer is useful and understandable: bookkeeping, cash flow advisory, fractional CFO support, and systems consulting.
- The homepage headline already points at growing small businesses instead of generic accounting.
- Kristine’s founder credibility and MBA background support premium trust.
- The service categories are relevant to real owner needs.
- The site already uses a consistent visual system and feels more established than many local service sites.
- The footer copy clearly states Lake Forest, IL plus nationwide service.

## Biggest conversion issues

### Critical
1. **The first CTA is still a little vague for a cold visitor.**
   - “Schedule a right-fit call” sounds thoughtful, but the page does not immediately explain what happens on that call or who it is best for.
2. **Top-of-page trust is lighter than it should be for a financial service.**
   - The visitor gets the promise before they get enough quick proof: who this is for, where the firm is based, and what kind of guidance they can expect.

### High
1. **The hero helps, but it does not split visitors into clear buyer paths fast enough.**
   - Owners who need books cleaned up, cash flow clarity, or strategic CFO support still have to interpret the next section themselves.
2. **The lead magnet interrupts the main conversion path.**
   - It is useful, but on the homepage it competes with the core consultation CTA before the service story is fully landed.
3. **The “more than bookkeeping” section is strong but scroll-heavy.**
   - The value proposition could be broken into faster-scanning trust points near the top.
4. **The services section is category-based rather than decision-based.**
   - First-time buyers often think in problems: “my books are messy,” “cash feels tight,” “I need higher-level insight.”
5. **The page could explain the first next step more clearly.**
   - A short consultation/process block would reduce uncertainty and make the CTA feel safer.

### Medium
1. The homepage title is decent, but could pull in Lake Forest / advisory intent more strongly.
2. The blog preview takes up meaningful space relative to higher-value homepage conversion content.
3. Some copy blocks are longer than necessary on mobile.
4. The final CTA could repeat trust cues instead of just asking for the call again.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. **Clarify the hero CTA.**
   - Keep the right-fit framing, but explain that the first call reviews current books, cash flow pressure points, and the best service fit.
2. **Add an early trust strip.**
   - Bring up founder-led guidance, Lake Forest base / nationwide support, and MBA/fractional CFO context immediately.
3. **Reframe services by buyer intent.**
   - One path for bookkeeping cleanup, one for cash flow visibility, one for strategic CFO support.
4. **Demote the lead magnet slightly.**
   - Keep it, but place it after the core service story so it supports the funnel instead of splitting it.
5. **Add a visible “what happens next” flow.**
   - Inquiry → right-fit call → recommended support plan.
6. **Tighten copy for mobile scanning.**
   - Shorter sections, smaller paragraphs, and clearer subheads without changing the site’s voice.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Adjust the homepage title to include Lake Forest or small-business accounting advisory intent more directly.
- Keep a single clear H1 focused on accounting and CFO support for small businesses.
- Add stronger internal-link anchor text around bookkeeping, cash flow advisory, and fractional CFO services.
- Keep the founder/location language higher on the page for local relevance and trust.
- Trim lower-priority homepage content so search and human visitors hit the core services sooner.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a strong **Solution A** prospect. The site does not need a redesign. It needs a surgical conversion cleanup that preserves the same visual identity while making the homepage easier to understand and easier to act on. The improved version should still look unmistakably like KG Accounting — just with a clearer buyer journey.
