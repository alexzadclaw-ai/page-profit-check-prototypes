# Attuned Studios — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Attuned Studios has a distinctive wellness niche and a live site that already hints at the right blend of body contouring, energetic work, and whole-person care. The problem is trust and clarity. The homepage currently looks unfinished in key places, repeats core sections, uses placeholder business details, and asks visitors to do too much interpretation before they understand what to book.

**Recommendation:** keep the same Squarespace-style visual family, calm wellness tone, centered section rhythm, and mind-body-soul positioning — but surgically tighten the hero, remove leftover template residue, clarify the service stack, and make the consultation path feel specific and safe.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Platform / feel:** classic Squarespace stack with centered content blocks, airy spacing, repeated nav/header pattern, and a simple brochure-site rhythm.
- **Color direction:** soft white backgrounds, charcoal text, muted sage / green-gray accents, and occasional dark image overlays.
- **Typography feel:** clean sans-serif body text with a refined editorial/wellness heading style.
- **Header pattern:** minimal centered navigation with the brand line repeated prominently.
- **Section rhythm:** hero statement, service summary, two or three featured pathways, consultation CTA, simple contact/footer.
- **Image treatment:** soft lifestyle / wellness imagery and founder-led personal-brand photography.
- **Builder quirks worth preserving:** lightly templated Squarespace structure, centered copy blocks, generous white space, modest button styling, and a calm boutique-wellness feel.

## What is already working
- The business has a memorable niche: body contouring, energetic healing, and integrative wellness in one place.
- The phrase **Mind.Body.Soul** gives the brand a recognizable frame.
- There is a real founder story and a large body of training / certification depth to pull from.
- Body sculpting and energetic work are already separated in navigation, which helps shape two main entry paths.
- The site does have a consultation / booking path and visible contact details.
- The tone is clearly personal, intentional, and wellness-oriented rather than generic medspa marketing.

## Biggest conversion issues

### Critical
1. **Template leftovers create an immediate trust leak.**
   - The homepage shows placeholder business details like “Street Address,” “City, State, Zip,” “Phone Number,” and “Your Custom Text Here.”
2. **The homepage structure is visibly broken / repetitive.**
   - The brand line and navigation are duplicated, making the page feel unfinished instead of intentionally designed.
3. **The hero does not explain the first next step clearly enough.**
   - Visitors see broad wellness language, but not a clean, trust-first path for how to begin.

### High
1. **The offer stack is too broad and abstract at first glance.**
   - New visitors must decode body contouring, energetic healing, and integration sessions on their own.
2. **Trust proof is buried instead of surfaced near the top.**
   - Founder credibility, St. Louis location, appointment format, and consultation approach should appear much earlier.
3. **Several pages contain dense or awkward copy blocks.**
   - Long explanations and repeated concepts reduce scan speed, especially on mobile.
4. **CTA language is inconsistent.**
   - “Explore,” “Learn more,” and “Book Sessions” exist, but the page needs one main primary action with clear expectation-setting.
5. **The contact experience feels more policy-heavy than welcoming.**
   - Important policy language appears, but visitors need reassurance about what happens before they commit.

### Medium
1. The homepage title and meta description are under-optimized.
2. Headings and internal links could better support SEO and scanning.
3. Some wording overlap between modalities creates positioning blur.
4. Footer content is sparse and misses a stronger trust recap.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. **Replace placeholder content with real trust signals.**
   - Surface Saint Louis, consultation-based booking, founder-led care, and personalized sessions.
2. **Make the hero more specific without changing the brand tone.**
   - Clarify who the business helps and what the first conversation is for.
3. **Organize services by buyer intent.**
   - One path for body refinement, one for energetic balance, one for integrated support.
4. **Add a simple “what happens next” section.**
   - Inquiry → consultation → personalized session plan.
5. **Pull credibility upward.**
   - Founder background, appointment format, and client-centered language should sit near the top instead of being buried deeper.
6. **Tighten copy into scan-friendly cards and shorter sections.**
   - Preserve the same feel while reducing effort to understand the offer.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title focused on body contouring, energetic healing, and St. Louis.
- Add a real meta description describing consultation-led wellness services.
- Keep one clear H1 and cleaner H2 sections for services and next steps.
- Replace vague repeated link text with service-specific internal links.
- Reduce duplicated header/navigation text that can dilute keyword focus.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a strong **Solution A** fit. The visual identity does not need reinvention. The opportunity is a trust-first cleanup that preserves the recognizable boutique-wellness style while removing broken template cues and making the homepage easier to understand. The best version still looks like Attuned Studios — just calmer, clearer, and more intentional.
