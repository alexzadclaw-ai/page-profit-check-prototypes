# CLT Wellness — Page Profit Check Audit

- **Contact/source:** mailto:carolyn@cltwellness.com
- **Extra source:** https://www.cltwellness.com/carolyn
- **Reviewed:** 2026-04-28
- **Priority:** A-
- **Positioning:** Strong pre/postnatal niche with obvious clarity and trust-lift opportunity

## Quick take

CLT Wellness has a genuinely strong niche: preconception, pregnancy, postpartum, rehab-minded movement, childbirth education, doula support, and practical mom-to-mom guidance. That combination is valuable.

The issue is that the site currently looks more like an unfinished placeholder than a polished specialist service. The homepage has obvious template residue, missing business details, minimal CTA structure, and almost no guidance for what a visitor should do next. That creates a very clean before/after story for a **$99 mini-audit plus optional cleanup sprint**.

## What is already working

1. **The niche is specific and desirable** — pre/postnatal wellness is much stronger than a generic wellness pitch.
2. **The founder story is compelling** — Carolyn’s personal transition to motherhood adds warmth and credibility.
3. **Credentials are real and broad** — doula, childbirth educator, health coach, pre/postnatal coach, and personal trainer is a strong stack.
4. **Service range is valuable** — movement, postpartum doula support, childbirth education, newborn care support, lactation, sleep, and nutrition can support multiple buyer intents.
5. **The core promise is emotionally resonant** — healthy and empowering transition to motherhood is a clear transformation.

## Biggest conversion issues

### 1) The page shows obvious placeholder/template residue
The site currently displays items like:
- “Street Address”
- “City, State, Zip”
- “Phone Number”
- “Your Custom Text Here”
- the homepage title showing as “/” in the content area

Those elements quietly damage trust, especially in a wellness category where reassurance matters.

### 2) The booking path is unclear
There is contact language, but the homepage does not clearly show:
- what the primary offer is
- which service to start with
- what happens in a free consultation
- whether support is virtual, local, or both

For a high-emotion, high-trust service, this friction matters a lot.

### 3) The hero is warm but under-structured
The main message has the right spirit, but it does not fully organize the offer. Visitors need faster answers to:
- who this is for
- what stage of motherhood is supported
- which services are included
- how to begin

### 4) Credentials live too far from the main CTA story
Carolyn’s background is one of the strongest sales assets on the site, but it is not surfaced early enough on the homepage.

A trust strip near the hero would help a lot.

### 5) Service categories need better packaging
The site has several useful service lines, but they read more like separate content blocks than a clear offer system.

A better structure would separate:
- ongoing membership / full-spectrum support
- postpartum doula support
- rehab + fitness coaching
- workshops / education

### 6) Testimonials are underdeveloped
The homepage references testimonials, but the proof section does not currently do enough work. In this niche, visitors want calm reassurance, safety, and evidence that support feels practical and personal.

### 7) Contact-page reassurance is too light
The contact page invites questions, but it does not reduce anxiety around reaching out. It should more clearly say:
- free consultation
- no pressure
- help choosing the right program
- support for partners/questions too

## Best Page Profit Check improvements

### 1) Rebuild the hero around one clear starting point
Suggested direction:
- headline focused on confident support from pregnancy through postpartum
- short subhead that explains the integrated model
- one primary CTA: **Book Your Free Consultation**
- one secondary CTA: **Explore Services**

### 2) Add an early credibility strip
Bring proof close to the first CTA:
- Certified Postpartum Doula
- Childbirth Educator
- Pre & Postnatal Health Coach
- Certified Personal Trainer
- Mom of 3

That turns Carolyn from “person behind the brand” into “trusted specialist” much faster.

### 3) Organize the offer by stage and support type
Instead of a broad list, create clear paths such as:
- **Planning for pregnancy**
- **Pregnancy movement and preparation**
- **Birth and postpartum support**
- **Rehab, recovery, and return-to-strength**

This makes the business easier to understand and easier to buy from.

### 4) Explain what happens after someone reaches out
A small 3-step block would reduce hesitation:
1. Share your stage, goals, and questions
2. Get matched to the right support option
3. Start with a personalized plan or package

### 5) Add a “who this is for” section
Strong fit examples:
- first-time moms who want guidance and reassurance
- pregnant clients wanting safe movement and birth prep
- postpartum moms navigating recovery and overwhelm
- families wanting support with newborn care, feeding, and sleep

### 6) Tighten local/format clarity
The brand name suggests Charlotte, but the site should say more explicitly whether services are:
- local in-person
- virtual
- hybrid

That helps both conversions and SEO.

### 7) Add FAQ content for trust + search
Useful questions:
- When should I reach out — preconception, pregnancy, or postpartum?
- Is support virtual or in person?
- Do you help with postpartum recovery and safe return to exercise?
- What happens in a consultation?
- Can partners be included in childbirth education?

## SEO basics to tighten

Low-effort but meaningful improvements:
- homepage title and meta description aligned to pre/postnatal wellness
- cleaner H1/H2 hierarchy
- service-specific internal linking
- clearer mention of Charlotte / CLT if local service is important
- stronger copy around postpartum doula, childbirth education, and pre/postnatal fitness intent

Best implementation story:

- surface credentials earlier
- package services into understandable buyer paths
- add consultation/process/FAQ support
- make the page feel calmer and more trustworthy on mobile
