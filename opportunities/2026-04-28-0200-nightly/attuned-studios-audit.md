# Attuned Studios — Page Profit Check Audit

- **Business:** Attuned Studios
- **Site:** https://www.lightloveenergy.com/
- **Contact/source:** mailto:info@lightloveenergy.com
- **Extra source:** https://www.lightloveenergy.com/contact
- **Reviewed:** 2026-04-28
- **Priority:** B+
- **Positioning:** Trust-first clarity cleanup for a distinctive wellness brand with visible template residue

## Quick take

Attuned Studios has a memorable niche: body sculpting, energetic healing, and integrative wellness under one brand. That gives the business a differentiated angle right away.

The problem is that the homepage currently makes visitors work too hard to understand **what is offered, who it is for, and why they should trust the next step**. On top of that, there are obvious template leftovers and placeholder elements that weaken credibility fast.

This makes it a strong **cleanup-and-clarity opportunity**, not because the niche is weak, but because the positioning needs firmer structure.

## What is already working

1. **The niche is distinctive** — combining body contouring with energetic and integrative healing is memorable.
2. **The brand language aims for gentle, intentional care** — that tone can work well for a trust-led consultation funnel.
3. **There is already a consultation CTA** — the site invites a next step instead of leaving visitors stranded.
4. **Service categories do exist** — body contouring, energetic healing, and mind-body support are all visible.
5. **Location and contact details are present** — Saint Louis, email, and phone are available on the contact page.

## Biggest conversion issues

### 1) Visible template leftovers damage trust
The live site currently shows several obvious unfinished elements, including:
- “Street Address”
- “City, State, Zip”
- “Phone Number”
- “Your Custom Text Here”
- duplicated navigation / header content

For a wellness business asking for trust, those details create immediate hesitation.

### 2) The homepage positioning overlaps instead of guiding
The page references body sculpting, energetic innovation, healing, mind-body integration, and wellness philosophy, but the offer stack is not sequenced clearly enough for a first-time visitor.

A new visitor may not quickly understand:
- what the main services are
- which service is right for them
- whether this is in-person, remote, or both
- what happens if they book

### 3) The consultation CTA needs stronger support
“Book Your Consultation” exists, but it is not backed up by enough friction-reducing context.

For this niche, visitors likely need reassurance around:
- what a consultation includes
- whether it is exploratory or sales-driven
- which services can be discussed
- whether virtual sessions are available

### 4) Trust signals are buried inside dense copy
The About page shows substantial background and certifications, but they are buried inside a very long paragraph-heavy section.

That means the strongest trust builders do not help enough near the top of the funnel.

### 5) Copy density hurts scannability
Several pages rely on long, abstract or modality-heavy paragraphs. That may work for deeply interested readers, but it slows down visitors who are just trying to answer basic questions:
- Is this for me?
- What do I book?
- Is this credible?
- What do I do next?

### 6) Service architecture is unclear
The site appears to have at least three practical lanes:
- body contouring / sculpting
- energetic healing / bioenergetic sessions
- coaching or integrative mind-body support

But those lanes are not framed as distinct buyer paths, so the service menu feels more complex than it needs to.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements

### 1) Rebuild the hero around a simpler promise
The top of the page should quickly explain that Attuned Studios offers personalized body contouring, energetic healing, and integrative wellness support in a guided, consultation-led format.

**Better direction:**
- one clean headline
- one supportive subhead
- one primary consultation CTA
- one secondary “explore services” CTA

### 2) Add an early trust strip
Move trust cues closer to the first CTA, such as:
- Saint Louis-based wellness studio
- body contouring + energetic healing services
- consultation-led recommendations
- personalized care and appointment-based sessions

If desired, a second trust layer could mention credentials more selectively instead of dropping the full long-form bio immediately.

### 3) Turn services into clearer paths
Instead of presenting the brand as one large conceptual umbrella, split it into buyer-friendly paths:
- **Body Contouring & Sculpting** — for visible body-refinement goals
- **Energetic Healing & Regulation** — for nervous-system, energy, and emotional-balance support
- **Integrative Mind-Body Sessions** — for deeper personalized support and alignment

This helps visitors self-sort without overwhelm.

### 4) Explain what happens in the consultation
A simple 3-step explainer would likely help conversion:
1. share your goals and questions
2. review which services fit best
3. book the right next session or care path

### 5) Clean the placeholder/template issues immediately
This is one of the highest-value fixes because it improves trust without changing the business itself.

### 6) Shorten and stage the credential story
The About page has real depth, but it should be reorganized into:
- quick credibility bullets near the top
- a shorter founder summary
- optional longer background below

### 7) Add FAQ support for trust and SEO
Useful FAQ topics:
- What is the best first session for a new client?
- Are sessions in person, remote, or both?
- What is the difference between body contouring and energetic healing?
- What happens during a consultation?
- Are these services intended as medical treatment?

## SEO basics to tighten

Low-effort improvements that would help:
- stronger homepage title and meta description
- cleaner H1/H2 hierarchy
- more specific Saint Louis local relevance near the top of the page
- clearer internal links between service categories
- less duplicated header/navigation content

## Realistic implementation angle

This is best pitched as a **clarity and trust cleanup sprint**, not a total redesign.

Strong scope:
- hero rewrite
- trust-strip addition
- service-path restructuring
- CTA support copy
- placeholder cleanup
- lighter/scannable founder section
- FAQ block
- light SEO/meta cleanup
