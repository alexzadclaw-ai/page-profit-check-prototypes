# Page Profit Check audit - Tidy Clean

## Quick take
Tidy Clean already has the essentials for a commercial cleaning lead: visible phone, clickable email, service-specific copy, real service imagery, repeated quote buttons, and clear office, church, green-cleaning, and high-filtration cleaning proof. The biggest opportunity is not a full redesign. It is a tighter services page that keeps the existing Wix look while making the first screen easier to scan and the quote path feel more intentional.

## What is already working
- The business name and “Quality Cleaning Services” positioning are clear.
- Phone and email are visible near the top: 248-592-7543 and tidyclean7@gmail.com.
- The services page has useful commercial-cleaning depth instead of generic one-line blurbs.
- Source imagery supports the main claims: office cleaning, church cleaning, green cleaning, carpet/vacuum work, clean office interiors, carpet care, and hard-floor care.
- The cyan brand color, white services panel, patterned Wix background, and condensed typography create a recognizable look that can be preserved.
- Multiple “GET A QUOTE” buttons already tell visitors what action to take.

## Biggest conversion issues
- The first screen has a lot of whitespace before the visitor reaches the actual service proof.
- The quote buttons repeat under each image, but the page does not clearly explain why someone should request a quote before they read the dense text.
- Commercial janitorial, carpet cleaning, hard surface floor care, window washing, power washing, church cleaning, and green cleaning are not grouped in a quick-scan way.
- The strongest proof, high-filtration equipment and green-cleaning benefits, is buried in paragraph copy.
- Service text is narrow and low-contrast, which makes the page feel heavier than it needs to be.
- The bottom image collage has useful assets, but it does not currently guide the visitor toward a next step.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Keep the same Wix-style services page, but add a first-screen service promise next to the Services heading.
- Move one clear quote CTA and the phone number higher in the white services panel so visitors do not have to hunt after the header.
- Keep the four existing service blocks, but rewrite them into scan-friendly proof points.
- Add short bullets for office cleaning, high-filtration vacuuming, church flexibility, and green-cleaning indoor-air benefits.
- Surface the extra services as a compact “Also available” row: carpet cleaning, hard surface floor care, window washing, and power washing.
- Keep the original image collage, but give it a clearer role as service proof and quote-path reinforcement.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a stronger H1/title around commercial cleaning and janitorial services in the Metro Detroit area.
- Add visible headings for office cleaning, church cleaning, green cleaning, carpet cleaning, hard surface floor care, window washing, and power washing.
- Add alt text to every service image instead of leaving the images empty.
- Format phone and email consistently across the header, service panel, and contact page.
- Add service-area language near the top so local visitors know Tidy Clean serves the Metro Detroit, MI area.
- Consider a short meta description focused on commercial janitorial and office cleaning rather than only “Services | tidyclean.”

## Realistic implementation angle
A focused Wix edit could preserve the current site structure, header, palette, image treatment, service blocks, and footer while reorganizing the services panel. The realistic implementation work would likely include editing the Wix services page layout, tightening copy, adding a top CTA/trust row, improving image alt text, and checking mobile spacing. A rough implementation ROM would be about $600 to $1,200 depending on how much Wix mobile cleanup is needed.

## Offer fit
This is a strong fit for a $99 Page Profit Check because the site has enough real content and source imagery to work with. The audit/diagnosis can show which improvements are highest-impact and provide a visual before/after direction without selling a full rebuild first.
