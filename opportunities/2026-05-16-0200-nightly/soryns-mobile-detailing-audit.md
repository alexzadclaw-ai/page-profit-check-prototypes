# Soryn's Mobile Detailing Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take

Soryn's Mobile Detailing already has the right core promise: premium detailing at the convenience of the customer's driveway. The site has real vehicle photos, email contact, quote/book actions, hours, a service area, and a form. The biggest opportunity is not a rebuild. It is making the first impression and service choice easier, then giving visitors a clearer quote path for exterior, interior, or full package detailing in Central PA.

## What is already working

- The hero clearly says this is mobile detailing and emphasizes driveway convenience.
- Real source vehicle photos make the page feel local and service-specific.
- The visual palette is consistent: black/dark teal backgrounds with bright blue detailing accents.
- The site lists the main service categories: Exterior Wash, Interior Detail, and Full Package.
- The live page includes quote and booking actions, a free quote form, hours, email contact, and social links.
- The Central PA and Elizabethtown service language is visible.
- Before/after imagery is present near the contact form, which can become a strong proof point.

## Biggest conversion issues

- The hero has a strong brand line, but it does not help visitors quickly choose between exterior, interior, and full package options.
- The page uses long centered paragraphs, so the value proposition takes effort to scan.
- The service rows describe each package but do not give quick "best for" guidance or package-specific quote prompts.
- The quote form is far down the page, and visitors are not told what info or photos will help get a useful quote.
- The live layout has horizontal overflow/cropping in several places, including body copy, service images, before/after labels, and contact/social content.
- There is no visible pricing or starting-price guidance, which may slow down quote submissions.
- No phone number is shown, so the email/form path needs to be especially clear and reassuring.
- The Wix promotional banner makes the page feel less polished and competes with the first impression.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements

- Keep the same black/teal/aqua detailing look, hero photo, real vehicle images, and simple Wix-built structure.
- Add a short hero guidance line that tells visitors they can choose exterior, interior, or full package mobile detailing.
- Add small trust cues near the hero: mobile service, hours, and service types.
- Tighten the "What do you get?" and "Our Goal" copy so it is easier to scan without losing the personal tone.
- Add "best for" labels to the service rows so visitors can self-select faster.
- Turn each service row into a clearer quote path with service-specific quote links.
- Improve the quote form intro by asking for vehicle details, desired service, and photos if the customer is unsure.
- Keep the before/after proof, but make it support the form instead of visually crowding it.

## SEO basics to tighten

- Add a meta description focused on Soryn's Mobile Detailing, mobile detailing, Elizabethtown, and Central PA.
- Use one clear H1, then real H2s for services, service area, and quote/contact sections.
- Add descriptive alt text to real vehicle photos instead of relying on image filenames.
- Add more local terms naturally, such as "Elizabethtown mobile detailing" and "Central PA driveway detailing."
- Consider adding starting-price ranges or quote-by-photo language if exact pricing depends on vehicle condition.
- Add basic local business/service schema later if Wix allows it.
- If the business has reviews, add a short testimonial or review snippet near the hero or quote form.

## Realistic implementation angle

This is a surgical Wix conversion pass, not a new website. The practical implementation would likely involve editing hero copy, adding a small quick-link/nav area, tightening existing copy, adding service guidance labels, improving CTA wording, adjusting overflow issues, improving image alt text, and clarifying the quote form instructions. A rough hands-on implementation range would likely be $300 to $700 depending on how much Wix layout and mobile cleanup is included.
