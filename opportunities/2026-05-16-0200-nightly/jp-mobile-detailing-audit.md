# JPs Mobile Detailing Page Profit Check audit

## Quick take

JPs Mobile Detailing already has the right raw material for a local mobile detailing page: Houston/Galveston positioning, email, phone number, RV specialty, former RV technician credibility, package prices, same-day availability language, add-on services, and real source photos. The biggest opportunity is to make the visitor path simpler: send photos, choose exterior/interior/full package or add-ons, confirm location and timing, then call or email JP.

## What is already working

- The page is clearly for JPs Mobile Detailing and keeps a simple Wix-style one-page layout.
- Houston and Galveston service-area language appears in the header and intro copy.
- The phone number and email are visible near the top and repeated in the page path.
- The site already explains that JP specializes in motorhome and RV detailing.
- Former RV technician experience is a strong trust cue for motorhome owners.
- The site mentions same-day appointments in many cases.
- The page already has Book Now, Get A Quote, and Get In Touch CTAs.
- The pricing is concrete: Full Package, Exterior, Interior, and add-on services are listed.
- The quote-by-photo idea is already present in the Services copy.
- The dark brown, white, and muted olive palette is recognizable and worth preserving.
- The source site has reusable Wix assets: logo/social icons, RV hero image, RV exterior photo, RV interior photo, and the blurred RV image band.

## Biggest conversion issues

- The first screen has a large blank top area and then a hero image with no headline or CTA overlay, so visitors do not immediately know what to do next.
- The strongest conversion idea, sending a photo of the RV for accurate pricing, is buried in the Services paragraph instead of being presented as the main quote path.
- Package lists are useful but dense. The visitor has to read long centered bullet lists to decide between Full, Exterior, Interior, and add-ons.
- Pricing is shown without enough context around RV size, condition, and the note that RVs over 28 feet may require additional fees.
- Cars and trucks are mentioned, but the car/truck path is not organized as a separate secondary service path.
- The page has no review/testimonial block, no guarantee language, and no quick trust cues beyond the former RV technician note.
- The lower page has a large empty-looking area and a dark category block with only two labels, which feels unfinished.
- The footer focuses on a Subscribe Form, which is not as useful as a quote request for a local mobile detailing business.
- Several copy issues reduce polish: “HOUSTONS,” “10years,” “Simple send,” “shinning,” “then” instead of “than,” and inconsistent capitalization.
- The mobile service model is valuable, but “home, work, or storage facility” could be surfaced earlier.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements

- Keep the same Wix-style header, hero image, centered intro, dark Services band, pricing sections, add-ons, dark category section, and footer structure.
- Add a small hero ribbon that makes the mobile RV detailing promise clear without replacing the original visual identity.
- Reframe the intro around Houston/Galveston RV and motorhome detailing, former RV technician experience, and the photo-based quote path.
- Make the CTAs clearer: Book Now, Get A Quote, and Call JP.
- Convert the buried Services paragraph into a simple three-step path: send photos, choose package, and JP comes to you.
- Keep the original package names and prices, but add short context lines so visitors understand which package fits their situation.
- Keep add-on pricing but clean up punctuation and clarify that larger RVs can require added fees.
- Replace the mostly empty lower-page area with a photo-based RV/interior service explanation using original source photos.
- Change the footer form intent from “Subscribe Form” to a quote request path.
- Preserve the original palette, centered typography, thin bordered buttons, white space, and dark section rhythm so it still feels like the same site.

## SEO basics to tighten

- Use a descriptive title such as “JPs Mobile Detailing | Houston & Galveston RV Detailing.”
- Add a meta description mentioning mobile RV detailing, motorhome detailing, car detailing, truck detailing, Houston, Galveston, photo quotes, and mobile service.
- Keep one clear H1 focused on the business and service, then use H2s for Services, Packages, Additional Services, RV Detailing, and Car & Truck Detailing.
- Add service-area copy above the fold: Houston, Galveston, and nearby areas.
- Add alt text for the logo, hero RV image, RV exterior photo, RV interior photo, and social icons.
- Use consistent `tel:` and `mailto:` links for the phone number and email.
- Add FAQ copy for RV size pricing, photo-based quotes, same-day availability, storage-facility service, roof cleaning, upholstery shampoo, and add-ons.
- Consider LocalBusiness or AutoRepair-style schema only if the business owner can validate the exact service categories and service area.
- Add review/testimonial content if JP has permission to use customer feedback.

## Realistic implementation angle

This is a focused Wix homepage conversion pass, not a full rebuild. The practical work would be copy hierarchy, CTA placement, quote-by-photo messaging, package explanation, add-on formatting, footer intent, image treatment, and SEO basics. The existing layout can stay recognizable, including the header, hero, white intro block, dark Services band, pricing rhythm, add-ons, and footer. Implementation should be separate from the Page Profit Check audit because it would require editing Wix content, layout, links, SEO fields, and possibly form behavior.
