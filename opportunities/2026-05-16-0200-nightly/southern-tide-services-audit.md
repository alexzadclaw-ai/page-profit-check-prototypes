# Southern Tide Services Page Profit Check audit

## Quick take

Southern Tide Services already has strong raw material for a commercial cleaning site: a clear niche, Gulf Coast positioning, a phone number, email path, consultation route, FAQ/navigation, service categories, and credible copy about customized cleaning plans. The biggest opportunity is to make the first screen easier to act on by replacing the visitor-blocking information popup with a clearer consultation request path and by making facility types, phone, and email easier to scan immediately.

## What is already working

- The page has a recognizable Southern Tide Services brand identity with the blue logo, bright blue text, neon-lime accents, and a high-contrast cleaning glove/sponge hero image.
- Commercial cleaning intent is clear, including office cleaning, medical office cleaning, bank cleaning, school cleaning, retail cleaning, janitorial services, common areas, and commercial cleaning.
- The phone number is visible at the top and repeated in the page body.
- The site already has a consultation link, quote language, contact form, and email link.
- The About copy explains that service plans are customized instead of sold as one-size-fits-all packages.
- The Why Choose Us section has useful trust cues: trained cleaners, environmentally friendly cleaning, satisfaction guarantee, and availability across days, evenings, and weekends.
- The services section has usable source images and service-card structure that can be improved without replacing the visual identity.
- The footer includes office hours, customer service availability, contact information, and service-area cities.

## Biggest conversion issues

- A popup appears over the first screen and dims the entire page, so a first-time visitor sees the form before they can understand the company.
- The top of the page is wordy: the brand, slogan, long service list, navigation, hero message, popup, quote button, and call prompt compete with each other.
- The quote path is present but not focused. Visitors see Get a Quote, Submit, Consultation, Contact, phone, and popup form language without one primary next step.
- The Wix promo banner at the very top makes the page feel less polished than the service quality the copy is trying to communicate.
- The hero headline is memorable, but it does not immediately explain service area, facility types, or what to do next.
- Facility types are listed, but not grouped into a quick decision path for offices, medical offices, banks, schools, retail, common areas, and post-construction needs.
- The About section has good substance but uses long copy that could be tightened and connected more directly to a consultation.
- The contact form asks for contact information but does not prompt for facility type, city, cleaning frequency, or schedule needs.
- The footer still includes Wix-created language, which weakens trust at the bottom of the conversion path.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements

- Replace the Wix promo strip with a same-height local service strip that says Southern Tide serves Alabama and Florida Gulf Coast commercial facilities.
- Keep the logo, phone box, navigation, blue/lime palette, cleaning-glove hero image, service sections, review, contact form, and footer structure so the page still feels like the same site.
- Convert the popup idea into an above-the-fold consultation card that asks for facility type, schedule, and cleaning needs without dimming the full page.
- Make the primary first-screen action Get a Quote, with Call 251-929-4853 as the secondary action beside it.
- Tighten the hero copy around customized commercial cleaning plans rather than a generic information request.
- Keep the Why Choose Us section but make the four benefits easier to scan.
- Add service-area and facility-type language near the top, not only in deeper page sections.
- Rewrite contact form placeholder copy so visitors know what information to send: facility type, city, cleaning frequency, and best contact method.
- Preserve the original source images and service cards while making captions and service grouping clearer.

## SEO basics to tighten

- Use a descriptive homepage title such as “Southern Tide Services | Gulf Coast Commercial Cleaning.”
- Add a meta description that mentions commercial cleaning, office cleaning, medical office cleaning, bank cleaning, school cleaning, retail cleaning, Alabama, and Northwest Florida.
- Use one clear H1 that pairs service and location intent, such as “Gulf Coast Commercial Cleaning Plans.”
- Add service-area language above the fold: Foley, Gulf Shores, Orange Beach, Fairhope, Daphne, Pensacola, Perdido Key, and the Alabama/Florida Gulf Coast.
- Add or improve alt text for the logo, hero cleaning image, and each service-card photo.
- Make the phone number a consistent `tel:` link and the email a consistent `mailto:` link.
- Consider FAQ copy for recurring office cleaning, medical office cleaning expectations, cleaning frequency, after-hours service, post-construction cleanup, and quote timing.

## Realistic implementation angle

This is a focused Wix homepage conversion pass, not a full rebuild. Southern Tide can keep the same one-page structure, logo, blue/lime color direction, hero image, service cards, review section, contact form, and footer. The practical work is copy hierarchy, first-screen CTA routing, form guidance, local SEO basics, popup handling, and trust-cue placement. Implementation should be separate from the Page Profit Check audit because it would require editing the Wix page, form behavior, navigation, and SEO settings.

## Offer fit

This is a good fit for a $99 Page Profit Check because the site already has enough content and brand assets to diagnose the highest-impact conversion issues. The audit can show a practical before/after direction for first impression, consultation flow, facility-type clarity, and trust signals. If Southern Tide wants the changes implemented afterward, that should be scoped separately from the $99 audit.
