# Vero Beach Handyman LLC - Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Vero Beach Handyman LLC is a strong fit for a focused Page Profit Check because the site already has the basics a homeowner needs: a clear local handyman name, phone number, email, address, free estimate promise, no service fee promise, and a broad service list.

The main opportunity is not a full redesign. It is a surgical cleanup of the existing Wix-style homepage so the first screen, service categories, trust cues, and estimate path are easier to scan. The prototype keeps the recognizable white header, turquoise callout, black navigation bar, dark gray content canvas, gray service panels, cyan headings, red phone number, original service photos, wood call banner, and teal footer.

## What is already working
- The phone number is highly visible and repeated: `772-321-1918`.
- The site clearly promises **Free Estimates** and **Never a Service Fee**.
- The business is locally positioned as a Vero Beach handyman.
- The homepage includes useful service categories: Interior Work, Exterior Work, Kitchen & Bath, Miscellaneous, and Property Caretaker / Maintenance Service.
- The service list is concrete and buyer-relevant: drywall repair, painting, carpentry, fixtures, appliances, minor plumbing leaks, gutters, siding, decks, fencing, pressure washing, tile, caulking, grout, disposals, vent fans, furniture assembly, and more.
- The site includes direct contact details: email, phone, and address.
- The original logo and small service images create a recognizable local business look.
- The older Wix layout has a clear visual identity: white masthead, teal estimate card, black nav, gray panels, cyan headings, and red phone emphasis.

## Biggest conversion issues
1. **Critical: the first screen is visually memorable but the next step is not action-oriented enough.** The phone is large, but there is no clear button-style “Request a Free Estimate” path.

2. **Critical: the service list is helpful but dense.** Homeowners need to quickly identify whether their repair belongs under interior, exterior, kitchen/bath, maintenance, or miscellaneous work.

3. **High: trust cues are present but not packaged.** Free estimates, no service fee, local service, and broad repair capability are there, but they are not grouped into a fast top-of-page trust strip.

4. **High: the right-column welcome copy is long.** The copy has a good message, but the page can make the benefit clearer with shorter paragraphs, trust chips, and a repeated CTA.

5. **High: the contact/quote flow could be more specific.** A visitor should know whether to call, text, or email photos/details, and the preferred next action should appear more than once.

6. **Medium: Wix/template residue reduces polish.** The visible Wix promotional banner and builder-style footer credits make the business feel less finished than the service offering.

7. **Medium: SEO structure is thin.** The live page title is generic, headings are limited, image alt text is sparse, and the homepage could use a clear local H1 plus a concise meta description.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Keep the same site geometry and visual DNA, but turn the top strip into a useful local trust/CTA strip.
- Add a visible **Request a Free Estimate** action near the main phone number.
- Preserve the large phone number and “Call or text” message.
- Group the services into cleaner scan paths without removing the original categories.
- Add quick trust cues near the welcome copy: free estimate, no service fee, local repairs, and call/text routing.
- Make the service copy slightly shorter and easier to scan while keeping the same jobs listed.
- Keep the Property Caretaker and Maintenance section, but tighten the copy and preserve the house image.
- Preserve the bottom wood-style phone CTA and make it clickable.
- Replace obvious Wix/template residue with business-specific footer language.
- Add basic SEO elements in the prototype: focused title, meta description, one H1, section headings, and descriptive image alt text.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a focused title such as `Vero Beach Handyman LLC | Home Repairs & Improvements in Vero Beach, FL`.
- Add a concise meta description mentioning Vero Beach, handyman repairs, interior/exterior work, kitchen/bath repairs, property maintenance, free estimates, and no service fee.
- Use one clear H1 in the hero area.
- Use semantic H2s for Interior Work, Exterior Work, Kitchen & Bath, Miscellaneous, Property Caretaker, and Contact.
- Add descriptive alt text for every logo/photo image.
- Add LocalBusiness or HomeAndConstructionBusiness schema later after confirming exact business name, service area, address preference, and contact formatting.
- If Wix remains the platform, update page SEO settings and reduce visible builder residue where the plan allows.

## Realistic implementation angle
This should be positioned as a focused homepage cleanup, not a rebrand. The practical implementation path is to keep the current Wix site style and original assets, then improve the first screen, CTA routing, service grouping, body copy hierarchy, footer contact polish, and basic SEO fields.

A separate implementation sprint would likely be modest because the business does not need new photography, a new brand system, or a complex multi-page redesign. The biggest lift is reorganizing the existing homepage content and improving the mobile version after the desktop direction is approved.
