# Scott's Suds and Softwash — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Scott's Suds and Softwash is a good fit for a surgical Page Profit Check because the site already has the right local-service ingredients: service-area language, phone, email, quote CTA, before/after photos, Facebook proof, and clear residential/commercial/routine/specialty service categories. The main opportunity is not a redesign. It is making the first screen, trust cues, and quote path easier to understand while preserving the clean Wix softwash look.

**Recommendation:** keep the white/light-gray Wix structure, blue CTA buttons, dark neon Scott's Suds logo, pressure-washer divider graphics, before/after cleaning photos, and four-column blue services band. Tighten the page around one action: **Get a Quote Now**.

## What is already working
- The business name, softwash/pressure washing category, and Providence Forge location are visible.
- The header already includes a phone number, Facebook link, navigation, and a quote CTA.
- The homepage uses real before/after cleaning images rather than generic stock photography.
- The intro already names important service areas: Charles City, New Kent, and Williamsburg.
- The site already explains residential, commercial, routine, and specialty/solar panel cleaning.
- The blue CTA color, logo, pressure washer graphics, and simple centered Wix layout are recognizable and worth preserving.
- The footer contains the key contact details: phone, location, and email.

## Biggest conversion issues
1. **Critical: the first screen does not make the quote path obvious enough.** The quote button exists, but the hero is dominated by logo space and the next step is not repeated in a structured way.
2. **Critical: trust cues are present but scattered.** Service area, before/after proof, Facebook presence, and service categories are separated across the page instead of packaged into a fast confidence path.
3. **High: the page has avoidable Wix/template residue.** The visible Wix promotional banner and awkward mid-page footer placement make the business feel less polished than the service quality suggests.
4. **High: the lower service section is buried after a large blank gap.** Visitors should not have to scroll through unused space to understand residential, commercial, routine, and specialty cleaning options.
5. **High: the quote path does not explain what happens next.** Homeowners and commercial buyers need quick prompts: what surfaces can be cleaned, what areas are served, and how to request an estimate.
6. **Medium: copy needs small grammar and clarity fixes.** Examples include “experts takes,” “can help you made,” and missing punctuation in service copy.
7. **Medium: SEO basics need a clearer page structure.** The page title is useful, but the homepage needs one stronger H1, improved meta description, descriptive alt text, and service-area wording that includes Providence Forge.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Replace the Wix ad strip with a business-specific local service strip while keeping the same 50px top rhythm.
- Keep the original header/nav shape, but make the quote CTA and phone number cleaner and more consistent.
- Preserve the dark neon logo hero, then clarify the local promise and service areas in the intro copy.
- Reuse the original pressure-washer divider and before/after image composites for instant visual continuity.
- Convert the right-column Facebook embed area into a Facebook-style proof and estimate card that explains the next step.
- Add quick service cues for residential, commercial, routine care, and solar/specialty cleaning.
- Move contact/footer behavior to a more logical bottom position while retaining phone, location, and email.
- Keep the blue “What We Do” section, but make it easier to scan and remove the unnecessary blank spacer.
- Add descriptive image alt text and a tighter title/meta description in the prototype.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a focused title such as `Softwash and Pressure Washing in Providence Forge, VA | Scott's Suds and Softwash`.
- Use one clear H1 with softwash, pressure washing, and service-area intent.
- Include Providence Forge alongside Charles City, New Kent, and Williamsburg in visible copy.
- Add descriptive alt text to the logo, divider, and before/after cleaning images.
- Give each service type a proper H2/H3 hierarchy: Residential Services, Commercial Services, Routine Services, Specialty Cleaning.
- Consider LocalBusiness or HomeAndConstructionBusiness schema after confirming the final business name, service area, and preferred public address details.
- Clean up grammar in service copy before publishing any live edits.

## Realistic implementation angle
This should be a focused Wix homepage improvement, not a full rebuild. The practical path is to keep the existing visual identity and assets, then adjust the top strip, header CTA, hero copy, proof/estimate section, service-section spacing, contact/footer placement, and SEO fields. A small implementation phase would likely be manageable because the site already has the images and core copy needed.
