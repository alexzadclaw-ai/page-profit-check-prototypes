# Salty Sea Pressure Washing LLC Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take

Salty Sea already has a real local-service foundation: service-area language, phone number, email, quote form, social links, service explanations, and visual proof. The main opportunity is not a full redesign. It is to tighten the first screen and quote path so visitors understand the core offer faster: roof washing, house washing, flat-surface cleaning, gutters, no-obligation quotes, and service in Seabrook plus nearby Houston-area communities.

## What is already working

- The business name and logo are prominent.
- The phone number and email are present in the hero.
- The page names local service areas clearly: Seabrook, Pasadena, La Porte, Deer Park, Houston, Crosby, Pearland, and surrounding areas.
- The quote form already exists and has useful fields.
- The page explains multiple services, including roof cleaning, flat surface cleaning, house washing, gutter cleaning, and bin cleaning.
- Facebook and Instagram links are visible.
- The black, white, royal-blue, red, and yellow Wix look is memorable and recognizable.

## Biggest conversion issues

- The hero says the business name and service area, but the highest-value services are not immediately summarized.
- The first call to action is split between the phone card, hero contact text, and a form much lower on the page.
- The quote form is useful but long, and it does not tell visitors what details help Salty Sea quote quickly.
- Trust cues are present indirectly, but they are not grouped near the main decision point.
- Navigation and service content feel like a long one-page Wix layout, so visitors must scan before knowing what to do next.
- There is a large blank lower section that does not help visitors take the next step.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements

- Add a direct hero CTA: “Get a no-obligation quote” plus “Call or text now.”
- Keep the coastal/local identity, logo, blue cards, black hero, gradient nav, yellow fields, and original service images.
- Add a simple service strip in the hero: Roof washing, House washing, Flat surface cleaning, Gutters.
- Add a short form helper note explaining that service, address, surface/stain details, and photos speed up the quote.
- Add small trust cues near the form: No-obligation quotes, HOA + commercial, Seabrook-area local.
- Tighten service copy so each service row leads with the service name and benefit.
- Add a bottom contact card so the page has a clear next step after the service list.

## SEO basics to tighten

- Use one clear page title around “Pressure Washing in Seabrook, TX | Salty Sea Pressure Washing LLC.”
- Add a meta description focused on Seabrook, Pasadena, La Porte, Deer Park, Houston, Crosby, Pearland, and nearby areas.
- Use service headings with local intent: roof washing, house washing, driveway cleaning, patio cleaning, gutter cleaning.
- Add descriptive alt text for the logo and each service/result image.
- Add internal anchors or section IDs for quote, services, reviews, and contact.
- Add a concise reviews/testimonials section to the home page or a stronger preview linking to the reviews page.
- Consider removing the Wix ad banner on a paid plan if budget allows, because it distracts from the local service CTA.

## Realistic implementation angle

This is a surgical Wix-page improvement, not a rebuild. The practical path is to keep the current visual identity and page structure, then update the hero copy, CTA links, quote-form helper text, service-row copy, image alt text, and bottom contact section. If Salty Sea wants this implemented in Wix, the rough ROM is a few focused hours depending on access, form behavior, and how much of the current page is editable versus generated by Wix components.

## Offer fit

This is a strong fit for a $99 Page Profit Check because the site is not broken. The value is in diagnosis and visual direction: identify the highest-impact conversion fixes, show how the same page can be clarified, and give the owner a low-pressure before/after reference. Actual Wix changes should be scoped separately.
