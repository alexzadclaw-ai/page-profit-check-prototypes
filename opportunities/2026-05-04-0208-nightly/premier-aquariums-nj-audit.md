# Premier Aquariums — Page Profit Check Audit

Premier Aquariums already has a recognizable Wix/aquatic visual identity: a clean white header, large Raleway wordmark, simple nav, full-width reef/eel hero image, and alternating aquarium photo/text sections. The conversion opportunity is not a wholesale redesign; it is a surgical cleanup that keeps the same aquatic language while making the first screen answer: what they do, who they serve, why to trust Karl, and how to request the free consultation.

| Finding | Severity | Why it matters |

| The hero says the business name and a broad tagline, but does not immediately state the service mix or provide a specific next step. | Critical | A new visitor has to scroll/read before knowing whether to call for installation, maintenance, or a move. |

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. **Rewrite the hero around the service and CTA.** Keep the big Premier Aquariums identity, but add: custom aquarium installation, scheduled maintenance, safe moves, reef/saltwater/freshwater, New Jersey, and a free consultation CTA.
2. **Move trust above the fold.** Add a compact proof line and trust strip: owner-led by Karl Tulp, 15+ years, free consultations, NJ service area, recurring maintenance.
3. **Convert paragraphs into service cards.** Organize around buyer intent: new installation, scheduled maintenance, children's/waiting-room aquariums, moving services.
4. **Explain the next steps.** Add a simple three-step flow: free consultation, plan the aquarium, install/maintain.
5. **Clean contact and footer friction.** Use direct `mailto:` subject lines, repeat the phone CTA, and remove visible Wix/template leftovers from the improved homepage.
6. **Tighten page metadata.** Use a service/location page title and meta description.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Replace `Home | Premier Aquariums` with a descriptive title such as `Premier Aquariums | Custom Aquarium Installation & Maintenance in New Jersey`.
- Add a meta description focused on custom aquarium installation, scheduled maintenance, aquarium moving, reef/saltwater/freshwater care, and New Jersey.
- Use one clear H1 and H2s for `About Us`, `Our Services`, `Installation & Maintenance`, `Children's Aquariums`, `Moving Services`, and contact intent.
- Add descriptive alt text to aquarium images instead of filename-style alt text.
- Consider dedicated local/service copy for Morris County, New Jersey, New York moves, reef aquarium maintenance, freshwater aquarium maintenance, and waiting-room aquariums.
