# NG's Group Transportation — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
NG's Group Transportation already has a recognizable freight/delivery visual identity: black header, hot-orange quote blocks, a large transportation hero image, and simple Wix-style service sections. The main opportunity is not a full redesign; it is a surgical cleanup that makes the page feel like the same site while removing trust-damaging template residue and turning the generic moving-quote flow into a freight/cargo quote path.

Priority: **A-**  
Location: **Bay Area / South San Francisco, CA**  
Vertical: **Cargo delivery and freight transportation**

## What is already working
- Strong first impression from the transportation hero image with airplane, cargo ship, trucks, and stacked boxes.
- Simple high-contrast brand palette: black, hot orange, white, and sky blue.
- Phone number is visible on the homepage: **650-238-8289**.
- Core services are present: local delivery, long-distance delivery, storage, and import/export.
- Footer has real contact details: **info@ngsgroupinc.com** and **320 Corey Way, South San Francisco, CA 94080**.
- The page has a usable Wix/builder rhythm that should be preserved rather than replaced with a premium-looking generic design.

## Biggest conversion issues
1. **Generic moving-language CTA conflicts with the business category — Critical**  
   The page sells cargo delivery and freight transportation, but the quote CTA says “Get a free moving quote in seconds” and the form asks for “Number of Rooms.” That creates buyer confusion for freight/cargo prospects.

2. **Trust-damaging template leftovers are visible — Critical**  
   The homepage includes a fake testimonial (“I'm a testimonial. Click to edit me...”), a placeholder quote paragraph, “Proudly created with Wix.com,” and a placeholder license number. The contact page is worse: “Fly Right Movers Main Office,” fake phone/email, and a template address.

3. **Trust proof appears too late and too thin — High**  
   “Drivers You Can Trust,” “Cargo Logistics,” and “95% Referral Rate” appear down-page. These should be moved closer to the hero so a first-time buyer gets reassurance before deciding whether to contact.

4. **Services are listed as generic categories instead of buyer task paths — High**  
   The service names are relevant, but the copy is broad and repetitive. Freight buyers need to quickly identify: local delivery, truckload/long-distance, storage, or import/export support.

5. **Next step is unclear — High**  
   The page asks users to start online, but it does not explain what details NG's Group needs, what happens after the request, or whether calling/emailing is also acceptable.

6. **Mobile first screen is heavy and CTA flow can be easier — Medium**  
   The original hero is visually memorable, but the CTA and trust signals need a cleaner sequence on small screens.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Keep the same black/orange/sky-blue freight-brand look and the original transportation hero asset.
- Replace “moving quote” language with **“Request freight quote”** and **“Get Your Free Freight Quote.”**
- Move trust proof directly under the hero: Bay Area based, short & long distance, cargo logistics, and 95% referral rate.
- Reorganize service cards by buyer intent:
  - Local Delivery
  - Truckload & Long Distance
  - Storage Services
  - Import & Export Support
- Replace the generic moving form with freight-relevant fields: pickup ZIP/city, destination ZIP/city, requested date, service needed, name, phone, and cargo details.
- Remove or avoid homepage template residue: fake testimonial, placeholder quote copy, fake license number, Wix attribution, and moving-room field.
- Add a simple “What Happens Next” block so prospects know how the quote request is handled.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use one clear homepage title around Bay Area cargo delivery and freight transportation.
- Add a specific meta description mentioning South San Francisco, cargo delivery, local freight, storage, and import/export support.
- Keep one primary H1 focused on the service/location rather than two separate H1 lines.
- Add service-specific headings that match buyer search language: “Bay Area Local Delivery,” “Long-Distance Freight,” “Storage Services,” and “Import & Export Support.”
- Make contact details consistent across homepage and contact page; the current contact page has unrelated template business data.
