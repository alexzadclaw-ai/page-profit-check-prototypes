# Page Profit Check Audit — Keep It Greeen, Inc.

## Quick take
Keep It Greeen, Inc. already has a recognizable green landscaping site with real work photos, local Winston-Salem contact details, free-estimate language, and strong owner-operated trust copy. The biggest opportunity is not a full redesign. It is a surgical Wix cleanup: keep the same green/tan landscaping look, but replace placeholder copy and architecture-template service labels with a clearer estimate path, licensed/insured proof near the top, and service categories that match what customers actually need.

## What is already working
- The site clearly belongs to a real landscaping company in Winston-Salem, NC.
- Phone numbers, email, office address, and free-estimate language are available.
- The site has strong trust copy: small family-run company, Fernando personally oversees projects, fully licensed and insured, including pesticide application.
- The existing work-photo gallery gives the page local proof and a practical landscaping feel.
- The original green/tan palette and large yard photo create enough brand DNA for a cleanup rather than a replacement.

## Biggest conversion issues

### Critical
1. **Placeholder Wix copy is visible above the service photos.** The homepage still includes text like “I'm a paragraph. Click here to add your own text...” which makes the business look unfinished.
2. **The first-screen message does not clearly explain the service offer.** The current top area has phone numbers and a seasonal promo, but the main message does not quickly say “landscaping and lawn care in Winston-Salem” with a clear estimate action.
3. **Wrong/template service labels damage credibility.** Labels like “Architectural Modelling,” “Construction Management,” and “Preconstruction Planning” do not match landscaping and can make visitors doubt the site is maintained.

### High
1. **Licensed/insured and owner-operated proof is present but not organized as a near-top trust strip.** These points should be visible immediately after the hero.
2. **Services are partly buried and partly generic.** Real services are listed lower on the page, but they need to be grouped by buyer intent: routine lawn care, landscape refresh, seasonal/project work.
3. **The next step is not specific enough.** Visitors should know to call/text/email for a free estimate, what to send, and that Fernando can walk the property.
4. **The contact/source URL provided as `/contact-us` returns a 404.** The contact content exists on the homepage, but the public contact path should either work or redirect.

### Medium
1. Phone numbers should be consistently formatted and tap-to-call on mobile.
2. The footer still says “Proudly created with Wix.com” and includes generic social/template residue.
3. SEO basics can be tightened with a stronger title/meta description and headings that include Winston-Salem landscaping terms.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Replace placeholder paragraphs with specific copy about lawn maintenance, cleanups, mulch, pine needles, planting, Bobcat work, snow removal, and free estimates.
- Change the hero/promo box into a clear estimate card: “Landscaping and lawn care in Winston-Salem, NC.”
- Keep the original green/tan header, hero garden photo, and service-photo grid so the page still feels familiar.
- Move trust proof directly under the hero: licensed and insured, pesticide application, owner-operated, free estimates, local service area.
- Reframe services around real customer needs and remove architecture/construction-template labels.
- Add a simple “what happens next” flow: call/text/email, walk the property, receive a free estimate.
- Clean the footer/contact area so it feels business-specific and removes template leftovers.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title like: `Keep It Greeen, Inc. | Landscaping Company in Winston-Salem, NC`.
- Add a concise meta description mentioning licensed/insured landscaping, lawn care, Winston-Salem, Lewisville, and Clemmons.
- Use visible headings for “Services,” “About Us,” “What Happens Next,” and “Contact Us.”
- Include service terms in body copy: lawn maintenance, spring/fall cleanups, leaf removal, mulch, pine needles, planting, Bobcat work, snow removal.
- Fix or redirect `https://www.keepitgreeen.com/contact-us` if it is meant to be the contact page.
- Keep NAP consistent: Keep It Greeen, Inc.; 5811 Fox Point Circle, Winston-Salem, NC 27104; 336-409-1401; 336-409-0993; keepitgreeen@outlook.com.

No premium redesign is needed.
