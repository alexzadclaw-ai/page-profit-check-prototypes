# Reform Painting Services — Bathrooms Page Audit

## Quick take
Reform already has a recognizable Wix contractor look: pale gray page frame, centered Poppins headings, a simple nav/header, square bathroom gallery photos, and a black closing call-to-action band. The bathroom page’s biggest issue is not design ambition — it is trust. The live page still shows template copy, a mismatched page title, vague CTA language, and almost no bathroom-specific reason to request an estimate.

The Solution A prototype keeps the existing site rhythm and identity but turns the page into a believable bathroom painting/remodeling lead page: specific hero copy, trust cues near the top, service paths organized by bathroom project needs, a clear four-step estimate flow, and a cleaner footer/contact path.

## What is already working
- The site has a real business identity: Reform Painting Services, phone number, Acworth/Georgia presence, social links, and a contact page.
- The existing bathroom page has strong visual bones: large white/gray canvas, centered typography, square image grid, and a bold dark CTA strip.
- The six bathroom images create a useful project-page feel even though the copy is unfinished.
- The contact page already gives visitors multiple ways to act: form, phone, email, and project description field.
- The About page has usable trust material: family-owned, Northwest Atlanta based, serving Georgia, rebrand from Norm Potter Painting, and a simple process.

## Biggest conversion issues
1. **Template copy is visible on the live page.**  
   Severity: Critical. The line “I'm a paragraph. Click here to add your own text and edit me.” immediately signals an unfinished site and can make visitors question whether the business is active or detail-oriented.

2. **The browser/page title says “Bathrooms | M&B Remodeling.”**  
   Severity: Critical. A mismatched business name is a major trust hit, especially on a contractor page where visitors are deciding who to let into their home.

3. **The hero does not explain what Reform does for bathroom projects.**  
   Severity: High. “Bathrooms” plus a generic sentence does not tell visitors whether this is bathroom painting, remodeling, cabinet refinishing, trim work, moisture-resistant coatings, or a quote page.

4. **The main CTA is generic and under-supported.**  
   Severity: High. “Get A Free Estimate” is useful, but the page does not say what happens after clicking or what information Reform needs.

5. **Trust proof is buried or absent on the bathroom page.**  
   Severity: High. Family-owned, Acworth-based, Georgia service area, company history, and the simple process are present elsewhere but not near the decision point on this page.

6. **Services are not organized by buyer intent.**  
   Severity: High. A bathroom visitor may care about walls/ceilings, vanities, trim, prep, finish durability, or small refresh vs. larger project scope; none of that appears.

7. **Footer/template residue dilutes professionalism.**  
   Severity: Medium. The Wix credit and repeated social icons are not as damaging as the placeholder paragraph, but they reinforce the unfinished-template feeling.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Replace the placeholder paragraph with bathroom-specific value: paint, prep, finish guidance, and refresh work for bathrooms.
- Change the page title and SEO metadata to Reform Painting Services, not M&B Remodeling.
- Keep the same visual fingerprint: simple Wix header, Poppins headings, square image grid, light gray canvas, black CTA band, and direct estimate button.
- Add a trust strip immediately below the intro: family-owned, Acworth/Georgia, company history, and direct phone estimate path.
- Reframe services by bathroom project task: walls/ceilings, trim/doors, vanity/cabinet refresh, prep/repair, finish/color guidance.
- Add a simple “what happens next” section based on the existing About page process: call/request quote, discuss scope, choose colors/finish, schedule the work.
- Clean the closing CTA and footer so the final impression is business-specific instead of template-specific.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Update the title tag from `Bathrooms | M&B Remodeling` to something like `Bathroom Painting & Refreshes in Acworth, GA | Reform Painting Services`.
- Add a meta description that mentions bathroom painting, refresh projects, Acworth/Northwest Atlanta/Georgia, and free estimates.
- Use one clear H1 focused on buyer intent: “Bathroom Painting & Refreshes in Georgia.”
- Add descriptive alt text to bathroom project images rather than leaving them blank.
- Link the CTA consistently to `/contact` and include phone/email text on the page for local relevance.
- Remove or replace generic template copy so Google and visitors see real service content.

## Realistic implementation angle
This does not require rebuilding the whole Wix site. The practical path is a focused cleanup of the existing Bathrooms page:

1. Update page SEO/title settings.
2. Replace the placeholder paragraph with concise bathroom-service copy.
3. Keep the gallery and page structure, but add trust/service/process blocks around it.
4. Update button labels and repeat the estimate CTA in the dark band.
5. Clean footer wording and avoid repeated/empty social-link clutter where possible.

The prototype is intentionally a surgical upgrade, not a new brand system.

## Offer fit
This is a strong Page Profit Check fit because the fix is easy to understand visually: the owner can compare the current unfinished page to the cleaned-up version and immediately see why visitors would trust it more. The $99 mini-audit is justified by the clear template residue and missed lead-flow opportunity. If Reform wants help implementing the changes in Wix, a small cleanup sprint would likely be appropriate after the mini-audit.
