# Park's Plumbing — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Park's Plumbing already feels like a real local Arlington plumbing company: plain language, family-owned positioning, visible phone number, service area, license number, and a handful of testimonials. The biggest opportunity is not a full redesign. It is a surgical cleanup that keeps the older Wix/local-plumber look while making the first screen answer three buyer questions faster: **Can they solve my specific plumbing problem? How do I reach them right now? Can I trust them?**

## Biggest conversion issues
| Finding | Severity | Why it matters |
|---|---:|---|
| The first screen says “Welcome!” before it says what urgent buyer problem Park's Plumbing solves. | Critical | Plumbing visitors often arrive with a leak, clog, no-hot-water issue, or fixture failure. The current hero delays the service/action match. |
| The call/text action is present but not structured as a primary next step with click-friendly options. | Critical | The phone number appears as text-style content; mobile users should immediately see “Call” and “Text for a free estimate.” |
| Trust proof is buried below the service list. | High | Family-owned, licensed, local service area, residential/commercial coverage, and testimonials should reassure visitors before they scan details. |
| Services are listed flat and densely, with slash bullets and uneven spacing. | High | Buyers scan by situation: emergency repair, water heater, drain/sewer, fixtures/remodeling, camera inspection, piping. The current list requires interpretation. |
| Contact page has a form-state fragment (“Your details were sent successfully!” in fetched content) and a plain “Send” without context. | High | Any form/template residue can make a small service business feel stale or unattended. |
| Footer includes Wix creator credit and 2016 timestamp. | Medium | It reinforces the impression that the site has not been maintained recently. |
| SEO title/contact title are thin (`Contact | 98223`) and headings are generic. | Medium | Local plumbing search pages benefit from clear city/service/phone intent in titles and headings. |

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Replace the generic hero emphasis with “Plumbing help in Arlington & Snohomish County” while preserving the original “Welcome!” feel.
2. Add clear primary actions: **Call 425-870-4940** and **Text for a free estimate**.
3. Move trust signals close to the top: family owned, licensed, Snohomish County service area, residential + commercial.
4. Reorganize services into buyer task paths:
   - Leaks, clogs & urgent repairs
   - Water heater repair & installation
   - Drain cleaning & sewer camera
   - Fixtures, disposals & remodel plumbing
   - Water piping repair & installation
   - Commercial plumbing help
5. Add a simple “What happens next” block so visitors know what to send by phone/text and how service gets scheduled.
6. Keep the original Wix visual DNA: dark teal header, tall banner, logo, white nav, red active tab/buttons, Arial typography, and original plumbing photos.
7. Remove visible template residue from the improved page footer/contact path.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a local-service title such as `Park's Plumbing | Arlington & Snohomish County Plumbing`.
- Add a meta description naming Arlington, Snohomish County, water heaters, drain cleaning, fixtures, leaks/clogs, sewer camera inspections, and call/text next step.
- Use one clear H1 on the homepage: `Plumbing help in Arlington & Snohomish County`.
- Keep supporting headings for Water Heater Repair, Drain Cleaning, Sewer Camera Inspection, Fixture Repair, Water Piping, and Commercial Plumbing.
- Make phone links clickable with `tel:` and mobile text links with `sms:`.
- Rename generic contact title from `Contact | 98223` to a local/service-oriented contact title if implemented on the live site.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The quickest realistic implementation would be to update the existing Wix homepage sections:
- keep the same header/banner/logo/nav shell;
- rewrite the first content block;
- add a top trust strip;
- replace the dense service list with task-based cards;
- add a “what happens next” row;
- simplify contact/footer residue;
- tune title/meta/H1.

No new photography or identity work is required for the first pass. The current public assets are enough.
