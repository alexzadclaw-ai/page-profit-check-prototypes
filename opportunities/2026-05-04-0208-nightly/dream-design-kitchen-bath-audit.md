# Dream Design Kitchen & Bath — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Dream Design Kitchen & Bath has a clean Wix remodeling layout, real kitchen/bath imagery, a recognizable logo system, and an obvious phone/contact path. The conversion problem is severe because the About page still contains multiple Wix placeholder paragraphs, Wix social links, a stale/incorrect page title (`About | M&B Remodeling`), and no credible explanation of why a Jacksonville-area homeowner should book a consultation.

The Solution A prototype keeps the existing visual DNA — white logo/header, gray section rhythm, large kitchen image, checkerboard process grid, dark final CTA — while replacing placeholders with buyer-ready process copy, local trust details, stronger consultation CTAs, and clearer next steps.

## What is already working
- Strong visual base: remodeling photos, gray/white/charcoal palette, and clean spacing already feel appropriate for kitchen and bath work.
- Phone number is highly visible in the header: `904.999.0652`.
- The contact page includes useful trust/context details: service areas, address, email, phone, and free in-home consultation language.
- There is already a simple CTA path through Contact and Book Online.
- The existing process section has a good layout idea; it just needs real content.

## Biggest conversion issues
1. **Critical — Wix placeholder copy is live.** The About, Process, step descriptions, and Quality Guarantee sections repeatedly say “I’m a paragraph…” and include Wix editor instructions. This is direct trust damage.
2. **Critical — Wix template residue appears in the guarantee/footer area.** The page contains Wix promotional copy and Wix social links, making the business look unfinished.
3. **High — first screen does not explain the local service offer.** The headline says “About Dream Design,” but does not clarify kitchen/bath remodeling, service area, consultation value, or next step.
4. **High — weak CTA hierarchy.** “Get A Free Estimate” appears late, while the header prompt is generic. The free in-home consultation path from the contact page is not brought forward.
5. **High — process steps are named but not useful.** The section labels are good, but the descriptions do not help a homeowner understand what happens next.
6. **High — trust proof is buried or missing near the top.** Service areas, local address, email, and free consultation language are available on Contact but absent from the About page’s top conversion area.
7. **Medium — SEO basics are loose.** The About page title references “M&B Remodeling,” and the visible page copy does not target Jacksonville kitchen/bath remodeling intent.
8. **Medium — mobile conversion path can be easier.** The live page has a long scroll before the main CTA, plus a cookie banner can obstruct the process grid.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Replace all placeholder paragraphs with concise, credible copy about kitchen/bath remodeling, design planning, estimates, in-home consultations, and finishing details.
- Upgrade the top section from “About Dream Design” to a buyer-intent headline: Jacksonville kitchen and bath remodeling with a free in-home consultation path.
- Add a trust strip directly under the intro: kitchen+bath focus, local service areas, free consultation, and clear next steps.
- Keep the existing large kitchen image and process checkerboard, but rewrite each process tile with practical homeowner-facing language.
- Add a service-intent section that separates kitchen remodeling, bathroom remodeling, design guidance, and consultation/estimate path.
- Clean the guarantee section so it communicates care, communication, and scope clarity instead of Wix template language.
- Replace the late generic CTA with a stronger dark consultation block: share project → schedule visit → plan scope.
- Improve mobile with stacked sections and sticky bottom actions for Free Consultation / Call Now.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Change the page title from `About | M&B Remodeling` to something like `Dream Design Kitchen & Bath | Jacksonville Kitchen & Bath Remodeling`.
- Add a meta description that mentions Dream Design Kitchen & Bath, Jacksonville-area homeowners, kitchen remodeling, bathroom remodeling, and free in-home consultations.
- Use a clear H1 with local service intent instead of a generic About heading.
- Add visible service-area copy for Duval County, St. Johns County, Clay County, and Nassau County.
- Remove Wix social/template links from the footer so crawlable page signals match the real business.
- Make the contact CTA descriptive: “Schedule a Free In-Home Consultation” instead of only “Get A Free Estimate.”

## Realistic implementation angle
This is not a full redesign. The fastest profitable cleanup is a surgical Wix content/layout pass:

1. Keep the current logo, header, gray section palette, kitchen photo, process grid, and dark CTA rhythm.
2. Replace every placeholder paragraph and Wix promotional section with final business copy.
3. Pull existing contact-page facts into the top of the About page.
4. Update title/meta/H1/CTA labels.
5. Add a mobile sticky call/contact path if the Wix template allows it, or at minimum move the free consultation CTA above the hero image.

A focused implementation cleanup sprint should be enough; the visual structure already exists.
