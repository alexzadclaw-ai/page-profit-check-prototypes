# Pacific Paving, Inc. — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take

Pacific Paving already has a recognizable local contractor website: real logo, phone number, Oregon CCB proof, licensing language, Southern Oregon service area, and actual paving project photos. The problem is that the supplied page at `/interiors` appears to be a misplaced remodeling-template page. Its title is `Interiors | M&B Remodeling`, the body includes placeholder copy, and the image gallery does not match Pacific Paving's asphalt services.

That makes this an A-priority cleanup opportunity: keep the existing Wix contractor skeleton, but turn this page into a clear paving/asphalt estimate path.

## What is already working

- The header has strong trust basics already visible: `541-772-4616`, `Oregon CCB# 50835`, and `Licensed, Bonded & Insured`.
- The logo and top asphalt-texture strip make the site feel like the same Pacific Paving brand.
- The main navigation is simple: Home, About, Services, Work, Contact.
- Same-domain pages contain useful service language and real paving photos for residential paving, commercial paving, sealcoating, repairs, crushed rock roads, and project planning.
- The contact page includes clear contact details: White City address, email, phone, and service areas.

## Biggest conversion issues

### Critical

1. **Wrong-business / wrong-vertical page title and content.** The page title and social title are `Interiors | M&B Remodeling`, the H1 is `Interiors`, and the copy says `I'm a paragraph. Click here to add your own text and edit me.` This is severe trust damage for a paving contractor.
2. **The page does not explain Pacific Paving's services.** A visitor landing on this URL sees interior/remodeling language instead of driveways, parking lots, asphalt repairs, sealcoating, or crushed-rock work.
3. **CTA path is buried after irrelevant gallery content.** The free-estimate CTA exists, but it comes after a large unrelated gallery and uses a generic remodeling line: `Create your dream home. Tell us about your project today.`

### High

4. **Trust proof is present but not connected to the offer.** The CCB/license proof is in the header, but the page body does not reinforce why Pacific Paving is the safe local contractor to call.
5. **Services are not organized by buyer task.** The better service list exists on `/services`, but this page gives no quick path for homeowners or commercial buyers to identify their project type.
6. **Footer/template residue weakens the finish.** The target page ends with `Proudly created with Wix.com`; on a trust-damaged placeholder page, that reads like unfinished template residue.

### Medium

7. **SEO basics are missing or mismatched.** The title, H1, and meta/social titles do not align with paving services in Southern Oregon.
8. **Mobile scan path is inefficient.** On a phone, a visitor has to pass through unrelated content before the page explains what Pacific Paving can do.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements

- Replace the `Interiors / M&B Remodeling` framing with a paving-specific H1, title, and estimate CTA.
- Keep the current header skeleton: asphalt strip, logo placement, phone block, CCB/license proof, and simple nav.
- Reuse Pacific Paving's real paving photos in a three-column service gallery, preserving the existing page rhythm while changing the substance.
- Add a near-top trust strip: since 1985, CCB number, licensed/bonded/insured, free estimates, and local service area.
- Organize service cards around the buyer's task: residential paving, commercial paving, sealcoating/crackfilling, asphalt repairs, crushed rock roads, and custom design/layout.
- Make the estimate path specific: `Request a Free On-Site Estimate`, phone call, and contact-form CTA.
- Remove the Wix/remodeling leftovers from the footer and page body.

## SEO basics to tighten

- Page title should become something like: `Paving & Asphalt Services | Pacific Paving, Inc.`
- Meta description should mention Southern Oregon, Medford, Ashland, Grants Pass, residential paving, commercial paving, asphalt repairs, sealcoating, and crushed rock roads.
- H1 should describe the real offer, not `Interiors`.
- Image alt text should describe paving/asphalt work instead of blank interior-gallery images.
- Internal links should point the visitor toward Services, Work, and Contact with a clear estimate CTA.

## Realistic implementation angle

This is a surgical Wix cleanup, not a full redesign. The fastest implementation path is to repurpose the existing `/interiors` page structure or remove the page from navigation/search if it is not needed. If kept, it should be converted into a services/estimate landing page using existing Pacific Paving assets and copy from the Home, About, Services, Work, and Contact pages.
