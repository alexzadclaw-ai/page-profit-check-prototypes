## Quick take
Prairie Path Painters already has a calm, recognizable Wix site with a painterly visual identity: centered serif name, soft white space, light-blue accents, large paint-supply photography, simple service tiles, and a quote/contact section. The biggest opportunity is not a full redesign. It is a surgical cleanup that keeps the existing feel while making the free-estimate path, trust proof, service groupings, and next steps visible sooner.

## What is already working
- The site communicates the basics quickly: painting, wallpapering, clean/considerate/trustworthy, free estimates, and color consultations.
- The brand has a gentle, home-friendly personality rather than a generic contractor look.
- Phone number and email are present on the homepage.
- Service variety is broad and useful: interior/exterior painting, drywall, stain/varnish, cabinets/furniture, murals, powerwashing, windows, gutters.
- The current visual assets are strong enough to reuse instead of replacing the site with a generic premium-agency style.

## Biggest conversion issues
### Critical
1. **Free-estimate action is not strong enough above the fold.** The CTA exists, but it competes with very sparse copy and does not add enough action context for mobile users.
2. **Trust proof is buried inside a paragraph.** “Clean, Considerate, and Trustworthy” is good language, but it is not surfaced as proof points or scannable reassurance near the top.

### High
1. **Services are listed as image labels without enough buyer guidance.** The tiles look nice, but homeowners may need one extra sentence to understand which project path fits them.
2. **The quote/contact section asks for information without explaining what to send or what happens next.** A visitor sees fields, but not a simple estimate process.
3. **Footer/template residue hurts trust.** The live footer includes “Proudly created with Wix.com” and a Facebook link that appears to point to Wix rather than the business.

### Medium
1. **SEO headings could be more specific.** The title is good, but page copy could better include Illinois, free estimates, and core services in structured headings.
2. **The supplied `/contact` URL returned a 404 during lightweight browsing.** The homepage quote area works as the real contact path, but a missing contact URL can leak leads if linked elsewhere.
3. **Mobile scanability could be improved.** The service grid and quote section need stronger small-screen hierarchy and a persistent call/text path.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Keep the existing brand DNA and photo assets, but tighten the first screen around one clear promise: interior/exterior painting, wallpapering, repair, clean service, free estimate.
- Add a trust strip immediately below the hero: free estimates, clean work, color help, Illinois homes.
- Keep the six original service categories, but add short homeowner-facing descriptions to each tile.
- Add a “What Happens Next” section so visitors know to call/text, share project details, and receive estimate guidance.
- Replace the generic form-first quote area with clearer call/text/email actions and a short list of helpful estimate details.
- Clean up footer trust issues by removing Wix attribution and the unrelated Wix Facebook link.
- Add a mobile call bar for faster action on phones.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a more specific title: “Prairie Path Painters | Interior, Exterior & Wallpapering in Illinois.”
- Add a meta description that includes painting, wallpapering, drywall repair, staining, cabinet painting, and free estimates.
- Keep one clear H1 for Prairie Path Painters and use H2s for services, next steps, and quote/contact.
- Make service names readable text, not only image overlay labels.
- Fix or redirect `/contact` if that page is meant to exist.

## Realistic implementation angle

The owner can keep the existing brand, images, and simple structure while improving hierarchy, CTA repetition, service explanations, contact clarity, footer cleanup, and mobile call access. Most of the changes are copy/layout updates that should be achievable in Wix without custom backend work.
