# PINO HANDYMAN LLC Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
PINO HANDYMAN LLC has a real local service foundation: owner-led handyman experience, a compact contractor logo, direct email, direct phone, and a clear Dallas-Fort Worth service context. The main conversion issue is that visitors land on an About-style page instead of an estimate-focused first screen.

## What is already working
- Real business name, logo, owner story, phone, and email are visible.
- The page communicates DFW experience dating back to 2019.
- The copy includes strong trust language: professional handyman, licensed and insured, affordable prices, and satisfaction guaranteed.
- The original owner/truck photo gives the page authenticity that should be preserved.

## Biggest conversion issues
- The first screen reads like an About page rather than a homeowner estimate page.
- Phone and email exist, but they are not framed as the obvious next action.
- Service categories are not scannable above the fold, so visitors have to infer whether their job fits.
- Trust points are buried in paragraph copy instead of being pulled into quick proof cues.
- Navigation sends attention sideways before the visitor sees why to call.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Reposition the hero around “Call for a Free Estimate” and “Email Project Photos.”
- Add three quick service buckets: repairs, installations, and maintenance.
- Keep the compact dark contractor layout, source logo, and original owner/truck photo.
- Convert licensed, insured, satisfaction guaranteed, and serving DFW since 2019 into quick trust badges.
- Preserve the owner story below the fold for visitors who want more background.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title like “PINO HANDYMAN LLC | DFW Handyman and Home Repair Estimates.”
- Add one plain H1 focused on DFW handyman services rather than only general professional services.
- Add short service copy around drywall, doors, fixtures, installations, mounting, rental maintenance, and odd jobs if those are accurate for the business.
- Include Dallas-Fort Worth wording near the top and in the meta description.
- Add descriptive alt text for the logo and owner photo.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused Wix homepage cleanup, not a full rebuild. A rough implementation estimate is 2 to 4 hours if the existing Wix structure and assets are reused: update the first screen copy, add service cards and CTA buttons, tighten mobile spacing, and keep the current logo/photo brand feel.

## Offer fit
This is a good fit for the $99 Page Profit Check because the site already has enough authentic material to improve quickly. The work is mainly conversion hierarchy, above-the-fold CTA clarity, and service organization.
