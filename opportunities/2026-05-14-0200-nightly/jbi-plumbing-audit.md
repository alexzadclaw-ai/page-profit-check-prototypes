## Quick take

JBI Plumbing already has several high-value conversion ingredients: 24/7 emergency availability, a clear phone number, email address, master plumber license number, Tulsa plumbing keywords, and a broad residential and commercial service list. The site feels like a real local plumbing contractor, but the most important action, calling for urgent help, competes with repeated copy, small service text, and license proof that is easy to miss.

## What is already working

- The phone number is prominent in the header and repeated again near the bottom.
- The site clearly says emergency maintenance is available 24 hours, 7 days a week, 365 days a year.
- The master plumber license number, OK LICENSE #089649, is visible on the homepage.
- The yellow, dark teal, red, and white Wix layout gives the page a bold contractor feel.
- The service list covers valuable jobs including water heaters, bathroom and kitchen renovation, meters, piping, steam, leaks, clogs, fixtures, and new residential or commercial plumbing.
- The original faucet image, wrench graphic, and callout style make the page recognizable.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. The hero does not separate emergency visitors from planned repair or renovation visitors.
2. The same emergency paragraph appears twice, which makes the page feel cluttered.
3. The license proof is useful, but it is presented as a floating note instead of a trust cue beside the call action.
4. The services are a long slash list, so visitors have to work to find their exact need.
5. The bottom CTA says free price estimation, but the page could make calling or emailing easier from multiple sections.
6. Mobile users would benefit from larger tap targets for the phone number and email.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements

- Keep the original Wix-style top banner, dark teal header, yellow wrench, yellow call bubble, red nav tab, faucet photo, and orange footer.
- Put a call-first emergency CTA above the fold with 918-207-5611.
- Add a small trust strip that highlights 24/7 availability, master plumber license proof, and Tulsa service focus.
- Group services into three cards: emergency repairs, installation and replacements, and kitchen/bath renovation.
- Keep the complete original service list below the cards for search relevance and familiarity.
- Add email as a secondary path for planned work and photo/details follow-up.

## SEO basics to tighten

- Use a page title and H1 that include Tulsa plumbing contractor, 24/7 plumbing, and JBI Plumbing LLC.
- Add plain-language service keywords for water heater repair, water heater installation, leak repair, drain and clog troubleshooting, gas meters, water meters, piping repair, fixture installation, kitchen renovation, bathroom renovation, residential plumbing, and commercial plumbing.
- Keep the master plumber license number in crawlable text.
- Add descriptive alt text to the wrench and faucet images.
- Make the phone and email links real tap targets with tel: and mailto: links.

## Realistic implementation angle

This is a focused Wix homepage cleanup, not a rebrand. The fastest win is to restructure the existing first screen, turn the service list into clear cards, make the phone CTA sticky or repeated, and tighten the license proof placement. A fuller implementation could also add separate Services and About sections later, but the homepage can be improved quickly with the current assets and copy.

## Offer fit
