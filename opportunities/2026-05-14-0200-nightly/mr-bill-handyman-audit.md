# Mr. Bill Handyman LLC - Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Mr. Bill has strong substance: license details, insurance, TWIC clearance, 25 years of experience, family story, service breadth, and many project photos. The leak is hierarchy - the most persuasive details are buried too deep in a long page.

## What is already working
The black/gold contractor look, horse handyman logo, real project photos, family story, license numbers, insurance, TWIC clearance, and broad service list are all strong proof cues worth preserving.

## Biggest conversion issues
The first screen is generic, services are hard to scan, trust proof is buried in paragraphs, the gallery is heavy, and phone/tel consistency should be checked so calls do not leak.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
Make the hero estimate-first, add call/email-photo CTAs, surface license/insurance/TWIC/experience near the top, group services into homeowner categories, shorten the gallery, and repeat contact details at decision points.

## SEO basics to tighten
Use clearer handyman/home improvement title copy, service headings for repairs, remodels, flooring/tile and exterior help, local service-area terms for South Jersey/Northern Pennsylvania, descriptive image alt text, and consistent phone schema.

## Realistic implementation angle
A starter tune-up can be implemented in roughly 3 to 5 hours if focused on homepage structure, CTA wording, trust proof placement, service grouping, gallery trimming, and phone/email consistency. A fuller refresh would depend on mobile tuning, forms, multi-page cleanup, and gallery management.

## Offer fit
This is a good fit for the $99 Page Profit Check starter audit because the current site already has enough real business proof to reuse, but the quote path and first-screen hierarchy need a focused cleanup before a larger rebuild is justified.
