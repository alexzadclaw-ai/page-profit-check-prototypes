# Celestial Cleaning LLC - Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Celestial Cleaning has real Bloomington contact details, commercial-cleaning positioning, disinfecting language, and an original cleaner photo. The current page lets Wix branding and donation-related content compete with the main quote path.

## What is already working
The page already has a business name, address, email, source photo, Minnesota outline mark, commercial-cleaning title/meta language, and community/family feel that can be preserved.

## Biggest conversion issues
The first screen does not clearly sell commercial cleaning, quote CTAs are weak, donation links compete with service intent, service details are thin, and the phone/contact presentation needs polish.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
Reframe the first screen around commercial cleaning and disinfecting in Bloomington, add quote/email/call buttons, turn services into scannable cards, move donation content lower, and explain what information to send for an estimate.

## SEO basics to tighten
Tighten title/meta, add headings for office cleaning, disinfecting, recurring plans, Bloomington/service area, image alt text, and more specific commercial-building language.

## Realistic implementation angle
A starter cleanup can be implemented in roughly 2 to 4 hours if focused on homepage hierarchy, CTA copy, service blocks, donation de-emphasis, and basic SEO. A fuller Wix refresh would depend on verified phone details, reviews, form changes, and domain work.

## Offer fit
This is a good fit for the $99 Page Profit Check starter audit because the current site already has enough real business proof to reuse, but the quote path and first-screen hierarchy need a focused cleanup before a larger rebuild is justified.
