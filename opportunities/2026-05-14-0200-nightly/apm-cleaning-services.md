# APM Cleaning Services, LLC. Audit

- Site: https://apmcleaningservice.wixsite.com/apmcleaningservices
- Captured: 2026-05-14
- Prototype: `prototypes/2026-05-14-0200-nightly/apm-cleaning-services/index.html`
- Prototype screenshot: `screenshots/2026-05-14-0200-nightly/apm-cleaning-services-prototype.png`

## What is hurting conversion

1. **The site advertises Wix before APM.** The top banner says the site was built on Wix, which makes the business feel unfinished before visitors see the cleaning offer.
2. **The offer is visibly expired.** The page still promotes “10% off selected services” with an expiration date of 12/31/15. That can make visitors question whether the business is still active.
3. **No clear quote path.** Phone and email are present, but there is no strong “Call for a Quote” or “Request a Quote” button near the top.
4. **Generic title and weak local SEO.** The title is “Cleaning, NJ” instead of a branded service phrase like “APM Cleaning Services, LLC. | South Jersey Cleaning Quotes.”
5. **Navigation is crowded.** Cleaning, concierge, linen, beach day, grocery, terms and contact compete equally. The highest-intent action, getting a quote, should stand out.
6. **Trust cues are buried.** Pet friendly service, Angie's List review language, shore changeovers and detail-focused cleaning are useful proof points, but they sit too low or appear as old copy.
7. **Images have no alt text.** The original page has empty image alt attributes, which weakens accessibility and search clarity.

## Prototype direction

The prototype keeps APM's recognizable look: mint patterned background, teal navigation, large playful headline, original phone/email, the “We are Crazy about Cleaning” line and the original Wix-hosted photos. It removes the Wix-first feel and turns the page into a clearer quote funnel.

## What changed

- Added a simple top positioning line for South Jersey and Jersey Shore cleaning.
- Kept the original header style, but made the quote action easier to see.
- Replaced the expired coupon block with a current quote-oriented value proposition.
- Added primary CTAs: “Call for a Quote” and “Email APM Cleaning.”
- Added a four-item trust strip for weekly cleaning, shore changeovers, pet friendliness and detail work.
- Grouped services into three easy-to-scan cards.
- Preserved the pet-friendly message, but tightened it into a modern trust section.
- Added accessibility-friendly alt text to reused assets.

## Highest-value next fixes

1. Replace the expired 2015 coupon with a current offer or remove discounts completely.
2. Publish on a real domain or at least remove the Wix advertising banner.
3. Add a short quote form with service type, town, bedrooms/bathrooms, rental changeover date and preferred contact method.
4. Add recent reviews or updated Angie's/Google review links.
5. Add service-area text for the specific South Jersey and shore towns APM wants to win.
