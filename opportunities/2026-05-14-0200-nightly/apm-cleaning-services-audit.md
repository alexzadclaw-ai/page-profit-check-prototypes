# APM Cleaning Services LLC - Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
APM has authentic cleaning-service copy, recognizable local personality, phone/email, and original photos, but the page still leads with Wix branding and an expired 2015 coupon. The best opportunity is to keep the teal/orange APM character while making quote requests feel current and easy.

## What is already working
The site has a memorable “Crazy about Cleaning” line, service variety, pet-friendly language, phone/email contact details, and real Wix-hosted images that make the business feel local rather than generic.

## Biggest conversion issues
The Wix banner and expired coupon hurt confidence, the quote path is not prominent enough, navigation gives equal weight to low-intent pages, and service/trust details are spread out instead of helping visitors act quickly.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
Replace expired promo clutter with a current quote CTA, group services into easy cards, put phone/email above the fold, bring pet-friendly and review cues forward, and make the page feel active without changing the underlying brand.

## SEO basics to tighten
Use a title like “APM Cleaning Services | South Jersey Cleaning Quotes,” add service-area language, descriptive headings, image alt text, and current meta copy focused on cleaning, changeovers, windows, organizing, and concierge services.

## Realistic implementation angle
A starter cleanup can be implemented in roughly 2 to 4 hours if limited to homepage structure, current CTAs, service grouping, expired coupon removal, and SEO basics. A fuller rebuild would depend on quote forms, reviews, custom domain, and service-area pages.

## Offer fit
This is a good fit for the $99 Page Profit Check starter audit because the current site already has enough real business proof to reuse, but the quote path and first-screen hierarchy need a focused cleanup before a larger rebuild is justified.
