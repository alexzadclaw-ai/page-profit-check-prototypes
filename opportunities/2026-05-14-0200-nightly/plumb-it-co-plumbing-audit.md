## Quick take

Plumb-It Co. already has the essentials a local plumbing visitor needs: a real logo, direct email, phone number, Facebook link, and a clear list of residential and commercial services. The site is simple and credible, but it reads more like a static service list than a high-intent plumbing landing page.

## What is already working

- Contact details are visible at the top and repeated in the footer.
- The logo and blue divider give the page a recognizable small-contractor identity.
- The service list covers valuable plumbing jobs including slab leaks, water heaters, drain cleaning, fixture installs, gas lines, water lines, and sewer lines.
- The page is uncluttered, so a refresh can improve conversion without throwing away the current style.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. The first screen does not tell urgent plumbing visitors what to do first.
2. Emergency, repair, and installation needs are mixed into one long uppercase list.
3. There is no prominent call button even though plumbing leads often prefer phone contact.
4. The site has very little trust framing beyond the business name and service list.
5. The layout leaves a lot of unused space that could carry quick decision cues without making the page feel busy.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements

- Keep the blue, white, gray, and logo-driven Wix layout.
- Add a direct call button near the top using 682.703.0777.
- Reorganize services into three visitor paths: urgent help, repairs, and installs.
- Keep the original service wording, but make it easier to scan.
- Add a short instruction for sending photos or descriptions by email.
- Repeat contact options after the service paths.

## SEO basics to tighten

- Use a homepage title that includes plumbing, North Texas or Dallas-Fort Worth, and the business name.
- Add an H1 that says what the company does, not only the company name.
- Add plain-language service keywords: slab leak repair, drain cleaning, water heater installation, gas line repair, sewer line repair, commercial plumbing.
- Add service-area copy if the business has defined towns or counties.
- Add descriptive alt text for the logo.

## Realistic implementation angle

This is a light Wix cleanup, not a ground-up rebuild. The fastest win is to revise the above-the-fold section, add CTA buttons, break the service list into cards, and adjust mobile spacing so phone and email are easy to tap. A deeper version could add reviews, photos, service-area pages, and a short contact form later.
