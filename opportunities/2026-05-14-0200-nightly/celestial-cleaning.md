# Celestial Cleaning LLC Audit

- Site: https://celestiallcleaning.wixsite.com/mysite
- Captured: 2026-05-14
- Prototype: `prototypes/2026-05-14-0200-nightly/celestial-cleaning/index.html`
- Prototype screenshot: `screenshots/2026-05-14-0200-nightly/celestial-cleaning-prototype.png`

## What is hurting conversion

1. **The first thing visitors see is the Wix banner.** The page advertises the builder before it explains why a Bloomington business should trust Celestial Cleaning.
2. **The hero waits too long to sell the service.** The source page opens with a large photo, then the headline “Celestial Cleaning, LLC.” and “Committed to Excellence.” It does not immediately say commercial cleaning, disinfecting, Bloomington, or request a quote.
3. **No clear primary CTA.** Email is visible and there is a form lower on the page, but there is no strong “Request a Cleaning Quote,” “Call,” or “Email” action near the top.
4. **Donation links compete with sales intent.** “Donations,” “Help Us,” and a large navy donation area make the business feel less focused than a commercial cleaning company should.
5. **Service details are missing.** The page does not explain what types of buildings are cleaned, what disinfecting includes, how recurring service works, or what a prospect should send when requesting a quote.
6. **Contact details need polish.** The address and email are useful, but the phone number is unformatted and should be verified before being promoted as click-to-call.
7. **SEO is thin.** The title mentions commercial cleaning and Bloomington, but the visible page content does not support that with service headings, service-area copy, or specific cleaning/disinfecting language.

## Prototype direction

The prototype keeps the recognizable Celestial feel: navy blue, white and light gray space, the Minnesota outline mark, the original cleaner photo, the Bloomington address, the Gmail address, the simple Wix-style one-page layout and the social icons. It shifts the page from a donation-heavy brochure into a commercial cleaning quote page.

## What changed

- Put the business name, email and quote action at the top.
- Reframed the first screen around commercial cleaning and disinfecting in Bloomington.
- Reused the original hero photo as the main proof image instead of adding outside stock.
- Added clear CTAs for requesting a quote, emailing, and calling the source phone number.
- Added service cards for office cleaning, disinfecting, common areas and recurring plans.
- Added a short “quote-ready” checklist so visitors know what to include.
- Moved donations into a small secondary community-support note instead of a large competing section.
- Added cleaner title/meta language, alt text and service headings.

## Highest-value next fixes

1. Replace the top Wix advertising banner by upgrading or publishing on a proper domain.
2. Verify and format the phone number, then make it clickable everywhere.
3. Add 3 to 5 specific services with short descriptions and one quote CTA.
4. Confirm whether BSCAI training is current, then state exactly what it means for disinfecting work.
5. Add recent reviews, client types, before/after photos or team photos if available.
6. Rename the donation navigation or move donations to a less prominent footer/community area.
7. Update Wix SEO settings with a stronger title, meta description, service area, and image alt text.
