# L & M Contracting, LLC. — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
L & M Contracting, LLC. is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current Wix site already has a recognizable contractor look, real contact information, and a useful service list — but the first screen does not quickly explain what they do, who they serve, or how to request an estimate.

**Recommendation:** preserve the black/yellow construction-site feel and rebuild the homepage around one clear action: **Request a Free Site Work Estimate**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Text-based “L & M CONTRACTING, LLC.” brand treatment in large Oswald-style uppercase type.
- **Dominant colors:** near-black #181818, construction yellow #ffdd32, white #ffffff, light gray #f5f5f5.
- **Typography feel:** Oswald-style condensed uppercase headings with simple Raleway/Arial-style body copy.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Dark full-width header with left-side brand, simple nav, and email/phone on the right.
- **Hero/image treatment:** Large job-site equipment/project photo above the about text, with dark background framing.
- **Section rhythm:** Dark header, dark intro/about block, bright yellow services block, dark service bars, contact block, simple footer.
- **Recognizable elements to preserve:** black/yellow construction palette, uppercase service headings, “full service Construction Company” positioning, residential/commercial wording, Casco MI address, email, phone, and free-estimate message.

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with public contact paths: lmcontracting1@yahoo.com and (586) 709-8606.
- The page names a real service area anchor/address: 3963 Church Rd, Casco, MI 48064, USA.
- The service list is specific enough to build buyer paths: complete site work, trucking materials, land clearing, concrete removal, residential/commercial demolition, snow removal, grading, home demolition, and pool prep.
- The black/yellow construction visual language is memorable and should not be replaced with a generic polished redesign.
- The page already mentions a free estimate.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The homepage opens with brand/navigation and a large image, but no clear first-screen headline that says what L & M does or who should call.
   - Prototype response: Add a direct hero headline for site work, demolition, grading, hauling, and land clearing in the service area.

2. **Critical:** “Call us today to get a free estimate” is buried in the About text instead of acting as the page’s main conversion path.
   - Prototype response: Make **Request a Free Site Work Estimate** the primary CTA in the header, hero, and contact section.

3. **High:** Services are listed one at a time in oversized dark bars, which creates a long scroll without helping visitors choose the right path.
   - Prototype response: Group the same services into scannable buckets: Site Work & Grading, Demolition & Removal, Trucking & Materials, Seasonal/Specialty Work.

4. **High:** Residential and commercial capability is mentioned, but trust cues for property owners, builders, and commercial clients are not visible near the top.
   - Prototype response: Add a top trust strip for residential & commercial work, free estimates, local Casco MI contact, and direct phone/email.

5. **High:** The contact section is present, but it does not explain what happens after someone reaches out.
   - Prototype response: Add a three-step estimate flow: describe the site, schedule a look, receive a clear plan/estimate.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Add a clear first-screen site-work headline and quote CTA.
- Keep the black/yellow construction identity while removing Wix/template residue from the buyer path.
- Put phone/email and free-estimate proof near the top.
- Turn the long service list into scannable buyer-intent service buckets.
- Add a simple “what happens next” section before the contact form.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title like: **Site Work, Demolition & Grading in Casco, MI | L & M Contracting, LLC.**
- Write a concise meta description around site work, hauling, land clearing, demolition, grading, snow removal, and free estimates.
- Use one clear H1 instead of starting with “About Us.”
- Use H2s for service groups and contact/estimate sections.
- Add LocalBusiness/Contractor schema after approved copy and service-area details are confirmed.
- Add descriptive alt text for any gallery/job-site photos used in Wix.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The Wix site can keep its recognizable black/yellow look, large contractor typography, and existing contact details while tightening the hero, CTA hierarchy, service organization, trust proof, and estimate expectations.
