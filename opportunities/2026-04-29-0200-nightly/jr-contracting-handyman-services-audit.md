# JR Contracting & Handyman Services LLC — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
JR Contracting & Handyman Services LLC is a strong fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current Wix site already has a recognizable contractor/handyman visual style, clear contact details, and a simple service list — but the page loses trust and conversion momentum through template residue, generic wording, and an unclear first-screen action.

**Recommendation:** preserve the existing Wix-style contractor look and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Request a Project Estimate**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Circular JR logo/photo mark and all-caps business name treatment.
- **Dominant colors:** white, black/charcoal, muted gray, Wix blue, and bright green accent from the existing contact/button treatment.
- **Typography feel:** Simple Wix sans-serif, uppercase contractor-style headings, broad spacing.
- **Header/nav pattern:** centered Home / About / Services / Contact navigation with email and phone shown prominently.
- **Hero/image treatment:** tall construction-worker imagery beside a large welcome headline.
- **Section rhythm:** header, nav, hero, about copy, three service categories, contact, footer.
- **Recognizable elements to preserve:**
  - Wix banner/site-builder texture
  - construction-worker imagery
  - Refurbishing / Repair / Remodeling service trio
  - Vancouver WA contact details
  - email-forward and phone-forward contact pattern
  - simple black, white, gray, and green visual direction

## What is already working
- The site has direct phone and email contact visible: mercury1250@gmail.com and (360) 600-4972.
- The business name and service category are clear enough to understand the company is a home improvement / handyman provider.
- The homepage has a simple structure visitors can follow without navigating multiple pages.
- Existing construction imagery gives the page a recognizable contractor feel.
- The service categories — Refurbishing, Repair, Remodeling — are useful and worth keeping.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The first screen says “Welcome” and “Contact,” but does not clearly tell a homeowner what action to take or what kind of project help to request.
   - Prototype response: Replace generic CTA hierarchy with **Request a Project Estimate** and a secondary phone CTA.

2. **Critical:** Wix template residue (“This website was built on Wix,” “Proudly created with Wix.com,” generic Submit) weakens trust for a contractor asking homeowners to invite them into their property.
   - Prototype response: Keep enough Wix visual fingerprint to preserve recognition, but make the business-specific content and contact path feel more intentional.

3. **High:** Trust proof is buried in a paragraph instead of being scannable near the top.
   - Prototype response: Add a top trust strip around local service, clear communication, and results-focused work.

4. **High:** The service trio is present, but the cards do not explain what each category means for a homeowner.
   - Prototype response: Keep Refurbishing / Repair / Remodeling and add short buyer-intent descriptions.

5. **High:** The contact area asks for a form submission without explaining what happens next.
   - Prototype response: Add a three-step “What Happens Next” block before contact.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Make **Request a Project Estimate** the primary CTA.
- Keep the existing visual family, imagery, logo treatment, and service trio.
- Move trust signals near the top in a scannable strip.
- Explain Refurbishing, Repair, and Remodeling in homeowner-friendly language.
- Add a simple next-step sequence before the contact form.
- Tighten SEO title and meta description around home improvement, handyman services, and Vancouver, WA.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Add a meta description; the live page currently does not expose a meaningful one in the audit capture.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for About, Services, Process, and Contact.
- Add descriptive alt text to logo and construction images.
- Add LocalBusiness / HomeAndConstructionBusiness schema after final copy and service area are confirmed.
- Make contact links proper `mailto:` and `tel:` actions.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing Wix site can keep its simple contractor look while tightening the hero, contact hierarchy, trust proof, service explanations, next-step clarity, and SEO basics.
