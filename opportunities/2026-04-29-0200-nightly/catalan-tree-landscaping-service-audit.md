# Catalan Tree & Landscaping Service — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Catalan Tree & Landscaping Service is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current Wix site already has recognizable tree-service imagery, a straightforward local-service structure, and direct contact details — but the page loses credibility through placeholder testimonials, generic contractor copy, and a vague first-screen action.

**Recommendation:** keep the earthy Wix tree-service look and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Request a Free Tree & Landscape Estimate**.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The first screen uses “Learn More” instead of a quote-focused action, so a visitor who needs tree work does not immediately know how to request help.
   - Prototype response: make **Request a Free Estimate** the primary CTA and keep phone/email as secondary contact paths.

2. **Critical:** The testimonial section contains visible placeholder quotes and names, which can actively damage trust for a local service business.
   - Prototype response: remove fake/placeholder testimonials and replace that area with honest trust cues and a “what happens next” process.

3. **High:** The services are listed, but the copy is generic and does not help a homeowner understand when to call, what is included, or what the next step is.
   - Prototype response: keep Tree Felling and Log Bucking while adding scannable homeowner-focused descriptions.

4. **High:** Trust proof is not surfaced near the top; the page says “leading and reputable” but does not give quick practical reasons to trust the company.
   - Prototype response: add a top trust strip around free estimates, local Magnolia service, and careful tree/log work.

5. **High:** The contact section gives information but does not explain the flow after someone reaches out.
   - Prototype response: add a three-step estimate process before the contact block.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Replace vague **Learn More** with **Request a Free Tree & Landscape Estimate**.
- Keep the existing dark/olive tree-service style and imagery.
- Remove placeholder testimonial content instead of letting fake review copy undermine credibility.
- Add a trust strip near the top with practical, believable service cues.
- Make Tree Felling and Log Bucking easier to scan and understand.
- Add a simple “what happens next” section before the contact details.
- Tighten SEO title/meta around tree service, landscaping, Magnolia, TX, and the business name.

- Make contact links proper `mailto:` and `tel:` actions.
