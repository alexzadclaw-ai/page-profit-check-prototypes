# On The Level Handyman LLC — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
On The Level Handyman LLC is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current Wix page already has real local-service signals — WV licensed, Belle WV, Billy Lawrence, phone/email, remodeling/repair/maintenance intent, and pressure washing — but the page leads with generic template wording and makes visitors work too hard to understand the service path.

**Recommendation:** preserve the clean black/white Wix service-site feel and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Request a Free Estimate**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Text-based business name: On The Level Handyman Llc.
- **Dominant colors:** White background, black text, black section/CTA accents, simple gray Wix utility bar residue.
- **Typography feel:** Avenir/Helvetica-style clean sans serif with large, plain service headings.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Email and phone at the top, business name/tagline, simple Home/About/Services/Contact anchors.
- **Hero/image treatment:** Minimal Wix hero with generic “At Your Service” heading and a Get in Touch button.
- **Section rhythm:** Top contact strip, business intro, About block, service blocks, contact form/footer.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:** Plain local-handyman wording, direct “Call Billy Lawrence” contact cue, WV licensed proof, Belle WV location, Remodel/Install/Repair/Pressure Washing service categories.

- The site has direct contact information: Onthelevelhandymanllc@yahoo.com and Call Billy Lawrence 304-993-3295.
- The page clearly states handyman/remodeling/repair/maintenance intent.
- “WV licensed” is a strong trust cue for a local trades service.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The first screen says “At Your Service,” which is generic and does not immediately explain what the business does, where it serves, or why to trust it.
   - Prototype response: Lead with “Belle WV handyman work, remodeling, repairs, and pressure washing with a clearer free-estimate path.”
2. **High:** The strongest trust cue, “WV licensed,” appears as plain text instead of being framed near the top with contact and location proof.
   - Prototype response: Add a top trust strip: WV licensed, Belle WV, free estimates, direct owner contact.
3. **High:** Services are broad and template-like; Remodel, Install, Repair, and Pressure Washing need clearer customer-facing examples and routing.
   - Prototype response: Turn services into scannable buyer paths with practical examples under each category.
4. **High:** The contact path exists but is generic (“Get in Touch” / “Submit”) and does not explain what happens after someone asks for an estimate.
   - Prototype response: Use “Request a Free Estimate” consistently and add a three-step next-step flow.
5. **Medium:** Wix/template leftovers and footer residue (“Proudly created with Wix.com,” generic about copy) can make the business feel less established than the licensing and direct contact imply.
   - Prototype response: Remove template residue from the prototype and replace generic about copy with service-specific positioning.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Replace the generic hero with a specific local promise and clear free-estimate CTA.
2. Move WV licensed, Belle WV, phone/email, and owner contact cues into the top trust area.
3. Group services by how homeowners choose: remodel/update, install/assemble, repair/maintenance, pressure washing.
4. Add a simple “what happens next” flow before the contact form.
5. Make the contact section feel intentional with phone, email, location, and a project-details prompt.
6. Remove obvious Wix/template leftovers and stale footer language while preserving the same simple site family.

## SEO basics to tighten
