## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The hero explains the business generally, but the next action is split between “Call Today,” “Our Services,” and “View All Services” instead of one specific estimate request.
   - Prototype response: Make **Schedule Your Estimate** the primary CTA and keep phone as the secondary CTA.

2. **Critical:** Placeholder/testimonial-like content damages trust. “John D.” and “Emily T.” appear without real quote context, and one testimonial area contains service copy instead of a client quote.
   - Prototype response: Remove fake-looking testimonial treatment and replace it with verifiable trust/process copy until real reviews are available.

3. **High:** The service trio is present but too thin to help a homeowner pick the right path.
   - Prototype response: Keep the same three categories and add short, homeowner-oriented descriptions and examples.

4. **High:** “Why Choose Us” lists good ideas, but they are not connected to specific proof or a clear homeowner benefit.
   - Prototype response: Convert them into a top trust strip plus a concise “why choose us” block focused on estimate clarity, affordable work, and satisfaction.

5. **High:** The contact section lists information but does not explain what happens after someone reaches out.
   - Prototype response: Add a three-step “What Happens Next” block before contact.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Change the primary action to **Schedule Your Estimate**.
- Keep the same green/white Wix visual family and service order.
- Preserve the **Call Today** phone path while making the estimate request more obvious.
- Replace placeholder testimonial residue with a credible trust/process section.
- Clarify Home Renovations, Landscaping, and Handyman Services with practical examples.
- Add a short next-step sequence so visitors know what to expect after contact.
- Tighten SEO title/meta around Madison home improvement, landscaping, handyman services, and the business name.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title combining service + location + brand.
- Keep one clear H1 focused on Madison home improvement and landscaping services.
- Use H2s for Services, Why Choose Us, What Happens Next, and Contact.

- Add descriptive alt text to project/home images.
- Add LocalBusiness / HomeAndConstructionBusiness schema after final service area and business details are confirmed.
- Make phone/email actions proper `tel:` and `mailto:` links.
