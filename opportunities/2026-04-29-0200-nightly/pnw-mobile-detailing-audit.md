## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The top of the page leads with a generic satisfaction line instead of a clear service/quote action.
   - Prototype response: Replace the vague first-screen copy with a specific mobile-detailing value proposition and two direct actions: quote request and call.

2. **Critical:** The current offer is a Christmas special, which reads stale and can make the business look unattended.
   - Prototype response: Remove seasonal residue from the homepage and replace it with an evergreen “current availability / gift certificates available” framing.

3. **High:** Services are not visible on the homepage in a scannable way, even though the nav suggests service and pricing pages.
   - Prototype response: Add clear buyer-intent cards for car, RV/boat, interior problem areas, and maintenance/protection detailing.

4. **High:** Trust proof is thin near the top; visitors get contact info but not enough reassurance before reaching out.
   - Prototype response: Add a compact trust strip around mobile service, vehicle types, direct owner contact, and by-appointment weekend availability.

5. **Medium:** The contact form and footer feel generic and template-like, including Wix residue.
   - Prototype response: Make the contact section business-specific and remove “Proudly created with Wix.com” style footer residue from the prototype.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Turn the hero into a clear quote path for mobile detailing.
2. Replace stale Christmas-special copy with evergreen availability and gift-certificate language.
3. Group services by the way customers think: car detail, RV/boat, interior cleanup, protection/maintenance.
4. Bring direct contact and trust signals above the fold.
5. Clean up the contact block so it asks for vehicle type, location, and preferred timing.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + region + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around mobile car, RV, and boat detailing plus quote intent.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for services, process, and contact.
- Add descriptive alt text for vehicle-detailing images.
- Add LocalBusiness/AutoDetailing schema after service area and preferred booking details are confirmed.
