# TOMY’S Mobile Detailing — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
TOMY’S Mobile Detailing is a strong fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current Wix page already has real service packages, starting prices, a Utah location, email, and phone number — but the buying path is mostly a long service list instead of a clear booking flow.

**Recommendation:** preserve the black/white Wix site family and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Call to Book Mobile Detailing**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

- **Dominant colors:** black/dark charcoal, white, light gray Wix banner, simple white text.
- **Typography feel:** Barlow-style uppercase headings with light Avenir-style body copy.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Large dark header, brand on the left, visible Instagram URL, Home/Contact links, email and phone.

- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:**
  - Wix top banner

  - starting-price package menu
  - West Jordan, UT contact details

## What is already working
- The page gives direct contact options: tomasmn11@gmail.com and (801) 584-9299.
- The business category is clear: mobile detailing in Utah.
- The services are concrete and buyer-relevant: car wash, wash & wax, paint correction, ceramic coatings, headlight restoration, interior cleaning, deep interior cleaning, and engine cleaning.
- Several services include entry prices, which helps visitors self-qualify.
- The contact section includes a physical West Jordan address and a simple form.
- The Instagram link gives visitors another public proof/recent-work path.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The page has contact info, but no strong above-the-fold booking message or primary action.
   - Prototype response: Add a clear mobile-detailing hero, “Call to Book” CTA, and email request CTA immediately after the preserved header.

2. **High:** The services are valuable but cramped; some package bullets run together, making the menu harder to scan.
   - Prototype response: Rebuild the same service menu as clean cards with headings, starting prices, and separated bullet points.

3. **High:** Trust and convenience cues are buried or implied instead of summarized near the top.
   - Prototype response: Add a dark trust strip calling out mobile service, starting prices, paint protection, and call/text booking.

4. **Medium:** The contact form appears late and does not tell visitors what information to send.
   - Prototype response: Add a simple “what happens next” section and a more specific contact prompt asking for vehicle and service needed.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Add a clear above-the-fold booking CTA without changing the brand identity.
2. Organize the service menu into scannable cards while keeping the original packages and prices.
3. Add top-of-page trust/convenience cues for mobile service, pricing, and protection options.
4. Make the contact section more appointment-oriented and specific.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that includes “Utah mobile detailing,” “West Jordan,” and the business name.
- Add a concise meta description focused on mobile car wash, interior detailing, paint correction, ceramic coating, and booking.
- Keep one clear H1/primary hero heading and use H2s for Services and Contact.
- Add descriptive alt text for any vehicle/detailing images in the live Wix editor.
- Add LocalBusiness/AutomotiveBusiness schema after approved service area and hours are confirmed.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is not a rebrand. It is a focused Wix homepage cleanup: keep the existing dark visual identity, brand wording, contact info, service list, and pricing, then tighten the top hero, service layout, trust strip, and contact path.
