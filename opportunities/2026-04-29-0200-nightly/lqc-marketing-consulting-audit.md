# LQC Marketing Consulting — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
LQC Marketing Consulting is a strong **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** candidate because the site already has credible experience, a calm professional visual identity, clear service categories, and direct contact details. The issue is not brand credibility; it is that the homepage asks visitors to read dense marketing copy before they understand the best inquiry path.

**Recommendation:** keep the existing understated Wix/professional-consultant feel, but reorganize the homepage around one primary action: **Schedule a Marketing Consult**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Text-forward LQC Marketing Consulting identity, understated and professional.
- **Dominant colors:** charcoal/dark gray, sage green, muted moss, taupe, soft off-white.
- **Typography feel:** Elegant serif headline feel paired with simple sans-serif body/navigation.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Simple top navigation with About, Services, Specialties, and Contact sections.
- **Hero/image treatment:** Large muted hero block with integrated marketing positioning and calm consulting tone.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, about narrative, services, specialties, testimonial, contact.
- **Recognizable elements to preserve:** “Integrated Marketing Solutions,” “Let’s Thrive and Grow Together,” extension-of-your-marketing-team positioning, Leah Corcoran contact details, Rinn Advisors testimonial, and the branding/print/digital/social/events/research specialty list.

## What is already working
- The business explains a real consulting model: contract or freelance support as an extension of the client’s marketing team.
- The site lists credible experience across B2B, B2C, B2B2C, public, private, and non-profit organizations.
- Services are relevant and commercially useful: marketing strategy, marketing execution, branding, print, digital, social, events, and market research.
- There is a real testimonial from Rinn Advisors that mentions impressions and qualified leads.
- Contact details are present: lqcmarketingconsulting@gmail.com, 617-930-8857, Leah Corcoran, and the Millis, MA address.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The hero is warm but broad; it does not quickly tell a visitor what kind of engagement to start or what action to take.
   - Prototype response: Reframe the hero around contract/freelance marketing strategy and execution with a specific **Schedule a Marketing Consult** CTA.

2. **High:** The service copy is dense and repeats broad marketing language, making strategy, execution, and customer-journey support blend together.
   - Prototype response: Split services into three buyer-intent paths: Marketing Strategy, Marketing Execution, and Customer Journey Programs.

3. **High:** Credibility signals are present but arrive after long paragraphs instead of helping qualify the consultant near the top.
   - Prototype response: Add a top trust strip for B2B, B2C, B2B2C, and public/private/non-profit experience.

4. **High:** The specialties list is useful but visually reads like a long inventory rather than a scannable set of capabilities.
   - Prototype response: Convert specialties into six short cards: Branding, Print, Digital, Social, Events, and Market Research.

5. **High:** The contact section asks visitors to reach out but does not explain what to send or what happens next.
   - Prototype response: Add a three-step next-process block before the contact form.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Make **Schedule a Marketing Consult** the primary CTA.
- Clarify the first-screen promise: marketing strategy and execution without adding a full-time seat.
- Surface B2B/B2C/B2B2C and organization-type credibility near the top.
- Group services by buyer intent instead of repeating marketing categories.
- Turn specialties into faster-scanning cards.
- Add a “what happens next” sequence so inquiries feel easier to start.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that pairs “Integrated Marketing Strategy & Execution” with the LQC brand.
- Keep one clear H1 focused on marketing strategy/execution support.
- Use H2s for About, Services, Specialties, Process, and Contact.
- Add a concise meta description around contract/freelance integrated marketing consulting.
- If implemented live, add Organization/LocalBusiness schema and mark up email, phone, and address consistently.

## Realistic implementation angle
This should be positioned as a focused homepage clarity sprint, not a redesign. The live site can keep the current colors, tone, simple navigation, and consultant identity while tightening headline clarity, service hierarchy, trust placement, and inquiry expectations.
