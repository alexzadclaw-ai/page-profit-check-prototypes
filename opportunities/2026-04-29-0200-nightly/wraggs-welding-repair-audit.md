## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The first screen uses a broad slogan, but does not immediately say what kind of welding jobs to send or what the next step is.
   - Prototype response: Make the hero specific to welding repair, fabrication, cutting, and arc-gouging, with direct phone/email CTAs.

2. **Critical:** Wix/template residue is visible, including the Wix banner/footer language and internal anchor text such as `Home: Homepage_about` / `Home: Service` / `Home: Contact`.
   - Prototype response: Remove template residue and make the page feel intentionally maintained.

3. **High:** The strongest proof — since 2003, guaranteed work, direct owner/welder contact — is buried in paragraph copy.
   - Prototype response: Add a compact trust strip immediately below the hero.

4. **High:** Service descriptions are repetitive and generic, so visitors may not quickly know whether their specific project fits.
   - Prototype response: Keep the same three services but rewrite them into clearer buyer-intent cards.

5. **High:** The contact form area says “I want to hear from you,” but the page does not explain what a good first inquiry should include.
   - Prototype response: Add a “what happens next” block and a cleaner contact panel with phone/email.
