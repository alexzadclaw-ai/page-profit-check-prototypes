## Quick take

West Coast Mobile Detailing has a real local service offer, visible phone/email, clear appointment availability, and genuine vehicle/detailing photos. The page currently feels like an older Wix starter site: the Wix ad banner, dated footer, typo-heavy service copy, vague hero CTA, and form-heavy contact flow make the business look less polished than the actual service likely is.

The Page Profit Check prototype keeps the same recognizable Wix-style visual language—white header, centered logo/brand, serif typography, red/dark charcoal palette, large photo hero, gray/red section rhythm, service photography, and simple stacked sections—but tightens the message around mobile detailing, Medford/Southern Oregon service area, appointment booking, and direct call/email actions.

## What is already working

- Real business identity is present: West Coast Mobile Detailing, “Your Paint & Interior Doctors,” Carlos Pierce, phone, email, and social links.
- The page communicates a useful buyer benefit: mobile detailing that comes to the customer.
- Hours are simple and flexible: open 7 days a week, all hours by appointment.
- Service categories are relevant and commercially useful: wash & wax, full interior detail, and treatment for scratches/bugs/tar.
- Real vehicle photos make the page feel more authentic than a generic stock-template site.
- Existing colors and typography create a recognizable personality: dark charcoal, muted red, gray/off-white blocks, Playfair/Didot/Caudex-style serif text, and a classic auto-detailing look.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Wix advertising residue appears at the very top.** This immediately tells visitors the site is on a free/unfinished builder tier and weakens confidence before they even see the business.
2. **The first CTA is vague.** “Get in Touch with Carlos Pierce” does not clearly say whether the visitor should call, book an appointment, request a quote, or submit the form.
3. **The page does not clearly explain the service area above the fold.** Medford appears in the footer and Southern Oregon appears in body copy, but a visitor should understand location fit immediately.
4. **Service descriptions contain visible typos and awkward phrasing.** Examples include “Canauba,” “Oscolating,” and “dryed.” These are small errors, but on a service business site they reduce perceived care and professionalism.
5. **The contact experience is form-heavy.** Phone and email exist, but the main contact area emphasizes a generic form instead of a fast mobile-friendly call/email booking path.
6. **Trust signals are not grouped near the decision point.** “Mobile service,” “7 days a week,” and “by appointment” are useful, but they are not packaged as quick confidence-builders near the top.
7. **Footer date is stale.** “©2017” makes the site feel neglected even if the business is active.
8. **Services are visually photo-forward but copy-light.** The service section uses photos and large whitespace, but the actual details and next-step guidance are too sparse.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements

- Remove free-Wix/ad-style residue from the customer-facing experience.
- Keep the same header/logo/nav/social layout, but add a direct “Call Carlos” booking CTA.
- Reframe the hero around a clear offer: mobile auto detailing for Medford and Southern Oregon by appointment.
- Add two primary hero actions: call for an appointment and email Carlos.
- Add a short trust strip near the top: mobile service, 7 days a week, Southern Oregon / Medford.
- Rewrite service descriptions with corrected spelling, clearer buyer language, and practical pre-booking details.
- Turn the contact section into a booking-focused section with call/email options before the form-style checklist.
- Refresh the footer year to the current year and keep the existing brand/contact/social pattern.
- Preserve the original site’s recognizable visual cues rather than replacing it with a generic modern landing page.

## SEO basics to tighten

- Replace the generic title “Home | West Coast Mobile Detailing” with a service/location title such as “West Coast Mobile Detailing | Mobile Auto Detailing in Medford, OR.”
- Add a meta description that mentions mobile detailing, wash/wax, interior detailing, Medford, and Southern Oregon.
- Make the H1 useful and specific while still preserving the brand name.
- Add clearer service-area language near the top of the page.
- Correct typos in service copy so search snippets and page content look professional.
- Use descriptive alt text for vehicle/detailing images.
- If the business has an active Google Business Profile or review source, link or reference it from the page when available.

## Realistic implementation angle

This is a small cleanup, not a full rebuild. The live site already has the right ingredients: logo, service photos, phone/email, hours, services, and a simple one-page structure. The most realistic implementation is to update the existing Wix page in place:

- remove the Wix banner by moving to the appropriate paid/custom-domain setup if needed;
- revise hero copy and CTA buttons;
- tighten service copy and spelling;
- add a top trust strip;
- reorganize contact into call/email-first booking actions;
- update page title, meta description, footer date, and image alt text;
- keep the existing visual language and photo assets so it still feels like West Coast Mobile Detailing.

## Offer fit

This is a good Page Profit Check fit because the business does not need a large strategy project to look meaningfully more trustworthy. A $99 entry audit/prototype is enough to show the owner what the site could look like after a focused cleanup: same brand, same service, clearer booking path.

A follow-on implementation sprint would likely be modest if the owner has Wix access. The highest-impact work is copy/CTA/contact cleanup and basic SEO polish, not custom engineering.
