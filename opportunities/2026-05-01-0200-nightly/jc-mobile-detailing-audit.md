# JC Mobile Detailing — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
JC Mobile Detailing already has useful conversion pieces: a recognizable Wix homepage, real detailing photos, bookable service packages, starting prices, duration estimates, and a direct email contact. The opportunity is not a full redesign. It is a **Solution A surgical cleanup** that keeps the current Wix/site-builder look while making the page easier to understand and book from.

**Recommendation:** preserve the teal-and-white Wix visual language, wheel-polishing hero image, split photo/about block, four service cards, and chat/contact rhythm, but tighten the first screen around one action: **choose a package and request a detailing appointment**.

## What is already working
- The homepage clearly belongs to a mobile detailing business.
- The hero and service imagery are relevant and recognizable.
- The service section already lists four useful buyer choices: High End Detail, Interior Only, Exterior Only, and Express Detail.
- Starting prices and service durations are visible, which is stronger than many small-service sites.
- There is a direct contact route via `jcmovement@gmail.com`.
- The existing design has enough brand DNA to preserve rather than replace.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The first-screen message is too generic.
   - The hero says “Exceptional Service,” but it does not immediately explain the practical buyer path: mobile detailing packages, starting prices, and booking.
2. **Critical:** The page has pricing and booking tools, but no clear top-of-page decision path.
   - Visitors have to scroll before seeing packages, prices, timing, or what to do next.
3. **High:** The service cards repeat price text and rely on “Read More” / “Book Now” without enough package guidance.
   - The cards show useful data, but the duplicated price blocks and thin descriptions make comparison harder than it should be.
4. **High:** Trust and convenience signals are not surfaced near the top.
   - Mobile convenience, package count, visible starting prices, and appointment timing are valuable but not framed as proof.
5. **High:** The contact section is vague.
   - “GET IN TOUCH” plus a generic form does not tell visitors what information to provide or what happens after submission.
6. **Medium:** Template residue lowers polish.
   - The Wix banner, login/search tools, and “Proudly created with Wix.com” footer are recognizable but make the site feel less finished if left uncurated.
7. **Medium:** SEO basics are thin.
   - The title and headings can be more service-specific, and image alt text / local service language should be tightened when the live site is updated.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Replace the generic hero subhead with a service-specific promise: mobile detailing packages with starting prices and easier booking.
2. Add a trust/value strip directly under the hero: four bookable packages, starting prices visible, 2–4 hour service windows, mobile convenience.
3. Keep the same four-card service structure, but remove duplicated price lines and add one-sentence package guidance.
4. Add a short “what happens next” block before the contact form so visitors know how booking is confirmed.
5. Make the contact form ask for useful details: vehicle year/make/model, preferred package, and preferred timing.
6. Keep the teal Wix-style visual rhythm, image assets, and card layout so the owner recognizes the site.
7. Clean footer/template residue in an implementation pass after the owner approves the direction.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a title like: `JC Mobile Detailing | Mobile Car Detailing Packages`.
- Write a concise meta description around interior, exterior, express, and high-end detailing packages.
- Keep one clear H1 on the homepage and use H2s for About, Services, What Happens Next, and Contact.
- Add descriptive alt text for the polishing, interior, exterior, and express-detail images.
- Add local/service-area language once the actual service area is confirmed.
- Add LocalBusiness or AutoDetailing structured data after copy and service area are finalized.
