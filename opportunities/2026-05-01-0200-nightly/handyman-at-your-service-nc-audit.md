# HandyMan At Your Service NC — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
HandyMan At Your Service NC has a real local-service foundation: a clear business name, phone number, visible Charlotte contact details, service photos, a handyman-specific tagline, and a simple Wix structure. The best opportunity is a same-site cleanup that keeps the white header, slideshow/photo hero, dark blue-green service band, white service section, yellow contact block, and practical handyman imagery while making the first screen and estimate path much clearer.

Priority: **A-**

## What is already working
- The business name and phone number are visible in the header.
- The tagline, “The Handyman You Can Count On,” fits the service category.
- The page already lists useful service categories: cabinet installation, deck staining, drywall repairs, painting, residential improvement, commercial work, outdoor/lawn jobs, and unusual jobs.
- The site has recognizable visual DNA: white Wix-style header, large slideshow hero photo, dark blue-green service sections, white “Any Service You Need” section, bright yellow contact section, and practical repair imagery.
- Contact details are present: Charlotte, NC, `Handyman.atyour.service9181@gmail.com`, and `704-840-2700`.

## Biggest conversion issues
| Finding | Severity | Why it matters |
|---|---:|---|
| The first visible hero area is mostly a slideshow; the main service message appears low on the page. | **Critical** | Visitors may not immediately understand service area, specific jobs, or why to call before scrolling. |
| The H1 says “HOME IMPROVEMENT PRO” but does not quickly name the common handyman jobs. | **Critical** | A visitor looking for drywall, staining, cabinet, painting, or small repair help has to work too hard. |
| “Get In Touch” is repeated but generic. | **High** | The CTA does not clarify whether the visitor should call, request an estimate, send photos, or book online. |
| Service content is long, repetitive, and duplicated across multiple sections. | **High** | Repetition makes the page feel template-built and slows down scanning. |
| Wix ad/banner, Book Online link, Wix social links, and “Proudly created with Wix.com” footer residue lower credibility. | **High** | Platform/template clutter competes with the owner’s contact path and can make the business look unfinished. |
| The contact form lacks enough visible guidance around what to send. | **High** | Homeowners often need to know whether to send photos, job details, or call first. |
| Trust proof is thin near the top. | **Medium** | The site mentions experience and punctuality lower down, but the first screen could reassure visitors faster. |
| Mobile scanability is likely weak because of the very tall hero/slideshow and repeated text blocks. | **Medium** | Handyman leads often come from phones and need a quick call/email path. |

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Keep the same Wix-style white header, slideshow/photo hero, blue-green palette, yellow contact area, and stock handyman imagery so the owner recognizes the site.
2. Move a clearer service-led headline into the first screen: Charlotte handyman repairs, installs, staining, drywall, painting, outdoor jobs, and small jobs.
3. Replace generic “Get In Touch” with direct estimate CTAs: **Call for a Free Estimate** and **Email Project Details**.
4. Add a trust strip directly below the intro: no job too small, free estimate call, Charlotte based.
5. Collapse repeated service copy into concise cards with scannable bullets.
6. Add a three-step “How to get started” section so visitors know what happens after they call or email.
7. Improve the contact section with direct phone/email, labeled form fields, and a reminder to send project photos/details.
8. Remove the Wix ad/banner, unused Book Online distraction, Wix social links, and Wix footer credit from the improved experience.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use one clear H1 with service + location language, for example: “Charlotte Handyman Repairs, Installs & Small Jobs.”
- Update the title tag to include the business name and primary local service keywords.
- Add a meta description mentioning Charlotte, cabinet installation, deck staining, drywall repairs, painting, outdoor jobs, and free estimate calls.
- Use descriptive H2/H3 service headings instead of repeated generic paragraphs.
- Add alt text to the slideshow/hero, cabinet, deck staining, drywall/tools, outdoor, and pipe-repair images.
- If keeping Wix, remove or hide unused Book Online pages unless they are active and properly configured.
