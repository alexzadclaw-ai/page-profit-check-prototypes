# CR's Mobile Detailing Services — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
CR's Mobile Detailing Services is a strong **Solution A** candidate because the public Wix site already has recognizable bones: a logo/name-forward header, a large buffing/detailing hero image, pale blue content sections, simple outlined "Get In Touch" buttons, service packages, direct phone/email, Waynesville location, and a real customer testimonial.

The main opportunity is not a rebrand. It is a cleaner booking path that keeps the same Wix/site-builder feel while making one action obvious: **get a free mobile detailing estimate**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## What is already working
- The business category is clear: mobile car detailing in and around Waynesville, MO.
- The header includes direct contact information: crsmobiledetail@gmail.com and 573-316-5557.
- The site has a real logo mark, a clear business name, and the tagline “The service you can depend on.”
- Popular packages are visible: Bronze, Silver, Gold, and Platinum.
- The Services page includes useful add-ons: free estimates, car seat cleaning, headlight restoration, engine cleaning, motorcycle package, wash and wax, and Platinum Plus.
- The quote page already has a form, and the services copy says estimates can be done in person or by pictures sent through Facebook, text, or email.
- A real testimonial from Rebecca Alexander supports the convenience of mobile service.

## Biggest conversion issues
1. **Critical:** The first screen is almost entirely a photo/header. It shows the brand but does not explain mobile service, service area, package choices, or why someone should click.
   - Prototype response: keep the same header/logo/nav and buffing-photo hero, but add Waynesville/100% mobile positioning plus clear “Get a Free Estimate” and call CTAs.

2. **Critical:** The quote path is technically present, but it is buried on a separate page and still contains generic Wix placeholder copy: “This is your Price Quote section paragraph…”
   - Prototype response: move a specific quote section onto the homepage and rewrite the prompt so visitors know to send package, vehicle, condition, photos, location, and timing.

3. **High:** Package CTAs repeat “Get in Touch” without clarifying which package or estimate step the visitor is starting.
   - Prototype response: change package actions to “Get an Estimate” and anchor them to the quote form.

4. **High:** Trust signals are scattered. “100% mobile,” “5 years of experience,” Waynesville, phone/email, free estimates, and testimonial proof appear on different pages or low on the page.
   - Prototype response: add a compact trust strip immediately below the hero and keep the testimonial near the quote path.

5. **High:** Services are split between the homepage and Services page, so add-ons like headlights, car seats, engine cleaning, motorcycles, wash/wax, and Platinum Plus are easy to miss.
   - Prototype response: keep the main package rhythm but add a scannable add-on grid after the popular packages.

6. **Medium:** Template residue weakens polish, especially the Wix promotional/footer line and placeholder quote paragraph.
   - Prototype response: remove the placeholder quote copy from the user-facing page and keep footer language cleaner.

7. **Medium:** SEO basics are thin. The title is broad (“United States”), and page structure does not clearly combine service + location + brand.
   - Prototype response: add a location-specific title/meta description, one clear H1-style brand header, descriptive section headings, and image alt text.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Make the hero instantly say: mobile detailing in Waynesville, MO, with a free estimate path.
2. Keep the recognizable Wix header, logo, thin nav, outline buttons, pale blue sections, and large detailing photography.
3. Replace generic quote-page copy with service-specific instructions.
4. Move trust proof up: 100% mobile, Waynesville, free estimates, and 5+ years of experience.
5. Convert repeated package CTAs into one consistent estimate path.
6. Add a “how to get a better quote” section so customers know what details/photos to send.
7. Surface add-on services without forcing visitors to dig through another page.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a page title like: “Mobile Detailing in Waynesville, MO | CR's Mobile Detailing Services.”
- Replace “United States” in the title with the actual service location.
- Add a meta description mentioning mobile car detailing, Waynesville, free estimates, and major services.
- Use one clear top-level heading and H2s for About, Services, Process, Quote, Testimonials, and Recent Work.
- Add descriptive alt text for the logo, buffing/detailing images, and recent-work gallery.
- If the owner confirms service-area radius, add LocalBusiness/AutoDetailing schema and service-area wording.

## Realistic implementation angle
This can be implemented as a light Wix cleanup. The existing logo, photos, navigation labels, package copy, contact details, testimonial, quote form, and pale-blue section style can stay. The high-impact work is reorganizing the homepage, replacing placeholder quote copy, tightening CTAs, and adding a few clearer service/estimate blocks.
