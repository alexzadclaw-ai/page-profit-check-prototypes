# Pure Form Cleaning Group — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Pure Form Cleaning Group already has the bones of a useful Wix cleaning-service site: a clean white layout, a memorable five-dot teal logo, a large cleaning hero image, bookable service blocks, starting prices, and direct email/phone contact. The opportunity is a **Solution A surgical cleanup**, not a redesign: keep the same minimal Wix/site-builder language while making the buyer path clearer for residential, construction, and janitorial leads.

**Recommendation:** preserve the white space, centered logo/nav, oversized hero photo, blue-gray buttons, horizontal service cards, simple contact form, and footer rhythm — but rewrite the first screen, separate the three buyer paths, add trust/next-step clarity, and remove confusing account/cart/subscription friction from the conversion path.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The first screen is brand-only and does not explain who the service is for.
   - “Exquisite. Affordable. Clean” sounds nice, but it does not tell a facility manager, contractor, landlord, Airbnb/rental owner, or homeowner which path to take.
2. **Critical:** The main conversion path is fragmented between Book Now, generic “Get in Touch,” login/cart, and a subscribe form.
   - Cleaning leads need a quote/request path, not e-commerce/account friction.
3. **High:** The service cards do not guide buyer intent.
   - Janitorial, construction, and residential/rental are listed, but there is little explanation of best-fit use cases, what to send, or whether pricing is hourly/per cleaner or job-based.
4. **High:** Trust and logistics are not surfaced near the top.
   - Visitors do not quickly see proof points such as insured/professional expectations, recurring service availability, construction turnover support, or clear response expectations.
5. **High:** The contact form is too generic for cleaning requests.
   - Fields like “Subject” and “Type of Cleaning...” are a start, but the form should prompt for property type, service needed, timing, and square footage/rooms or site details.
6. **Medium:** The site contains visible template/platform residue that can lower trust.
   - The Wix banner, login/cart UI, duplicate starting-price text from booking widgets, and subscribe form make the page feel less finished.
7. **Medium:** SEO basics are inconsistent.
   - The homepage title/meta still reference “Optimum Cleaning Solutions,” while the page brand says Pure Form Cleaning Group. Service/location terms should be made consistent before implementation.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Rewrite the hero around the real buyer promise: janitorial, construction cleanup, and residential/rental cleaning with clear quote/book paths.
2. Add a top trust strip under the hero with direct proof/logistics: three service paths, starting prices, direct phone/email, and fast request guidance.
3. Keep the original stacked service-card layout but add “best for” copy and clearer CTAs for each category.
4. Replace generic “Book Now” repetition with action labels that match the buyer: “Request Janitorial Quote,” “Plan Construction Cleanup,” and “Schedule Residential Cleaning.”
5. Add a short “what happens next” section so leads know Pure Form will confirm scope, timing, crew size, and pricing.
6. Make the contact form cleaning-specific: property type, service need, address/site area, timing, and notes.
7. Keep the simple Wix look, large image blocks, muted buttons, and centered footer so the site feels like an improved version of the same business.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Change the homepage title to something like: `Pure Form Cleaning Group | Janitorial, Construction & Residential Cleaning`.
- Update the meta description so it matches the current business name, not Optimum Cleaning Solutions.
- Use one clear H1 focused on cleaning services rather than just the brand name.
- Add H2 sections for Services, What Happens Next, and Get in Touch.
- Add descriptive alt text for the hero, janitorial, construction, and residential images.
- Confirm and add the actual service area before adding local keywords.
- Add LocalBusiness/CleaningService structured data after phone, email, service area, and business details are confirmed.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a practical Wix homepage cleanup. The live site already has the images, logo, service cards, booking/pricing data, and contact information. Implementation would mainly reorganize existing sections, rewrite the hero and service-card text, remove or de-emphasize login/cart/subscription elements, improve the contact form prompts, and update SEO title/meta. No custom app or brand rebuild is needed.
