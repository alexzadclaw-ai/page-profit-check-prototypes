# High Shine Mobile Detailing — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
High Shine Mobile Detailing is a strong fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current Wix site already has a recognizable red/black auto-detailing look, real service photos, a logo, Sacramento contact details, and a clear mobile-detailing capability. The opportunity is not to redesign the brand. It is to make the first screen immediately answer: what do they do, where do they come to me, and how do I book?

A tighter version can preserve the Wix/site-builder rhythm, red panels, image grid, Lato-style typography, and service imagery while adding stronger CTA hierarchy, trust cues, and scannable packages.

**Recommendation:** preserve the red/white/black Wix design language and rebuild the homepage around one dominant action: **Book Mobile Detailing**.

## What is already working
- The site clearly identifies the business as **High Shine Mobile Detailing**.
- The homepage says they are mobile and can come to the customer’s home, business, beauty salon, grocery store, and other locations.
- The business states it is fully equipped with water, pressure washer, electricity, a carpet extractor, and detail supplies.
- Contact details are visible: PO Box 348656 Sacramento CA 95834, `highshinemobiledetails@gmail.com`, and `916-613-3966`.
- The site already has buyer-relevant services: car washing, ceramic coating, paint correction, wash & wax, and full detail.
- Real vehicle/detailing imagery gives the page a relevant automotive feel.
- There is a Book Online navigation item and a Recent Work navigation item, which can become strong conversion/trust paths.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The first screen is dominated by brand imagery and mission copy instead of a clear buyer promise and primary booking action.
   - Prototype response: keep the large black/logo hero but overlay a concise Sacramento mobile-detailing message and primary **Book Mobile Detailing** / **Call 916-613-3966** CTAs.

2. **Critical:** The booking hierarchy is weak: “Book Online” is only a small nav item, the live chat widget competes with contact options, and the form button is a generic “Submit.”
   - Prototype response: make booking the primary action in the header, hero, service package cards, and contact section; use specific CTA labels.

3. **High:** Strong trust/convenience details are buried in a paragraph instead of being scannable near the top.
   - Prototype response: add a compact trust strip calling out mobile service, self-contained water/electricity, carpet extraction, and Sacramento contact.

4. **High:** The service content is visually dense and hard to choose from; package bullets are treated like repeated headings and the page does not explain which service is for which need.
   - Prototype response: reorganize Wash & Wax, Full Detail, Ceramic Coating, and Paint Correction into clear service cards with short buyer-intent labels and bullets.

5. **High:** The “Our Mission” section starts with business-centered copy, while a visitor likely needs service area, package fit, and next step first.
   - Prototype response: rewrite that section as “Mobile detailing brought to you” while preserving the red panel and original mobile-equipment claims.

6. **Medium:** SEO basics are thin: the page title is generic, H1 is “OUR MISSION,” and many package bullets appear as H2s.
   - Prototype response: use a descriptive title/meta description, one service-focused H1, and cleaner H2/H3 hierarchy.

7. **Medium:** The contact section is present but does not tell visitors what to include in the request.
   - Prototype response: add a short “what happens next” / booking-prep section and ask for vehicle, location, and desired service.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Add a clear above-the-fold service promise: mobile detailing in Sacramento with wash, wax, full detail, ceramic coating, and paint correction options.
- Make **Book Mobile Detailing** and **Call 916-613-3966** the dominant actions.
- Preserve the original black logo hero, deep-red panels, white header, three image tiles, and gray package-photo section.
- Convert dense service lists into scan-friendly cards organized by buyer intent.
- Add top trust/convenience cues using public claims already on the page.
- Clarify what a visitor should send when requesting a booking: vehicle, location, desired service, and timing.
- Improve mobile experience with stacked sections, shorter paragraphs, tap-friendly phone/email links, and a sticky call/book bar.
- Tighten SEO title, meta description, H1, and heading hierarchy.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a descriptive homepage title such as `High Shine Mobile Detailing | Sacramento Mobile Car Detailing`.
- Write a concise meta description around Sacramento mobile detailing, wash & wax, full details, ceramic coating, paint correction, and on-site service.
- Replace “OUR MISSION” as the only H1 with a service/location-focused H1.
- Use H2s for Services, Mobile Convenience, What Happens Next, and Contact; avoid making every bullet an H2.
- Add descriptive alt text for the logo and vehicle/detailing images.
- Make phone and email links tap-friendly.
- Add LocalBusiness / AutoDetailing schema after final public service area, hours, and booking URL are confirmed.
