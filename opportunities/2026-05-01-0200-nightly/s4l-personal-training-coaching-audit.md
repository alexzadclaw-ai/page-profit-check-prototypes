# S4L Personal Training & Coaching — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
S4L Personal Training & Coaching is a strong **Solution A** candidate because the public Wix site already has recognizable assets: the bold blue S4L mark, the “Optimal Fitness Lifestyle” tagline, the large “3 FREE SESSIONS” offer, dark fitness imagery, yellow accent headings, blue booking buttons, Marietta contact details, and real training/program copy.

The main opportunity is not a rebrand. It is a focused Wix cleanup that keeps the same energetic S4L look while making one next step obvious: **book a first training session and understand what happens next.**

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## What is already working
- The S4L brand mark is distinctive and memorable.
- The site has a clear fitness category: personal training and coaching.
- “3 FREE SESSIONS” is a strong offer hook.
- A booking path exists through Book Online/Mindbody.
- The site includes a real Marietta location, email, and phone number.
- The program list covers strong buyer intents: weight loss, strength training, functional training, group training, nutrition counseling, meal plans, and program design.
- The philosophy has useful trust value: certified fitness professionals review goals and health history before laying out the program.
- The “hundreds” helped claim gives the page proof beyond generic fitness copy.
- Gym/training and transformation-style imagery help the site feel specific to S4L rather than generic.

## Biggest conversion issues
1. **Critical:** The first screen is visually memorable but does not clearly explain what a new visitor should do first or what they get when they book.
   - Prototype response: keep the same Wix/video-background feel and blue offer button, then add a clearer first-session hero panel.

2. **Critical:** CTA hierarchy is crowded. “Book online,” “3 FREE Sessions,” mailing-list free-session copy, subscribe, chat, shop/cart, and navigation links all compete.
   - Prototype response: make “Book a First Session” the primary path, with phone/contact as secondary actions.

3. **High:** The strongest trust signals are buried in paragraph copy: certified professionals, Marietta location, hundreds helped, goal review, health-history review, and the 3-phase program structure.
   - Prototype response: bring these proof points into a top trust strip immediately below the hero.

4. **High:** Services are presented as a dense “we do it all” list instead of goal-based paths.
   - Prototype response: reorganize services into scannable cards for weight loss, strength, functional movement, group training, program design, and nutrition.

5. **High:** The philosophy/process content is valuable but hard to connect to booking.
   - Prototype response: add a simple “What happens in the first session?” flow.

6. **Medium:** The “3 FREE SESSIONS” offer and mailing-list “1 FREE Session” offer create possible confusion.
   - Prototype response: keep the main 3-session offer dominant and make the mailing list secondary.

7. **Medium:** Template residue and placeholder-quality elements weaken trust, including the Wix-created footer/template feel and a YouTube link pointing to Wix.
   - Prototype response: remove obvious template residue from the user-facing page while preserving the Wix/site-builder design language.

8. **Medium:** Local SEO signals could be stronger. The title and headings should combine personal training, coaching, and Marietta more clearly.
   - Prototype response: add a Marietta-focused title/meta description and clearer service/location headings.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Preserve the recognizable S4L/Wix visual language: blue logo, blue CTAs, black panels, yellow headings, and fitness imagery.
2. Make “Book a First Session” the main action near the top.
3. Keep “3 FREE SESSIONS” as the offer hook but reduce competing free-session messages.
4. Move trust proof near the top: certified professionals, Marietta location, hundreds helped, and 3-phase plan.
5. Reorganize programs by visitor goal.
6. Add a first-session process so visitors know what happens after they click.
7. Improve readability by placing key text on clean dark panels.
8. Tighten local SEO structure around “Marietta personal training and coaching.”
