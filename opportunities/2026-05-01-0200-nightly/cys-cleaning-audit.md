# CYS Cleaning — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
CYS Cleaning is a strong fit for a **Solution A surgical cleanup** because the site already has a recognizable Wix service-business style: clean white space, simple black text, Raleway/Avenir-style typography, a plain header, real pricing, a testimonial, and direct phone/email contact. The biggest opportunity is not a visual rebrand; it is making the existing service-first experience easier to understand and easier to act on.

**Recommendation:** keep the same simple Wix look, three vertical cleaning images, centered headings, and plain black/white styling, but turn the service page into a clearer quote path with scan-friendly packages, pricing context, trust proof, and one stronger primary action: **Request a Cleaning Quote**.

**Priority:** B+

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** text-only `CYS CLEANING` wordmark; no graphic logo required.
- **Dominant colors:** white page background, black text, light gray header/nav band, translucent light-gray overlays over service images, Wix gray promo banner.
- **Typography feel:** Raleway-style uppercase headings for brand/page titles; Avenir-style light sans-serif body copy and navigation.
- **Header/nav pattern:** top Wix banner, large white header with brand at upper left, nav in a pale gray horizontal band, email/phone on the right, small Facebook icon.
- **Hero/page rhythm:** service page begins with centered `SERVICES`, `What We Do`, and a short intro, then a full-width three-column image section.
- **Image treatment:** three tall vertical service photos: cleaning supplies, mirror cleaning, and mopping; text floats on translucent gray panels over the lower parts of each image.
- **Service structure:** three package columns: Regular Cleaning starting at $175, Deep Cleaning starting at $375, and Empty Homes/Apts/Offices starting at $400.
- **CTA style:** understated text/button links such as `Get in Touch`; quote/contact forms exist on separate pages.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:** spacious white top section, centered uppercase headings, thin/plain buttons, exact package-price structure, direct `cyscleaning@yahoo.com` and `347-721-7825` contact details, and the testimonial from the homepage.

## What is already working
- CYS Cleaning lists real package pricing, which is a major trust advantage for a cleaning service.
- The three service categories are concrete and easy to understand once a visitor reaches them.
- Phone and email are visible in the header and footer.
- There is a dedicated Get a Quote page asking visitors to describe their space and service needs.
- The homepage includes a positive testimonial from Andrea Scott that supports trust and should be closer to the decision point.
- The live site feels calm and uncluttered, which fits a home-cleaning business.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** Visitors land on the Services page, but the first screen has no immediate quote path.
   - The page starts with `SERVICES`, generic copy, and no above-the-fold primary CTA. A visitor has to scroll to the packages or use the navigation to understand what to do next.

2. **Critical:** Package details are too dense inside image columns.
   - Pricing is useful, but long centered checklists over tall images make it difficult to compare Regular, Deep, and Empty Home cleaning quickly.

3. **High:** CTA hierarchy is buried and repetitive.
   - Each package ends with `Get in Touch`, but the wording is generic and the actual quote form lives elsewhere. The page should make `Request a Cleaning Quote` the obvious next step from the top, each package, and the final section.

4. **High:** The service-page-first navigation skips the strongest homepage proof.
   - The homepage has a useful testimonial and contact form, but a visitor entering `/services` will not see that trust proof before deciding whether to inquire.

5. **High:** Pricing lacks expectation-setting around scope.
   - The packages show starting prices, but key scoping information is buried in notes. Visitors need quick context such as best for, starting price, included highlights, and what details affect the quote.

6. **Medium:** The quote request does not appear inline with the service comparison.
   - The visitor may decide on a package while on the Services page, but then must navigate to a separate page or click a mailto link instead of having a guided quote request right there.

7. **Medium:** SEO basics are thin.
   - Title/OG title is only `Services | CYS Cleaning`; there is no visible location/service-area language in the page copy, and headings do not fully target cleaning-service search intent.

8. **Medium:** Template residue dates the site.
   - The Wix banner, small social icon, and `©2022` footer make the site feel less current, even though the business information is useful.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- Keep the same Wix-style header, typography, white/gray palette, and three cleaning photos so the owner recognizes the page immediately.
- Add a clearer top section: `Cleaning packages with upfront starting prices` plus phone/email and a primary quote CTA.
- Add a compact trust strip near the top: starting prices shown, direct contact, residential/empty-space options, and quote details requested before booking.
- Rebuild the three service packages into scan-friendly cards over the same photo columns: best for, starting price, 4–5 core inclusions, and one package-specific quote CTA.
- Move the testimonial from the homepage onto the service/quote journey.
- Add a simple “How to choose your cleaning” section that explains Regular vs Deep vs Empty Home/Office in plain language.
- Add an inline quote panel asking for service type, space size, timing, and special details before contacting CYS.
- Update footer date/contact and reduce template-feeling clutter where the Wix account allows.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a stronger title such as `CYS Cleaning | Home Cleaning Packages & Quote Request`.
- Use one H1 focused on service intent, e.g. `Cleaning packages with upfront starting prices`.
- Add a concise meta description that mentions regular cleaning, deep cleaning, empty homes/apartments/offices, quote requests, and the phone/email contact path.
- Add visible service-area language once the owner confirms the exact areas served.
- Use H2/H3 headings for the three package types, quote process, testimonial, and contact section.
- Add LocalBusiness / HouseCleaningService schema after name, phone, email, address/service area, and same-as links are confirmed.

## Realistic implementation angle
This should be positioned as a small Wix cleanup rather than a redesign. The work can be done by reordering and rewriting existing sections, reusing the current three cleaning photos, adding a stronger top CTA, turning the checklist content into comparison cards, pulling the testimonial into the service page, and making the quote form easier to reach. No new brand system is needed.
