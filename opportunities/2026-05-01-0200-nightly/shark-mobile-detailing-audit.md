## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** Wix advertising and “Proudly created with Wix.com” residue make the business look unfinished before a visitor judges the service.
   - Prototype response: remove Wix/banner/footer residue and replace it with a clean business footer.

2. **Critical:** The hero says “Welcome to Shark Mobile Detailing” and “Always at Your Service,” but it does not immediately say Dallas mobile auto detailing or show a strong phone-first booking CTA.
   - Prototype response: rewrite the hero around “Mobile Auto Detailing in Dallas,” add the phone number as the primary CTA, and keep the recognizable hero photo.

3. **High:** The about copy reads like keyword stuffing instead of a trustworthy local-service explanation.
   - Prototype response: rewrite the about section in normal customer language while preserving Dallas, Preston Hollow, and service-area context.

4. **High:** The service section is a list of search phrases with repeated generic blurbs, so buyers cannot quickly tell what to request.
   - Prototype response: turn the keyword list into clear service options: mobile detailing, exterior car cleaning, interior detailing, and package guidance.

5. **High:** There is no clear “what happens next” path before the form.
   - Prototype response: add a simple three-step call → choose service → get cleaned flow.

6. **Medium:** The contact form is generic and the submit path is less useful than the phone number for a mobile-detailing lead.
   - Prototype response: keep the form visual language but make the phone CTA prominent and add a sticky mobile call action.

7. **Medium:** SEO basics are thin: no meta description observed, generic title, keywords meta, and several H2s are lowercase keyword phrases.
   - Prototype response: add a cleaner title/meta description, one clearer H1, and H2/H3 structure that still includes Dallas and mobile auto detailing language naturally.
