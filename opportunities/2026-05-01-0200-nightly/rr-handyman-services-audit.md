# R&R's Handyman Service's — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
R&R's Handyman Service's already has the bones of a useful Wix homepage: a real logo area, a large handyman hero image, a visible estimate CTA, service imagery, local contact details, and email/phone access. The biggest opportunity is not a full redesign. It is a trust-and-scanability cleanup that keeps the dark Wix header, blue hero band, teal service sections, and three-service rhythm while making the page read like a credible local handyman service.

Priority: **A-**

## What is already working
- The homepage immediately says this is a handyman service and includes a prominent **Get An Estimate** button.
- The header includes logo, business name, phone CTA, navigation, email, and Facebook link.
- The page uses recognizable visual assets: a square blue logo, dark navy header, deep blue hero area, teal content sections, and practical service photos.
- Three core services are shown with images: deck staining, drywall repairs, and cabinet installation.
- Contact details are public and easy to find: Mardela Springs, MD, email, and phone.

## Biggest conversion issues
| Finding | Severity | Why it matters |
|---|---:|---|
| Business-name punctuation reads awkwardly throughout: “R&R'S Handyman Service's.” | **Critical** | It creates avoidable credibility friction before a visitor evaluates the work. |
| Hero headline is oversized and repetitive instead of saying what the visitor can hire them for. | **Critical** | The first screen does not quickly explain service area, job types, or why to click the estimate CTA. |
| The Wix ad/banner, Book Online/Bookings Checkout links, subscribe form, and “Proudly created with Wix.com” footer residue distract from the service path. | **High** | Template/platform clutter makes the business look less established and can send visitors away from the estimate action. |
| Service copy is long, repetitive, and generic across cards. | **High** | Visitors have to read too much to understand whether deck, drywall, cabinet, or small repair work fits their need. |
| Trust proof is thin near the top. | **High** | There are contact details, but no fast reassurance around locality, small-job friendliness, estimate process, or what happens next. |
| Contact path is split between phone, email, chat, subscribe, and booking labels. | **High** | Too many competing actions make the best next step less obvious. |
| Mobile scanability is likely weak because the desktop layout relies on large fixed sections and long centered text. | **Medium** | Home repair visitors often come from a phone and need quick call/email actions. |

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Clean the business-name treatment to **R&R's Handyman Services** while preserving recognizable wording.
2. Replace the repetitive hero headline with a service-led line: repairs, staining, drywall, installs, and local handyman help.
3. Keep the original hero photo and section colors, but improve overlay contrast and CTA clarity.
4. Add a concise trust strip right after the hero: local service, clear estimate path, and small jobs welcome.
5. Rewrite service cards into short buyer-intent summaries with quick bullet cues.
6. Replace the loose booking/subscribe flow with a simple three-step estimate process.
7. Keep email and phone visible in the header and contact section, with consistent estimate-focused CTAs.
8. Remove or downplay template residue in the improved page experience.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use one clear H1 with service + location language, for example: “Reliable Handyman Repairs in Mardela Springs, MD.”
- Add a meta description that names the main services and location.
- Keep service headings as H2/H3 content instead of relying on generic copy blocks.
- Add descriptive image alt text for the logo, handyman hero image, deck staining, tools/drywall, cabinet/window work, and tool banner.
- Replace apostrophe-heavy title formatting with a cleaner title: **R&R's Handyman Services | Mardela Springs, MD**.
- If this remains on Wix, remove unused booking pages from navigation if they are not active conversion paths.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a small Wix cleanup, not a rebuild. The existing site can keep the same logo, dark header, hero photo, blue/teal palette, three-service layout, and contact rhythm. The practical work is copy cleanup, CTA consolidation, removing unused template sections, adding a trust strip and estimate process, and making the page easier to scan on mobile.
