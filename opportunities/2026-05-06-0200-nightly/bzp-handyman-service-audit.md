# Biggest conversion issues

- **Critical:** The provided live URL currently resolves to a Wix 404 page, so prospects may never reach BZP's service information or contact details.
- **Critical:** The first screen needs an immediate, specific reason to call or email. A generic or missing hero message costs jobs from mobile visitors who only need a quick repair.
- **High:** Contact actions need to be more prominent and repeated. Phone and email should appear in the header, hero, and final contact block.
- **High:** Services need to be grouped by buyer intent rather than as a long mixed list. Homeowners scan for their exact problem, such as kitchen/bath, exterior, garage, or electrical fixtures.
- **High:** Trust should appear closer to the top. Local owner story, service area, free estimate, and customer comments should not be buried.
- **Medium:** SEO basics are thin if the page title, H1, and local service area are not explicit.
- **Medium:** Mobile scanning needs tighter cards, shorter paragraphs, and obvious next steps.
