# Patriot Pros GA, LLC Audit

## Quick take
Patriot Pros GA has a credible, local, service-first website with a strong owner story and useful contact details, but the homepage buries the service paths and trust signals behind Wix/template clutter and broad copy. A surgical cleanup should keep the patriotic/local visual feel while making the first screen answer: what they clean, where they serve, why to trust them, and how to request a free estimate.

- Header/nav pattern: top contact strip with email and phone, centered logo/business name/tagline, simple nav links, prominent “Get a Quote” style CTA.
- Structural cues to preserve: large photo hero, centered section titles with generous whitespace, side-by-side image/text blocks, simple card-like feature/services sections, patriotic ownership story.
- Cleanup direction: preserve the same Wix-style rhythm and imagery, but remove Wix promotional banner/template residue, replace vague CTAs with clear estimate actions, bring licensed/insured, service area, veteran/LEO ownership, and service choices closer to the top.

## What is already working
- **High:** Strong trust story: veteran and law-enforcement owned, local ownership, licensed and insured.
- **High:** Phone and email are visible on the page and repeated in the footer/contact area.
- **Medium:** The hero image immediately communicates exterior cleaning and pressure washing.
- **Medium:** The site has authentic local language for Chatham, Effingham, Bryan and Liberty Counties.
- **Medium:** Existing imagery, including before/after brick and flag/local-owned visuals, gives the site a recognizable local-business feel.

## Biggest conversion issues
- **Critical:** Wix promotional banner appears at the very top, making the business feel unfinished and distracting from the estimate action.
- **Critical:** The hero CTA is truncated as “GET A FREE ESTIMA...”, which weakens the main action and looks broken.
- **Critical:** The first screen says “Residential & Commercial Pressure & Power Washing” but does not immediately list specific jobs customers are trying to buy, such as house washing, driveways, storefronts, sidewalks, brick, patios, or property maintenance.
- **High:** Trust signals are present but not packaged near the first CTA. Licensed and insured, veteran/LEO owned, local counties served, and discount info should be scannable above or immediately below the hero.
- **High:** The homepage leans on broad quality language instead of direct buyer paths, so visitors must work to decide whether Patriot Pros handles their specific need.
- **High:** Navigation and CTAs send users to multiple places, while the fastest conversion path should be “call, email, or request a free estimate.”
- **Medium:** Mobile scanning is text-heavy. Long paragraphs should be split into short service cards, proof strips, and next-step bullets.
- **Medium:** Footer/contact form is useful but late in the page. A stronger mid-page CTA would catch visitors before they leave.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- **Critical:** Remove template/Wix promotional clutter from the improved homepage experience.
- **Critical:** Replace the truncated hero button with a clear “Get a Free Estimate” CTA and secondary “Call (912) 421-9310” action.
- **Critical:** Add task-based service cards near the top for residential, commercial, concrete/brick, and recurring property cleanup.
- **High:** Add a trust strip directly under the hero: licensed and insured, veteran and LEO owned, locally operated, military/first responder discount.
- **High:** Keep the same patriotic/local images but tighten copy into concise, conversion-focused sections.
- **High:** Add a “How to get started” section with three steps: send location/photos, receive estimate, schedule cleaning.
- **Medium:** Add service-area wording in the hero and footer for Chatham, Effingham, Bryan and Liberty Counties.

## SEO basics to tighten
- **High:** Homepage title should include primary service and geography, for example “Pressure Washing in Chatham, Effingham, Bryan & Liberty Counties | Patriot Pros GA.”
- **High:** The H1 should include “Residential & Commercial Pressure Washing” and the Georgia service area.
- **High:** Add crawlable service terms on the homepage: house washing, driveways, sidewalks, brick, patios, storefronts, commercial properties, power washing, pressure washing.
- **Medium:** Add descriptive alt text for major images, especially the hero and before/after work image.
- **Medium:** Include consistent NAP details: Patriot Pros GA, LLC, patriotprosga@gmail.com, (912) 421-9310, service counties.
- **Medium:** Link clearly to Facebook, Instagram, and Google Places with accessible labels.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a small cleanup sprint, not a rebuild. The existing Wix site already has the right ingredients: logo, hero image, local story, service area, phone/email, and credibility. The fastest path is to update the current homepage sections, remove Wix/template artifacts, tighten the hero/CTA, add task-based service blocks, and improve mobile spacing. A realistic ROM is **$350-$900** depending on how much Wix section editing, image cleanup, and mobile QA is included.
