## Biggest conversion issues
- **Critical:** The first screen does not immediately say the highest-value buyer promise in plain English. “Free Estimates” and warranty are present, but “licensed Arizona HVAC repair and installation” is not made into a clear hero headline.
- **Critical:** Primary calls to action are not specific enough for emergency/service buyers. “GET A FREE QUOTE!” exists, but the phone CTA is not presented as the obvious next action near every major section.
- **Critical:** Template leftovers hurt credibility: title reads “HOME | Mysite” and footer says “© 2023 by The Handy Gang. Proudly created with Wix.com.”
- **High:** Long intro/about paragraphs are hard to scan, especially on mobile, and contain grammar/spelling issues such as “Licenced,” “Comercial,” “availalbe,” and inconsistent capitalization.
- **High:** Services are a long flat list instead of task paths. Buyers need to quickly choose repair/service, replacement/installation, custom ductwork, or commercial/refrigeration.
- **High:** Trust proof is scattered. ROC number, insurance/bonding, warranty, discounts, Spanish support, and family ownership should appear close to the CTA.
- **Medium:** SEO basics are weak: generic page title, no clear H1 around “HVAC contractor,” and service-area language is not connected to service keywords.
- **Medium:** Contact section lacks a compact “how to get started” path for call, text, or email.
