# Quick take
Garcia Installation and Carpentry LLC already has the right ingredients for a small local carpentry site: a real company name, a simple promise, direct email/phone contact, service specificity, Instagram/social proof, and project photos. The main issue is that the Wix homepage feels unfinished and under-directed: the hero says quality carpentry, but it does not immediately tell a Tampa-area homeowner what to do next, what jobs are best-fit, or why to trust them before scrolling.

# Biggest conversion issues
- **Critical:** The live homepage has a Wix ad/template banner at the top, which distracts from the business and lowers trust before the user sees the contractor.
- **Critical:** The hero CTA is too vague. “Contact Me” does not say what the visitor gets, and the strongest contact message appears lower on the page.
- **Critical:** There is no clear service-area/local cue near the top, so Tampa-area relevance is weaker than it should be.
- **High:** Trust proof is buried in the About section rather than placed near the first decision point.
- **High:** Services are listed as a long raw bullet list instead of grouped around buyer intent: doors, trim/molding, windows, and repair/finish work.
- **High:** Email inconsistency appears in the live links: one CTA uses `garciainstallation@gmail.com`, while the visible contact and opportunity data use `garciainstallationllc@gmail.com`.
- **Medium:** Some service copy has capitalization/wording issues, such as “services” lowercase and “window installment,” which makes the page feel less polished.
- **Medium:** The contact form is generic and asks for input without explaining what happens after submission.
