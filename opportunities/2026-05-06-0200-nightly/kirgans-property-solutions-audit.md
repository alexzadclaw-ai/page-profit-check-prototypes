# Kirgan's Property Solutions, LLC

## Quick take
Kirgan's Property Solutions has the bones of a trustworthy local contractor page: real names, direct phone numbers, clear service categories, and real work photos. The biggest issue is that the current Wix page reads more like a basic brochure than a job-winning landing page. It has a Wix ad banner at the top, a very large generic welcome headline, a long ungrouped service list, duplicated phone text, and no fast path for urgent tree, storm cleanup, excavation, or land clearing requests.

## What is already working
- **High:** Real business names are present. Chad and Troy Kirgan are introduced, which helps the site feel local and owner-operated.
- **High:** Services are specific and useful: tree removal, storm clean-up, land clearing, excavating, hauling, driveways, ponds, ditches, and dirt work.
- **High:** Direct contact information is visible, including email and two phone numbers.
- **Medium:** The site uses real work imagery instead of generic stock-only visuals.
- **Medium:** The orange `Get In Touch` button creates a small brand accent that can be reused for stronger calls to action.

## Biggest conversion issues
- **Critical:** The Wix promotional banner is the first visible item, making the business look less established and distracting people before they see the offer.
- **Critical:** The hero headline says `WELCOME TO KIRGAN'S PROPERTY SOLUTIONS` but does not immediately explain what problems they solve or where they work.
- **Critical:** The main call to action is not specific enough for urgent contractor searches. A homeowner with storm damage, a tree issue, or excavation need should instantly see `Call for a Free Estimate`.
- **High:** Trust is buried in paragraph copy instead of being pulled near the top as quick proof: family-owned, dependable, honest, free estimates, equipment ready.
- **High:** The service list is a single long block, so buyers cannot quickly find their path: tree service, storm/debris cleanup, excavating/dirt work, land clearing/under brushing.
- **High:** The phone number appears duplicated, which makes the page feel unfinished.
- **Medium:** There is no clear service area language beyond the broader opportunity data, so local searchers may not know if the company serves their property.
- **Medium:** Image captions and section labels do not explain the types of jobs shown.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- **Critical:** Replace the generic welcome hero with a task-based hero: tree service, excavating, land clearing, storm clean-up, and free estimates in the Quad Cities area.
- **Critical:** Move phone and email actions into prominent buttons above the fold.
- **High:** Add a compact trust strip directly under the hero with `Family-owned`, `Free estimates`, `Tree and dirt work`, and `Weekend/equipment-ready help`.
- **High:** Convert the long service list into four buyer paths: Tree Work, Storm and Debris Clean-up, Excavating and Dirt Work, Land Clearing and Outdoor Projects.
- **High:** Keep the authentic work photos but use them with cleaner spacing, captions, and mobile stacking.
- **Medium:** Remove duplicated contact text and make the contact section read like a quote request path.
- **Medium:** Add plain local SEO copy for Illinois / Quad Cities-area service searches.

## SEO basics to tighten
