# JPDS General Contractors, LLC. Audit

## Quick take
JPDS General Contractors has the bones of a trustworthy local remodeling site: real project photos, clear Cincinnati contact details, and a simple Wix-style structure. The biggest opportunity is not a redesign. It is a surgical cleanup that keeps the same light, centered, contractor portfolio feel while making the first screen explain what JPDS does, where it works, why to trust it, and exactly how to request a free estimate.

Visual fingerprint captured before coding:
- Source URL: https://jpdsgc.wixsite.com/website
- Screenshot captured: `captures/2026-05-06-0200-nightly/jpds-general-contractors/home-full.png`
- Logo/name treatment: text-only uppercase `JPDS GENERAL CONTRACTORS, LLC.` in a thin Brandon Grotesque/Open Sans style.
- Dominant colors: white and very light gray, dark charcoal text `rgb(57, 63, 68)`, pale blue-gray section band `rgb(229, 240, 241)`, muted teal active-nav accent around `rgb(112, 178, 185)`, dark CTA button.
- Type feel: light-weight, spacious, uppercase headings, Arial/Open Sans/Brandon-style sans serif.
- Header/nav pattern: top contact strip with email and phone, centered brand line, simple right-side Home/Services nav.
- Structural cues to preserve: large centered hero image, oversized centered headline/subheadline, pale blue-gray intro band, stacked service photo panels with centered uppercase titles and small CTA text, simple contact/footer.
- Cleanup direction: keep the same Wix/site-builder rhythm and real project-photo feel, but remove template promotion leftovers, sharpen hero copy, add trust/area details near the top, group services by buyer intent, and make phone/email CTAs obvious on desktop and mobile.

## What is already working
- **High:** Real project images make the company feel legitimate and hands-on.
- **High:** Phone number, email, address, and Facebook link are present.
- **High:** The site clearly says it serves Greater Cincinnati/Northern Kentucky and has been in business since 2014 with over 20 years in remodeling.
- **Medium:** The simple two-page structure is easy to understand.
- **Medium:** Service categories match valuable remodeling searches: decks, basements, kitchens, baths, tile, carpentry, exterior improvements, painting.

## Biggest conversion issues
- **Critical:** The live page shows Wix promotional/template leftovers at the top and footer, which weakens credibility for a contractor asking homeowners to trust them with remodeling work.
- **Critical:** The hero headline says `INTRODUCING JPDS GENERAL CONTRACTORS, LLC.` instead of quickly saying what they do, where they work, and what action to take.
- **Critical:** The main CTA is generic `Get In Touch`; it does not directly reinforce the free estimate offer, phone option, or email path.
- **High:** Trust proof is buried in paragraph text instead of being visible near the top: since 2014, 20+ years industry experience, customer satisfaction priority, Greater Cincinnati/Northern Kentucky coverage.
- **High:** Services are listed as broad labels with repeated generic CTAs, but not organized around buyer tasks such as decks/exteriors, kitchens/baths/basements, and custom carpentry/repairs.
- **High:** The mobile experience likely requires too much scrolling before a homeowner understands services, trust, and next steps.
- **Medium:** Service photos are strong but need captions that connect each image to buyer intent.
- **Medium:** The contact form area has minimal guidance and the footer repeats the default Wix credit.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
- **Critical:** Replace template promotion and generic hero copy with a credibility-first local remodeling message.
- **Critical:** Make the primary action `Request a Free Estimate` and pair it with visible phone/email options.
- **High:** Add a compact trust strip directly under the hero: serving Greater Cincinnati/Northern Kentucky, since 2014, 20+ years remodeling experience, free estimates.
- **High:** Rebuild the services section as task paths while preserving the existing photo-card rhythm.
- **High:** Move next-step instructions above the contact section: call/email, describe project, schedule estimate.
- **Medium:** Tighten microcopy for common homeowner projects and improve scanability on mobile.

## SEO basics to tighten
- **High:** Use a clearer H1 such as `Remodeling, Decks & Custom Carpentry in Greater Cincinnati / Northern Kentucky`.
- **High:** Add location/service language naturally near the top: Cincinnati, Northern Kentucky, decks, remodeling, kitchens, bathrooms, basements, custom carpentry, exterior improvements.
- **Medium:** Improve image alt text from file names to descriptive project phrases.
- **Medium:** Add a concise meta title and description if this is implemented in the actual Wix site.
- **Medium:** Create service detail sections or pages for decks, kitchens, bathrooms, basements, and custom carpentry if the owner wants stronger local search reach later.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a safe cleanup sprint, not a full rebuild. The fastest realistic path is to update the existing Wix site: remove the Wix ad/credit if possible by plan upgrade or custom publishing, rewrite hero/services/contact sections, tighten mobile spacing, add trust strip, and keep the existing photo assets. If staying on the current platform, a practical cleanup sprint is likely **$350-$900** depending on how much mobile polish, copywriting, and service-page work is included.

## Offer fit
Good fit for a $99 Page Profit Check because the site has visible conversion leaks that are easy to explain and easy to fix without requiring a brand overhaul. The owner should immediately recognize the improved version as the same business, just clearer and more credible.
