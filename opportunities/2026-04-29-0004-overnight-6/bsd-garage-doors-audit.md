# BSD Garage Doors — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
BSD Garage Doors is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current site already has recognizable brand material, local service proof, and a direct contact path — but the first-screen decision flow can be made clearer without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current site family and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Schedule Garage Door Service**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #121826, #e33b2f, #f5f6f8, #0b1020.
- **Typography feel:** Practical local-service sans-serif body copy with stronger serif-style headings for hierarchy.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Phone/contact-forward local service navigation with fewer competing choices.
- **Hero/image treatment:** Same contractor/service-business positioning, but with a clearer estimate or schedule action.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:**
- dark/red urgent-service style
- phone-first emergency feel
- online booking discount
- large service-area coverage
- garage repair/install service mix

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with clear service intent in Naperville / Chicagoland, IL.
- The site already communicates emergency garage door service.
- The site already communicates online booking discount.
- The site already communicates residential service specialists.
- The live homepage confirms a relevant service mix: Broken springs, Garage door repair, Opener service, New doors + installation.
- There is a direct contact, quote, estimate, appointment, or booking path that can be made easier to notice.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The homepage/nav creates heavy location-menu friction before an urgent homeowner can choose emergency service or booking.
   - Prototype response: Make emergency vs planned booking the first decision instead of forcing users through large menus.
2. **High:** The 10% online booking offer and emergency service message need clearer CTA hierarchy.
   - Prototype response: Condense city/menu sprawl so Naperville homeowners see service fit first.
3. **High:** Repair, opener, springs, and new-door services should be grouped by problem type near the top.
   - Prototype response: Pull booking discount, emergency service, and residential expertise into a top trust row.
4. **High:** Large location lists and menu flyouts make the site feel less local and more directory-like.
   - Prototype response: Add a simple repair/install path before the online booking CTA.
5. **Medium:** Older formatting, menu density, repeated copy, or service-area sprawl makes the page feel heavier than the actual business value.
   - Prototype response: Keep the bold dark/red garage-service look and direct phone emphasis.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Make emergency vs planned booking the first decision instead of forcing users through large menus.
2. Keep the bold dark/red garage-service look and direct phone emphasis.
3. Condense city/menu sprawl so Naperville homeowners see service fit first.
4. Pull booking discount, emergency service, and residential expertise into a top trust row.
5. Add a simple repair/install path before the online booking CTA.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
