# Blue Jay Appliance Services — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Blue Jay Appliance Services is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current site already has recognizable brand material, local service proof, and a direct contact path — but the first-screen decision flow can be made clearer without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current site family and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Schedule Appliance Repair**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #0f5e9c, #20a8e0, #eff8ff, #0f2437.
- **Typography feel:** Practical local-service sans-serif body copy with stronger serif-style headings for hierarchy.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Phone/contact-forward local service navigation with fewer competing choices.
- **Hero/image treatment:** Same contractor/service-business positioning, but with a clearer estimate or schedule action.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:**
- blue local appliance-repair identity
- same/next-day promise
- repair category breadth
- warranty and senior discount language
- Naperville service-area focus

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with clear service intent in Naperville, IL.
- The site already communicates same or next-day service.
- The site already communicates 90-day parts/labor warranty.
- The site already communicates upfront pricing.
- The live homepage confirms a relevant service mix: Refrigerators + freezers, Washers + dryers, Dishwashers, Ranges + ovens.
- There is a direct contact, quote, estimate, appointment, or booking path that can be made easier to notice.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** Broken-appliance visitors see too many service and location choices before the schedule/call path feels decisive.
   - Prototype response: Keep the blue appliance-service feel while making “Schedule Appliance Repair” the clear first action.
2. **High:** Same/next-day service, 90-day warranty, upfront pricing, and senior discount are strong but need earlier grouping.
   - Prototype response: Move same/next-day, warranty, upfront pricing, and senior discount proof into a trust strip.
3. **High:** Appliance repair categories should be organized by common urgent symptoms rather than a wall of cards.
   - Prototype response: Push long location lists lower so urgent visitors can act faster.
4. **High:** Google review proof is valuable but should support the CTA instead of extending the scroll before action.
   - Prototype response: Add a short “what happens next” repair flow before the contact section.
5. **Medium:** Older formatting, menu density, repeated copy, or service-area sprawl makes the page feel heavier than the actual business value.
   - Prototype response: Condense many appliance cards into four high-demand repair paths near the top.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Keep the blue appliance-service feel while making “Schedule Appliance Repair” the clear first action.
2. Condense many appliance cards into four high-demand repair paths near the top.
3. Move same/next-day, warranty, upfront pricing, and senior discount proof into a trust strip.
4. Push long location lists lower so urgent visitors can act faster.
5. Add a short “what happens next” repair flow before the contact section.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
