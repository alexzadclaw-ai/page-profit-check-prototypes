# Paint-Right Painting Inc. — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Paint-Right Painting Inc. is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current site already has recognizable brand material, local service proof, and a direct contact path — but the first-screen decision flow can be made clearer without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current site family and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Request a Free Painting Quote**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #213b6b, #bf1e2e, #f6f3ee, #17213a.
- **Typography feel:** Practical local-service sans-serif body copy with stronger serif-style headings for hierarchy.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Phone/contact-forward local service navigation with fewer competing choices.
- **Hero/image treatment:** Same contractor/service-business positioning, but with a clearer estimate or schedule action.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:**
- family-owned Naperville identity
- 1980 longevity proof
- red/blue traditional contractor feel
- interior and exterior service split
- direct phone/email quote path

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with clear service intent in Naperville, IL.
- The site already communicates family owned since 1980.
- The site already communicates a+ bbb rating.
- The site already communicates 40+ years in naperville.
- The live homepage confirms a relevant service mix: Interior painting, Exterior painting, Residential projects, Commercial projects.
- There is a direct contact, quote, estimate, appointment, or booking path that can be made easier to notice.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The quote path is present but visually underpowered after seasonal text, social links, and older navigation.
   - Prototype response: Make “Request a Free Painting Quote” the primary action above the fold.
2. **High:** A+ BBB rating and 40+ year story are strong trust proof but not packaged for quick scanning.
   - Prototype response: Pull BBB rating, 40+ years, and trusted paint brands into a fast trust strip.
3. **High:** Interior and exterior service choices need clearer buyer paths before the contact form.
   - Prototype response: Separate interior and exterior paths so seasonal buyers can self-select quickly.
4. **High:** Older copy/formatting reduces the perceived polish of a very established local company.
   - Prototype response: Turn the bottom quote form into an intentional next-step section.
5. **Medium:** Older formatting, menu density, repeated copy, or service-area sprawl makes the page feel heavier than the actual business value.
   - Prototype response: Keep the family-owned/since-1980 story but reduce scattered announcements and duplicate menus.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Make “Request a Free Painting Quote” the primary action above the fold.
2. Keep the family-owned/since-1980 story but reduce scattered announcements and duplicate menus.
3. Pull BBB rating, 40+ years, and trusted paint brands into a fast trust strip.
4. Separate interior and exterior paths so seasonal buyers can self-select quickly.
5. Turn the bottom quote form into an intentional next-step section.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
