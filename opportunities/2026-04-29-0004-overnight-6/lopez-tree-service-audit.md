# Lopez Tree Service Corp. — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Lopez Tree Service Corp. is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current site already has recognizable brand material, local service proof, and a direct contact path — but the first-screen decision flow can be made clearer without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current site family and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Schedule a Tree Service Estimate**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #1f5b32, #f2a51a, #f7f4ec, #153220.
- **Typography feel:** Practical local-service sans-serif body copy with stronger serif-style headings for hierarchy.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Phone/contact-forward local service navigation with fewer competing choices.
- **Hero/image treatment:** Same contractor/service-business positioning, but with a clearer estimate or schedule action.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:**
- green/orange local tree-service look
- Oliverio Lopez owner story
- call and text behavior
- tree/landscape/winter service breadth
- Naperville address and hours

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with clear service intent in Naperville, IL.
- The site already communicates established in 2001.
- The site already communicates family owned and operated.
- The site already communicates fully insured and bonded.
- The live homepage confirms a relevant service mix: Tree trimming, Tree removal, Stump removal, Landscaping + winter services.
- There is a direct contact, quote, estimate, appointment, or booking path that can be made easier to notice.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** A contact form appears before the value story, so visitors are asked to act before trust and service fit are clear.
   - Prototype response: Lead with the owner/local story before the form so visitors understand who they are contacting.
2. **High:** Established 2001, family-owned, insured/bonded, and owner credibility should appear earlier.
   - Prototype response: Move established-2001, family-owned, bonded/insured, and call/text proof near the top.
3. **High:** Tree service, landscaping, and winter offerings need buyer-friendly grouping instead of scattered menu links.
   - Prototype response: Organize tree trimming, removal, stump, landscaping, and winter paths into clean cards.
4. **High:** Duplicated contact/footer sections and payment-fee notes distract from scheduling an estimate.
   - Prototype response: Reduce duplicated footer/contact blocks and make payment-fee notes secondary.
5. **Medium:** Older formatting, menu density, repeated copy, or service-area sprawl makes the page feel heavier than the actual business value.
   - Prototype response: Make “Schedule a Tree Service Estimate” the consistent CTA across hero and contact.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Lead with the owner/local story before the form so visitors understand who they are contacting.
2. Make “Schedule a Tree Service Estimate” the consistent CTA across hero and contact.
3. Move established-2001, family-owned, bonded/insured, and call/text proof near the top.
4. Organize tree trimming, removal, stump, landscaping, and winter paths into clean cards.
5. Reduce duplicated footer/contact blocks and make payment-fee notes secondary.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
