# Marquise Plumbing & Backflow Inc. — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Marquise Plumbing & Backflow Inc. is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current site already has recognizable brand material, local service proof, and a direct contact path — but the first-screen decision flow can be made clearer without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current site family and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Make a Plumbing Appointment**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #1155a3, #2db5e8, #eef7fc, #0e2438.
- **Typography feel:** Practical local-service sans-serif body copy with stronger serif-style headings for hierarchy.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Phone/contact-forward local service navigation with fewer competing choices.
- **Hero/image treatment:** Same contractor/service-business positioning, but with a clearer estimate or schedule action.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:**
- blue hometown plumber identity
- Naperville local positioning
- mom-and-pop language
- Backflow RPZ and plumbing service mix
- phone/text appointment emphasis

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with clear service intent in Naperville, IL.
- The site already communicates 30+ years of experience.
- The site already communicates licensed, bonded, insured.
- The site already communicates backflow rpz testing.
- The live homepage confirms a relevant service mix: Backflow RPZ testing, Sump pumps, Water heaters, Drain cleaning.
- There is a direct contact, quote, estimate, appointment, or booking path that can be made easier to notice.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** Trust is hurt by duplicated feature labels and visible copy errors near the top of a high-trust plumbing site.
   - Prototype response: Make “Make a Plumbing Appointment” the dominant action in the hero and contact block.
2. **High:** License/bond/insurance and 30+ years are valuable proof but sit too low and too small.
   - Prototype response: Move license/bond/insurance, 30+ years, and backflow specialty into the trust strip.
3. **High:** Backflow, sump pump, water heater, and drain services need simpler paths than repeated generic service links.
   - Prototype response: Organize plumbing, backflow, sump, water heater, and drain paths by common homeowner need.
4. **High:** The appointment CTA is present but competes with social icons, duplicated phone blocks, and broad “qualified agents” language.
   - Prototype response: Add a short appointment expectation so urgent visitors know what happens next.
5. **Medium:** Older formatting, menu density, repeated copy, or service-area sprawl makes the page feel heavier than the actual business value.
   - Prototype response: Replace duplicated icon labels and typo-prone copy with concise proof points.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Make “Make a Plumbing Appointment” the dominant action in the hero and contact block.
2. Replace duplicated icon labels and typo-prone copy with concise proof points.
3. Move license/bond/insurance, 30+ years, and backflow specialty into the trust strip.
4. Organize plumbing, backflow, sump, water heater, and drain paths by common homeowner need.
5. Add a short appointment expectation so urgent visitors know what happens next.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
