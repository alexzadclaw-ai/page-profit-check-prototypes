# Nortek Environmental, Inc. — Page Profit Check Audit

## Quick take
Nortek Environmental, Inc. is a good fit for a **Solution A surgical homepage cleanup** because the current site already has recognizable brand material, local service proof, and a direct contact path — but the first-screen decision flow can be made clearer without making the business feel redesigned.

**Recommendation:** preserve the current site family and rebuild the homepage around one clear primary action: **Schedule HVAC Service**.

**Visual resemblance:** High  
**Audit coverage target:** High

## Site fingerprint
- **Logo/brand assets:** Business-name treatment preserved in a cleaner header.
- **Dominant colors:** #153d6f, #d7432f, #f3f7fb, #0d223a.
- **Typography feel:** Practical local-service sans-serif body copy with stronger serif-style headings for hierarchy.
- **Header/nav pattern:** Phone/contact-forward local service navigation with fewer competing choices.
- **Hero/image treatment:** Same contractor/service-business positioning, but with a clearer estimate or schedule action.
- **Section rhythm:** Hero, trust strip, service cards, proof band, process block, contact section.
- **Recognizable quirks to preserve:**
- blue/red HVAC contractor identity
- family-owned local story
- heating/cooling/air-quality/maintenance categories
- schedule-service CTA
- review and guarantee tone

## What is already working
- The business has a live website with clear service intent in Naperville / Aurora / DuPage County, IL.
- The site already communicates family-owned local hvac.
- The site already communicates 25+ years of 5-star mindset.
- The site already communicates residential + commercial.
- The live homepage confirms a relevant service mix: Heating, Cooling, Indoor air quality, Maintenance plans.
- There is a direct contact, quote, estimate, appointment, or booking path that can be made easier to notice.

## Biggest conversion issues

1. **Critical:** The giant navigation and service-area menu create decision overload before visitors choose heating, cooling, IAQ, or maintenance.
   - Prototype response: Keep the established HVAC color/brand feel while simplifying the first-screen choices.
2. **High:** Family-owned, local, 25+ years, and review proof should support the first CTA more directly.
   - Prototype response: Move family-owned, 25+ years, reviews, and residential/commercial proof near the hero.
3. **High:** The homepage needs four clear service paths instead of a long menu dump.
   - Prototype response: Group the huge HVAC menu into heating, cooling, IAQ, and maintenance paths.
4. **High:** Schedule-service and phone CTAs should be consistent across the top, process, and contact sections.
   - Prototype response: Place service-area detail lower so the homepage starts with customer intent.
5. **Medium:** Older formatting, menu density, repeated copy, or service-area sprawl makes the page feel heavier than the actual business value.
   - Prototype response: Make “Schedule HVAC Service” the consistent primary action.

## Best Page Profit Check improvements
1. Keep the established HVAC color/brand feel while simplifying the first-screen choices.
2. Make “Schedule HVAC Service” the consistent primary action.
3. Move family-owned, 25+ years, reviews, and residential/commercial proof near the hero.
4. Group the huge HVAC menu into heating, cooling, IAQ, and maintenance paths.
5. Place service-area detail lower so the homepage starts with customer intent.

## SEO basics to tighten
- Use a homepage title that combines primary service + location + brand.
- Write a concise meta description around the main service and CTA.
- Keep one clear H1 and use H2s for service categories.
- Add descriptive alt text for brand and service images if implemented in the live CMS.
- Add LocalBusiness/Service schema after the approved copy is finalized.

## Realistic implementation angle
This is a focused homepage cleanup sprint, not a rebrand. The existing site should keep its recognizable look while tightening the top section, CTA hierarchy, service grouping, trust proof, and contact expectations.
