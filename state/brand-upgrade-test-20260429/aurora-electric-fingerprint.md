## Dominant colors
- Red: `#cb1e26` / deep overlay red in hero and angled divider areas.
- Yellow CTA: `#f0de36` with dark text and round/pill buttons.
- Teal-blue identity: `#065c75`, used in logo and supporting brand color.
- Light gray page base: `#e7e6e6`.
- Dark text: `#242424`; white text over hero.

## Typography feel
- Contractor/local-service sans-serif body, close to Ubuntu.
- Handwritten/brush-style uppercase accent headings, close to Kalam, used for eyebrow phrases like “YOUR TRUSTED LOCAL ELECTRICIAN...” and “OUR SERVICES.”
- Large bold rounded sans-serif hero/service headings rather than sharp corporate display type.

## Header/nav rhythm
- Wide hero header over a photographic background.
- Logo sits in a white rounded rectangle/card at the upper left, not as plain text.
- Compact top navigation floats across the top center with small white links and a Services dropdown cue.
- Large yellow pill phone button at the upper right.
- The live site has duplicate desktop/mobile nav in HTML, but visually the desktop rhythm is logo card + compact nav + phone pill.

## Recognition sentence
Aurora Electric is recognizable through the real bulb/AE logo in a white card, red-tinted electrician hero image, yellow pill CTAs, red angled wave divider, Kalam-style accent labels, four white service cards overlapping the red band, and the service truck image below.
