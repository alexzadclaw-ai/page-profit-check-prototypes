## Visual DNA
- Dominant colors: red `#cb1e26`, yellow `#f0de36`, teal-blue `#065c75`, light gray `#e7e6e6`, dark text `#242424`.
- Typography feel: practical rounded contractor sans-serif close to Ubuntu, with Kalam-style handwritten uppercase accent labels.
- Header/nav rhythm: white logo card at upper left over a photo hero, compact white nav across the top, oversized yellow pill phone CTA at upper right.

## Non-negotiable structural cues
1. Photo hero with centered headline/CTA stack over a red-tinted electrician background, ending in a large red angled/wave band.
2. Four white service cards overlap the red band immediately under the hero, with yellow circular arrow buttons and residential/commercial/industrial/emergency service rhythm.
3. Light-gray intro section follows with the real Aurora Electric truck image offset beside a bold local-electrician message and compact trust/discount/warranty proof.

## Recognition note
Aurora Electric should remain recognizable through the real AE bulb logo in a white card, red/yellow/truck/angled-band composition, Kalam-style service labels, and the four-card service overlap rhythm.

## Upgrade note
The current prototype already preserves the structural skeleton while improving the first-screen CTA stack, making 24/7 / 40+ years / A+ BBB / warranty proof visible immediately, and clarifying the four service paths without falling into a generic contractor template.

## Control decision
No prototype code change was justified in this rerun: the current Aurora Electric direction is the good control case referenced by the standard, and changing it would risk making it worse.
