## Reuse/fidelity notes

- Prototype uses only original Wix-hosted Loper Painting photos/images extracted from the live DOM.
- No Unsplash, Pexels, Pixabay, or generic stock image URLs were added.
- The prototype preserves the black/gold palette, serif headline feel, photo-led hero, gallery/review/contact rhythm, Overland Park contact details, and painting-service identity.
- Live social icons pointed to Wix default social URLs, so they were not reused as business proof.
- The source logo image appeared low-resolution/broken in the captured header, so the prototype uses a text LP mark that matches the site identity instead of inventing a new logo.
